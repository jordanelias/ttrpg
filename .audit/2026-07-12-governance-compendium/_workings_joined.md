# Section 2.10 — Trade-route monopoly/franchise dynamics and merchant-capital power shifts (Economic)

*Key: `trade_route_monopoly_merchant_capital_events`. The through-line: **a Concession/Compact/Charter is a tag that drifts out of sync with the real balance of Standing**, and the verbs that reconcile it (Charter Review, Sunset Review, Underwrite, Capital Partnership, Monopsony Enforcement) either convert political leverage into extraction rights or convert accumulated Debt/Standing into a rank-ladder shift — often via Conquest.*

---

### HEV-TRADE-01 — Hanseatic Kontor system / revocation of the London Steelyard (London/Hanseatic League, 1598) · Petition
- **Historical grounding:** The revocation of the Hanseatic League's London Steelyard privileges in 1598, ending centuries of the Kontor foreign-franchise trading-post system. Citations: Lilienfeld, Eras Journal (Monash); Britannica; MoEML.
- **Trigger:** `a foreign-franchise Concession (customs/jurisdiction exemption) age≥N AND a rival domestic Guild's Standing ≥ threshold AND no reciprocal Concession abroad AND the exempted Levy yield reduces net Treasury by ≥X%`
- **Response branches:**
  - Comply (revoke): domestic Guild Standing+, foreign Disposition−, writes `Charter Revoked Under Petition` Precedent.
  - Bargain (renegotiate reciprocity): AP/Clerk cost, Concession renewed with a reciprocity clause.
  - Defy (protect the old franchise): domestic Guild Disposition−, resentment accrues.
- **Follow-on (FO):** Defy escalates to Friction/Crisis (guild smuggling/boycott); Comply seeds a retaliation Intrigue abroad (against Valorian merchants in foreign ports).
- **Introduces (Action):** Charter Review (Quo Warranto sub-option on Levy): audit a long-standing foreign Concession's reciprocity before a rival Guild's Petition forces it.
- **Loop:** An unreviewed unreciprocated Concession drains Levy yield while the rival Guild's Standing climbs until the Petition auto-fires; Charter Review pre-empts the escalation on the Crown's terms, while neglect either way writes a Precedent that changes how every future foreign Concession is negotiated.

---

### HEV-TRADE-02 — Fugger mining monopolies / Habsburg election financing (Holy Roman Empire, 1519) · Opportunity
- **Historical grounding:** The Fugger banking house's financing of Charles V's 1519 imperial election in exchange for mining/extraction concessions. Citations: Wikipedia Fugger/Jakob Fugger; Atlas Obscura; fuggerstrasse.eu.
- **Trigger:** `Treasury short of a Directive-mandated quota (election/war levy) AND a Banking/Guild faction Standing ≥ threshold with capital AND no existing Debt tag links it to the Crown`
- **Response branches:**
  - Comply (accept the loan): immediate infusion, but a Debt tag AND a Concession tag — mining/extraction rights transferred — plus the faction's Standing rises.
  - Bargain (partial loan/partial concession).
  - Defy (decline): opportunity forgone, Directive risks unmet → Grudge with the PA.
- **Follow-on (FO):** An unresolved Debt seeds recurring Levy "interest" Friction; repeated Comply seeds an Ambition where the Banking faction leverages stacked Concessions to claim a Ministry/Consulta seat.
- **Introduces (Action):** Underwrite (Sponsor sub-option: a capital-holding faction finances a Directive shortfall for a Concession on a specific extractive resource).
- **Loop:** Underwrite converts a one-time shortfall into a durable Debt+Concession pair raising the lender's Standing and Ministry eligibility; each subsequent unmet Directive raises the odds the Opportunity re-fires with the same faction, compounding its capture of extraction rights.

---

### HEV-TRADE-03 — Mali Empire's trans-Saharan gold-salt monopoly (Mali Empire, medieval) · Thread
- **Historical grounding:** The Mali Empire's sustained concealment of the exact source/route of its trans-Saharan gold-salt trade, protecting toll revenue. Citations: Oxford Global Capitalism Project (Mansa Musa); gold-trade histories on production secrecy.
- **Trigger:** `a settlement on the sole node of a trade route (chokepoint) AND Levy transit-toll income exceeds a threshold share of Treasury AND no "Route Known" tag exists for a rival`
- **Response branches:**
  - Sustained concealment keeps toll Prosperity elevated (the baseline held state).
  - Bargain (selling route knowledge): yields a one-time gain but risks writing "Route Known".
  - Defy (a rival investigates): risks the Thread resolving to Crisis if the secret breaks.
- **Follow-on (FO):** If broken, a "Route Bypassed" Crisis (permanent Levy-yield loss); if held across its arc, an Ambition where the Guild petitions for elevated rank on sustained wealth.
- **Introduces (Action):** Conceal Source (Keep Order information-control sub-option: spend AP/Clerk Capacity each season to suppress the "Route Known" tag, keeping the Thread dormant).
- **Loop:** Spending Keep Order on Conceal Source preserves toll Prosperity; skipping lets Π creep toward Crisis discovery, and once discovered the toll's yield permanently drops — which also retires Conceal Source, since there's no secret left to protect.

---

### HEV-TRADE-04 — VOC conquest of the Banda Islands / nutmeg monopoly (Banda Islands, 1621) · Petition
- **Historical grounding:** The Dutch East India Company's 1621 conquest of the Banda Islands to enforce its nutmeg monopoly after repeated local repudiation of exclusivity terms. Citations: Wikipedia Dutch conquest of Banda; Duke FinReg Blog.
- **Trigger:** `a chartered Company/Guild holds a Compact with a single-resource settlement AND its compliance with exclusivity falls below threshold (repeated Defy on Levy terms) AND the Company's Muster ≥ Conquest threshold`
- **Response branches:**
  - Comply (settlement honors exclusivity): modest Levy flows.
  - Bargain (renegotiate): escalation delayed.
  - Defy (settlement repudiates): escalates to Crisis, unlocking Conquest eligibility.
- **Follow-on (FO):** Repeated Defy seeds "Compact Repudiated" Crisis → a Conquest installing a Company-run Governor Assignment (Charter overwrite) over the native elite.
- **Introduces (Action):** Monopsony Enforcement (Conquest gated by a repudiated-Compact Crisis tag — a legitimacy precondition rather than bare aggression; success installs a Company Governor).
- **Loop:** Repeatedly-Defied Compact terms accumulate toward a Crisis-gated Conquest flag, turning failed negotiation into legitimate casus belli; a successful Conquest strips the native elite's rank entirely and hands every future Levy/Develop decision to the Company.

---

### HEV-TRADE-05 — Muscovite conquest of Novgorod / end of the merchant-boyar republic (Novgorod, 1478) · Intrigue
- **Historical grounding:** Ivan III's 1478 conquest of Novgorod, ending the merchant-boyar republic (veche-bell removal, boyar confiscations, land-concentration). Citations: EBSCO; Wikipedia Novgorod Republic.
- **Trigger:** `a Guild-type faction's Recognition/Discipline below threshold AND the settlement's Disposition toward a rival external Crown exceeds threshold (being courted) AND its own patron faction's Standing independently declining`
- **Response branches:**
  - Comply (accept the rival's overtures): home-PA Disposition−, Grudge.
  - Bargain (hedge both ways): temporary, no resolution.
  - Defy (reject): requires Keep Order/Levy investment to shore up Discipline.
- **Follow-on (FO):** Unresolved courting + declining patron seeds "Annexation Bid" Crisis → a Conquest dissolving the Guild ladder (Dismissal + Rival Cohort tags on its top rank, reassignment via Governor Assignment).
- **Introduces (Action):** Assembly Discipline (Keep Order feeding the ladder's Recognition/Rival Cohort dimensions: raise internal standing to counter the erosion that makes it a target).
- **Loop:** Letting Discipline/Recognition erode while the patron falls raises Π and Intrigue odds; Assembly Discipline lowers them directly, while an Intrigue resolving into Conquest permanently deletes the ladder and forecloses the Assembly Discipline option at that settlement forever.

### HEV-TRADE-06 — Mongol ortoq merchant-partnership under the Pax Mongolica (Mongol Empire, 13th–14th c.) · Opportunity
- **Historical grounding:** The ortoq system of state-capitalized merchant partnerships operating across the Pax Mongolica's trade network. Citations: Wikipedia Ortogh/Paiza; Central Asian Survey ortoq.
- **Trigger:** `PA Treasury surplus ≥ threshold AND a Guild faction lacks a Concession AND Π low`
- **Response branches:**
  - Comply (fund the partnership): the faction gains a portable cross-settlement Concession — tax exemption + relay/travel privilege — and the Crown a profit-share.
  - Bargain (partial capitalization, capped exposure).
  - Defy (decline): the faction may seek capital from a rival Crown.
- **Follow-on (FO):** A funded partnership seeds recurring "Over-leveraged Caravan" Friction (debt-relief requests); repeated unresolved seeds a debt-repudiation Crisis damaging Crown-Guild Disposition.
- **Introduces (Action):** Capital Partnership (Sponsor sub-option issuing a *cross-settlement* Concession — usable at any settlement — rather than a settlement-bound Compact; flagged for the proposed Concession Ledger family).
- **Loop:** Sponsoring raises a Guild's mobility/profit-share via Treasury-funded Concessions, but each accrues Debt whose unresolved Friction pushes Π up; a Crown that never audits those Debt tags drifts toward a repudiation Crisis erasing the Concession's goodwill in one Disposition hit.

### HEV-TRADE-07 — Battle of Plassey / the Jagat Seth bankers / the Diwani of Bengal (Bengal, 1757) · Intrigue
- **Historical grounding:** The 1757 Battle of Plassey, in which the Jagat Seth banking house's financing of a coup against Siraj ud-Daulah led to the East India Company acquiring the Diwani of Bengal. Citations: National Army Museum; Britannica; Wikipedia Plassey/Jagat Seth.
- **Trigger:** `a Banking/Guild faction holds high Standing via Debt tags on the ruling Crown AND a rival external faction has Disposition contact with a disaffected high-rank Crown officer (Rival Cohort) AND Treasury dependency on the Banking faction ≥ threshold`
- **Response branches:**
  - Comply (officer stays loyal): Intrigue fizzles but Debt dependency persists.
  - Bargain (officer extracts concessions from both): Grudge all around, delayed.
  - Defy (officer defects, backing the external faction in a funded coup): triggers an Ambition enabling a Govern/Conquest against the province.
- **Follow-on (FO):** A successful coup Ambition resolves into Govern granting the external faction a diwani-equivalent Levy assignment, then a second-order follow_on lets that faction *demote the Banking faction that financed the coup* (Demotion Magnitude drop) once its leverage is spent.
- **Introduces (Action):** Court Officer Disposition (Keep Order/Treat lever shoring up a high-rank officer's loyalty, denying a rival the Rival Cohort opening).
- **Loop:** Heavy Treasury reliance on a Banking faction's Debt creates the Standing imbalance that makes an officer's defection attractive; skipping loyalty spend raises the Intrigue→Ambition escalation odds, and a successful external Govern both grants that faction new Levy rights and then demotes the Banking faction whose leverage enabled the chain.

### HEV-TRADE-08 — Kilwa Sultanate's Sofala gold monopoly / Portuguese Sack of Kilwa (Kilwa/East Africa, 1505) · Crisis
- **Historical grounding:** The Portuguese Sack of Kilwa in 1505, breaking the Kilwa Sultanate's tributary control over the Sofala gold trade. Citations: Wikipedia Sack of Kilwa / Portuguese Sofala expedition; africanhistoryextra.com.
- **Trigger:** `a tributary settlement holds a Directive tribute obligation to an external conquering power AND it Defies (non-payment) ≥1 season AND the external power's Muster ≥ Conquest threshold`
- **Response branches:**
  - Comply (pay): vassal status holds, Levy flows out.
  - Bargain (renegotiate rate): partial + Grudge.
  - Defy (refuse): escalates to "Tribute Withheld" Crisis opening a Conquest/Sack — trade-hub facilities destroyed, Prosperity collapsed.
- **Follow-on (FO):** Post-Sack an Opportunity seeds for a neighboring trade-hub (displaced merchants redirect trade there), raising its Prosperity/Levy potential.
- **Introduces (Action):** Harbor Defenses (Fortify mitigation paired with the tributary Comply/Bargain/Defy: reduce Conquest severity/odds specifically if the settlement later Defies).
- **Loop:** Defy on a tribute Directive raises Conquest odds, and an under-Fortified settlement suffers a worse Sack (facility destruction); the Sack removes the settlement's trade-hub status from future Levy calculations while creating a fresh Opportunity elsewhere — redistributing which settlement's chokepoint verb-set is even usable next.

### HEV-TRADE-09 — Aztec pochteca merchant guild's chartered long-distance monopoly (Aztec Empire, pre-Columbian) · Intrigue
- **Historical grounding:** The pochteca, the Aztec Empire's chartered long-distance merchant guild that also served intelligence/diplomatic functions ahead of military campaigns. Citations: Wikipedia/Britannica Pochteca; Frontiers in Human Dynamics (2025).
- **Trigger:** `a Muster/Conquest is queued (pre-conquest planning) AND a Guild holds a long-distance-trade Concession/Compact AND its Standing ≥ threshold for a self-jurisdiction ("own courts") dimension`
- **Response branches:**
  - Comply (Guild provides intelligence/diplomatic cover): reduced-risk/better-terms Conquest, Guild Standing/Recognition rises.
  - Bargain (Guild demands expanded self-jurisdiction): trades Crown oversight for intelligence value.
  - Defy (Crown uses the Guild informally): weaker intelligence, no Standing gain.
- **Follow-on (FO):** A formalized Compact seeds recurring Intrigue tied to the Guild's espionage role before each future Muster/Conquest; if the Crown later reneges on self-jurisdiction, a Friction/Petition from the Guild.
- **Introduces (Action):** Charter Intelligence Compact (Sponsor cross-linking into the Muster/Conquest pipeline: licenses a Guild for both a trade monopoly and pre-Conquest reconnaissance, tying a Sponsor payoff to future Conquest-planning quality).
- **Loop:** Sponsoring raises the Guild's Standing/self-jurisdiction and lowers the Crown's Conquest-planning risk via better Intrigue resolution; but each renewal expands the Guild's own-courts autonomy, an accumulating Concession constraining future Keep Order actions inside Guild-controlled quarters.

### HEV-TRADE-10 — Ottoman capitulations / Bursa silk trade reversal (Ottoman Empire, Bursa) · Opportunity
- **Historical grounding:** The Ottoman capitulations system granting extraterritorial trade privileges to foreign powers, and its later reversal as domestic Guild capital in the Bursa silk trade outgrew the foreign privilege-holders. Citations: depts.washington.edu Silk Road (İnalcık); Wikipedia Capitulations.
- **Trigger:**
  - *Initial grant:* `PA seeks external Disposition with a foreign polity AND current leverage favors granting extraterritorial privilege at apparent low domestic cost`
  - *Later reversal:* `a domestic Guild's Standing/capital in the licensed good ≥ threshold AND the foreign Guild's local Standing has fallen below threshold despite an unrevoked Concession still on the books`
- **Response branches:**
  - *Initial:* Comply (grant): external Disposition rises, a durable no-expiry Concession. Bargain (grant with sunset/reciprocity clause). Defy (refuse): preserves domestic dominance, forgoes the diplomacy.
  - *Reversal:* Comply (sunset the stale Concession via Levy renegotiation). Bargain (update to the domestic Guild's now-dominant Standing). Defy (leave it nominally intact and inert).
- **Follow-on (FO):** The initial grant's Concession is itself the hook, carried silently across seasons until domestic Guild Standing crosses threshold → a low-severity Petition ("Formalize what's already true"), no rupture required.
- **Introduces (Action):** Sunset Review (low-AP periodic Levy/Directive sub-action checking whether a long-standing Concession's terms still match the real balance of Standing, forcing reconciliation instead of stale drift).
- **Loop:** Granting an uncapped Concession raises external Disposition immediately, but absent Sunset Review the tag persists inertly while domestic Guild Standing quietly overtakes it; only the Sunset Review action (or an eventual forcing Petition) updates the Ledger to match the real balance — this category is defined by the long-run *absence* of the action, not its presence.

# Event Cards — Section 2.1: Succession Crises, Civil Wars, and Elite Power-Struggle Collapses (Geopolitical)

*Key: `succession-crises-civil-wars-elite-power-struggles`. Binds to the §2.x succession-split resolver: Trigger Matrix, Stage-1/Stage-2 contest, G-based Unified/Split, Split Persistence, plus Ministry/Consulta (Part 7). The through-line: **granting independent Military/Standing to heirs is what makes the next leader-loss fire the mandatory-contest row** — and the only durable off-ramps are institutional (Ratify, Consulta, Oath, Fratricide), each with its own recurring cost.*

Source: `designs/audit/2026-07-10-historical-concerns-action-catalogue/historical_concerns_action_catalogue_v1.md`, §2.1.

---

### HEV-SUCC-01 — Year of the Four Emperors (Rome, 69 CE) · Crisis
- **Historical grounding:** Rome's rapid sequence of military-acclaimed emperors following Nero's fall — Galba, Otho, Vitellius, Vespasian — per catalogue citation *§2.1–2.4 + Part 7 + §1.3c Ratify/Reject/Amend.*
- **Trigger:** `leader eliminated AND heir.Disposition<3 AND ≥2 contenders AND |Standing gap|≤1`
- **Response branches:** Comply (recognize the sitting military claimant) binds you to a possibly-doomed claimant; Bargain buys concessions; Defy backs a rival, feeding their External-backed strength.
- **Follow-on (FO):** A bare military win reseeds the Crisis next season (Galba→Otho→Vitellius→Vespasian); a win + Ratify writes a `military-acclamation-legitimate` Precedent that closes the chain but permanently lowers the retrigger threshold.
- **Introduces (Action):** Donative + Ratify (extends *Levy* and the Hold-Court *Ratify/Reject/Amend* branch): spend Treasury to buy a contender's Stage-1 military-Loyalty check, then convert a fait-accompli into legitimacy via a Consulta vote.
- **Loop:** Donative and Ratify are complements not substitutes — a bought win leaves Mandate depressed and Π high (reseeding the Crisis) unless the separate Ratify writes the closing Precedent; Otho's donative bought weeks, Vespasian's *lex de imperio* bought a dynasty.

### HEV-SUCC-02 — The Anarchy (England, 1138–1153) · Friction→Crisis
- **Historical grounding:** The English civil war between Stephen and Matilda following broken succession oaths — per catalogue citation *§2.2 claim-basis, §2.3 G≤1 SPLIT, §2.5 re-merge, §1.6 Compact/Grudge/Reputation.*
- **Trigger:** A `Succession Guarantee` Compact exists but is *not* one of the four recognized claim-basis types AND a rival's Standing is comparable.
- **Response branches:** Comply (a sworn Inner-Circle member honors the oath) defends the heir but strains local Order; Defy (backs the rival) writes a severe Grudge + `Reputation:Oathbreaker` on that NPC.
- **Follow-on (FO):** Mass reneging auto-triggers G≤1 SPLIT → recurring "Baronial Free Agency" Crisis, closable only by a Bargain-Directive §2.5 treaty re-merge on a next-generation compromise heir (Wallingford → Henry II).
- **Introduces (Action):** Extract Succession Oath (new Compact-family tag + mass social contest, parallel to §1.3a Negotiate Quota): gives the named heir a *fifth, oath-based* claim basis and taxes later defectors with Grudge+Oathbreaker — the enforcement teeth the 1127/1131 oaths to Matilda lacked.
- **Loop:** Extracting the Oath early costs Mandate/AP but brakes the mandatory-contest row; skip it and any comparable rival hits it with no brake, and the resulting Split erodes the Mandate needed to ever extract a credible Oath afterward — the nineteen-winter doom loop.

### HEV-SUCC-03 — Ottoman Interregnum / Fetret Devri (1402–1413) · Crisis
- **Historical grounding:** The Ottoman succession vacuum among Bayezid I's sons following the Battle of Ankara — per catalogue citation *§2.2 institutional claim-basis from Governor Assignment, §2.4 splinter residual Standing, §1.0a Demotion Magnitude as template for a Fratricide tier.*
- **Trigger:** `leader eliminated/captured AND ≥2 contenders each holding a Governor Assignment` (institutional basis auto-satisfied); the distinct post-resolution choice is whether the winner invokes Fratricide.
- **Response branches:** Post-resolution choice between sparing losing claimants or invoking Fratricide against them.
- **Follow-on (FO):** Sparing claimants seeds a guaranteed Ambition card next Generational-Shift ("a spared rival claims again"); invoking writes `Reputation:Hated`/Grudge raising Intrigue (assassination) draw-weight.
- **Introduces (Action):** Fratricide Law (Crown-tier post-Contest option amending §2.4): spend a severe Demotion-Magnitude-"total" Reputation/Grudge cost to strip all losing claimants rather than let them persist as splinters.
- **Loop:** Granting real Governor Assignments to multiple heirs is what makes the next leader-loss near-certainly contested; Fratricide closes that reopening loop but converts a recurring *external* civil-war risk into a recurring *internal* assassination risk — it trades escalation paths, not eliminates risk.

### HEV-SUCC-04 — Mughal War of Succession (1657–1661) · Intrigue
- **Historical grounding:** The four-way war among Shah Jahan's sons (Dara Shikoh, Shah Shuja, Aurangzeb, Murad Baksh) turning partly on religious orthodoxy accusations — per catalogue citation *§1.0c Court Attendance/Hostage-Kin, §1.0a Tribunal severity, §2.2 contender formula, §7 social-contest resolver.*
- **Trigger:** `succession contest active AND a contender's Church-Disposition/Conviction diverges from the dominant framework` (seeded by Grudges).
- **Response branches:** Denounce-as-Heretic routes the target through the Church Tribunal — conviction guts their Influence-based Stage-1 pool, acquittal backlashes on the accuser.
- **Follow-on (FO):** A convicted-but-not-eliminated contender's still-loyal Governors seed a "Loyalist Holdout" Crisis keeping the contest live.
- **Introduces (Action):** (a) Court Attendance/Hostage-Kin for heirs (extends §1.0c from absentee-governors to Crown heirs); (b) Denounce as Heretic (new Intrigue social contest via the Tribunal branch).
- **Loop:** Keeping an heir at court trades a Suspicion/Treasury cost now for lower future claim-basis strength; leaving heirs as independent subadar-governors (as Shah Jahan did) inflates all their strengths symmetrically, guaranteeing a multi-front contest — and once open, Denounce cuts a rival's pool without a battle, at a priced tit-for-tat.

### HEV-SUCC-05 — Inca Civil War / Huáscar vs Atahualpa (c. 1529–1532) · Crisis
- **Historical grounding:** The Inca succession war between half-brothers Huáscar and Atahualpa immediately preceding Pizarro's arrival — per catalogue citation *§2.1 Smooth/Regency rows, §2.3–2.4, `faction_action.py` Conquest.*
- **Trigger:** `succession_rule=="Partible/Panaca" AND leader eliminated AND ≥2 heirs each already holding independent territory+Military`; no political Stage-1 resolution exists (no shared territory to allocate) — routes straight to armed Conquest.
- **Response branches:** No political resolution branch — routes directly to armed Conquest between heirs.
- **Follow-on (FO):** The winner emerges Military/Treasury-exhausted ("Weakened Victor" modifier) raising an external-faction Opportunity/Crisis draw (Pizarro 1532).
- **Introduces (Action):** Partible Succession (durable faction-configuration Precedent flag set at founding): maximizes the living dynasty's footprint but disables the peaceful Smooth/Regency rows forever.
- **Loop:** Adopts near-term expansion for a structural guarantee that the same independent Military that made each heir strong is what makes them fight; set-once and irreversible (panaca as multi-generation institution), it can only be avoided by never adopting it, not managed well after.

### HEV-SUCC-06 — Muscovite Time of Troubles (1598–1613) · Intrigue
- **Historical grounding:** Russia's dynastic collapse after the Rurikid line's end, marked by the False Dmitry pretenders and eventual Zemsky Sobor election of Mikhail Romanov — per catalogue citation *§2.1 Regency/3-Accounting collapse, Part 7, §1.3a stale-high Assessment revolt logic.*

- **Loop:** Appanage grants raise Contest strength *and* let a loser convene a rival assembly and Defy the verdict, so the same policy that decides who wins determines whether the loser's refusal is survivable — turning a recoverable splinter into a permanent fracture the Recall lever was never used to prevent.

### HEV-SUCC-08 — Kongo Civil Wars (17th–18th c.) · Ambition→Crisis

# Event Cards — Trade Blockade, Embargo, Chokepoint Loss

*Key: `historical-concerns-catalogue/trade-blockade-embargo-chokepoint`. The through-line: **an Embargo/Directive is a weapon a hegemon points at a third faction through its own subordinates**, and a chokepoint is a geography that converts Fortify into extraction — both generate deferred Grudge/Debt that seeds later Muster/Conquest.*

### HEV-BLOC-01 — The Megarian Decree (Athens, 432 BCE) · Crisis
- **Historical grounding:** Athenian trade embargo against Megara; source: Wikipedia; Livius.org; McDonald, Electronic Antiquity 2.3.
- **Trigger:** `target holds bloc-network trade-dependent Prosperity AND hegemon Grudge-tagged toward it AND hegemon cross-faction Standing ≥ target's, mid-high Π`
- **Response branches:** Resolved hegemon-side as Sponsor+Directive — target's governor gets a forced Comply/Bargain/Defy over "how much of my own trade do I sacrifice."
- **Follow-on (FO):** Unresolved 2+ seasons escalates to a bloc-wide Muster among the target's treaty allies (the embargo becomes casus belli).
- **Introduces (Action):** Embargo (new Directive type targeting a *third* faction, not the settlement).
- **Loop:** A high-Standing hegemon with a Grudge crashes a rival's trade network at zero direct AP cost, converting political leverage into economic warfare; the target's only counters are Bargain or Defy-via-Treat, and non-resolution feeds Π/Grudge until it seeds a bloc Muster — the cost is deferred to the hegemon's allies' war-readiness.

### HEV-BLOC-02 — Danish Sound Dues / Øresundstolden (1429–1857) · Opportunity
- **Historical grounding:** Toll levied on ships passing through the Øresund strait; source: Wikipedia; STRO/UNESCO; havneguide.dk.
- **Trigger:** `chokepoint tag AND FacilityTier≥2 AND low-mid Π`
- **Response branches:** Unlocks the Toll Levy method (auto-succeeding income, no roll); no Comply/Bargain/Defy — it's a standing income stream.
- **Follow-on (FO):** Sustained Toll seeds a "rival contests the strait" Friction once a foreign faction's own toll-Debt crosses threshold (→ negotiated Compact or escalating Grudge/Conquest weight).
- **Introduces (Action):** Toll (chokepoint-gated Levy method, hard-gated on prior Fortify FacilityTier).
- **Loop:** Fortify at a chokepoint pays for itself through Toll extraction (FacilityTier is the multiplier), but sustained extraction accrues Debt/Grudge that seeds the Friction contesting the strait — the toll's success is what raises the Conquest-weight against the settlement that built it.

### HEV-BLOC-03 — Portuguese Cartaz System (Indian Ocean, 16th c.) · Friction
- **Historical grounding:** Portuguese licensing system requiring passes (cartazes) for shipping in the Indian Ocean; source: Wikipedia; historicalleys; jihku.org.
- **Trigger:** `faction holds ≥2 Fortify-built naval settlements ringing a zone AND standing naval Muster AND a rival's traffic lacks a License/Compact`
- **Response branches:** Unlicensed-vessel encounter → Hold-Court adjudication (seize/burn for Treasury+Grudge, or retroactive licensing fee→ongoing License).
- **Follow-on (FO):** 3+ Grudge tags seed Intrigue (smuggling, false-flag passes) not open war.
- **Introduces (Action):** License Waters (new verb, joint *Levy+Fortify* precondition licensing a *zone* not a settlement, with patrol upkeep).
- **Loop:** Enforcement credibility is a direct function of prior Fortify+Muster; upkeep lapses spawn smuggler Opportunity cards; successful enforcement is self-financing via License income but the accumulating Grudge feeds rivals' Conquest weight against the ports — self-financing but not self-stabilizing.

### HEV-BLOC-04 — Closure of the Hanseatic Kontor at Novgorod (1494) · Intrigue
- **Historical grounding:** Closure/seizure of the Hanseatic League's trading post at Novgorod; source: Wikipedia Peterhof; Springer, The Hanse in Medieval & Early Modern Europe (2022).
- **Trigger:** `a foreign faction holds a Charter granting trade-monopoly rights AND their upstream hinterland Prosperity declining ≥2 seasons AND your Standing over them rising`
- **Response branches:** Seize agents (severe Grudge/Debt + one-time Treasury/Prosperity gain as trade reroutes) vs leave intact.
- **Follow-on (FO):** The expelled faction's allies get an "exiles seek redress" Intrigue, and local Petition dip if Actors depended on the Kontor.
- **Introduces (Action):** Revoke Franchise (Charter-seizure bypassing the Quo Warranto window, only vs a *foreign* Charter once its leverage has eroded).
- **Loop:** Available only once the holder's upstream Prosperity has independently declined (read off their sheet, not caused by you); taking it redirects trade immediately but writes a durable Grudge/Hated tag seeding long-tail Intrigue.

### HEV-BLOC-05 — Barbary Tribute System (16th–19th c.) · Friction
- **Historical grounding:** Tribute paid to Barbary states to avoid piracy on shipping; source: NMAD "Piracy and Passports"; Wikipedia Barbary Wars; Clements Library.
- **Trigger:** `coastal Treasury above a raider-attractiveness threshold AND no naval Fortify/Muster on file AND raider aggression active`
- **Response branches:** Without prior investment the fork collapses to Comply-only (a recurring per-season Treasury levy + Debt tag); with investment, Defy (a punitive fleet) becomes available.
- **Follow-on (FO):** Unresolved tribute Debt auto-seeds a "tribute unsustainable" Crisis (the shift to the First/Second Barbary Wars).
- **Introduces (Action):** Standing Fleet (Fortify+Muster investment gating a Defy branch — the punitive-fleet option literally doesn't exist until banked).
- **Loop:** No investment = mechanically locked into Comply (Treasury bleed + Debt) every firing; investing converts the card into a real fork and a failed Defy escalates it to Crisis — the investment adds an option *and* raises the stakes of using it.

### HEV-BLOC-06 — Napoleon's Continental System (1806–1814) · Friction
- **Historical grounding:** Napoleon's embargo against Britain, enforced across subordinate/allied states; source: Wikipedia Berlin Decree; napoleon-series.org; Mises Institute.
- **Trigger:** `settlement subordinate to a hegemon's active Embargo Directive AND unmet Needs from foregone trade exceed available AP/Treasury, mid Π`
- **Response branches:** Comply (full enforcement, unmet-Need strain), Bargain (petition a License exception via Treat — a chit called in later), Defy/Divert (quiet non-enforcement, Standing-debt, smuggling-Reputation risk).
- **Follow-on (FO):** Widespread License exceptions seed a smuggling Opportunity/Intrigue, and accumulated hegemon Standing-debt seeds an "embargo is dead in practice" Crisis forcing the hegemon's own Directive to be revised.
- **Introduces (Action):** License Exception (Treat-chit extension of the Embargo Directive — a bounded licensed corridor, each a Debt-family chit).
- **Loop:** Strict Comply crashes local Prosperity/Order (Π up, smuggling cards seeded); Treat-exceptions relieve local pressure but each is a standing Debt chit that bloc-wide erodes the embargo — individually rational governor choices *are* the historical system's self-undermining collapse, reproduced emergently.

### HEV-BLOC-07 — Union Blockade / Anaconda Plan (1861–1865) · Crisis
- **Historical grounding:** Union naval blockade of the Confederacy during the American Civil War; source: Encyclopedia Virginia; Wikipedia; USNI Naval History.
- **Trigger:** `rival Prosperity sourced overwhelmingly from one export (single-commodity Develop tag) AND hostile naval Muster + river-chokepoint Conquest targets active`
- **Response branches:** Export Prosperity progressively zeroed as chokepoints fall; the blockaded governor narrows to Comply (Develop-diversify, slow) or Sponsor a blockade-runner (Treasury clawback at Order/Corruption risk).
- **Follow-on (FO):** Sustained success seeds successive Crisis escalations (garrison funding collapse), a broken runner network seeds a corruption Intrigue.
- **Introduces (Action):** Blockade-Runner Sponsorship (Sponsor defender-exploit) / Naval Blockade Directive+Conquest (attacker).
- **Loop:** A prior single-commodity Develop concentration is disproportionately vulnerable; the defender's only short-of-military lever is runner-Sponsorship, which recovers Treasury but risks Corruption seeding further Crises — the commodity-concentration choice made seasons earlier decides survivable vs existential.

### HEV-BLOC-08 — ABCD Line & US Oil Embargo on Japan (1940–41) · Crisis
- **Historical grounding:** Coalition embargo (American-British-Chinese-Dutch) cutting off strategic resources ahead of Pearl Harbor; source: Wikipedia ABCD Encirclement / Pearl Harbor prelude; Defense Media Network.
- **Trigger:** `rival Fiscal Stance draws a supermajority of one strategic resource from a single external source AND coalition Grudge/Standing crosses a high threshold at high Π`
- **Response branches:** The coalition embargo *removes the Bargain branch* — only Comply (capitulate, forced Charter/territory concessions) or Defy (Muster into open war).
- **Follow-on (FO):** Defy auto-seeds a full Conquest cycle, Comply seeds a hawkish internal Ambition chain (coup/hardline succession).
- **Introduces (Action):** Multilateral Embargo (coalition-coordinated Directive gated through Ministry/Consulta).
- **Loop:** A prior single-resource Fiscal-Stance concentration makes the faction targetable; the coordinated embargo collapses the three-way fork to binary, and either choice feeds `faction_action.py` Conquest/Muster weighting next season — the card structurally forecloses a core response option.

### HEV-BLOC-09 — Berlin Blockade (1948–49) · Crisis
- **Historical grounding:** Soviet blockade of land/water routes to West Berlin, countered by the Allied airlift; source: State Dept Office of the Historian; Wikipedia.
- **Trigger:** `settlement is an enclave (no contiguous land supply route) AND a rival's Standing/Muster advantage in the surround crosses a threshold`
- **Response branches:** Land-Levy/supply auto-fails; if an alternate-access FacilityTier was banked, Sponsor an Alternate Corridor (ongoing AP+Treasury, bleeds Π down); if not, Order/Prosperity decays toward loss (Conquest-by-attrition, no roll).
- **Follow-on (FO):** A sustained corridor seeds a "different pretext" Friction, a failed corridor seeds automatic territory-transfer.
- **Introduces (Action):** Alternate Corridor (Sponsor extension, hard-preconditioned on a prior Fortify airbase/corridor FacilityTier — cannot be reacted into existence mid-Crisis).
- **Loop:** Survival is set entirely by a Fortify investment made years earlier; the Crisis is a retroactive audit; Sponsoring the corridor once it fires bleeds Π down and denies the attrition-Conquest, converting siege into a stalemate the besieger must formally lift.

### HEV-BLOC-10 — Suez Crisis & Canal Closure (1956) · Crisis
- **Historical grounding:** Nationalization of the Suez Canal and the ensuing international crisis; source: Wikipedia Suez; History.com Aswan; Forbes.
- **Trigger:** `a foreign faction's prior Sponsor/Treat funding to a chokepoint settlement is withdrawn (Friction fires) AND the Charter is foreign-held AND local Grudge is high`
- **Response branches:** Seeds a Nationalize Charter opportunity (seize outside the 16-season window), risking a Charter-holder coalition Conquest — resolved *not* by the battle but escalating to a Ministry/Consulta arbitration that can force reversal or ratify regardless of the battlefield.
- **Follow-on (FO):** Arbitrated reversal writes a `chokepoint charters are contestable by force` Precedent lowering future bypass bars; ratification seeds a long Reputation/Grudge chain.
- **Introduces (Action):** Nationalize Charter (crisis-gated Quo Warranto bypass).
- **Loop:** Only attractive once an external Friction (patron withdrawal) has weakened the holder — you exploit an opportunity the world generated; the outcome depends on Standing/alliances feeding the arbitration as much as the campaign, closing the loop back into faction-ladder Standing.

# Event Cards — Siege / Sack / Occupation / Garrison (Section 2.3)

*Key: `siege_sack_occupation_garrison_crises`. Two spines: **unpaid garrisons treat plunder as wages** (Contribution System / Settle Arrears / mutiny contagion), and **pre-crisis Fortify tier is the win/loss table** (Inundation, Coalition Pool, Circumvallate).*

### HEV-SIEGE-01 — Sack of Magdeburg (1631, Holy Roman Empire) · Crisis
- **Historical grounding:** Unpaid Catholic League garrison storms and sacks the Protestant city after Tilly's siege breaks down into massacre and fire — §1.3/§1.3a/§1.4/§1.6.
- **Trigger:** `Extract/Tax at severe magnitude AND Defy AND Π≥8 AND no relief in range AND besieger.Treasury < garrison upkeep (unpaid-troops flag)`
- **Response branches:** On storm: Order→0, catastrophic Prosperity, a durable regionally-legible `Hated`/legendary-infamy Reputation; the besieger's unpaid-garrison Debt turns plunder into wages, worsening the severity table even under restraint orders.
- **Follow-on (FO):** An empire-wide `magdeburgisieren` Precedent that *lowers* Defy-appeal locally (terror compliance) while *raising* Grudge/Intrigue regionally — a Precedent that cuts opposite ways by scale.
- **Introduces (Action):** Contribution System / Kontributionssystem (new negotiated-extorted Levy method, sibling to Encabezamiento, funding garrison upkeep from the occupied region).
- **Loop:** Besieger Treasury below upkeep modifies the Crisis severity roll upward regardless of officer intent; the Contribution System keeps it solvent pre-siege and removes the modifier, at the cost of Debt/Grudge tags in the *occupied* territory taxed.

### HEV-SIEGE-02 — Spanish Fury at Antwerp (1576, Low Countries) · Crisis
- **Historical grounding:** Unpaid Spanish tercios mutiny and sack their own garrisoned host city — §1.3/§1.6 Debt "fires when due"/Treat chit, ED-SE-0005.
- **Trigger:** `Debt(garrison_pay) unresolved ≥3 seasons AND besieger Treasury critically low AND host Prosperity high`
- **Response branches:** Garrison mutinies and sacks its own host — Order/Prosperity collapse + a Grudge written at *every loyalist settlement that hears* (contagion).
- **Follow-on (FO):** A "loyalist provinces petition to remove the garrison" Friction chaining to a cross-settlement Treat/Bargain coalition (Pacification-of-Ghent analogue).
- **Introduces (Action):** Settle Arrears (new Levy sub-action: pay down garrison-pay Debt before it escalates, resetting the clock).
- **Loop:** Letting the Debt go uncalled raises Mutiny draw-weight and severity; Settle Arrears removes the trigger; if it fires, contagion Grudge at *other* settlements raises their future Defy-propensity — mutiny as contagious political-capital loss.

### HEV-SIEGE-03 — Siege of Leiden (1574, Dutch Republic) · Crisis
- **Historical grounding:** Leiden endures prolonged Spanish siege, is relieved by deliberate flooding of the polders, and is rewarded with a founding charter — §1.3 Fortify/Sponsor, §1.6.
- **Trigger:** `under active siege AND Π≥8 AND Defense below survival threshold`
- **Response branches:** Order/Prosperity attrition scales with duration and starting Defense, prior Fortify reduces both.
- **Follow-on (FO):** *Surviving* unlocks a Sponsor "Found an Institution" (university/charter) card converting the ordeal into durable `Generous`/`Just` Reputation + Precedent.
- **Introduces (Action):** Inundation (mid-Crisis-only Fortify-adjacent method: trade Prosperity — flood productive land — for an immediate Defense/relief bonus).
- **Loop:** Pre-crisis Fortify sets the baseline casualty/duration table exactly when the card fires; Inundation is the costly fallback when that was insufficient, and surviving unlocks the Sponsor payoff no Fortify-only path grants.

### HEV-SIEGE-04 — Great Siege of Malta (1565, Mediterranean) · Crisis
- **Historical grounding:** The Knights Hospitaller withstand an existential Ottoman invasion of Malta on the strength of prior fortification and eventual relief — §1.3 Fortify/Sponsor, §1.6 Grudge, mass_battle_v30 relief.
- **Trigger:** `large invading force AND Fortify tier insufficient alone vs force-ratio AND strategic_value==existential`
- **Response branches:** Survival probability reads directly off cumulative pre-crisis Fortify.
- **Follow-on (FO):** Surviving seeds a rare "Pan-Faction Sponsorship" Opportunity (allied/Church Treasury into a new fortress-settlement) + a Precedent easing future cross-faction pooling.
- **Introduces (Action):** Coalition Fortify Pool (new acquisition path: multiple factions jointly pre-fund a threatened settlement's Fortify ahead of a known Crisis).
- **Loop:** Accumulated Fortify (often motivated by an old unresolved Grudge) is the single stat deciding the win/loss table; surviving unlocks cross-faction pooling that raises future capacity beyond any one faction — investment and survival compound across crisis cycles.

### HEV-SIEGE-05 — Siege & Sack of Drogheda (1649, Ireland) · Crisis
- **Historical grounding:** Cromwell's forces deny quarter after the town's defiance, driven by unresolved atrocity grudges, and follow with mass confiscation of the population's land — §1.4/§1.6 Grudge+Outlawed, mass_battle_v30, faction_action.py.
- **Trigger:** `Grudge(unresolved_atrocity) active AND Directive.type∈{Suppress,harsh} AND Defy`
- **Response branches:** A "quarter denied" branch — severe Order/Reputation loss beyond the normal table + a new Outlawed tag marking the population for confiscation.
- **Follow-on (FO):** Seeds a "Settle & Confiscate" Govern/Conquest action (mass roster/land reallocation) writing a fresh Outlawed tag raising Grudge/Intrigue for generations.
- **Introduces (Action):** Settle & Confiscate (new post-Conquest domain action: forced subnational faction-roster replacement).
- **Loop:** An old Grudge raises the punitive branch's severity ceiling; executing Settle & Confiscate writes a new Outlawed tag raising future Grudge/Intrigue — a self-reinforcing spiral the Ledger makes explicit and inheritable across governor succession.

### HEV-SIEGE-06 — Colonial Billeting / Quartering Acts (1765–1774, British American colonies) · Friction
- **Historical grounding:** Forced quartering of troops on colonial hosts escalates from resented Force-method Keep Order through Petition and into Boston-Massacre-style violence — §1.3 Keep Order Consent/Force/Clergy, §1.4 Host, §2.3 escalate.
- **Trigger:** `Keep Order.method==Force (or Directive Quarter) in a Prosperity-above-threshold settlement AND PS trending negative`
- **Response branches:** Ignoring the Petition raises Π and escalates to a Boston-Massacre-style Crisis (Order loss, Actor-Disposition collapse, Grudge).
- **Follow-on (FO):** The Crisis chains to a Directive-tier authority shift from negotiated to unilateral Force-method, seeding a longer rebellion Ambition arc.
- **Introduces (Action):** Quarter (new Directive type, sibling to Host, forcing Force-method Keep Order over the governor's preference).
- **Loop:** Force-method accrues PS loss + Grudge each season; an ignored Friction fires a Crisis that hardens Actor-Disposition and makes Consent politically *unavailable* going forward — a ratchet narrowing the governor's own future menu toward more Force (the 1765→1774 shift).

### HEV-SIEGE-07 — Mongol Siege of Baghdad (1258, Abbasid Caliphate) · Crisis
- **Historical grounding:** The Mongols besiege and raze Baghdad after the Caliph refuses submission, permanently destroying the irrigation infrastructure underlying its prosperity — §1.3 Develop, §1.4 Defy, §1.6 durable-infrastructure-loss extension.
- **Trigger:** `capital-tier Defy with no submission AND no relief coalition AND internal Grudge/split unresolved`
- **Response branches:** A rare branch *permanently caps* the Develop ceiling (destroyed irrigation) — a multi-generation wound, not a one-season loss.
- **Follow-on (FO):** Long-horizon Develop-degradation state + an Intrigue among survivors over the diminished settlement.
- **Introduces (Action):** Rebuild Infrastructure (high-cost multi-season Develop sub-project restoring a capped ceiling).
- **Loop:** An unresolved internal split raises the probability of the *permanent* loss branch; once fired, the ceiling stays capped until Rebuild completes, narrowing the governor's viable menu (heavier Levy/Treat reliance) for the rebuild's duration.

### HEV-SIEGE-08 — Nadir Shah's Sack of Delhi (1739, Mughal Empire) · Crisis
- **Historical grounding:** Riots against Nadir Shah's occupying force trigger open-ended reprisal massacre, checked only by a negotiated fixed indemnity — §1.3 Treat/Force, §1.3a Compact fixed-term, §1.4 Bargain, faction rank-gated authority.
- **Trigger:** `occupation active AND Keep Order Force fails (riot) AND Π≥8`
- **Response branches:** Open-ended reprisal massacre unless a sovereign-rank actor invokes Bargain converting it to a bounded Compact indemnity (fixed payment ending the plunder).
- **Follow-on (FO):** Successful Bargain writes a fires-once Compact + Reputation shift; unresolved reprisal seeds further Grudge/Intrigue.
- **Introduces (Action):** Sovereign Bargain (rank-gated Directive-Bargain, Standing-6+ only, converts open-ended loss to a one-time Compact).
- **Loop:** A Force-failure raises severity proportional to existing Friction; a high-rank Sovereign Bargain caps it (better conversion at higher Standing) — a rank-scarce mitigation unavailable to an ordinary governor.

### HEV-SIEGE-09 — Siege of Numantia (134–133 BCE, Roman Hispania) · Crisis
- **Historical grounding:** After repeated failed assaults and a broken treaty, Scipio Aemilianus circumvallates Numantia into slow starvation; the defenders choose mass self-destruction over surrender — mass_battle_v30/faction_action.py Muster/Conquest, §1.3a broken-treaty flag, §1.6.
- **Trigger:** `besieger repeated failed Conquest attempts ≥N against same settlement AND Grudge(broken_treaty) active`
- **Response branches:** Circumvallate resolves slowly but near-guarantees capitulation without assault casualties, but broken trust forces the defender toward the worst-case branch (total resistance / mass self-destruction).
- **Follow-on (FO):** Writes a "no negotiated peace available" flag permanently removing Bargain/Treat between them for the conflict.
- **Introduces (Action):** Circumvallate (new besieger Conquest method: trade tempo — multi-season, AP/Treasury-heavy — to near-eliminate assault casualties).
- **Loop:** A previously reneged Compact writes a Grudge closing the Bargain option, pushing the besieger toward Circumvallate and the defender toward maximal resistance — the earlier broken deal narrows both sides' menus in the eventual siege.

### HEV-SIEGE-10 — Siege of Orléans (1428–1429, Kingdom of France) · Crisis
- **Historical grounding:** The relief of besieged Orléans, led by Joan of Arc's improvised muster, reverses the Dauphin's contested legitimacy and fast-tracks his formal coronation — §1.3 Hold Court/Precedent, §1.4, §1.0b Recognition Fork, Standing-3 Formal Recognition.
- **Trigger:** `settlement legitimacy_linked to a contested claimant's Recognition track AND active siege AND Recognition contested/pending`
- **Response branches:** The outcome writes directly into the claimant's Recognition Fork (a fall costs a rank-track setback regardless of local stats; a relief win auto-triggers a Formal Recognition / coronation Event).
- **Follow-on (FO):** A successful relief seeds an Ambition card fast-tracking the low-Standing NPC who led the improvised relief, bypassing the normal Initiation Gate.
- **Introduces (Action):** Improvised Relief (new Muster variant bypassing exhausted Levy capacity, paired with a "Legitimacy Siege" flag routing the Crisis into a Recognition Fork).
- **Loop:** Legitimacy-flagging means a fall costs a faction-ladder rank independent of local Fortify/Order; a relief that saves it triggers a Recognition Event for the leading NPC — an alternate non-Initiation-Gate advancement path that exists only because the Crisis was legitimacy-flagged.

# 2.4 Earthquake, Eruption, Terrain-Altering Events (Geographical/Climatic)

*Key: `catastrophic-terrain-events-mechanical-distillation`. The through-line: **a Crisis card is a retroactive audit of a Fortify/Develop method skipped seasons earlier** (Rebuild-to-Code, Housing Code, Water Infrastructure, Relocate) — and the *disaster becomes the political lever*, not just the loss.*

### HEV-QUAKE-01 — 1755 Lisbon Earthquake/Tsunami/Fire (Lisbon, 1755) · Crisis
- **Historical grounding:** The 1755 Lisbon Earthquake/Tsunami/Fire — *§1.3/§1.6/§2.1–2.3.*
- **Trigger:** `Π≥8 AND Weight≥high AND no Rebuild-to-Code tag AND a feast/high-attendance crowd multiplier active`

# Event Cards — Flood, Drought, Siltation, Resource-Geography Shifts

*Key: `historical-concerns-catalogue-flood-drought-siltation-resource-geography`. The through-line: **slow hidden stats (SiltLevel, OreGrade, SalinityLevel, SubsidenceRisk, RisingWaterLevel) are long fuses** — an engineering/extraction method choice at one node silently changes another node's Crisis odds seasons later, and standing institutions (Water Board, Water Magistracy, Ever-Normal Granary, River Conservancy) are the only pre-emptive defusers.*

### HEV-WATER-01 — The Nilometer / flood-indexed taxation (Egypt) · Friction→Crisis
- **Historical grounding:** The Nilometer flood-gauge system underpinning Egyptian flood-indexed taxation. Citation: Wikipedia Nilometer; NatGeo.
- **Trigger:** `Gauge facility AND FloodIndex < relief_threshold AND Levy fixed/unadjusted AND Directive extraction pending`
- **Response branches:** Comply (honor the gauge, cut the rate: Order+1, Disposition+1); Bargain (split with the quota, `partial-relief-precedent`); Defy (PS−1, Order−1, Grudge, Π+2)
- **Follow-on (FO):** Defy seeds a "Famine Unrest" Crisis; repeated Comply seeds a `gauge-bound assessment` Precedent locking future Levy to the reading.
- **Introduces (Action):** Gauge-Indexed Levy — a new Levy method reading its rate off a tracked environmental stat, auto-scaling without a per-season roll.
- **Loop:** Installing it ties the Levy formula to FloodIndex so a bad flood auto-reduces extraction and denies the Friction its escalation; a fixed rate re-rolls the same Friction every low-flood season with rising Π/Grudge until a Defy chain forces the costlier emergency Bargain/Defy the gauge would have pre-empted.

### HEV-WATER-02 — Yellow River course change / Pan Jixun's engineering (Ming) · Crisis (Friction precursor)

- **Follow-on (FO):** Scour seeds a delayed "Rising Lake Threatens [Settlement]" Crisis firing only once the downstream tag crosses threshold — potentially decades later, independent of that settlement's choices.
- **Introduces (Action):** River Conservancy Directorship — a Fortify Scour-vs-Broad-Relief fork plus a province-tier standing office like a Ministry seat.
- **Loop:** Scour raises the capital-adjacent settlement's Prosperity/Defense but writes a slow-incrementing tag onto a different settlement that nothing there can see or defuse — a Fortify choice at one node silently changes Crisis odds at another many seasons later (the multi-decade fuse).

# 2.6 — Famine and multi-year crop failure (Climatic/Geographical)

*Key / through-line: `historical-concerns-catalogue:famine-multiyear-crop-failure`. The through-line: a standing storage/entitlement Facility (Granary Prefecture, Ever-Normal Granary, Grain Ministry) determines whether a harvest-collapse roll is survivable, and an ideological/fixed Precedent (rigid price ceiling, fixed quota, free-trade doctrine) set earlier constrains which relief options the Crisis even offers.*

### HEV-FAM-01 — Great Famine of 1315–1317 (Northern Europe, 14th c.) · Crisis
- **Historical grounding:** W.C. Jordan, *The Great Famine* (Princeton 1996); Wikipedia.
- **Trigger:** `Harvest_Yield<0.5 for 2 seasons AND Treasury_Grain_Reserve below a Facility-gated threshold` → "Grain Riot"
- **Response branches:**
  - Comply (rigid price ceiling): Order gain but plants Precedent raising next-Crisis severity as merchants withhold.
  - Bargain (merchant compact/export exemptions): Compact tag, avoids escalation.
  - Defy: immediate Order loss, no durable tag.
- **Follow-on (FO):** Comply-with-ceiling seeds an escalated Grain Riot next season (traders defect); Bargain seeds a "Merchant Compact Renewal" Opportunity.
- **Introduces (Action):** Price Ceiling / Export Embargo (method-variant on Levy/Treat).
- **Loop:** The Price-Ceiling method suppresses immediate Order loss but attaches a Precedent increasing escalation weight if the shortage persists; prior Develop in grain-reserve FacilityTier lowers the odds the threshold is crossed at all and, if it fires anyway, unlocks a cheaper Bargain instead of forcing Comply/Defy.

- **Introduces (Action):** Granary Prefecture (capital-only Facility unlocking a Levy "Subsidized Distribution" method-variant).
- **Loop:** The Facility raises FacilityTier lowering the AP cost/failure of Subsidized Distribution when the Petition fires; but Complying plants an Entitlement Precedent making any future Defy of a grain obligation cost extra Disposition — a granted entitlement is politically hard to revoke.

# 2.7 Plague and epidemic disease, climate/trade-correlated (Climatic ↔ Geographical/Economic)

*Key: `historical-concerns-plague-epidemic-v1`. The through-line: **the vector is a governance action** — Refuge Walls overcrowding, an un-staged Muster redeploy, a single-corridor Develop, a famine-relief Treat, a Host Directive routing troops, a waived quarantine inspection — and the response verb (Force/Segregation) that ends the epidemic manufactures the next political Crisis.*

### HEV-PLAGUE-01 — Plague of Athens (430 BCE) · Crisis
- **Historical grounding:** Thucydides II; NatGeo; HistorySkills.
- **Trigger:** `Π≥8 AND recent Fortify(Refuge Walls) within 2 seasons AND Order≤3 AND Overcrowding tag`
- **Response branches:** Sponsor relief (Order+1, Π−2, downgrades but doesn't clear Overcrowding); Force (Order+1 but Disposition−1 all Actors, Grudge); Ignore (Π+3, patron-NPC mortality check).
- **Follow-on (FO):** A failed patron mortality check seeds a succession Crisis (Pericles' death mid-plague); a persisting Overcrowding reseeds the Crisis next eligible season.
- **Introduces (Action):** Refuge Walls (fourth Fortify method: shelter rural population for Defense+1/PS bump but writes a durable Overcrowding tag unless paired same-season with Sponsor relief or Fortify:Walls sized to the influx).
- **Loop:** Refuge Walls without same-season relief raises Π and stacks Overcrowding — the literal trigger of the Epidemic card, so the defensive choice reproduces itself; if it fires and the patron check fails, the succession vacancy removes the AP/Standing the governor needed for the relief Sponsor — the historical trap in mechanical form.

### HEV-PLAGUE-02 — Antonine Plague (165–180 CE) · Crisis
- **Historical grounding:** Wikipedia; WHE; PLOS One (2025) SIR/SEIR; Masaryk CEDRR.
- **Trigger:** `last action Conquest(frontier) AND next action Muster(redeploy to interior) AND NOT used Staged Demobilization AND Order≤4`
- **Response branches:** Fires in every interior settlement receiving troops (Order−1, Prosperity−1, Mil−2/−3 reversing the season's troop purchase) unless Sponsor/Consent absorbs it.
- **Follow-on (FO):** Repeated hits across the redistribution wave seed a faction-level "the muster route is a plague road" Friction raising the Ob on the faction's next Conquest via a lowered mil-advantage signal.
- **Introduces (Action):** Staged Demobilization (Muster sub-choice: extra Wealth + a season's delay to route legions through a quarantine interval, trading faster Mil gain for suppressing the trigger).
- **Loop:** Frontier Conquest + an un-staged Muster is the mechanism raising the Crisis weight in every receiving settlement; if it fires it strips Mil from the very Muster that caused it, lowering the faction's mil-advantage signal and its Conquest weight next dispatch cycle — a direct write-back from event severity into the faction AI's action-selection odds.

### HEV-PLAGUE-03 — Plague of Justinian (541–549 CE) · Crisis
- **Historical grounding:** Wikipedia; OeAW; Mordechai & Eisenberg, Past & Present 244 (2019); Mordechai et al., PNAS 116(51) (2019).
- **Trigger:** `Precedent("single-corridor-trade") AND external_shock(climate) AND Prosperity drop ≥2 in one season AND Π≥7`
- **Response branches:** Sponsor Emergency Wage Edict (Order+1, floor held, Debt tag — the edict must be renewed or Disposition−2); Force (Order+1 but PS−1 + Grudge); Ignore (Π+2, Prosperity−2, `chokepoint confirmed fragile` deepens).
- **Follow-on (FO):** The deepened Precedent raises repeat-Crisis weight on the next external_shock + seeds a Guild Treat/Compact petition to formalize an alternate route.
- **Introduces (Action):** Diversify Corridor (Develop funding sub-method: 1 extra AP + slower Prosperity to route through 2+ independent corridors, writing a `route-diversified` Precedent lowering future weight).
- **Loop:** The cheaper single-corridor Develop funding *writes the trigger predicate*, so a Prosperity-maximizing governor is most exposed when a climate shock lands; once it fires Keep Order narrows to the costlier Force unless Diversify Corridor or a Wage Edict was banked — the event retroactively punishes the earlier method choice.

### HEV-PLAGUE-04 — The Black Death (1347–1351) · Friction
- **Historical grounding:** Bauch & Büntgen, Comm Earth & Env (2025); Wikipedia Black Death/migration; Cambridge news.
- **Trigger:** `Prosperity≤2 AND external_shock(climate/famine) AND Treasury≤threshold`
- **Response branches:** Treat (Emergency Grain Compact with a distant supplier: Prosperity+1 but writes a Debt tag AND a Contagion Vector flag); Directive-Bargain for Crown relief (partial, no Contagion flag, Standing-debt); Ignore/refuse (no flag but escalates to a Famine Crisis next season).
- **Follow-on (FO):** A Contagion-Vector-flagged settlement near-certainly draws a Plague Crisis next season regardless — the double-bind: grain now/plague later, or famine now with no plague.
- **Introduces (Action):** Emergency Grain Compact (Treat famine sub-case: Influence+history roll vs a distant supplier, Debt-chit + a Contagion Vector flag when the supplier is on an unvetted long-distance corridor).
- **Loop:** The famine-relief Treat branch trades an immediate Prosperity save for a guaranteed-weight Plague draw via the Contagion Vector — the same climate shock that makes the Treat attractive is what carried the pathogen on the relief shipment, reproducing the historical double-edge instead of a free relief action.

### HEV-PLAGUE-05 — Venice Lazzaretto / 1630–31 quarantine failure · Crisis
- **Historical grounding:** Sapiens.org; CDC EID (2013); Alfani/Melegaro, Scientific Reports (2020); Wikipedia 1629–1631 Italian plague.
- **Trigger:** `Quarantine_Tier≥1 (trade-vector protected) AND external_shock(military_transit — Host Directive or Levy troop movement) AND Π≥8`
- **Response branches:** Because the tier only discounts *trade*-vector triggers, this fires at near-full severity regardless (Order−2, Prosperity−1, Reputation→Weak); responses apply at no discount.
- **Follow-on (FO):** Seeds an Intrigue circulating the failure as evidence the Sanità is captured/incompetent → a Petition to defund it (rolling back the tier investment).
- **Introduces (Action):** Sanità Lazzaretto (Fortify facility, 2 AP+Treasury, granting a Quarantine Tier 0–3 reducing draw-weight of Plague cards whose trigger references a *trade/sea* vector — explicitly not military-transit).
- **Loop:** The facility lowers Plague odds only along trade predicates, so a governor treating it as blanket protection and complying with a Host Directive routing troops through eats a full-severity Crisis, which writes the Reputation/Intrigue follow_on threatening the very investment that didn't cover the actual vector — asymmetric mitigation as mechanics, not flavor.

### HEV-PLAGUE-06 — Cocoliztli epidemics 1545 & 1576 (New Spain) · Crisis
- **Historical grounding:** Acuna-Soto et al., EID 8(4) (2002); PMC7110390; Wikipedia.
- **Trigger:** `Assessment(assessed_base) AND seasons_since_Survey≥8 (never refreshed) AND external_shock(drought, multi-season) AND Prosperity < assessed_base×0.5`
- **Response branches:** The Extract Directive keeps charging the stale base through the drought (§1.3a failure mode), stripping below subsistence (Prosperity−3, Order−2, mass-mortality flag, Grudge(Crown)).
- **Follow-on (FO):** Seeds a repeat harder-Ob Crisis each season until a Survey/Negotiate-Quota resets the figure — an escalating loop.
- **Introduces (Action):** Emergency Resurvey (extends the live Survey sub-verb: usable outside its ~8-season cooldown when an external_shock flag is active, at an Ob penalty, to refresh the Assessment before Extract strips the settlement).
- **Loop:** Skipping Survey during a megadrought is a modeled trap: Extract reads a stale-high base and drives Prosperity below subsistence (the trigger), the Crisis spikes Π and writes Grudge(Crown) that raises the Ob on the *next* Survey/Negotiate attempt (Reputation Weak/Hated makes renegotiation harder) — the neglect that caused the collapse also makes it harder to fix.

### HEV-PLAGUE-07 — Chongzhen drought / Great Plague of 1633–1644 · Crisis
- **Historical grounding:** Climate MDPI 11(3):71 (2023); Copernicus CoP (2024); Wikipedia late-Ming plague.
- **Trigger:** `Granary_FacilityTier==0 (never Developed) AND external_shock(drought ≥3 seasons) AND faction military Levy-extraction ≥ high (war Directives crowding out settlement AP)`
- **Response branches:** Famine→plague→unrest at full severity (Prosperity−3, Order−3, a rebel-warlord Ambition on a named Actor); Sponsor relief is the only mitigation but competes for the same AP the Extract Directive consumes.
- **Follow-on (FO):** If under-resourced, seeds a rebel Ambition (Li Zicheng) accelerating with every unserved famine Need → an open Conquest-target for a rival.
- **Introduces (Action):** Ever-Normal Granary (Develop funding method stockpiling a famine buffer, releasable via Sponsor, competing for the AP a Directive's military Extract already draws).
- **Loop:** Heavy Levy/Extract starves the settlement's AP for the Granary Develop, so when the drought fires no buffer exists and the cascade hits full severity; the Order collapse + rebel Ambition further degrades Disposition, raising future Levy resistance and the faction's own extraction costs — granary neglect and extraction pressure feed each other bidirectionally.

### HEV-PLAGUE-08 — Great Plague of Marseille (1720) · Intrigue
- **Historical grounding:** Wikipedia; STAT News (2020); Rodama blog; edwardworthlibrary.ie.
- **Trigger:** `Quarantine_Tier≥1 (Sanità exists) AND Guild Standing ≥ threshold AND Guild.Disposition(governor) high (patronage) AND a trade-fair Treasury deadline`
- **Response branches:** Hold Court gains a Ratify/Reject/Amend branch — Ratify (waive inspection: ship clears, Guild Influence+1, but the Quarantine_Tier discount is voided for that shipment, spiking the Plague Crisis weight to near-certain), Reject (Guild Master−2, Grudge, no spike), Investigate first (exposes the bribery vector for the four-way disposition).
- **Follow-on (FO):** Ratify seeds the Plague Crisis at near-guaranteed weight; if it fires, a Demotion chain against the Guild Master who pressured the waiver.
- **Introduces (Action):** Quarantine Waiver Petition (new Hold Court branch, structurally identical to Ordenanza Ratification §1.3c, Ratify voiding that shipment's tier protection).
- **Loop:** A high-Standing/Disposition Guild spends patronage to push Hold Court toward Ratify, zeroing the Sanità discount for one shipment and spiking the Plague weight; if it fires, the Grudge/Reputation collapse hits both governor and the Guild Master's own Standing (a public-scandal-class §1.0a trigger dropping the Master 2 ranks) — the event punishes the same faction-ladder leverage that produced it.

### HEV-PLAGUE-09 — Third Plague Pandemic (from 1894) — Hong Kong, Bombay, steamship spread · Crisis
- **Historical grounding:** Wikipedia 1894 HK plague / Third pandemic; JMVH; Grokipedia Bombay.
- **Trigger:** `recent Keep Order(Force:Segregation Camps) within 2 seasons AND Order still ≤2 AND Grudge(magnitude≥2) AND Π≥8`
- **Response branches:** The prior forced-segregation resolves the epidemic pressure (Order recovers, Π drops) but the card is the political bill — an Intrigue "assassination plot" against the governor/administrator scaled to the Grudge the Force generated.
- **Follow-on (FO):** A successful assassination (W.C. Rand, 22 June 1897) forces a Recall/succession + Π spike; detected/countered → a durable Reputation hit without the succession shock.
- **Introduces (Action):** Segregation Camps (harsher Force sub-branch under Keep Order, buying a larger/faster Π/Order recovery at double the Disposition penalty + a guaranteed Grudge + an assassination-class Intrigue draw-weight flag).
- **Loop:** Segregation Camps end a high-Π Plague Crisis fast (Order/Π recover) but the Disposition/Grudge cost is exactly the trigger for the assassination Intrigue — the action that solves the Crisis manufactures the next; because the underlying sanitation underinvestment that made Force "necessary" is untouched, repeated reaching for it accumulates Grudge faster than Order, converging on the Rand outcome rather than stability.

# Event Cards — 2.8 Multi-year climate-shift and storm/hurricane fleet destruction (Climatic)

*Key: `historical_concerns_climate_storm_fleet_destruction`. Two spines: **staging/timing exposure** (an anchored/late fleet feeds a shared storm-severity roll — Sea Wall, Storm-Season Withdrawal, Sheltered Dockyard, Flota Scheduling), and **correlated world-level shocks** (the Cooling flag / General Crisis — Remit vs Extract, Civic Granary, Adopt Local Practice, Annona route diversification).*

### HEV-CLIM-01 — Kamikaze typhoons vs the Mongol fleets (1274 & 1281) · Crisis (Opportunity/Ambition follow-on for the defender)
- **Historical grounding:** Two typhoons destroyed invading Mongol fleets off Japan in 1274 and 1281. *Britannica; Woodruff et al., Geology (2014); Wikipedia Mongol invasions/Genkō Bōrui.*
- **Trigger:** `enemy Conquest targets a coastal settlement whose Defense is below the attacker's Mil-advantage AND the fleet staged/anchored ≥1 season within the storm-season window AND Π≥8`
- **Response branches:** A prayer Sponsor gives only a flat low bonus — the real fork is Defy (Fortify Sea Wall multiplying the attacker's storm-loss, or a preemptive Muster before the window closes); a storm firing against the attacker drops their Mil + writes a Grudge/Demoralization tag lowering future Muster-weight.
- **Follow-on (FO):** Ignoring resolves Conquest at full Mil; a storm vs the attacker seeds a defender "Divine Reputation" Precedent boosting Recognition odds + an attacker command-failure Demotion Intrigue.
- **Introduces (Action):** Sea Wall (new Fortify method lowering coastal storm-Crisis severity) + Storm-Season Staging Exposure (Muster/Conquest timing rule: multi-season anchoring accrues a storm-loss multiplier scaled to season-of-year, rushed Levy/Muster shoddy hulls multiply it further).
- **Loop:** Sea Wall lowers the storm-Crisis roll against an anchored fleet, reducing the attacker's effective Mil; an attacker who Musters on a compressed timeline or overstays the window raises that same roll against itself — both sides feed one shared roll from opposite ends, its outcome (Grudge on the loser, Precedent on the survivor) resetting both factions' future weighting.

### HEV-CLIM-02 — Spanish Armada destroyed by Atlantic storms (1588) · Friction→Crisis
- **Historical grounding:** The Spanish Armada, sent against England, was destroyed largely by Atlantic storms forced upon it by a failed rendezvous. *Wikipedia Armada / Armada in Ireland; INA "Twelve Apostles" (Casaban); NASA Science.*
- **Trigger:** `a Conquest requiring fleet+land-army rendezvous fails its coordination check ("Rendezvous Failure") forcing the fleet onto a high-exposure route while Π is high AND the funding Levy under-supplied Treasury (spoiled-provisions flag)`
- **Response branches:** Comply (retreat as planned: full roll), Bargain (spend AP/Treasury to re-provision/re-route, e.g. a Treat safe-harbor deal), Defy (push the original route: command-Friction + full roll); failure writes Debt/`Weak` on the Crown + Grudge on the failed ally.
- **Follow-on (FO):** A resolved Crisis seeds a Directive-level "Rebuild" (Purpose-Built Galleon), funded by Treasury and gated by repaying the Crisis's Debt tag.
- **Introduces (Action):** Purpose-Built Galleon (new Levy quality/quantity fork: cheap/fast Levy fields more hulls at a higher storm multiplier — the 130-ship Armada; Purpose-Built costs more per hull but lowers it — the Twelve-Apostles asiento, unlocked only after a Crisis fires once).
- **Loop:** Cheap Levy for a larger fleet raises the storm-severity roll once a Friction forces an exposed route; the Crisis's Debt/Reputation tags then gate the Treasury needed to fund the Purpose-Built upgrade — one bad Friction/Crisis chain can lock a faction out of exactly the mitigation it now needs most.

### HEV-CLIM-03 — The Great Hurricane of 1780 · Crisis (symmetric, multi-faction)
- **Historical grounding:** A single hurricane in the Caribbean caused massive fleet losses across multiple rival naval powers simultaneously present in the region. *Wikipedia; Britannica; JAR (2022); American Heritage.*
- **Trigger:** `≥2 rival factions each maintain Conquest/Muster fleet presence in the same contested territory through the storm window (Π≥8 territory-wide) without a Storm-Season Withdrawal`
- **Response branches:** No Directive fork (it's positioning) — "Hold Station" (full roll against every co-present fleet) or "Storm-Season Withdrawal" (cede tempo, no roll); a fired Crisis reduces Mil for all present roughly proportional to fleet size, symmetrically — it does not by itself shift relative standing.
- **Follow-on (FO):** A shared "Rebuilding Race" Opportunity — whoever funds a compensatory Levy fastest regains Mil/tempo first, converting a symmetric Crisis into an asymmetric recovery contest.
- **Introduces (Action):** Storm-Season Withdrawal (Muster/Conquest timing sub-choice, parallel to Levy's Encabezamiento fork — the Royal Navy's real seasonal withdrawal convention).
- **Loop:** Holding presence through the window raises trigger/severity for *every* co-present rival; because losses are correlated the storm decision doesn't change relative standing — pre-storm Treasury/Ledger state (who funds the post-Crisis Levy fastest) is the actual lever deciding who loses ground.

### HEV-CLIM-04 — Great Famine of 1315–1317 (climate-shift framing) · Crisis (multi-season, Needs-driven)
- **Historical grounding:** Sustained crop failures across Northern Europe from a climate shift produced multi-year famine and governance strain. *Wikipedia; Ludlow et al./Baek et al. (2020), Comm Earth & Env.*
- **Trigger:** `a famine-relief Need unserved ≥2 seasons compounding Π via Σ(unserved Needs) until Π≥8 with Prosperity below floor`
- **Response branches:** Comply (Extract/Tax held constant despite failed harvests: Order−1/PS−1 stack, Grudge, `Hated`), Bargain (petition the PA for remission: softens terms at suspicion cost), Defy/Divert (redirect Levy to relief: protects the settlement, Standing-debt up-tier, Disposition/PS up).
- **Follow-on (FO):** Unserved 3+ seasons escalates to Order-collapse/revolt + a rival-patron-recruiting Intrigue.
- **Introduces (Action):** Civic Granary (fourth Develop funding method, Bruges/Ypres/London civic reserves, lowering both the famine severity roll and the Σ(unserved Needs) Π contribution) + Relief (new Directive type the PA issues instead of Extract/Tax).
- **Loop:** Prior Civic Granary spend lowers this card's trigger weight/severity; a governor who instead Complies with a fixed Extract/Tax through the famine accelerates Π and locks in Grudge/`Hated`, reducing future Develop/Levy yields — the P5 Ledger-memory spiral anchored to a documented mitigation against a documented failure mode (fixed in-kind rent through failed harvests).

### HEV-CLIM-05 — General/Global Crisis of the 17th century (Maunder Minimum onset) · Crisis (world/multi-settlement correlated)
- **Historical grounding:** A world-wide cooling episode (Maunder Minimum) correlated with simultaneous crises across many polities in the 17th century. *Parker, Global Crisis (Yale 2013); Wikipedia General Crisis.*
- **Trigger:** `a world-level "Cooling Epoch" flag persists N year-arcs, applying a uniform Π-baseline increase + Prosperity-ceiling reduction across every settlement (inflating Σ(unserved Needs) system-wide)`
- **Response branches:** Per-settlement it plays as ordinary Comply/Bargain/Defy but *correlated* — factions issuing Extract/Tax repeatedly accumulate Grudge/Suspicion/Debt across their whole roster in the same seasons, triggering multiple simultaneous Demotion Magnitude events on their own ladder; Remit-issuing factions absorb the shock at Treasury cost.
- **Follow-on (FO):** Sustained rigid response seeds a cascading multi-settlement Crisis chain — several settlements independently crossing into collapse within the same 1–2 year-arc window.
- **Introduces (Action):** Remit (new Directive type: tax/tribute forgiveness at Treasury cost; a relief-configured Ministry (Part 7) gets an AP/Treasury discount on Remit during a Cooling flag — Parker's "flexible institutions weather the shock").
- **Loop:** The Cooling flag uniformly raises Π and lowers ceilings everywhere, so the PA's aggregate Extract-vs-Remit choice (and whether it built a relief Ministry) decides whether the shared shock is isolated survivable Crises or a simultaneous cascade — and a cascade forces concurrent Demotion Magnitude events stripping the governors needed to manage recovery, deepening the next Π spike.

### HEV-CLIM-06 — Collapse of the Norse Greenland colonies (c. 1350–1450) · Ambition/Crisis (slow-burn, multi-decade)
- **Historical grounding:** The Norse Greenland colonies declined and vanished over roughly a century amid climatic cooling and metropole disinvestment. *Wikipedia Norse Greenland/Hvalsey; Brown/D'Andrea et al., PNAS (2011).*
- **Trigger:** `a distant settlement's Prosperity ceiling declines across year-arcs under a persistent Cooling flag AND it holds a Compact/Debt to a metropole AND its Standing-4+ governor accrues Suspicion via repeated Court Attendance failures (§1.0c)`
- **Response branches:** Comply (maintain metropole Develop practice, keep servicing the Compact: Prosperity keeps declining), Bargain (petition for resupply: mild relief if Treasury/attention available, increasingly unlikely), Defy/Divert (Adopt Local Practice: a Disposition hit with the metropole for real resilience).
- **Follow-on (FO):** Sustained Court Attendance failure + unresolved Debt seeds the metropole's implicit "abandonment" Directive — no more ships, a slow Dismissal-equivalent.
- **Introduces (Action):** Adopt Local Practice (new Develop method-fork for isolated/frontier settlements: trade a Precedent/Disposition shift with the metropole — read as drift/heterodoxy — for a durable resilience bonus against Cooling decline; models Thule marine subsistence vs Norse mixed-farming rigidity, flagging the post-2011 isotopic nuance that diet did shift without full technique adoption).
- **Loop:** A settlement that never spends on Adopt Local Practice keeps bleeding Prosperity, lowering its AP and making both the pivot and Court Attendance's Treasury cost less affordable; on the metropole side, failing Court Attendance/Compact raises Suspicion and reduces willingness to fund resupply Sponsors — a two-sided disinvestment spiral ending in implicit abandonment rather than one dramatic Crisis.

### HEV-CLIM-07 — The Great Storm of 1703 · Crisis
- **Historical grounding:** A catastrophic storm struck England, destroying warships moored in exposed roadsteads. *Wikipedia; RMG Greenwich; History.com.*
- **Trigger:** `a naval-basing settlement's Fortify method on file is "Roadstead Mooring" (exposed/cheap) rather than "Sheltered Dockyard" AND Π≥8 within the storm window`
- **Response branches:** Comply (accept the roll: full ship/Mil loss proportional to fleet moored in the open), Defy-in-advance (pay to relocate to Sheltered Dockyard before the window closes, sharply reducing severity).
- **Follow-on (FO):** A fired Crisis unlocks a Develop "Lighthouse" (Eddystone rebuild) + an "Underwriting" Opportunity (marine-insurance Sponsor/Treat mitigating future storm Treasury loss at an ongoing premium).
- **Introduces (Action):** Sheltered Dockyard (new Fortify basing-location method) + Lighthouse (Develop follow-on locked until a storm Crisis fires once).
- **Loop:** Cheap Roadstead-Mooring saves AP now but raises the storm roll's trigger/severity; the Crisis's resolution is what unlocks the Sheltered Dockyard upgrade and the Lighthouse/Underwriting follow-ons — the mitigation most needed is gated behind having first suffered the loss.

### HEV-CLIM-08 — 1622 hurricane destruction of the Spanish treasure fleet (Nuestra Señora de Atocha) · Friction→Crisis
- **Historical grounding:** A hurricane destroyed the Spanish treasure fleet, including the Nuestra Señora de Atocha, after a delayed departure pushed it into the storm season. *Wikipedia Atocha; Mel Fisher's Treasures; EBSCO.*
- **Trigger:** `a Levy convoy (scheduled flota method) has an unresolved "Loading Delay" Friction still open when the departure window closes, pushing actual departure into the storm-season Π-high band`
- **Response branches:** Comply (depart late: full roll against Treasury-in-transit), Bargain (spend AP/Treasury to expedite loading before the window), Defy (delay a full further season: Standing-debt/tempo but no roll).
- **Follow-on (FO):** A fired Crisis (total convoy loss) seeds a Sponsor/Opportunity "Salvage Concession" recovering partial value over seasons for a Compact-like concession to salvor Actors/Guilds.
- **Introduces (Action):** Flota Scheduling (new Levy scheduled-convoy method with a hard seasonal storm-avoidance bonus inside its window / penalty if a Loading-Delay Friction is left unresolved past it — the Casa de Contratación fixed-calendar doctrine and its 1622 failure) + Expedite (Friction-resolution spend).
- **Loop:** Leaving the Loading-Delay Friction unresolved pushes departure later (raising the storm trigger against that shipment); a fired Crisis destroys that season's remittance, reducing next season's AP/Treasury — including the funds to expedite next year's convoy — while the Salvage Concession offers only slow Compact-gated recovery.

### HEV-CLIM-09 — Late Antique Little Ice Age (536–660 AD) + onset of the Justinianic Plague · Crisis chain (Friction→multi-settlement)
- **Historical grounding:** A prolonged climatic cooling event coincided with the onset of the Justinianic Plague, straining the Byzantine Empire's centralized grain-supply system. *Wikipedia LALIA; Büntgen et al., Nature Geoscience (2016).*
- **Trigger:** `a world-level Cooling flag centered on one high-yield route feeding a centralized Annona-style Levy combines with Crown Treasury already committed to a concurrent Conquest (Treasury-depleted flag) and Π≥8 in the grain-source province`
- **Response branches:** Comply (maintain the centralized route through the shock: if the source province itself Crises, empire-wide Remit capacity collapses simultaneously), Bargain (petition for diversified routes: costly, slow), Defy/Divert (issue Remit in affected provinces despite the Conquest spend: protects Order at severe Treasury strain, risking the campaign's funding).
- **Follow-on (FO):** An unmitigated Crisis on the source province cascades into simultaneous Crisis draws on every settlement dependent on that route + frontier-faction Intrigue exploiting the fiscal+plague strain.
- **Introduces (Action):** Annona Route Diversification (Levy centralization-vs-redundancy fork, parallel to Encabezamiento/Flota Scheduling; models Justinian's Egyptian-grain dependency and mirrors the stale-Assessment failure mode at empire scale) + Directive Remit.
- **Loop:** Centralizing relief on one province's output lowers per-season cost but means that province's own Crisis removes the empire's Remit capacity everywhere the route feeds, converting a regional shock into a correlated cascade; a Crown also spending Treasury on a concurrent Conquest has less capacity to fund emergency Remit or diversification — the Levy/Conquest choices that build tempo directly shrink the slack needed to survive the shock on the chosen chokepoint.

# 2.9 Currency debasement, fiscal collapse, sovereign default, tax-base crises (Economic)

*Key: `currency_debasement_fiscal_collapse_distillation_v1`. The through-line: the proposed **Capital-Posture Ledger family** (Debased / Speculative) as a one-way ratchet — Levy:Debase / Charter Fiat / Farm buy Treasury by stacking a tag that raises all future Levy Ob and fiscal-Crisis weight, and the only exits (Recoinage / Currency Reset / Funding & Assumption) grow more expensive the longer deferred.*

### HEV-COIN-01 — Roman Third-Century Crisis debasement / Diocletian's edicts (Roman Empire, 3rd century CE) · Crisis
- **Historical grounding:** TheCollector; Wikipedia Edict on Maximum Prices.
- **Trigger:** `Capital-Posture:Debased present AND Prosperity down 2+ seasons AND Π≥8`
- **Response branches:** Comply (Tribunal price/wage cap: Order+1 short-term, `Harsh` Reputation, seeds a black-market Intrigue rather than fixing anything); Bargain (Guild-scoped cap, Concession); Defy (prices run, Order−1, debasement unaddressed).
- **Follow-on (FO):** Comply→ a "Black Market" Intrigue (Clerk-Corruption evasion), Bargain→ a "Guild cites the Concession" Petition; either way Capital-Posture:Debased persists — the edict manages the symptom, never removes the tag.
- **Introduces (Action):** Levy:Debase (third Levy method: auto-succeeds, extracts Treasury with a smaller Order/Prosperity draw this season but writes/stacks Capital-Posture:Debased permanently raising future Levy Ob + price/Crisis draw-weight; a companion high-severity "Tribunal" price-cap method is this card's Comply made proactive).
- **Loop:** Levy:Debase eases this season's extraction but stacks a tag raising Π's fiscal contribution + the price-panic Crisis weight; resolving that Crisis via Comply (price caps) doesn't clear the tag — it converts the pressure into a recurring black-market Intrigue chain until an actual Recoinage/Currency Reset.

### HEV-COIN-02 — England's Great Debasement / Elizabethan Great Recoinage (England, 1544–1551 / 1560–61) · Crisis
- **Historical grounding:** Wikipedia Great Debasement (recoinage-profit disputed £50k vs £14k).
- **Trigger:** `Capital-Posture:Debased count ≥2 AND an active Directive:Extract still unmet`
- **Response branches:** Comply (debase again to hit the Directive: Treasury+, a third stack, Reputation−1); Bargain (partial reform, mixed old/new coin at a negotiated rate: Concession, slower decay); Defy (refuse, force the Directive unmet: Standing-debt with the PA, no further stacking).
- **Follow-on (FO):** Repeated Comply escalates to a "Gresham's Hoarding" Crisis (good coin vanishes, Prosperity stalls though nominal Treasury looks healthy).
- **Introduces (Action):** Recoinage (rare Directive-level action requiring PA approval beyond a single governor: retires debased coin, clears all Capital-Posture:Debased tags, resets Levy Ob — costed steep, mirroring Elizabeth's single 1560–61 reform after Edward VI's rounds).
- **Loop:** Each Levy:Debase season compounds the tag, raising the Gresham's-Hoarding severity/weight *and* the eventual Recoinage price; Recoinage is the only clearing action, so deferral makes the exit more expensive — a legible one-way ratchet.

### HEV-COIN-03 — Edward III's default on the Bardi & Peruzzi (England/Florence, 1340s) · Crisis
- **Historical grounding:** The Financial Engineer; Wikipedia Bardi.
- **Trigger:** `a Debt-family tag owed to a specific Sponsor-tier actor outstanding AND Treasury < owed AND a Directive:Repay unmet 1+ season`
- **Response branches:** Comply (repay: Debt cleared, Sponsor Disposition+, AP/Treasury squeeze); Bargain (renegotiate: Debt reduced + partly Concession); Defy/Default (Debt flips to an Outlawed tag on the Crown, Sponsor Disposition collapses, and if concentrated, seeds their bankruptcy Crisis).
- **Follow-on (FO):** Default seeds a "Creditor Collapse" Crisis on the defaulted-against Sponsor if their exposure was undiversified, removing them as a future financing option (Peruzzi 1343 / Bardi 1346).
- **Introduces (Action):** Levy:Farm (tax-farming variant: pledge a future season's Levy yield to a Sponsor-tier actor as collateral for an immediate Treasury/AP infusion, writing a Debt tag).
- **Loop:** Levy:Farm trades immediate relief for a Debt gating a future Directive:Repay; Defy converts it to a permanent Outlawed tag blocking all further Sponsor/Treat with that faction, and if the pledge was concentrated, chains into that Sponsor's own Crisis — permanently shrinking the province's financing options for the one-time windfall.

### HEV-COIN-04 — Ottoman akçe debasement / 1585–86 monetary crisis (Ottoman Empire, 1585–86) · Crisis
- **Historical grounding:** Pamuk, Price Revolution Reconsidered; ResearchGate 1585 crisis (headline figures confirmed; 3-year-doubling/30–40% real-wage flagged lower-confidence).
- **Trigger:** `Capital-Posture:Debased active AND a garrison/salaried cohort Obligation paid at a fixed nominal rate (not re-indexed) AND that cohort's Disposition below threshold`
- **Response branches:** Comply (pay at old nominal rate: Disposition falls via real-wage erosion, Grudge, raises Ambition risk); Bargain (raise nominal pay partially — "Treat: Index Wages": Concession, extra Treasury, partial relief); Defy (ignore the gap: Order collapses, Keep Order Ob rises).
- **Follow-on (FO):** Sustained Comply/Defy without indexing seeds an Ambition card — the underpaid garrison commander advances their own agenda (mutiny/defection/covert dealing).
- **Introduces (Action):** Treat: Index Wages (minor side-deal re-pegging a fixed-nominal Obligation to current Capital-Posture at an ongoing Treasury cost).
- **Loop:** Levy:Debase raises Treasury now but erodes the real value of any fixed-nominal Obligation; every unaddressed-Grudge season raises Keep Order Ob in that settlement and advances the commander-NPC's ambition-engine, converting a Levy-side convenience into a Keep-Order-side liability that can remove Keep Order as a viable option there.

### HEV-COIN-05 — Ancien Régime France / summons of the Estates-General (France, 1787–89) · Crisis
- **Historical grounding:** TutorChase (French American-war support corrected to ~1.3bn livres; Calonne rejected 1787).
- **Trigger:** `a Debt tag's magnitude relative to aggregate Prosperity exceeds a fiscal-crisis threshold AND privileged Standing-holders (nobility/clergy ranks) hold enough rank to exercise collective veto over a fiscal-reform Directive`
- **Response branches:** Comply (universal-tax reform breaking exemptions: privileged Disposition craters faction-wide, Grudge across the tier); Bargain (partial reform, top exemptions preserved: Concession, deficit persists); Defy (shelve reform, cook the books — a Compte-rendu concealment: writes a hidden "Precedent: Concealed Deficit" invisible on the surface Ledger that does *not* release Π).
- **Follow-on (FO):** Concealed-Deficit Defy delays the reckoning but auto-escalates the next drawn Crisis ("Loan Failure Riots"/forced Estates-General summons) to materially higher severity.
- **Introduces (Action):** Directive: Fiscal Reform (reverse-scale — the PA issues it to *itself*, resolved collectively by privileged faction ladders via Ministry/Consulta as the veto surface).
- **Loop:** Defy (cooking the books) avoids the immediate Grudge but writes a hidden Concealed-Deficit tag raising Π invisibly every season, guaranteeing a harsher forced Crisis later; Comply permanently lowers future Debt accrual but spends Disposition/Standing with every privileged NPC at once, making every subsequent Sponsor/Treat with that cohort harder.

### HEV-COIN-06 — John Law's Mississippi Bubble / Banque Royale collapse (France, 1716–1720) · Opportunity
- **Historical grounding:** Market Histories; Britannica Money.
- **Trigger:** `low-mid Π AND a large outstanding Debt tag AND a Sponsor-tier actor offers to absorb it for a monopoly Charter/Concession`
- **Response branches:** Comply (swap Debt for equity-like paper backed by the Charter: Treasury relief, temporary Prosperity+, writes Capital-Posture:Speculative pegged to the Sponsor's solvency); Bargain (cap the Concession, diversify backing: smaller tag); Defy (refuse: Debt persists).
- **Follow-on (FO):** If the Speculative tag's issued size exceeds the settlement's real backing ratio, auto-escalates to a "Bank Run" Crisis (the note-to-metal breach: ~2.6bn notes vs ~1.2bn backing).
- **Introduces (Action):** Levy:Charter Fiat (issues paper Treasury backed by a granted Charter/Concession asset instead of extracting real Prosperity — high yield, each unit adding to a Capital-Posture:Speculative tag whose backing ratio sets the Bank Run trigger).
- **Loop:** Charter Fiat converts an immediate shortfall into a Speculative tag sized by paper-vs-backing; crossing threshold deterministically fires the Bank Run, which permanently burns the method (a Ledger tag blocking reuse) and imposes an emergency Keep Order tax on every settlement holding the paper — a province-wide convenience into a province-wide punitive cleanup.

### HEV-COIN-07 — French assignat hyperinflation (France, 1790s) · Crisis
- **Historical grounding:** Market Histories; Wikipedia Assignat.
- **Trigger:** `Capital-Posture:Speculative/land-backed tag active (Charter Fiat backed by contested land) AND the backing asset's seizure carries a Grudge from the dispossessed faction AND Π escalating`
- **Response branches:** Comply (keep issuing against the contested land: nominal Prosperity+ but Reputation collapses, the dispossessed faction's Recognition Fork §1.0b trends to Denial); Bargain (cap issuance, partial land settlement: Concession, slows not stops); Defy/reset (triggers Currency Reset).
- **Follow-on (FO):** Ignoring chains to a harsher forced-requisitioning rung (the historical Maximum) before a Currency Reset is forced.
- **Introduces (Action):** Currency Reset (Directive-level, same family as Recoinage but writes a permanent "Precedent: Monetary Failure" raising baseline Π for several seasons — a land-backed fiat collapse cannot reset as cleanly as a metallic debasement).
- **Loop:** Backing Charter Fiat with a Grudge-tagged confiscated asset means every Speculative unit issued deepens the dispossessed faction's Grudge and worsens their Recognition Fork, so the overprinting both drives the Crisis trigger *and* removes the Treat/Sponsor tools that could soften the eventual Reset — fiscal and political failure tracks coupled by design.

### HEV-COIN-08 — Continental currency collapse / Hamilton's 1790 funding & assumption (United States, 1770s–1790) · Crisis
- **Historical grounding:** Liberty Street Economics; Wikipedia First Report on Public Credit.
- **Trigger:** `a Directive:Extract issued identically to multiple settlements, each resolving Comply/Bargain/Defy independently (no unified Compact), AND aggregate Capital-Posture:Debased across them exceeds a province-wide threshold`
- **Response branches:** Comply per-settlement (each debases/extracts locally: fragmented tags stack fastest); Bargain (a cross-settlement Encabezamiento pooling into one Compact: slower collapse, requires coordination); Defy (a settlement refuses: Directive unmet, Standing-debt, no further stacking).
- **Follow-on (FO):** Once fragmentation crosses threshold, seeds a "Funding & Assumption" Opportunity/Ambition from a centralizing actor.
- **Introduces (Action):** Funding & Assumption (Directive-level: consolidates multiple settlements' separately-accrued Debt/Capital-Posture into one faction-level Compact for a Concession — e.g. a capital-relocation Precedent — permanently strengthening the PA's direct extraction reach).
- **Loop:** Because the shared Directive:Extract has no independent cross-settlement enforcement, each uncoordinated Comply/Defy writes its own Capital-Posture tag; the more fragmented, the higher province-wide Π and the more expensive (in Concession-cost) the eventual Funding & Assumption — coordination failure inflates the price of its own fix (Congress's lack of taxing power forcing Hamilton's bargain).

### HEV-COIN-09 — Weimar hyperinflation (Germany, 1923) · Crisis
- **Historical grounding:** Britannica; Alpha History.
- **Trigger:** `an external-shock "Occupation" Crisis fires (a rival's Conquest on a border settlement) AND Treasury already strained by a prior war-reparations Debt tag`
- **Response branches:** Comply (fund passive resistance/general strike — "Fund Resistance": Treasury→zero, Capital-Posture:Debased spikes to forced-reset severity, populace Reputation+ but PA Disposition−); Bargain (partial compliance with the occupier: Concession, smaller shock); Defy (no resistance funding: local Order/Reputation hit, populace Grudge).
- **Follow-on (FO):** Comply auto-escalates to a *mandatory* Currency Reset at forced-trigger severity next season — a stat-floor breach, not optional.
- **Introduces (Action):** Fund Resistance (emergency uncapped Levy sub-choice / Keep Order: funds resistance directly from Treasury with no AP ceiling, driving Capital-Posture:Debased to maximum in one season).
- **Loop:** Fund Resistance spends Treasury into Capital-Posture:Debased at emergency severity for an immediate Reputation/Order win against the occupation; because it pushes the tag past the forced-reset threshold in one move, it converts the optional Currency Reset into a mandatory one — the real choice is timing, not whether.

### HEV-COIN-10 — Zimbabwe hyperinflation (Zimbabwe, 2000s) · Friction
- **Historical grounding:** Cato Journal (Hanke & Kwok; headline confirmed, 51%/28%→4%-of-GDP flagged lower-confidence).
- **Trigger:** `a Develop:Redistribute action has stripped charter-holders (Outlawed tag) AND Prosperity trends negative 2+ seasons post-action`
- **Response branches:** Comply (proceed: Prosperity−1/season for several seasons — a modeled multi-season yield penalty — Grudge from dispossessed holders, Reputation shift); Bargain (compensated redistribution: smaller penalty, Concession); Defy (abandon under pressure: political-capital loss with the demanding faction).
- **Follow-on (FO):** A sustained negative-Prosperity trend auto-escalates Levy's Ob (a thinner base needs harsher extraction to hit an unchanged target), feeding a Levy:Debase spiral.
- **Introduces (Action):** Develop:Redistribute (new Develop method reallocating land/charter rights away from Guild/Actor holders; ties into the Assessment mechanic — redistribution invalidates the current Assessment, and until a Survey refresh, Levy/Extract keeps targeting the stale pre-redistribution figure).
- **Loop:** The Prosperity penalty erodes the real tax base while the Assessment stays pegged to the pre-redistribution figure until a Survey; every uncorrected season Extract/Levy demands the stale higher figure off a smaller economy (the Scott's-extraction-invariance failure mode), pushing Π up and making Levy:Debase the fastest way to square the gap — compounding rather than resolving the tax-base collapse.

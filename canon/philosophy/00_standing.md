## §0.1 What this suite is, and what kind of authority it has

This is a **reference document**. It records what the framework holds, why it holds it, and what
vocabulary is being used in what sense. It is not a mechanism. If it were deleted, nothing in the
world would resolve differently — the resolution lives in code, and a design document may not be
cited as the reason a behaviour is correct. Where this document and an implementation disagree, that
is a defect in one of them, and it is settled by deciding which is wrong and changing that one. It
is never settled by declaring the prose authoritative.

What this document *is* authoritative for is **intent, history, and vocabulary**: what a term means,
what a distinction is for, what was ruled and when, and what remains open.

Two consequences worth stating plainly, because the source suite violated both:

- **A `## Status:` line is not a mechanism.** A document declaring itself canonical does not make
  its claims true, and four documents declaring themselves canonical while contradicting each other
  is what produced the present state.
- **Numeric bands are not foundations.** Thread Sensitivity thresholds, Coherence tracks, obstacle
  numbers and stability indices are mechanism. This suite says *that* Coherence is indexed and *that*
  outcomes are sensitivity-gated; it does not own the numbers. Where the source amendments carried
  dice-facing consequences into a foundations document, those are marked for relocation rather than
  reproduced here.

## §0.2 Precedence

The five source documents were mutually inconsistent partly because none of them said which one
wins. The head Foundations §16 carried an editorial note declaring that it *absorbed* philosophical
content from both amendments — while both amendments continued to declare themselves canonical, and
one of them closed with the sentence *"This amendment extends the Philosophical Foundations. It does
not contradict any existing canonical claim."* That sentence is false against the suite's own text,
and its falsity was invisible because no precedence rule existed to make it checkable.

**The rule this suite adopts, and the rule it recommends be adopted generally:**

1. **This suite is the sole owner of every claim it states.** Where it states a claim, the sources
   are historical.
2. **Where this suite is silent, the source amendments remain the owners of their unabsorbed
   content** — the three-layer model's derivation, the observable degradation bands, the
   sensitivity-gated outcomes at Coherence 0, the knot-profile mechanics.
3. **A later document that absorbs an earlier one must mark the absorbed sections superseded, in the
   same change.** Absorption without a supersession marker is how two live statements of one
   doctrine drift apart.
4. **Restatements are forbidden.** A condensed rule set, a constraint table's paraphrase, and a
   vocabulary gloss are all restatements. Each must either cite the owning section rather than
   re-say it, or be generated from it mechanically. Every doctrinal drift found in the source suite
   entered through a hand-written restatement.

Rule 4 is the load-bearing one. The suite did not drift because anyone changed their mind. It
drifted because the same claim was written by hand in four places, and hand-written copies rot
independently.

## §0.3 Lineage — what is borrowed, what is adapted, and what is owed

The framework is an **amalgam with its own cosmology**, not an exegesis of anyone. The traditions
below are sources it draws on, not standards it answers to. A departure from a source is therefore
presumed deliberate.

But a departure is not free, and this is the distinction the source suite failed to make. When a
concept is borrowed by name, it arrives with load-bearing structure attached. Take the name and drop
the structure, and some job the structure was doing becomes a **debt** the framework now owes from
its own resources. The lineage is honest only if it says which debts are paid.

| Tradition | What is taken | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Husserl** | Inner time-consciousness (retention, protention); the epoché as a figure for the Leap; the pre-predicative stratum; intersubjective constitution | **Adapted.** Three pivots, two of which incur debts — see below |

| **Heidegger** | The ontical/ontological distinction; uncanniness; thrownness | **Contested.** See §9.1; the source claim of "correct orientation" is withdrawn |

### The four pivots, and whether their debts are paid

**The Real.** Lacan's Real is defined *relative to the symbolic* — it is what that order cannot
reach, and its resistance is built into that definition. This framework redefines it ontologically,
as infinite positive being. The pivot buys a Real that is plenitude rather than lack, which is what
lets monstrosity be surfeit rather than pathology. **The debt:** having dropped the structural
definition, the framework can no longer borrow Lacan's explanation of *why* the Real resists. It must
explain resistance itself. **Paid** — §5.3's finitude argument does exactly this, and does it well:
resistance is not a property of what arrives but of the finitude it arrives at. This is the
framework's best pivot and it should be stated as one.

**The epoché.** Husserl's reduction brackets the natural attitude, is reversible, and preserves the
transcendental ego as absolute residue. The Leap suspends the residue itself and can fail to
re-engage. The pivot buys a suspension that is genuinely dangerous. **The debt:** the guarantee of a
surviving constituting subject. **Paid, and productively** — the forfeited guarantee is precisely
what makes the retention roll a real risk rather than a formality. The suite has never said this;
saying it converts an apparent misuse into a deliberate and well-motivated inversion.

**Ein Sof without agency.** In Luria the Ein Sof is God and performs its own withdrawal. This
framework strips the ground of agency entirely. The pivot buys a cosmology with no theodicy: the
Calamity is structural, not a fall, and nothing is being punished. **The debt:** the explanation of
why contraction occurred at all. **Partially paid** — §1.1's epistemic charter converts the question
into a principled refusal rather than an unanswered question, which is legitimate, but the framework
should say that it is doing this, and should note that it thereby forgoes an origin story rather
than possessing one it declines to tell.

**The ontological difference.** This is the one pivot the source suite claimed *not* to be making.
§17 asserted Heideggerian terminology "in its correct orientation." **The debt is not paid**, and
§9.1 now withdraws the fidelity claim rather than pretending otherwise.

### On Derrida

*(Ruled 2026-09-07: Derrida, Kant and Freud/Jentsch are all named. The alternative — scrubbing the
borrowed vocabulary at about twenty sites — was available and not taken.)*

Derrida was named by the author as a source and appeared in none of the source documents' lineage.
His machinery is nonetheless everywhere in the suite: *always-already* is used some twenty times as
a term of art; §5.3's "somatic echo retaining traces the mind cannot retrieve" is the trace; layer
three supplementing a failed layer two is supplementary logic in its textbook form, including the
reversal in which the supplement turns out to be what the "original" always depended on; §1.2's
regress of grounds is the aporia of the origin.

And the framework's central figure is his. *Thread, weave, texture, textile* and *text* share a root;
the woven text is Derrida's own image for a structure with no outside. A cosmology whose substrate is
a weave has arrived, independently, at the figure he used for exactly this problem. That convergence
is evidence the structure is sound, and it belongs in the record.

The point at which he is most useful to the framework is §2.1, where threads are asserted to be both
the constituted substrate and the constituting condition. The suite states this doubleness and never
interrogates it. Whether it can be held as a *distinction* at all is the framework's deepest open
question, and it is treated at §2.1 rather than buried.

# §7 — Drift

**Status:** PROPOSED
**Owns:** how Coherence is lost; the drift phenomenology; propagation; the bands; reality-strain; Coherence 0
**Sources:** F §16.1 (second half), §16.2, §16.3, §16.4; A Am. 3 bands, Am. 4

---

## §7.1 How Coherence is lost

Not every act of threadwork produces drift.

**Operations directed toward the futural-potential-legible produce no Coherence cost.** Their result
is a shape the configurations were tending toward, so once actualized it stands in harmony with what
surrounds it and needs no holding. Nothing returns through the knot.

**Operations that impose a shape off the attractor must be held against the draw**, and the cost of
holding such a configuration is a configurational alteration to the practitioner. This is the
operational origin of drift: not threadwork as such, but threadwork directed away from the harmony
configurations tend toward.

The second channel is confrontation — environmental, and not confined to practitioners (§6.8).

**Drift accumulates from misalignment and from exposure. It does not accumulate from threadwork.**

## §7.2 The phenomenology of drift

Configurations are drawn toward the futural-potential-legible (§3.4): the equilibrium in which they
stand in harmony, needing no sustaining. A practitioner at full Coherence is aligned with that draw —
their layer-two self-maintenance holds them in a shape that is close to one they tend toward anyway,
so the holding is cheap.

**Drift is the progressive decoupling of the practitioner's configuration from that equilibrium.** As
Coherence drops, self-maintenance no longer holds the configuration in human-mode shape, and it takes
other shapes — shapes standing in harmony with nothing, which the tendency therefore draws nowhere.
The further the drift, the more of the being's persistence has to be *held* rather than tended toward.

**Early.** The practitioner seems slightly off — uncanny in small ways, harder to read, less
predictable. Their presence exerts a faint dissonance on the shared rendering around them. This is not
a social perception but an ontological effect: their threads, less held in human configuration, are
harder for others' renderings to apprehend as a human subject. It shows in actuality (small material
wrongness — a posture that does not quite match a body, a presence occupying more space than it
should), in intelligibility (others speaking as if through glass, addressing what was meant rather
than what was said), and in temporality (micro-slippages in the ordering of action and response).

**Advanced.** The drift approximates the strain produced by proximity to monstrous beings. The
practitioner approaches the unintelligible pole; the rendering must work harder to integrate them, and
that effort produces the same unease, wrongness and destabilization monstrosity produces, scaled to
the partial drift. **The wrongness is not in what the practitioner does. It is in the shape their being
now holds** — a shape decoupled from the equilibrium that human-mode existence is sustained by.

*(One caution on wording. What admits of degree is others' capacity to integrate the practitioner, not
the practitioner's proximity to a categorical remainder. A drifting practitioner does not become
incrementally more Real; the rendering becomes incrementally less able to hold them.)*

## §7.3 Propagation

A practitioner's drift is not private. Their threads are knotted to family, fellow practitioners,
community; their frays wrap around everyone they encounter. When the drifting configuration is altered
tridimensionally, the knotted threads carry that alteration — and the propagation is tridimensional
too. Connected people experience configurational pull (actuality), apperceptive degradation in their
relation to the practitioner (intelligibility), and retention/protention disturbance regarding shared
experience (temporality).

This is not magical. If one end of a knotted thread is altered along all three dimensions, the other
end bears force along all three.

**Two distinct channels, which the source suite ran together:**

- **Rendering** — presence-based. Requires people actually apprehending the practitioner. Reaches
  strangers in proximity. This is §7.2's dissonance.
- **Knots** — relationship-based, and **distance-independent**. Knots persist without co-presence, so
  this channel reaches only the bound, but reaches them anywhere.

So a drifting practitioner who leaves the community stops straining the local shared rendering and
**keeps pulling on knotted kin**. Exile does not protect the people who love them. A stranger nearby is
affected by the rendering channel only — until wrapping forms a bond.

**Exposure is bivalent.** Cumulative exposure to a drifting practitioner is itself low-grade
confrontation, and confrontation has the consequences described at §5.4: it can develop sensitivity in
the exposed person, if their configuration can hold it, or initiate drift in them, if it cannot. Which
branch occurs turns on whether the registration is held or overwhelms.

Drift is therefore propagable through community. A single drifting practitioner is, over time, a
vector by which others' Coherence may begin to fail — which is why early detection and communal
accountability are structurally necessary rather than merely prudent. The practitioner is not the only
one at risk.

## §7.4 The observable bands

The degradation has recognizable stages. **The numbers below are mechanism and are not owned by this
document** — the philosophy holds only that Coherence is indexed and that the stages are ordered. The
phenomenology is foundations material; the thresholds are not.

**Stable.** No perceptible change. Self-rendering re-engages fully after each Leap.

**Dissonant.** Subtle uncanniness. The configuration has shifted enough that others' renderings work
harder. Temporal micro-slippages: actions fractionally ahead of or behind the moment. Responses that
address what you meant rather than what you said. Loved ones describe speaking to someone through
glass.

**Fragmented.** Visibly wrong. Materiality behaves inconsistently — shadows that do not match posture,
presence occupying more space than the body, flickers between being fully in the scene and partially
elsewhere. Temporality perceptibly non-linear: references to events that have not happened, reactions
before causes. Intelligibility fractures: the practitioner is apperceived differently by different
observers — not in opinion, but in what was perceived.

**Fractured.** Physical form manifests substrate alteration directly. Weight distribution wrong. Light
interacting at a register that produces unease. Temporal existence perceptible to anyone —
conversations skip, loop, proceed at inconsistent rates. Knowledge arrives chaotically rather than as
integrated perception. Relational propagation is constant and uncontrolled.

**Coherence failure.** Self-rendering as human has ceased. The ground still spools the being; they are
not threadcut and not dissolving. What is gone is the layer that maintained the configuration as
human. They are being spooled into whatever raw spooling produces with nothing shaping it.

**Every band is written from the observer's side, and that is a consequence rather than a stylistic
choice.** Temporal Disjunction is a gap between retention and thread-history; if the retention pole is
itself degrading, the gap becomes indeterminate *for the practitioner* while remaining perfectly
determinate for observers, who retain normally. **A drifting practitioner does not experience mounting
disjunction. They experience less and less of it, while being more and more disjoined.** This is the
temporal instance of the self-judgment asymmetry (§4.3): the faculty that would register the failure is
the faculty failing.

## §7.5 Reality-strain

A practitioner whose Coherence has failed and who has the perceptual reach to maintain themselves
through deliberate threadwork is holding a **configuration standing in harmony with nothing around
it**. By §6.6 that is manipulative by definition — off the attractor, held only by the being itself.

The cost channel for manipulative holding is Coherence. **But there is no layer 2 left to bear it.**
With nothing to absorb the load into the being's own configuration, it is borne by the threads the
being draws on: its vicinity. **That is reality-strain**, and it is the externalized form of what
would otherwise have been Coherence cost.

The scale differs from the Einhir lattice — one being rather than a civilization, a lifetime rather
than generations — but the mechanism is the same: a shape held against the draw of the equilibrium,
drawing the fabric taut. Strain is the summed difference, across everything affected, between what is
held and where the configurations tend. Sustained long enough and deep enough, it produces the conditions the Calamity
produced: anchor-points strain, the fabric tears, incursions and gaps and failures occur in the
strained vicinity.

**This is not a moral fact and not an indictment.** Such a being may be entirely benign in intention.
The strain follows from what they must do to continue existing, not from anything they choose beyond
that.

**Two consequences the source suite did not draw:**

- **Every manipulative operation leaves some strain.** For a practitioner with Coherence remaining, the
  load is *split* — part absorbed by layer 2 as Coherence loss, part left in the substrate. Coherence-0
  beings are the limiting case, not a separate phenomenon. This is also why proximity to third-mode
  presences erodes Coherence in others: they are standing in strained fabric.
- **Threadcut beings strain reality too.** The profile is the same minus spooling, and the derivation
  is untouched by the difference. Solmund strained the substrate for the whole generation of his
  presence — and the record notes nothing of it. See supplement, ruling R-7.

**The history is therefore not only backstory.** It is the structural consequence of exactly the
configuration such a being sustains, localized and reduced in scale but identical in mechanism. The
warning applies forward as well as backward.

## §7.6 Coherence 0 and what follows

At Coherence 0 the same structural event occurs for everyone: layer 2 ceases. What follows depends on
**perceptual reach** — how much of the substrate the being can perceive and deliberately work.

**Sensitivity does not increase at Coherence 0.** The being does not suddenly perceive more because
their human rendering collapsed. They have exactly the perceptual architecture they built, and that
architecture determines what they become.

**Shallow reach — freefall.** No capacity to threadwork oneself at the depth lost self-rendering
required. The configuration is exposed: raw spooling plus environmental thread-forces begin reshaping
it. Consciousness persists — they are not unconscious — but it experiences a being it can no longer
render as human and cannot work into any stable alternative. The environment writes itself into them
because nothing resists it. They become increasingly alien and increasingly unable to communicate what
is happening, because the words would require a rendering they no longer have. This is not
transcendence. It is freefall.

**Relational reach — persistence through others.** Enough reach to perceive and partially maintain the
knots connecting them to other beings. They can hold onto who they were *in relation to others* while
what they are in themselves becomes unstable. Identity becomes relational rather than personal;
communication remains possible, but only through existing relationships. Their knots become
structurally load-bearing — the relationships are literally holding the being together — and strain on
those knots becomes existential. If a knot severs, part of what holds them in any recognizable
configuration goes with it.

**Structural reach — reconstitution.** Enough reach to threadwork oneself into a stable non-human
configuration: not the human one that was lost, but something organized, self-consistent and
self-maintaining. Their self-maintenance is deliberate and continuous — functionally what a threadcut
being does, except that they are still spooled. Every moment of their continued existence is an
operation, and every operation strains the substrate.

**Full reach — reconstitution and strain.** The being reorganizes around a beyond-human rendering that
is comprehensive, self-consistent and enormously capable. And every moment of their existence involves
threadwork at a scale and depth that strains the substrate the way the Einhir lattice did — not for
civilizational refinement, but for self-maintenance. Such a being can be benign in intention and
catastrophic in structural effect. Their continued existence creates the conditions for localized
Calamity-scale events, not because they intend it, but because the physics of what they must do to
exist demands it.

**The endpoint is not one state.** It is a reach-gated set of structurally distinct trajectories, and
the distinction is structural rather than moral or narrative.

*(The source suite gave four bands in one document and a two-way split in another, without saying
which superseded which. The four-way account above is the finer and is treated as canonical; the
numeric thresholds attached to it are mechanism, not foundations.)*

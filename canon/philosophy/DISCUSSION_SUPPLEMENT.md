This records what was found in the five source documents, what was resolved and on what authority,

## §1 The headline: is the threadwork rigorous and resolved?

**Partly, and the shortfall is locatable rather than diffuse.**

The framework has one genuinely load-bearing invention — the three-layer model of being-persistence —
and one genuinely good analysis, the finitude pivot at §5.3. It is strongest where it refuses
explanation and where it derives structure from the layer model. It is weakest exactly where it was
most confident: three separate claims to have adopted a borrowed apparatus *correctly* are false
against the suite's own text.

**On the sharper question — is the work justifiable from its foundations?** A census of 38 distinct
operational claims:

| | Count | Share |
|---|---|---|
| **Entailed** by an argument that is on the page and valid | 9 | 24% |
| **Entailed**, but by a chain never written or mis-cited | 13 | 34% |
| **Underdetermined** — consistent, but not uniquely yielded | 6 | 16% |
| **Stipulated** — asserted with the appearance of derivation | 4 | 10% |
| **Underivable** — the foundations push the other way | 6 | 16% |

**As written, about a quarter of the operational layer is justified by arguments the framework
actually makes. About three-fifths is justifiable.** Five of the six underivable items are closable by
derivations now written out in §1–§10; one should be struck. After those repairs the figure is
roughly **71% entailed**, with the remaining stipulations being three legitimate posits plus the class
of numbers, which no foundations could derive.

The honest summary: *as written, mostly not; in principle, mostly yes; and the gap between the two is
two derivations, one reading, and one admission.*

**Where the seam falls.** In three places, and the first is a single clause.

1. **One sentence in the source §16.1** slides mid-clause from "the rendering's stabilising tendency"
   to "what the substrate's own spooling would resume." Everything upstream of that slide is entailed;
   everything downstream — the operation-type taxonomy, the cost channel, Mending's immunity,
   reality-strain, the Locked Zone account — is stipulated or contradicted. The whole operational
   layer stands on which noun that sentence means.
2. **Kind versus magnitude.** Every *kind* of consequence examined is entailed; every *number* is
   stipulated. The suite never marked the boundary, so the foundations assert quantities they cannot
   derive.
3. **Head versus amendments.** The head document asserts what the amendments derive. A reader of the
   foundations alone meets stipulations that are theorems one file away.

**Unresolved things sort into three bins, and the distinction matters:**

- **Open by design** — deliberately indeterminate and better for it. Solmund's intent and the moral
  verdict on him (§8.9). This is the framework's strongest refusal and should be defended against
  pressure to resolve it.
- **Under-specified** — no contradiction, but an answer is owed. Most of §3's rulings, and the gaps at
  §5 below. Recoverable by writing.
- **Contradictory** — two surfaces assert incompatible things; requires a decision, not more prose.
  R-1, R-2, R-3, R-12 and the P-08/P-13 collision were all of this kind. Four are now resolved
  editorially; R-1 is not.

### 2.4 Resolved as attribution hygiene

The framework is an amalgam; departures from a source are presumed deliberate. But three claims
asserted *fidelity*, and a claim of correctness is checkable.

- **"After Husserl: the I-think that accompanies all representations"** is Kant. Corrected, and Husserl's
  own terms substituted — which makes the outward facing and the intersubjectivity claim rigorous
  rather than decorative (§9.3).
- **"Adopts the Kabbalistic usage correctly."** Overclaims: the tradition is not univocal, and
  "infinite positive being" is a positive predication the framework's own apophatic commitments forbid.
  Restated as a **regulative inference from the rendered side** (§1.3).
- **"Adopts Heideggerian terminology in its correct orientation."** Withdrawn (§9.1). The labels were
  swapped; the treatment of both tiers as tiers of stuff was not changed.
- **Process philosophy was credited with valorizing being-without-becoming.** It holds the opposite —
  that is the least real thing there is. Sentence struck (§5.2); the framing the framework actually
  needs survives without it.
- **"Thrownness" was claimed in the lineage and never used in the text.** Now bound: layer 1 *is* it
  (§4.1).
- **Derrida, Kant, and Freud/Jentsch added to the lineage** (§0.3), all three being present in the text
  and previously uncredited.

### 2.5 Resolved by naming a pivot's cost

Four borrowings depart from their source, and the departure is legitimate — but each drops
load-bearing structure and leaves a debt. §0.3 states each, and whether it is paid. Two are paid well
and should be presented as strengths rather than defended as approximations: **the Real's ontological
redefinition** (the debt — explaining resistance — is paid by the finitude argument), and **the epoché**
(the debt — a guaranteed constituting residue — is exactly what makes the Leap dangerous).

## §3 Open rulings — twelve things only you can decide

Each is a live choice where two defensible options lead to materially different frameworks.

**R-1. The Calamity's mechanism.** ✅ **RULED 2026-09-07 — tensile, holding-against, with cascade.**
The Einhir held a precarious balance; when it broke, every configuration associated broke, because
none stood at its own equilibrium. Named *the Calamity*. See `RULINGS.md`. Superseded text follows.
The two source accounts put the cause on different sides of the boundary and cannot be edited into
agreement.
- **(A) Tensile.** Strain exceeds a limit; no event, no operator error. The Calamity is a consequence of
  scale, and the forward warning at §7.5 holds literally. *Costs:* the inversion account, and the
  Lurianic breaking-of-vessels parallel, which is a break at maximum intake rather than fatigue.
- **(B) Inversion.** A working's directional vector flips during execution, releasing everything the
  network accumulated. *Costs:* the rule that the Calamity was "not a spell gone wrong"; the claim that
  a Coherence-0 being's strain is "identical in mechanism"; and it needs a potential/actuality axis the
  foundations never define.
- **(A+B) Phased** — strain as precondition, inversion as trigger. Available, but must be *stated* as
  phased, and "identical in mechanism" weakens to "identical in precondition."
- **Narrowed by the audit:** the tensile account survives only on the *holding-against* reading of
  strain (§8.1), which also requires editing "in pursuit of ever-greater coherence" — because a working
  aligned with what rendering held could not have strained anything.
- *§8.1 provisionally states (A) on the holding-against reading, and names the event the Calamity.*

**R-2. Where the tendency lives.** ✅ **RULED 2026-09-07 — neither option.** The tendency belongs to
the *futural-potential-legible*: the equilibrium in which configurations stand in harmony. A property
of configuration-space, not of the ground or the rendering. See `RULINGS.md`. Superseded text follows.
- **(A) In the ground's spooling.** Gives the taxonomy cosmic backing. *Costs:* the ground acquires a
  direction, which §1.5 forbids; and it fails in Locked Zones.
- **(B) In the rendering.** §1.5 safe; Mending re-describes cleanly. *Costs:* "restorative" then depends
  on what a *rendering* would hold, which ties this ruling to R-4.
- *§3.4 takes (B). The audit finds the taxonomy underivable on (A) and entailed on (B).*

**R-3. Is the Leap risky in itself?** *Dissolved rather than decided — confirm the dissolution.*
The source options were "yes, every suspension can fail" and "no, only operation type costs." The
derivation at §6.8 yields **neither**: risk is environment × duration, plus type × scale. The
vulnerability window is real *and* Mending in a quiet place is genuinely free. Confirm you want this
rather than one of the flat answers.

**R-4. One rendering or many.** ✅ **RULED 2026-09-07 — the dichotomy was rejected.** Rendering is
*positional*: threads are real and unfixed, a being is threads, and acting determines imbrication
which determines perceiving. Agreement is overlapping imbrication. See `RULINGS.md`. Superseded text
follows.
- **(A) Co-constitution.** The shared world is made by the community of renderings. Coherence's outward
  facing becomes genuinely constitutive; "collective rendering" acquires a definition; a person unrendered
  by others is at structural risk. Strange, strong, and consistent with the intersubjectivity claim.
- **(B) Realism about threads**, with per-mind apprehension. Simpler. *Costs:* the denial that rendering
  is an overlay on an independent world, and constitution/experience reverts to reality/perception.
- *§3.5 leans (A) and does not commit. The audit finds (A) comes out **entailed** rather than optional,
  once rendering is granted to actualize.*

**R-5. Own the reification, or re-scope "ontological."**
- **(A) Own it.** Keep the labels; state that the framework deliberately renders the condition of
  possibility as a manipulable substrate, and drop the fidelity claim.
- **(B) Re-scope.** Reserve *ontological* for the disclosure register; thread-constitution becomes
  *substrate*; sensitivity splits into ontic access plus ontological loosening.
- *§9.1 recommends (B) and states (A)'s cost. Either is honest; the present arrangement is not.*

**R-6. Does layer-two self-rendering count as an operation?**
- **(A) Exempt it.** "Operation" means a layer-three act. Clean, and §6.1 adopts the vocabulary.
- **(B) Include it.** Then ordinary human experience is **constitutively disjoined from its own
  history** — a real thesis, not an absurdity. It gives memory's reconstructive character a deeper
  reason, makes the Leap's disjunction continuous with ordinary life rather than alien to it, and costs
  only that disjunction mechanics measure *excess over baseline*.
- *The audit recommends (B) as a thesis worth adopting. §2.7 and §6.1 keep it open.*

**R-7. Threadcut persistence, and Solmund.** Three linked questions.
- Do threadcut beings deteriorate absent a failure of maintenance? Both source answers are
  underdetermined until one premise is ruled: *does rendering yield temporal depth, or only spooling?*
  If rendering does, a threadcut being **stabilizes with age**.
- What dissolved Solmund? A mechanism is derivable — others' rendering holds such a being, so a change
  in how the community rendered him would weaken what held him, and the essentialist reinterpretation
  is exactly such a change — but that is suggestive, not an account.
- **Did his presence strain reality?** §7.5 says every layer-3-sustained being does. A generation of his
  presence should have left marks the record does not report. This one needs an answer either way.

**R-8. The Church's God and the Ein Sof.**
- **(A) Same referent, cataphatically misrendered.** The Church holds an inverted truth; the schism is
  apophatic against cataphatic. Tragic, and it preserves §8.9's refusal of "bad all along."
- **(B) Distinct.** The Church's God is a fiction. *Costs:* §8.9's refusal becomes much harder to hold.
- *The tradition supplies structure for (A): a ground without attributes, and a personal willing God as
  an aspect of it.*

**R-9. Who performs the contraction.**
- **(A) Originary and uncaused.** Consistent with the epistemic charter; costs nothing; must be *said*.
- **(B) The rendering is the contraction**, performed from the finite side. Fully compatible with §1.5,
  and it makes threads the line of continuity between the contracted region and what lies beyond —
  which is what §8.1 already calls the anchors.
- **(C) The ground withdraws.** Traditional fidelity; violates §1.5 unless that is scoped to the
  post-contraction ground.
- *§1.4 names (B) as strongest and does not commit.*

**R-10. What Thread Sensitivity is.**
- **(A) Capacity built.** *Costs:* the cup grows and the finitude pivot weakens.
- **(B) Commitment loosened, plus sub-threshold access.** The pivot holds. *Costs:* re-wording claims
  about what "no human rendering can access."
- *§5.5 and §9.1 take (B); the Leap's derivation independently supports it.*

**R-11. Name Derrida, or scrub the vocabulary.**
Naming costs one paragraph owning the constituting/constituted doublet. Scrubbing costs about twenty
phrase-edits and loses the trace and supplement structures the framework has actually built. *§0.3
names him.*

**R-12. Which "Providence."** The word names a mode of emergence in one document and the stabilizing
tendency in another. One referent must go — and the word imports foresight into a framework whose
ground has none, so it may be that neither should keep it. *§9.2 flags it; §4.6 does not use the word.*

The framework already uses two terms from this tradition. Four more are structurally present and
unnamed. **These are offered as choices, not gaps** — the amalgam may well prefer to stay unnamed at
these joints, and naming them makes the borrowing louder.

| Figure | Where it already is | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **The vessel** | §5.3's cup-and-ocean figure *is* this | **Name it.** The figure is already yours; the tradition just has a word |
| **The breaking of the vessels** | The tear at the anchor points | **Name it, with the inversion stated.** In the tradition the break is on the light's side; here it must stay rendered-side. It also favours R-1(B), since the break comes at maximum intake rather than from fatigue |
| **Repair** | Mending; the restorative type | **Name it, strip the teleology.** It is *human* labour completing what the shattering interrupted — which is precisely the definition of "restorative" that §6.6 needed and the source lacked. No completion is promised |
| **The ray** | The anchors: threads continuous between the contracted region and beyond | Falls out of R-9(B) if you take it |
| **The husks** | Residue fields, Locked Zones, and strikingly the high-reach Coherence-0 being | **Mention at most.** They carry a moral association P-02 and P-04 forbid |
| **The residue of light** | §5.3's "trace of exactly what it was not"; the passive stratum | **Only if the passive stratum is formalized.** Otherwise it over-determines |

**Why this tradition and not another:** it is the one cosmology in which catastrophe is *structural
rather than a fall*, and repair is *human rather than divine*. Those are two commitments the framework
already holds independently.

## §6 What was not migrated, and why

- **The condensed A-rule set.** A third hand-written restatement of a doctrine already stated twice. Two
  of the framework's worst drifts entered through it — the doubled *Providence*, and the claim that
  threadcut beings came through the tear — plus the epistemic restatement of the Locked Zones. **A
  condensed rule set that changed the claims it condensed is worse than none.** If a loadable summary is
  wanted, generate it from §10 plus section headers rather than writing it by hand. This is the
  strongest instance of the precedent at §0.2 rule 4.
- **The game-design constraints.** Mutable directives about victory conditions, AI action selection and
  faction emergence. They cite no foundation, and the source file itself separates them as a different
  kind of thing. They belong in their own file, unchanged.
- **The knot-profile mechanics.** The four *kinds* of consequence are derivable and are stated at §6.3
  and §7.3. The magnitudes, the dice-facing wording, and an undefined stability instrument are
  mechanism, and a foundations document asserting them asserts what it cannot derive.
- **The Level-4 hook table.** Every row provisional and pending; enforcement status is not foundations
  material.
- **Numeric thresholds throughout.** Sensitivity bands, the Coherence track, obstacle numbers. The
  philosophy holds that outcomes are reach-gated and Coherence-indexed; it does not own the numbers.

## §7 Ordering: the contestable calls

Seven, marked rather than presented as obvious.

- **(a) Limit before threads.** Opening with threads and deferring the ground reads more like a game's
  premise. The limit is placed first because the epistemic charter governs what may be said in every
  later section.
- **(b) Rendering before the layers.** Layer 2 is rendering-of-self and §3 is rendering-of-world; if
  self-rendering is primary, the layers should come first. §3 is placed first because R-4 and R-2
  condition what layer 2 can mean.
- **(c) Monstrosity as ontology or phenomenology.** Placed with confrontation, because it is defined by
  the rendering's limit. A reader who takes threadcut beings as a *mode of being* would place it in §4.
- **(d) Whether history belongs here at all.** The mechanism is philosophy; the narrative is timeline. A
  defensible alternative keeps §8.1 and §8.5 and moves the rest out.
- **(e) Whether the A-rule file survives.** §6 recommends retirement; this is reversible.
- **(f) Whether numeric bands belong in a philosophy canon.** They are excluded here.
- **(g) Whether to name Derrida** (R-11).

## §8 Method, and what would falsify this

**Reading.** All five sources read in full. The two Leap-mechanism uploads are byte-identical
duplicates, so the suite is five documents, not six. Sources are preserved at `_sources/` with
checksums matching the uploads.

**Review.** Conducted read-only by Fable 5.1 under four lenses plus a cross-document consistency sweep
and a derivation audit. Read-only was structural: the reviewing agent had no write, edit or shell
tools. Full report at `_review/`, section by section.

**Convergence control.** An independent pass was recorded *before* the review returned, so findings
could be ranked by genuine rediscovery rather than by agreement after the fact. Convergence was high
on every structural item — both readings independently found the Calamity's two mechanisms, the
tendency's contested locus, the doubled *Providence*, the multiple accounts of Coherence loss, the
cup-versus-development tension, the one-rendering-or-many gap, the misattributed apperception, the
epoché pivot, and the constituting/constituted time collapse. Two readings converging without contact
is the strongest confidence signal available here.

**Verification.** Every quote used was checked verbatim against the sources before being built on.
Spot-checks of the highest-severity findings all passed.

**What was not done, stated plainly.** The reviewer declined to retrofit a derivation audit it had not
run when the instruction arrived late, and said so rather than fabricating one; it was then run as a
separate piece. One passage of the review — the opening of the Lurianic-figures finding — was lost in
delivery and is marked as missing rather than reconstructed. No finding was carried into this suite on
the strength of a quote that could not be located in a source.

**What would falsify the central claims here.** The derivation at §4.2 depends on reading "to move one
is to move all" as a general fact about threads rather than a rule about practitioners. The source
text calls it a metaphysical fact, which licenses the general reading — but if you intend it narrowly,
the chain breaks, and with it the tendency's location, the operation-type taxonomy's grounding, and
about a third of what §1–§10 now claims is entailed. **That is the single assumption most worth
checking.**

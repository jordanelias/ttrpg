# Valoria — Philosophical Foundations (reorganized suite)

**Status:** PROPOSED. Nothing here is ratified. This suite is a reorganization of five existing
documents, with the inconsistencies between them resolved where resolution was editorial and
flagged where it requires an authorial ruling.

**What this is.** The five source documents said the same doctrine four times over, at four dates,
and had drifted apart. This suite says it once, in an order that runs from foundations upwards, with
every claim stated in exactly one place. It is *reference*, not mechanism: if this document were
deleted, the game would behave identically. The formula is in the code; this says what the formula
is for.

**What it is not.** It is not a new metaphysics. Every structure here is the author's. The work done
was: reordering, deduplicating, repairing broken references, resolving contradictions where the
suite's own resources decided them, and marking the remainder for a decision that is not mine.

## Why this order, and why not metaphysics → ontology → phenomenology

The obvious ordering — metaphysics, then ontology, then phenomenology — was considered and
rejected, for a reason internal to the framework rather than a matter of taste.

**It would begin with the ground as though the ground were known.** §1.2 and §2.2 of the source
Foundations establish that the Ein Sof is available only forensically, from the rendered side, and
that no causal claim about its constitution is supportable. A document that opens by describing
infinite positive being performs exactly the cataphatic move the framework reserves for the Church
and denies to itself. So this suite states the *limit* first and reaches the ground as an inference
from it. The ground is not the first thing known; it is the last thing inferred.

**Rendering cannot be filed under either heading.** Threads are simultaneously constituting and
constituted; rendering is therefore both a mode of being and a mode of givenness. That doubleness is
the framework's central thesis, not a filing problem to be solved by choosing a drawer. It gets its
own place, after threads and before the beings that threads compose.

**The three-layer model has to come early.** It currently sits in an amendment, arriving as a patch
after the document it patches. But Coherence, the Leap, threadcut beings, Coherence 0, orphaned
configurations and reality-strain are all *defined in terms of it*. It is the spine, and it is
promoted to the position a spine occupies.

**History is a worked case, not a foundation.** The source suite places the Calamity in Part Three,
before the knotting mechanics it depends on — §7.1 forward-references a Part Five that has not
happened yet. And the source §16.4 says outright that the Calamity is "the structural consequence of exactly
the configuration" a high-reach Coherence-0 practitioner sustains. If the history is a consequence
of the mechanism, it belongs after the mechanism.

The ordering calls that are genuinely contestable — there are seven — are listed in
`DISCUSSION_SUPPLEMENT.md` §5 rather than presented here as though they were obvious.

## Relationship to the five source documents

This suite **does not delete anything**. It sits beside the sources at `_sources/`, which remain
byte-identical to what was supplied.

Adopting it means retiring those five files, and that is a decision for the author, not for this
branch. The concordance in `00_standing.md` §0.4 maps every old section to its new home so that
existing citations elsewhere can be repointed mechanically rather than hunted by hand.

One source file is recommended for retirement rather than migration: the condensed A-rule set. It is
a third restatement of a doctrine already stated twice, and two of the suite's worst drifts (the
doubled "Providence", and the claim that what came through the tear were threadcut beings) entered
through it. A condensed rule set that changed the claim it condensed is worse than none. Reasoning
in `DISCUSSION_SUPPLEMENT.md` §6.

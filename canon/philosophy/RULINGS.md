### D-0 — Co-movement is general
**Recorded as: general.** "To move one is, necessarily and inescapably, to move all" holds for **any**
movement of a thread by any process, not only for deliberate practitioner manipulation — as the
source text itself says, calling it a fact about the nature of threads.

*Consequence accepted:* rendering actualizes what it renders.
*Unlocks:* the layer-2 derivation (§4.2), the tendency's derivation, the operation-type taxonomy's
grounding, the outward facing as a holding force, the rendered-side origin of third-mode beings.

### D-1 — Neither option; the framing was wrong

**The author's ruling, verbatim:**

> "Disagree that realism about threads becomes reality vs perception given that threads are both
> constitutive of and constituting, and the reality of threads is unfixed as they become imbricated
> with or separated from other threads. To put another way, your realism about threads assumes that
> reality is a factual static state that we simply perceive as, but the ontology here also has people
> as threads becoming tied into reality in different ways based upon their acting within it and
> therefore perceiving."

**What was wrong with the question.** It offered co-constitution or realism, and characterized realism
as a static factual state that minds perceive well or badly. That characterization imports a fixity
the ontology denies. The dichotomy was false.

**The ruling: positional constitution through imbrication.**

- Threads are real, and their reality is **unfixed**: it shifts as they become imbricated with, or
  separated from, other threads.
- A person **is** threads. So a person is not a viewpoint onto the weave; they are a position *within*
  it, and that position is constituted by how they have become tied in.
- **Acting determines imbrication; imbrication determines perceiving.** Perception is positional, not
  spectatorial.
- Therefore agreement between observers needs no shared static substrate and no appearance/reality
  split. Two beings render alike to the extent their imbrications overlap.

*Would apply at:* §3.5, §2.2, §4.3, §4.6, §7.3. **Not applied.**

### D-2 — Neither option; the tendency is futural

**The author's ruling, verbatim:**

> "Stabilizing tendency belongs to the futural-potential-legible I would argue, as a state of
> equilibrium wherein which the thread configurations are in harmony"

**What was wrong with the question.** It offered the rendering or the ground. The tendency is neither
a hold exerted from the present nor a push from behind.

**The ruling: the futural-potential-legible.** The tendency is toward an equilibrium — the state in
which thread configurations stand in **harmony**. Three terms, each load-bearing:

- **Futural** — the equilibrium is ahead, not behind. This is a draw toward, not a return to.
- **Potential** — it is not actual. It is what could coherently come to be.
- **Legible** — it is renderable. What cannot be rendered is not among the states configurations tend
  toward.

*P-07 is untouched:* the tendency is a property of configuration-space, not of the ground. Nothing
intends, responds, or acts.

*It resolves what neither option could:* in a Locked Zone the futural-potential is itself damaged —
no harmonious configuration is reachable — so nothing is drawn anywhere. Mending is the restoration
of reachability.

*Would apply at:* §3.4, §6.6, §7.1, §7.2, §7.5, §8.1, §8.5. **Not applied.**

### D-3 — Tensile, holding-against, with cascade

**Recorded as: tensile.** The author's gloss:

> "The precarious balance of configurations at some point broke in such a manner that all
> configurations associated broke"

**What this settles.** The Calamity is a consequence of scale, not an operator error. The Einhir held
a **precarious balance** — configurations in a harmony that was not self-sustaining and required
constant holding, because it was not the equilibrium the configurations themselves tended toward.
When the balance broke, everything associated with it broke, because those configurations were held
*as part of the balance* rather than each standing at its own equilibrium.

*Required edit, accepted:* "in pursuit of ever-greater coherence" becomes a refinement the
configurations of their time did not tend toward.
*Name:* **the Calamity**, throughout. The *Catastrophe* usage is retired.
*Would apply at:* §8.1, §8.4, §7.5. **Not applied.**

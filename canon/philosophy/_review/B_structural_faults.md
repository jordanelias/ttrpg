# Section B. STRUCTURAL FAULTS

*Fable 5.1 read-only review of the Valoria foundations suite — section B of A–I.*

---

**SF-1. Two incompatible Calamity mechanisms, under two names, one of which the rule set explicitly excludes.**

(i) Head §6.1: "The Einhir's sustained precision Weaving over generations … pulling their own
substrate tighter and tighter … Eventually, the structural limits of the substrate were exceeded. The
fabric tore at the anchor points." Rules A14: "The Calamity was an over-drawing of the substrate —
not a spell gone wrong but a fundamental structural event." Doc 3 Am 4: "The Catastrophe was not
caused by threadwork per se. It was caused by a foundational-scale manipulative operation that
inverted during execution … The directional vector of the working flipped, converting the entire
output toward maximum potential rather than actualisation. Every thread the network had accumulated
released simultaneously." Same event (Einhir site-network, Southernmost, Solmund emerges through the
breach); the head says "Calamity" throughout, Doc 3 says "Catastrophe" throughout and never
"Calamity."

(ii) §6.1/A14 is a tensile failure: continuous load exceeds a limit; no event, no operator error. Doc
3 is an inversion event during a working — exactly "a spell gone wrong," which A14 excludes, and A14
is one of the three rules D-notes make unconditionally binding ("Any mechanic that violates A2 … A11
… or A14 … is a canon violation"). Doc 3 also converts the failure into a Coherence event ("The
Coherence cost returning through the network's knots was beyond any practitioner's capacity to bear.
The network collapsed") — practitioner-side — whereas §6.1 is substrate-side ("a structural failure
in the substrate itself"). §16.4 compounds it: "Sustained over sufficient time, at sufficient depth,
the strain produces the same conditions the original Calamity produced: anchor-points strain, the
fabric tears" — spontaneous tearing from strain alone, no inversion, "identical in mechanism." Under
Doc 3 it is not identical: Doc 3 needs a flipped intentionality. Further, Doc 3's "maximum potential
rather than actualisation" introduces an actualisation/potential axis the foundations never define
and treats the actualised dimension as an isolable output, brushing against §1.1.

(iii) Repair: the two are reconcilable as phases only if ruled so — strain (§6.1) as precondition,
inversion (Doc 3) as trigger — but then A14's "not a spell gone wrong" must be struck, §16.4's
"identical in mechanism" must be weakened to "identical in precondition," and one name chosen. This
needs an authorial ruling (see I-1); it cannot be edited into agreement, because the two accounts
assign the cause to different sides of the boundary.

**SF-2. "The tendency" is located in the rendering in one document and in the substrate in the others, and the operation-type taxonomy stands on the substrate version — which strains P-07 and contradicts §7.3.**

(i) Head §4.2: "The rendering privileges stable actualisation … a consequence of the rendering being
a finite process operating on an infinite ground." §16.2: "the tendency the rendering exerts on what
it is holding … the rendering performs continuous, unconscious work to maintain the configuration in
its stable shape." But §16.1's causal paragraph slides mid-sentence: "Operations aligned with the
rendering's stabilising tendency — those that accelerate what the substrate's own spooling would
resume given sufficient time, restoring configurations the substrate was already moving toward —
leave the practitioner's configuration aligned with the direction the substrate is going in its own
tendency." Doc 3 Am 3: "Restorative operations are aligned with the substrate's tendency toward
coherence: they do what the constitutive spooling would do, granted sufficient time." A8: "the
rendering's tendency toward stable configuration."

(ii) §4.2's tendency is a selection limit (the rendering can only hold the finite and stable). §16.2
upgrades it to an active pull. §16.1/Doc 3 relocate it to layer-1 spooling, which Doc 2 Am 1 defines
as "Passive, from the ground." A ground whose spooling "would resume," "was already moving toward,"
has a "tendency toward coherence," is being given a restorative direction — a return-to-baseline
behaviour. P-07's violation test: "Does any rule attribute agency, intention, or responsiveness to
the Ein Sof or the ground? If yes → FAIL." Doc 3 disclaims agency ("The substrate does not resist
actively") but not responsiveness; a spooling that resumes prior configurations after disruption is
responsive to disruption. Worse: §7.3 flatly denies the substrate tendency where it matters most —
"New threads cannot spool through regions where the fabric's structural integrity is broken." So
Mending a torn region is not "what the constitutive spooling would do, granted sufficient time" (it
never would), and by Doc 3's own definition Mending torn substrate is not restorative. The Edeyja's
zero-cost lifetime Mending (Doc 3 Am 3) is thereby ungrounded exactly where the setting needs it.

(iii) Repair available from within: put the tendency back where §4.2 and §16.2 have it — in rendering
— and specify it as the *target's own layer-2 self-maintenance* (Doc 2 Am 1) for beings, and the
shared rendering's equilibrium (§16.2) for places. Then "restorative" = accelerates the target's own
always-already self-threadwork; "manipulative" = imposes against it; P-07 is untouched; and Mending
torn substrate is re-described as re-enabling the conditions under which spooling can resume (i.e.,
human labour completing what the breach interrupted — see Q-4, tikkun). The choice between "tendency
in ground" and "tendency in rendering" is nonetheless a ruling (I-2), because it decides whether the
ground has a direction.

**SF-3. Three-to-five causal accounts of Coherence loss, and the two primary ones contradict on whether the Leap is risky per se.**

(i) Doc 2 Am 2: "The retention roll at the end of a Leap … is a test of whether the always-already
self-rendering successfully reconstitutes after suspension. Failure means some portion of the
automatic self-threadwork did not fully restore." P-15: "The Leap cannot occur without briefly
suspending layer 2 — this is why Leaps have a vulnerability window"; violation test: "allow a Leap
without a vulnerability window? If yes → FAIL." Doc 3 Am 3: "The practitioner's Coherence is not
inherently reduced by restorative operations. Mending's Coherence cost is zero … The Edeyja's
capacity to Mend across lifetimes without cumulative Coherence degradation rests on this alignment."
Doc 3 header: "Resolves the question left open by `canon/01` Amendment 2: *why* some threadwork
operations degrade Coherence and others do not." §16.1: "Drift accumulates not from threadwork as
such, but from threadwork that opposes the rendering's stabilising tendency." Then two more channels:
Doc 3 Am 3, "Warden Coherence erosion is environmental — the result of sustained proximity to Gap
margins, dissolution residue fields, and third-mode presences — not operational"; §16.3, exposure to
a drifting practitioner "can … initiate drift in them as well." Doc 2 Am 3 adds a fourth, now-dangling
one: "The drift described in §16.1 is one path by which TS and Coherence loss become correlated … A
practitioner who accumulates Coherence loss through retention failures without residue use or
transformation follows a different trajectory entirely."

(ii) Doc 2 Am 2 did not leave the question open; it answered it — any suspension risks incomplete
reconstitution. Doc 3 replaces that answer with operation-type without saying so. If a Mending Leap
still has a retention roll, Mending is not zero-cost; if it has none, P-15's universal vulnerability
window is false. There is a further, unnoticed collision: P-11 makes Temporal Disjunction universal
("all thread ops produce some TD"); §1.3 defines TD as a gap in what is "retained"; §16.1 defines
Coherence's temporal facet as "retention and protention decohere." Unless TD is expressly walled off
from Coherence, every operation including Mending produces a retention-side effect the suite
elsewhere counts as Coherence drift.

(iii) Repair from within: unify the operational channel using Doc 3 Am 2 — knots form during
suspension and feedback arrives "after the operation completes"; make the retention roll *the arrival
of knot feedback*, so that restorative feedback is "in aggregate, stabilising" and reconstitution is
guaranteed, while manipulative/destructive feedback is what can prevent re-engagement. Then fold
environmental and relational channels into one "confrontation channel" (§16.3 already calls exposure
"low-grade confrontation"). State explicitly that TD is a temporal-dimension effect on the
*world-history/retention gap*, distinct from Coherence's *retention decoherence*, or rule they are
one. Whether the bare suspension carries risk is a ruling (I-3).

**SF-4. One rendering or many is never decided, and §3.1 and §3.2 answer it differently.**

(i) §3.1: "rendering is not a subjective overlay on an independent objective world. What rendering
produces is the world as world." §3.2: "Non-sensitive observers see the healing because the threads
have changed, and their rendering of those threads changes accordingly." §4.1: "the shared reality
upon which all co-worlding occurs." §7.1: "collective rendering." §16.1: "Coherence is, in this
sense, structurally intersubjective"; the outward facing is "the configuration as it is rendered to
others' apperception." §16.2: "the rendering performs continuous, unconscious work to maintain the
configuration." Doc 2 Am 3 band 4–3: "the practitioner is apperceived differently by different
observers … not in opinion, but in what they perceived." P-03: "GM is the rendering engine."

(ii) §3.1 denies an independent objective world; §3.2 explains agreement between observers by an
independent substrate that each mind's rendering tracks — that is exactly the appearance/reality
picture §3.1 rejects, with "threads" in the "reality" slot. "The rendering" (definite, singular)
maintains configurations in §16.2; "their renderings" (plural, per-mind) fail to integrate a drifter
in §16.1. If rendering is per-mind, "collective rendering" and "shared reality" are undefined and the
outward facing of Coherence depends on being observed (a hermit's Coherence?). If there is one
rendering, whose is it, and how is P-03's "consciousness-performed" preserved? P-03 answers
mechanically ("GM is the rendering engine") without the metaphysics answering.

(iii) Two repairs, materially different (I-4): (a) Husserlian transcendental intersubjectivity — the
shared world is co-constituted by the community of renderings; threads are the correlate of that
co-constitution; a Coherence-0 practitioner's outward facing failing is the community failing to
co-constitute them as human (this makes §16.1's "structurally intersubjective" literally true and
gives "collective rendering" a definition); or (b) realism about threads with per-mind apprehension —
then §3.1's denial must be struck and "constitution/experience" reverts to "reality/perception."

**SF-5. The "ontological" register is treated ontically throughout; §17's "correction" relabelled the tiers without changing the structure.**

(Full argument at H-1.) The consequence for coherence, not just for Heidegger-fidelity: the suite has
*two* accounts of Thread Sensitivity — perception of a hidden substrate (Doc 2 Am 3: "how much of the
below-waterline Thread-substrate the practitioner can perceive"; §15: "the world's actual constitution
beneath the rendering's simplified presentation"; Doc 3 Am 1: substrate "screened-out … as noise")
versus a loosening of the givenness of the rendering (§14: "incrementally loosens the practitioner's
commitment to the rendering as given"; §9.1: "to feel the rendering as a process rather than
inhabiting it as a given"). These are not the same faculty; the first is a perceptual reach into a
layer of entities, the second is a change in one's relation to one's own constituting. The suite uses
both and never says which TS *is*. Repair: own the reification (the game requires a manipulable
substrate; say so, and stop claiming Heideggerian correctness), and define TS as the second thing
with the first as its consequence — or the reverse. Ruling I-5.

**SF-6. Layer 2 is defined as threadwork, all threadwork produces Temporal Disjunction, and layer 2 is called "always-already" while being suspendable.**

(i) Doc 2 Am 1: "the always-already rendering of one's own being is a form of threadwork:
unconscious, automatic, constitutive." P-11: "all thread ops produce some TD, not just Past-Pulls";
A3: "Every operation produces Temporal Disjunction." Doc 2 Am 2: the Leap is "the radical suspension
of layer 2 (always-already self-rendering)." §16.1: at Coherence 0 "What has ceased is the
always-already self-threadwork."

(ii) By transitivity, ordinary human self-rendering produces TD continuously in everyone — either a
reductio or a major unstated thesis (human being is constitutively disjoined from its own history).
And a condition that can be suspended and can cease while the being persists is not "always-already"
in the transcendental sense that phrase carries in every source the suite borrows from; it is a
default ontic process. The phrase (20 uses across the suite) is doing rhetorical work where the
framework needs a distinction between *transcendental* (rendering as such, which cannot be suspended
— §2.2: "rendering is always already occurring within the space they concern") and *default*
(layer-2 self-maintenance, which can).

(iii) Repair from within: reserve "always-already" for rendering-as-such (§3.1, §2.2); describe layer
2 as "continuous, unconscious, default"; and either exempt layer 2 from P-11 by defining "operation"
as layer-3 activity (the word "operation" is never defined — see E) or accept universal disjunction
and re-describe TD mechanics as measuring *excess* over the baseline. Ruling I-6.

**SF-7. Threadcut beings: categorical or layer-profile; persist or deteriorate; and Solmund's dissolution has no cause.**

(i) Doc 2 Am 5: "The distinction between organic beings and threadcut beings (§5.3) is now a
difference in which layers of self-maintenance are operative, not a categorical difference in kind,"
followed three lines later by "Threadcut beings remain categorically distinct because they lack Ein
Sof spooling entirely." §5.3: "their mode of being contradicts the mode in which everything else
exists." P-05: "Mode 3: continuous Thread work, indefinite persistence." A9: "Deteriorates because it
lacks the continuous renewal that grounded beings receive." §13.2: "both deteriorate when that
maintenance is insufficient." §8: "Solmund is active for a generation or less before his
dissolution."

(ii) Doc 2 contradicts itself in one paragraph; §5.3's "contradicts the mode in which everything else
exists" is false once layer 2 is threadwork (their mode is ours minus layer 1); A9 makes
deterioration unconditional where §5.3/P-05 make persistence conditional on Thread work; and the
suite's one canonical threadcut being dissolved for no stated reason — ceased Thread work,
insufficient maintenance, or the reality-strain of §16.4 tearing his own vicinity (which §16.4
predicts and §8 never mentions).

(iii) Editorial for the first three (adopt "categorically distinct by layer profile: layer 3 only"
and strike "contradicts the mode"; align A9 to §13.2's conditional). Solmund's dissolution and
whether §16.4 applied to him require a ruling (I-7).

**SF-8. The cup argument and the development of sensitivity are both asserted; they survive together only under a reading the suite never states — and TS is defined circularly.**

(i) §4.3: "Its finitude is not a limitation upon a potentially-unlimited capacity but a constitutive
feature of what the rendering is … an encounter the rendering's specification was never going to
include." §14: "Each confrontation … incrementally loosens the practitioner's commitment to the
rendering as given, creating the perceptual opening through which thread-level reality becomes
accessible." Doc 2 Am 4: "They have exactly the perceptual architecture they built through
confrontation." Doc 2 Am 4 (Resonant): "understand Thread-level structures that no human rendering
can access." Circularity: §18: "Confrontation: Felt encounter with what exceeds the rendering's
capacity. The developmental mechanism for thread sensitivity"; §10.1: "Without the felt encounter
that thread sensitivity provides, the knowledge has nothing to anchor against."

(ii) If TS is capacity "built," the cup grows and §4.3's constitutive finitude is false. If TS is
loosening of commitment to the given, the cup argument survives, but Doc 2's "architecture built" and
"no human rendering can access" must go. And the felt encounter both produces and is provided by TS:
the first confrontation cannot be registered by a faculty it is supposed to create.

(iii) Repair from within, never joined: §4.3's "primitive system, which registers valence and
intensity with lower epistemological demands than the rendering proper" is the pre-TS registrar — the
first confrontation is registered passively (valence, somatic trace, fragments); what develops is not
capacity for the surfeit but access to the *substrate below layer 2's threshold* (Doc 3 Am 1), which
is intelligible-side. State: TS = loosening + sub-threshold access; capacity for the surfeit is
fixed; the trauma signature does not diminish with TS (only equanimity, §15, does). Ruling only on
the definitional choice (I-10).

**SF-9. A second constitutive stratum is posited four times under four names and never acknowledged as one thing — and it breaks "rendering is *the* way the world is given" unless placed inside rendering.**

(Full argument at U-4.) §4.3 "primitive system"; §16.1 others "register the failure first — through
the somatic and perceptual labour"; Doc 3 Am 6 "Passive seismographic awareness … somatic impression,
no dice roll required"; Doc 3 Am 1 substrate "screened-out … as noise below the threshold of
intelligible experience." Repair: name a passive, pre-predicative stratum *of* rendering and house all
four there; this also supplies SF-8's bootstrap.

**SF-10. The Leap's target is stated four ways.**

§9.3: "to be simultaneously beyond and without their consciousness … an entire suspension of all
grounding to reality one might have." Doc 2 Am 2: "radical suspension of layer 2." Doc 3 Am 1: "The
Leap suspends the reflexive facing specifically … The outward facing persists … Layer 1 (Ein Sof
spooling) continues unchanged." P-15: "briefly suspending layer 2." §9.3 (never updated when §16
absorbed the amendments) is incompatible with Doc 3: "all grounding" includes layer 1, which Doc 3
says continues. Editorial once Doc 3 is chosen as canonical (it should be — it is the only version
compatible with the practitioner remaining "locatable in shared reality"); §9.3 must be rewritten.

**SF-11. The origin of third-mode organisation is a gap P-07 makes acute.**

§5.2 (mode 2): "Their organization is accidental." §5.3 (mode 3): "beings who arrive organized and
maintain their rendering through continuous Thread work … through an existential-essential excess,
gap, tear, or error." Since the ground may not organise anything (P-07), mode-3 organisation is
either accidental — then mode 2 and mode 3 differ in degree, contradicting P-05's demand that they be
categorically distinct — or self-caused, which is a bootstrap (a being must already be doing layer-3
work to arrive doing layer-3 work). "Excess, gap, tear, or error" are four undefined disjuncts.
Requires new commitment (see F).

**SF-12. "Providence" names two unrelated things.**

§5.2 / P-05: the second mode of emergence, "temporary organization event." A8: "Providence is not
divine intervention but the rendering's tendency toward stable configuration. When instabilities
arise in the thread-substrate, the rendering works toward resolution." A8 is condensed from nothing in
the head; it is §4.2's tendency wearing §5.2's name. Also, the word itself imports foresight into a
framework whose ground has none (A8's own disclaimer shows the authors felt this). Editorial: retire
the word from one referent; a ruling on which (see I).

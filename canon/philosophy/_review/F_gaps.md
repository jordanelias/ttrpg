1. **What "operation" denotes, and whether layer-2 self-rendering is one.** Every quantitative principle (P-11, TD, knot formation, operation-type) quantifies over "operations"; the word is undefined; SF-6 turns on it.
2. **One rendering or many** (SF-4). Nothing about shared reality, outward-facing Coherence, or "collective rendering" is determinate until this is answered.
3. **Where the tendency lives** (SF-2). The operation-type taxonomy, Mending's zero cost, and the Locked-Zone account all depend on it.
4. **The origin of third-mode organisation** (SF-11): what organises a being that arrives already doing layer-3 work, given P-07.
5. **Who contracts** (Q-2). The tsimtsum has no subject.
6. **Whether the substrate is real independently of rendering.** §3.1 says no; §3.2, §6.1 ("literal mechanics"), and every mechanic say yes. The suite needs one sentence of ontology it does not have: what a thread *is* when nobody renders it.
7. **What dissolved Solmund**, and whether a threadcut being's presence produces reality-strain (§16.4 says every layer-3-sustained being does; §8 records a generation of Solmund's presence with no strain reported).
8. **Whether TD affects Coherence** (SF-3's third collision).
9. **The relation of the Church's God to the Ein Sof** (Q-3).
10. **The TS ladder below 30**, and what a non-sensitive's rendering does at a confrontation (the prophylaxis account in §9 assumes something happens; §4.3 says the signature is universal; Doc 2 starts at 30).
11. **Whether the trauma signature diminishes with TS**, or only equanimity grows — the cup argument (SF-8) needs the answer.
12. **Whether Coherence exists for non-practitioners** (§16.1 defines it for "the practitioner"; Doc 2 Am 1 defines layer 2 for all humans; §16.3 lets drift propagate to non-practitioners, so they must have a Coherence to lose).

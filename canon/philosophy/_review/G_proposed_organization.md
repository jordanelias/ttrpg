# Section G. PROPOSED ORGANIZATION

*Fable 5.1 read-only review of the Valoria foundations suite — section G of A–I.*

---

**Against "metaphysics → ontology → phenomenology."** The proposal would begin with the ground as if known. §1.2 and §2.2 make the ground accessible only forensically, from the rendered side; a document that opens by describing the Ein Sof performs the cataphatic move the framework reserves for the Church (Q-3). The rendering cannot be filed under ontology *or* phenomenology, because threads are both constituting and constituted (§1) — rendering is a mode of being *and* a mode of givenness, and that doubleness is the framework's thesis, not a filing problem. The three-layer model, currently in an amendment, is the spine every later section actually uses (Coherence, Leap, threadcut, Coherence 0, reality-strain, orphaned configurations) and must precede all of them. History is a *worked case* of the mechanism (§16.4 says so: the Calamity is "the structural consequence of exactly the configuration") and currently precedes the knotting mechanics it depends on (§7.1 forward-references "Part Five").

**Recommended order:**

0. **Standing.** What this document is (reference, not mechanism); the precedence rule (X-19); which borrowings are owned departures; lineage including Derrida, Kant, Freud/Jentsch.
1. **The limit first, then the ground.** §2.2 (epistemic opacity, the regress, the aporia of the origin) as the epistemic charter; then §2.1 (Ein Sof as *inferred* fullness, Q-1), tsimtsum with its contractor named (Q-2), P-07 stated here as the charter's consequence.
2. **Threads.** Dual role, with the constituting/constituted doublet owned (D-5); three dimensions; §1.2 intelligibility-as-epistemic; §1.3 split into constituting time (practitioner's living present) and constituted time (thread-temporality) (U-1); Inseparability; Temporal Disjunction.
3. **Rendering.** §3.1–3.2 rewritten (H-2); the passive stratum named (U-4/SF-9); the one/many ruling (SF-4); the tendency, located (SF-2); shared reality defined.
4. **Being-persistence.** Doc 2 Am 1's three layers (layer 1 = thrownness, H-4); layer 2's two facings with Husserl's own names (U-2); Coherence defined; threadcut as layer profile (SF-7); orphaned configurations; the three modes of emergence re-expressed as layer profiles (mode 1: no layers, deteriorates; mode 2: transient accidental organisation; mode 3: layer 3 only) with SF-11 answered.
5. **Confrontation and Thread Sensitivity.** §4.1–4.2 (the Real, corrected for D-4) → §4.3 (finitude pivot) → §14 (development, bootstrapped via the passive stratum, SF-8) → §10 (barrier, inert knowledge, religious poetry) → §15 (deepening/destabilisation, the two uncannies unified, H-3).
6. **Operations.** The Leap (Doc 3 Am 1 canonical; epoché corrected, U-3); knot formation (Doc 3 Am 2); knotting/wrapping (§12.1); collective operations (§12.2); the operation-type taxonomy on the located tendency, "restorative" as tikkun (Q-4); memory pulling (§13.1); Coherence-loss causation unified as operational + confrontation channels (SF-3).
7. **Drift.** §16.1–16.4 with Doc 2 Am 3's bands and Am 4's TS-gated outcomes moved here (or to a mechanics document — see contestable (f)); reality-strain.
8. **History as worked case.** One Calamity mechanism (SF-1), what came through (X-9), Locked Zones (X-10), collapse (§7); Solmund (§8, dissolution explained); the Church — prophylaxis (§9), category error (Doc 3 Am 5), God vs Ein Sof (Q-3); the betrayal (§11).
9. **Terminology.** §17 rewritten to H-1's resolution; vocabulary table regenerated.
10. **Constraints.** P-01..P-15 regenerated from the new section numbers with the Foundations Ref column verified row by row; GD-1..GD-3 moved out (they cite no foundation, are "mutable," concern victory conditions and AI action selection, and doc 4 itself separates them as a different kind of thing: "§A … derives from immutable Philosophical Foundations, §B … is mutable canon"). The A-rule file should be retired: it is a third restatement of a doctrine already stated twice, it introduced two of the worst drifts (A8, A14), and a condensed rule set that "changed the claim" is worse than no rule set. If a loadable summary is needed, generate it from P-XX plus section headers rather than hand-condense.

**Contestable ordering calls (marked):**
- (a) *Limit-first vs threads-first.* One could open with §1 (threads) and defer the ground to a later section on what threads are woven *from*. I prefer limit-first because §2.2 governs what may be said in every later section; but threads-first reads more like a game's premise.
- (b) *Rendering before or after the layers.* Layer 2 is rendering-of-self; §3 is rendering-of-world. If self-rendering is primary (Doc 2's implication), the layers precede §3. I have put §3 first because the one/many and tendency rulings condition what layer 2 can mean.
- (c) *Monstrosity as ontology or phenomenology.* I have placed §4.1–4.2 with confrontation, because monstrosity is defined by the rendering's limit. A reader who takes threadcut beings as a mode of being would place it under 4.
- (d) *Whether history belongs in the philosophy canon at all.* The mechanism (§6.1, §16.4) is philosophy; the narrative (Einhir, Solmund, Church centuries) is timeline. A defensible alternative keeps only the mechanism here and moves the rest out.
- (e) *Whether the A-rule file survives.*
- (f) *Whether the numeric bands (TS 30–100, Coherence 10–0, Ob, MS) belong in a philosophy canon.* They are mechanics; the philosophy needs only "TS-gated" and "Coherence-indexed."
- (g) *Whether to name Derrida* (I-11).

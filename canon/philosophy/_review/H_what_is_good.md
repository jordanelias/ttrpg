# Section H. WHAT IS GENUINELY GOOD

*Fable 5.1 read-only review of the Valoria foundations suite — section H of A–I.*

---

- **§4.3, the finitude pivot.** "The specification does not include what has arrived … The remainder was not lost. It was not constituted." Re-reading the trauma signature as structurally correct rather than pathological, and the cup figure, is the best paragraph in the suite; it also disarms the Church's reading in one move. Keep it; fix only "not constituted" (U-4).
- **The three-layer model (Doc 2 Am 1).** It makes the threadcut/Coherence-0 distinction precise, makes the Leap definable, and generates §13.2, §16.1's trajectories, and §16.4 as consequences rather than stipulations. It is the spine; promote it.
- **§2.2 + P-07.** Refusing the ground any agency keeps the framework out of theodicy and makes the Calamity non-moral without making it meaningless. Do not soften P-07; fix the things (SF-2) that lean on it.
- **§16.1's asymmetry:** "A practitioner cannot fully judge their own Coherence: the apperceptive self-presentation that is failing is the same apperceptive self-presentation that would be required to perceive the failure." This is a real result and it becomes stronger, not weaker, under U-2's correction.
- **§10.1 inert knowledge** ("information without comprehension, like reading the notation of a symphony without ever having heard music") and **§10.2** ("What is transmissible is the form of attention; what is not transmissible is the content the attention discloses"). A correct epistemology of tacit knowledge, and it explains the barrier without agency.
- **§9.2 emergence-not-design** and the refusal of narrative resolution about Solmund; **Doc 3 Am 5**, the Church's error as sincere and structural ("a category-identification error at the layer beneath doctrine").
- **Doc 3 Am 2**, permanent unretrievable knots as the biographical/mechanical residue of every operation — the suite's own trace-logic, and the device that can unify Coherence causation (SF-3).
- **§13.2 orphaned configurations** as threadcut "at the operational level" — a genuine generalisation.
- **§11**, the betrayal as epistemological rather than cynical.
- **§6.2's Lurianic fidelity** — the breach admitting uncontracted fullness is exactly what the tsimtsum's boundary exists to prevent — is right and should be said.

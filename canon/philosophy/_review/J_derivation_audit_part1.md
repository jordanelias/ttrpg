**Premise register.** Foundational premises as the suite states them, labelled for the chains below. Every chain cites these by label; nothing is imported.

- **F1** (§1): "Threads are both a noun and a not-noun. They are simultaneously the condition of possibility for being able to render being as being, and the constitutive ground upon which an entity is able to be a being … Threads weave organic beings and inorganic matter as well as thoughts, concepts, and memories. All that is experienceable, both internally and externally, is woven from threads as both substrate and oscillating tension of temporal, epistemic, and actualized bounds."
- **F2** (§1.1): "the three dimensions are not three separate properties that happen to be correlated, but three aspects of a single unified structure. To move one is, necessarily and inescapably, to move all." Stated as "a metaphysical fact about the nature of threads."
- **F3** (§1.2): "The intelligibility axis runs from 'fully rendered' (epistemically available to consciousness) to 'the unintelligible ground' (epistemically unavailable)."
- **F4** (§1.3): retention/protention; "Every thread operation, because it necessarily moves the temporal dimension (per the inseparability principle), produces some degree of disjunction."
- **F5** (§2.1): Ein Sof "infinite positive being … everything, uncontracted."
- **F6** (§2.2): "no causal claims about the ground's internal constitution, mechanisms, or responses are supportable within the framework." P-07's test: no "agency, intention, or responsiveness."
- **F7** (§3.1): "Rendering is something that is always already being performed by our minds … What rendering produces is the world as world … it is the thread-substrate as it is given to consciousness."
- **F8** (§3.2): thread operations "change both the conditions of possibility and the constitutive ground."
- **F9** (§4.2): "The rendering can process what is finite, determinate, and temporally accumulated. It cannot process what is infinite, indeterminate, or radically present without becoming."
- **F10** (§4.3): the rendering's finitude is constitutive; "Emotional residue persists in the primitive system."
- **F11** (§5.3): organic beings are "continuously spooled by the ground, their threads gaining temporal depth through lived experience"; threadcut beings "maintain themselves through continuous Thread work."
- **F12** (§6.1): "Drawing upon them with increasing precision and density is analogous to drawing a fabric taut from one side."
- **F13** (§12.1–12.2): a knot "creates a point of mutual influence: what happens to one knotted thread exerts force on the other"; in operations "Their own threads are knotted to the target configuration."
- **L1–L3** (Doc 2 Am 1): layer 1 "Passive, from the ground"; layer 2 "shapes the raw spooling into a human configuration and maintains that configuration against environmental thread-forces"; layer 3 "Deliberate threadwork … Requires temporary suspension of layer 2."

One interpretive commitment is required by several chains and I state it once: F2's final sentence ("To move one is … to move all") is read as *general* — any movement on any dimension, by any process — not as restricted to practitioner manipulations. §1.1 licenses this reading by calling it "a metaphysical fact about the nature of threads," not a rule about practitioners. Where a chain depends on it I mark **[F2-gen]**.

## 1. The three-layer model (Doc 2 Am 1)

*The chain as written:* "If rendering is consciousness-performed, and the being's own threads are part of the substrate being rendered, then consciousness is rendering its own threads. And if rendering threads constitutes a form of threadwork (§3.2 — changing threads changes both facticity and rendering), then the always-already rendering of one's own being is a form of threadwork: unconscious, automatic, constitutive."

*Validity, step by step.*
- S1: rendering is consciousness-performed — F7. Sound.
- S2: the being's own threads are among what is rendered — F1 ("internally … woven from threads"; "thoughts, concepts, and memories"). Sound.
- S3: therefore consciousness renders its own threads — valid from S1, S2.
- S4: "if rendering threads constitutes a form of threadwork (§3.2 …)". **This step is invalid as cited.** §3.2 (F8) runs *from* operations *to* rendering: operations move threads, and moved threads are rendered differently. Doc 2 needs the converse — that rendering moves threads. F8 does not contain it. The parenthetical "changing threads changes both facticity and rendering" is the sentence doing the disguising: it restates the forward direction while the argument requires the backward one.

*Is the conclusion nonetheless entailed?* Yes, by a chain the suite never wrote. Call it **Δ1**:
- Δ1.1 Rendering is a position on the thread's own epistemic dimension: "fully rendered" is one end of the intelligibility axis (F3), and that axis is one of the thread's three "bounds" (F1). So to render a thread is to move it along its epistemic dimension.
- Δ1.2 To move one dimension is to move all **[F2-gen]**. So rendering a thread moves its actuality and its temporality.
- Δ1.3 What moves threads is a thread operation in F8's sense. So rendering is a thread operation.
- Δ1.4 By S3, consciousness renders its own threads. So self-rendering is a thread operation on one's own threads — which is what "threadwork" means throughout the suite.
- Δ1.5 That operation is continuous (F7, "always already"), unconscious, and holds the being's configuration (L2). That is layer 2.

**Classification: ENTAILED** — via §1.2 + §1.1, not §3.2. Doc 2's conclusion is right and its citation is wrong. **Name it as an achievement:** the being-persistence spine follows from the foundations by a five-step chain using only F1, F2, F3, F7. The repair is a one-line citation change in Doc 2 Am 1 plus the general reading of F2.

*What Δ1 yields that the suite has left on the table* (D-3 is load-bearing here; each of these is entailed, none is stated):

- **(a) Rendering actualises what it renders** (Δ1.2). Continuous rendering of a configuration continuously actualises it in the shape rendered — i.e., rendering *holds*. This is the derivation of §16.2's active tendency ("the rendering performs continuous, unconscious work to maintain the configuration"), which §16.2 asserts and does not derive, and it decides SF-2 (claim 3 below).
- **(b) Others' rendering of a being is threadwork on that being.** The outward facing (§16.1) is not merely how one appears; it is a force that holds one's configuration. "Collective rendering" (§7.1), undefined in the suite, is thereby defined: the aggregate thread-force of all renderings of a configuration. This is SF-4's intersubjective option, and it comes out *entailed*, not optional (claim 10).
- **(c) The three modes of emergence are layer profiles.** Mode 1 (§5.1: "no organization, no self-awareness, and no capacity to sustain their own rendering … They deteriorate") = no layers. Mode 2 (§5.2: "enough internal organization to persist temporarily … without … continuous Thread work") = transient organisation, no layer 3. Mode 3 = layer 3 only. §5 is derivable from Doc 2 Am 1 and P-05's "mechanically distinct" is grounded rather than demanded.
- **(d) The missing row.** Doc 2 Am 5 lists profiles 1+2, 1, 1+3, 3. Absent: **2 without 1** — a spooled human whose spooling stops. §7.3 supplies the case: "New threads cannot spool through regions where the fabric's structural integrity is broken." A human in a Locked Zone is layer 2 with nothing to shape; by F11's own logic ("If the Thread work ceases, the being's rendering collapses, because there is no organic spooling to maintain it"), a layer-2 self-rendering with no layer-1 input has nothing to hold. That is *what a Locked Zone does to a person*, derived — the suite says only "dangerous." And a high-TS practitioner in a Locked Zone becomes threadcut in the strict sense (layer 3 only, no spooling): a second origin route for third-mode beings, never mentioned, bearing directly on SF-11.
- **(e) "Residual maintenance"** (§13.2: orphaned configurations "persist only through some form of active or residual maintenance") is defined by (b): residual maintenance = others' continuing rendering of the configuration.
- **(f) Baseline Temporal Disjunction** (claim 9, Part 2).
- **(g) "Humans are the threadcut being with a supplement"** is entailed (profile 1+2 vs 3; L2 and L3 are the same kind of act). Consequently §5.3's "their mode of being contradicts the mode in which everything else exists" is **UNDERIVABLE** from the model that now grounds §5.3 — the model yields *same mode, one layer fewer*. The sentence should be struck rather than reconciled.

## 2. The Leap's threshold (Doc 3 Am 1)

*The claim:* "The threshold reflexive-layer-2 imposes — the screening-out of substrate as noise below the threshold of intelligible experience — is what rendering does. Suspending it opens perceptual and operational access to the substrate that reflexive rendering was, moment to moment, excluding."

*Test against F1 + F7.* Chain **Δ-threshold**:
- T1 Rendering presents the thread-substrate *as world* — as beings, facts, experience (F7: "produces the world as world"; "the thread-substrate as it is given").
- T2 Therefore rendering never presents threads *as threads*: presenting-as-world is what it is, not one of several things it might do.
- T3 Therefore substrate-qua-substrate is not among rendering's deliverables — not filtered, simply not delivered.
- T4 Therefore any access to threads-as-threads requires that presentation-as-world not be occurring for those threads.

**"The threshold is what rendering does": ENTAILED** from §3.1 alone, and cleanly. Achievement.

Two things in Doc 3's sentence are *not* in that chain:

- **(i) "screening-out … as noise."** T3 says the substrate is not presented; Doc 3 says it is registered and discarded (a filter). A filter is a further mechanism with no premise behind it. **STIPULATED.** It is not idle: "noise" is the premise that makes Thread Sensitivity a *perception of a hidden layer* (the account SF-5/H-1 found ontic), whereas the entailed version supports the other account (TS as a change in the mode of apprehension — §14's "loosens the practitioner's commitment to the rendering as given"). The derivation decides between the suite's two TS accounts in favour of the second.
- **(ii) Which suspension.** T4 requires suspending presentation-as-*world*; that is §9.3's original target ("sublimate their always-already rendering of the world as world"). Doc 3 suspends the *reflexive self-facing* ("I am this person, in this time, in this world"). Nothing in the suite joins them. **UNDERDETERMINED** as written — equally compatible with the foundations would be a Leap that suspends world-rendering while self-rendering continues. One premise closes it, and it is U-2/U-3's Husserlian one: *world-rendering is anchored in the self's positing of itself as a being in that world.* Written out: A1 the reflexive facing is the being's self-positing *in* the rendered world (§16.1's "in this world"); A2 rendering-as-world is rendering-for-a-worldly-standpoint (F7 — performed by a consciousness, which has its standpoint by A1); A3 suspend the self-positing and the standpoint from which threads are presented-as-world lapses → T4 satisfied; A4 others' rendering (outward facing, Δ1(b)) continues to hold the practitioner as a being in the world, which is why they remain "locatable in shared reality" (Doc 3 Am 1). With A1–A4, Doc 3's target and §9.3's target are one claim, and SF-10 closes by derivation rather than by choosing.

## 3. The operation-type taxonomy, on each tendency location

**(a) Substrate-located.** Doc 3 Am 3: "Restorative operations are aligned with the substrate's tendency toward coherence: they do what the constitutive spooling would do, granted sufficient time." §16.1: "what the substrate's own spooling would resume given sufficient time, restoring configurations the substrate was already moving toward."

Required premise: layer-1 spooling has a direction — it resumes prior configurations. Test: F6 forbids "causal claims about the ground's internal constitution, mechanisms, or responses"; P-07 forbids responsiveness; L1 defines spooling as "Passive, from the ground"; F5 makes the ground "everything, uncontracted," which has no preferred configuration to resume; F9 locates the preference for the determinate and stable *in the rendering*, not the ground. And §7.3 denies the tendency where it is needed: "New threads cannot spool through regions where the fabric's structural integrity is broken." **UNDERIVABLE.** Both commitments, named: F6/P-07 + F5/F9 (the ground has no direction; direction belongs to rendering) against Doc 3 Am 3/§16.1 (spooling "would resume"). The sentence doing the disguising is Doc 3's "The substrate does not resist actively — the ground has no agency (P-07) — but the operation's configuration is not supported by the tendency the substrate provides": it concedes agency and keeps direction, and direction is what P-07's "responsiveness" clause excludes.

**(b) Rendering-located.** Chain **Δ2** (the tendency in the rendering):
- Δ2.1 Rendering actualises what it renders (Δ1.2).
- Δ2.2 Rendering is continuous (F7). So a configuration that is continuously rendered is continuously re-actualised in the shape rendered: **rendering holds.**
- Δ2.3 The rendering can hold only the determinate and stable (F9). So what it holds, it holds *toward* stability — the "preference for stable actualisation" (§4.2) is not a taste but the shape of what holding can do.
- Δ2.4 Temporal depth is accumulated holding (F11: "threads gaining temporal depth through lived experience"; §13.2). So configurations long and widely rendered are held hardest.
- ⇒ §16.2's "the rendering performs continuous, unconscious work to maintain the configuration in its stable shape" is **derived**, and "the tendency the rendering exerts on what it is holding" is Δ2.2.

Then the taxonomy: **restorative** = the operation's result is a shape that rendering (the target's own layer 2 for beings; the collective rendering of Δ1(b) for places) already holds → nothing further needs to hold it → no residual load on the practitioner. **Manipulative** = a shape no rendering holds → held only by the practitioner's rendering → sustained load. **Destructive** = removal of a shape rendering holds → rendering's hold persists as an unresolved force (the orphan structure of §13.2) → the practitioner bears the severance. **ENTAILED** on location (b). A second, independent route from F9 alone: a configuration unstable-for-rendering cannot be held by rendering, so it must be held by someone; the someone is whoever made it. Two routes converging is the strongest result in the audit. **Achievement — but only once the tendency is relocated.**

§7.3 is handled on (b) without special pleading: a Locked Zone is a region the collective rendering cannot hold; Mending builds a shape rendering *can* hold; once held, rendering maintains it; Mending's cost is the interval before the hold transfers. That cost *structure* is derived; its magnitude is not (it is a number, and no premise yields numbers).

**Net for claim 3:** the taxonomy is **UNDERIVABLE on (a), ENTAILED on (b)**. The suite currently states it on (a). The seam of the whole audit runs through the §16.1 sentence where "the rendering's stabilising tendency" slides mid-clause into "the substrate's own spooling."

## 4. Why misalignment cost falls on the practitioner rather than the target

*The claim:* Doc 3 Am 3: "It must be held by the practitioner's sustained intentionality against the absence of that support. Feedback through the knot carries the operation's structural instability: the practitioner bears, as Coherence reduction, the cost of having held a configuration the substrate's tendency did not supply." §16.1: "the substrate's non-support is borne, structurally, by the practitioner's own threads."

*Chain* (on location (b), since (a) is dead):
- K1 In an operation the practitioner's threads are knotted to the target (F13, §12.2).
- K2 A manipulative result is held only by the practitioner (Δ2, manipulative).
- K3 A knot is mutual influence; the unheld shape's instability is a force on the target-threads and therefore, through the knot, on the practitioner-threads (F13).
- K4 Layer 2 is what "maintains that configuration against environmental thread-forces" (L2). An inbound force through the knot is exactly what layer 2 must resist; if it exceeds what layer 2 can hold, layer 2 degrades — which is Coherence loss by definition (§16.1).
- K5 Why the practitioner and not the target: the held shape lives where the holding is. The target's own layer 2 (if a being) *opposes* the imposed shape; nothing on the target side is holding it; the tension between held and unheld is located in the holder.

**ENTAILED**, given the knot. Does Doc 3 Am 2 supply the channel? Yes — K3 is its "Knots are bidirectional. What happens to the target-thread afterward propagates back through the knot to the practitioner." Do the documents join? Within Doc 3, Am 2 → Am 3 joins explicitly ("Feedback through the knot"). The head does not: §16.1 states K5's conclusion with no channel, and §16's absorption note names only Doc 3 Am 1, not Am 2–3. So the head asserts what Doc 3 derives, and a reader of the head alone meets a stipulation.

Two stipulations remain inside the channel: "Once formed, the knots are permanent. They form at substrate depth" (Doc 3 Am 2). No premise yields permanence or depth; §12.1's *wrapping* ("looser but still consequential") would have been equally compatible with K1–K5, and would give a decaying rather than lifelong feedback. **Permanence: STIPULATED.** Directionality ("they follow the operation's intentionality") is derivable from K1 — the knot forms along the path of contact — so **ENTAILED** in kind.

*Left on the table:* K5 also yields a **target-side cost** the suite never states. If the target is a being, a manipulative operation imposes a shape its layer 2 opposes; that is an environmental thread-force on the target's layer 2 — Coherence loss for the target, by the same K4. §13.1 gives memory-pulling's target only Temporal Disjunction ("creates Temporal Disjunction in the person's experiential continuity"); by derivation it also costs them Coherence. This is a consequence the mechanics would need to carry.

## 5. Mending's zero cost "at any scale" against §6.1's magnitude-and-density mechanism

*The claims:* Doc 3 Am 3: "Scale makes destructive operations worse; alignment makes restorative operations, at any scale, non-corrosive." Doc 3 Am 4: "The framework identifies no mechanism by which Mending, at any historically-achievable scale, could produce a Catastrophe-equivalent event." Against F12 (§6.1): "Drawing upon them with increasing precision and density is analogous to drawing a fabric taut from one side … they were pulling their own substrate tighter and tighter in pursuit of ever-greater coherence and civilizational refinement," and §16.4: "drawing on the substrate the way the Einhir site-network drew on it."

*Two costs must be separated.* Coherence cost (practitioner-side) and substrate strain (reality-strain).

- **On Coherence:** zero for restorative operations is **ENTAILED on location (b)** (Δ2: nothing unheld flows back through the knot) and **UNDERIVABLE on (a)**. Doc 3's own "in aggregate, stabilising" concedes non-zero instantaneous feedback — which is the retention-roll question (claim 6).
- **On strain:** F12 as written is *type-blind*. "Drawing upon" threads "with increasing precision and density" strains the anchors; the Einhir were pursuing "coherence" — the very word Doc 3 uses for what restorative operations align with. As §6.1 stands, it entails that Mending at density strains the substrate, and Doc 3 Am 4's "no mechanism" is contradicted by the head's one mechanism. **UNDERIVABLE** — both commitments: F12 (any drawing at density strains) versus Doc 3 Am 4 (restorative cannot strain).

*Is a derivation available?* Yes, and it is SF-1's ruling in derivational form. Re-read "drawing taut" through Δ2: strain is not *use* of the substrate but *holding against* it — the summed difference, across the lattice, between what practitioners hold and what the collective rendering holds. Then: restorative operations reduce that difference (they move things toward what rendering holds) and cannot strain; manipulative operations increase it and strain in proportion to magnitude × duration — which is exactly F12's "precision and density," now read as density-of-holding. Under this reading Doc 3 Am 4's claim closes, and the Einhir must have been holding a shape the collective rendering did not hold — which Doc 3 Am 4 asserts ("a configuration the site-network had been sustaining for generations through active, ongoing manipulation") and §6.1 contradicts with "in pursuit of ever-greater coherence." The closure therefore costs one edit: §6.1's "coherence" must become something like "a refinement the rendering of the time could not hold," and the tensile account survives *only* as the holding-against account. That is the derivation the author's ruling on SF-1 should be made with.

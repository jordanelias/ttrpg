## 6. The retention roll's failure mode

*The claim:* Doc 2 Am 2: "The retention roll at the end of a Leap is not a test of psychological resilience. It is a test of whether the always-already self-rendering successfully reconstitutes after suspension. Failure means some portion of the automatic self-threadwork did not fully restore. The being's configuration has been permanently altered in ways the self-rendering can no longer integrate."

*What would yield failure?* Nothing in the foundations says a suspended constitutive process might not resume; "always-already" implies the opposite. The failure mode has to be built. Chain **Δ-roll**:

- R1 During suspension the reflexive facing is not holding the configuration (Doc 3 Am 1). Layer 2 is what "maintains that configuration against environmental thread-forces" (L2). So for the duration of the Leap, environmental thread-forces act on the configuration unresisted — Doc 2 Am 4's own freefall logic ("raw Ein Sof spooling plus environmental thread-forces … begin reshaping them"), applied briefly.
- R2 The configuration therefore changes during suspension in proportion to environmental force × Contact Duration — less whatever the outward facing continues to hold (Doc 3 Am 1: it "persists — residually"; Δ1(b): others' rendering is a hold).
- R3 On re-engagement, layer 2 re-holds whatever configuration now exists. The rendering can hold only determinate stable shapes near what it was holding (F9); drift beyond that leaves "alterations from the Leap that the automatic self-maintenance can no longer correct" (Doc 2 Am 3).
- R4 Independently, knot feedback arrives "after the operation completes" (Doc 3 Am 2) — at or just after re-engagement — as an inbound force on layer 2, weighted by operation type (Δ2, K3–K4).
- ⇒ Reconstitution failure = layer 2 unable to re-hold against **[drift during suspension] + [knot feedback]**. The first term is environment × duration; the second is type × scale.

**Classification:** the failure mode is **ENTAILED** as a *structured* risk. Doc 2's flat "every Leap has a roll" is **UNDERDETERMINED** — consistent, but the foundations yield a risk that goes to zero for a restorative operation in a null environment and is large for a destructive one at a Gap margin. The roll's probability is **STIPULATED** (no premise yields a probability).

*What Δ-roll does:* it reconciles all three surfaces of SF-3 in one chain, which is the highest-value conversion in this audit. P-15's "vulnerability window" is entailed (R1–R3: the window is real). Doc 3's "Mending's Coherence cost is zero" is entailed for the knot term (R4 = 0 for restorative). Doc 3's "Warden Coherence erosion is environmental — the result of sustained proximity to Gap margins … not operational" is entailed as the R1–R3 term: Wardens Mend where environmental force is high, so their environmental term is large while their operational term is zero — Doc 3's exact phrase, now derived. Doc 2 Am 2's "test of whether the always-already self-rendering successfully reconstitutes" is entailed as the moment both terms land. The answer to ruling I-3 ("is the Leap risky per se?") is therefore *neither option as posed*: risky in proportion to environment × duration, and zero in a null environment. **Achievement.**

## 7. Reality-strain at Coherence 0 / high TS, against the taxonomy

*The claim:* §16.4: "A practitioner whose Coherence has failed and who has the perceptual reach to maintain themselves through deliberate layer-three threadwork is, by structural necessity, drawing on the substrate the way the Einhir site-network drew on it (§6.1)." Doc 2 Am 4: "every moment of their continued existence involves active threadwork at a scale and depth that strains the substrate the way the Einhir site-network strained it."

*Chain:*
- S1 At Coherence 0 layer 2 has ceased (§16.1); the being is held by layer 3 (plus spooling).
- S2 The self-maintained shape is "a stable, non-human, self-maintained configuration" (§16.1), which the collective rendering does not hold (§16.2: "decoupled from the equilibrium-tendency"; "The rendering must work harder to integrate them"). Under Δ2 that is manipulative by definition: held only by the being.
- S3 The taxonomy's cost channel for manipulative holding is Coherence (K4) — but there is no layer 2 to bear it. Under the holding-against reading of F12 (claim 5), the load is the held/rendering-held difference and lives in the threads doing the holding. With no layer 2 to absorb it into the being's own configuration (§16.1's "borne, structurally, by the practitioner's own threads"), it is borne by the threads the being draws on — its vicinity. That is reality-strain.

**Classification: ENTAILED**, given the holding-against reading of F12 — which claim 5 showed is the only reading on which the taxonomy and §6.1 cohere at all.

Two corollaries the suite does not draw:

- **(i)** For a Coherence > 0 practitioner the manipulative load is *split*: part absorbed by layer 2 (Coherence loss), part left in the substrate (strain). So **every manipulative operation produces some reality-strain**, not only those of Coherence-0 beings; Doc 3 Am 3's "proximity to … third-mode presences" as an erosion source is a special case.
- **(ii)** A threadcut being is the same profile minus spooling; S1–S3 apply unchanged. Solmund strained the substrate for the generation of his presence, and §8 records nothing of it. Entailed and unstated (SF-7, I-7). Whether that strain is what ended him is **UNDERDETERMINED** — consistent, not yielded.

On §16.4's "identical in mechanism": under holding-against, the Einhir lattice and the Coherence-0 being *are* identical in mechanism (both hold an unheld shape); under Doc 3 Am 4's inversion, the Calamity needs an extra trigger and "identical" overstates. §16.4 is entailed only on the reading claim 5 already requires. Consistent.

## 9. Is Temporal Disjunction constitutive of ordinary human experience?

*The premises:* F4/P-11: "Every thread operation, because it necessarily moves the temporal dimension (per the inseparability principle), produces some degree of disjunction"; "all thread ops produce some TD, not just Past-Pulls." Doc 2 Am 1: layer 2 "is a form of threadwork."

*Chain:*
- B1 Self-rendering is a thread operation (Δ1.3–1.4).
- B2 Every thread operation moves temporality and produces some TD (F4) [F2-gen].
- B3 Therefore layer-2 self-rendering produces TD continuously, in every human.

**ENTAILED.**

*Reductio or thesis?* Take TD's definition: "a gap between what is retained and what now factually was" (§1.3). For ordinary self-rendering, what is retained is the being's retentional past; what factually was is the temporal dimension of their threads, which their own rendering continuously moves (B1–B2). So the retained past and the thread-past never coincide exactly, because retention is itself a rendering that moves what it retains. This is not absurd; it is F4's own structure ("the past as a horizon of retention" — the past given only retentionally from the living present), and it yields three things the suite wants: (a) memory is reconstructive, which gives §13.1's "one does not cleanly 'delete' a memory" a deeper reason (no memory was ever a clean record); (b) TD is already a magnitude ("The degree of disjunction varies," §1.3), so a non-zero floor is compatible and operational TD is the baseline magnified; (c) the Leap's TD is continuous with ordinary experience rather than a foreign intrusion, which §15's "the world becomes more real" needs. The cost is mechanical: C2's "Track cumulative Temporal Disjunction … Natural cost curve" must measure *excess over baseline*, or every human's TD rises unboundedly over a life — and "past-present mismatch grows with age" is not an absurd consequence either. **Verdict: thesis, adopt.** The alternative (exempting layer 2 from "operation," SF-6) is available but costs the equivocation on "threadwork" that Δ1 has just made unnecessary.

*U-1's corollary.* §16.1: at low Coherence "the practitioner's retention and protention decohere." TD is a gap between retention and thread-history; if the retention pole degrades, the gap becomes indeterminate *for the practitioner* — they cannot register a disjunction whose retained side is dissolving — while remaining determinate *for observers*, who retain normally. **ENTAILED.** And it matches Doc 2 Am 3's phenomenology exactly: at 4–3, "Temporality perceptibly non-linear: references to events that haven't happened, reactions before causes"; at 2–1, "Temporal existence perceptible to anyone" — every band is written from the observer's side. The derivation explains why: temporal drift is externally visible and internally unregisterable, which is the temporal instance of §16.1's asymmetry ("A practitioner cannot fully judge their own Coherence"). **Achievement:** the self-judgment asymmetry and the temporal phenomenology are one structure, and the bands' third-person voice is a consequence rather than a stylistic choice.

## 10. The outward facing: dispositional or occurrent?

*The claims:* §16.1: "The outward facing is the configuration as it is rendered to others' apperception — the practitioner appearing as a unified human subject to those whose rendering is processing them. Both facings are integral to Coherence; degradation of either is Coherence loss." §16.2: "Their presence begins to exert a faint dissonance on the shared rendering of those around them." §16.3 (I take the brief's "§7.3" to mean §16.3, Tridimensional Propagation Through Knots; §7.3 is the Locked Zones): "The practitioner's threads are knotted to other people … When the drifting practitioner's configuration is altered tridimensionally (§16.1), the knotted threads carry that alteration to those bound to them."

*Chain:*
- O1 Others' rendering of the practitioner is threadwork on the practitioner's threads (Δ1(b)) and holds the configuration in the shape rendered (Δ2.2).
- O2 On the Husserlian basis (U-2), another apperceives the practitioner by analogising pairing with their own lived body. The *act* is the other's; its *possibility* depends on the practitioner's configuration being pairable-as-human — a property of the configuration.
- O3 So the outward facing has two components: a **disposition** (renderability-as-human, determinate without observers) and an **occurrent holding** (actual renderings, absent without observers).
- O4 The hermit: the disposition is determinate, so their Coherence is determinate; the occurrent holding is zero, so their configuration is held by layer 2 alone with no supplementary hold from others → more exposed to environmental thread-forces (L2) → hermits drift faster, other things equal. **ENTAILED** and unstated; consistent with §16.3's "the community must intervene" and §14's concentration of sensitivity near Locked Zones.
- O5 Propagation does not require observers. Knots persist without co-presence (§12.1: "A person's threads are knotted to the threads of their relationships"; Doc 3 Am 2: permanent); F13's mutual influence needs relationship, not presence. **ENTAILED:** §16.3's channel is observer-independent.
- O6 Therefore the suite conflates two channels: **rendering** (§16.2 — presence-based, requires occurrent apprehension, reaches strangers) and **knots** (§16.3 — relationship-based, distance-independent, reaches only the bound). A drifting practitioner who leaves the community stops straining the local shared rendering but keeps pulling on knotted kin; a stranger in proximity is affected by rendering only, until wrapping (§12.1: "frays wrap around the people and things they encounter") forms a bond.

**Classification:** outward facing as disposition-plus-occurrent-holding — **ENTAILED**. Hermit's Coherence determinate — **ENTAILED**. Propagation observer-independent — **ENTAILED**. The suite's single undifferentiated "outward facing" is **UNDERDETERMINED** between its two components; the derivation supplies both, and P-10's "Both reflexive and outward facings must produce structural effects" acquires two distinct effect-channels rather than one.

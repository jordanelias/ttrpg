Four read-only Fable reviews of the live suite, run in parallel: **coherence**,
**ruling-fidelity**, **derivation soundness**, **philosophical rigour**. None saw the
others; none saw my reasoning. I produced the suite, so this is the agonist→antagonist
relay of `CLAUDE.md` §10 with me as the agonist.

**Ranked by independent rediscovery.** A finding four readers reached separately is
bankable without further checking; a single-source finding is verified against disk before
it is acted on. That ordering, not severity as claimed, is what governs below.

**The verdicts, in one line each.** Coherence: *not internally coherent.* Fidelity: *the
core metaphysics is carried faithfully; the defects are at secondary sites and in the
register's verifiability.* Derivation: *the rulings survive; the claim to have **derived**
the operational content does not — roughly a third entailed, a third underdetermined, a
third unsupported.* Rigour: *the load-bearing walls are real; the lineage accounting is
unreliable and the foundation has two organizing principles it assigns to nothing.*

## TIER 3 — the derivations that do not derive

The suite's content is fine; the arguments offered for it are not. **The fix is almost
always to stop offering the argument** — demote "follows" to "ruled" and cite the ruling.

- **§4.2's chain** (the suite's most load-bearing derivation) equivocates on
  *intelligibility axis* — accessibility-degree where it enters at §2.3, as-what content
  where it is used at §2.5 — and slides from *determination* to *movement* without a
  premise. Its list of nine consequences includes three the suite has since struck.
- **§2's Inseparability** does not follow from the empirico-transcendental doublet. It does
  not need to: it is **D-0, ruled**. Cite the ruling.
- **§2.5's "three moments are exhaustive"** is asserted, not shown. And *place* — used
  throughout as vicinity, region, margin — is neither a moment nor accounted for. One
  sentence fixes it: place is imbrication, a relation among configurations rather than a
  determination of one.
- **§6.2's "the Leap's necessity is entailed by §3.1 alone"** — §3.1 adds a premise in its
  next paragraph.
- **§2.7 / P-11's "every operation produces some disjunction"** fails on §2.7's own
  definition: a restorative operation restarts a becoming rather than pulling anything out
  of one, so it produces zero. Scope P-11 to manipulative and destructive.
- **§8.4a's Gap derivation** uses the rewind model §2.4 struck. Re-derivable on the ruled
  model: the pull breaks the *holding*, and with nothing spooling behind it the
  configuration is unmade rather than displaced.
- **§6.1's operation criterion** is defined relative to "a finite ordinary human", so it
  cannot cover threadcut or Coherence-0 threadwork, which the suite calls operations.
  Repair: "threadwork not performed from within a layer-2 hold" — for humans this entails
  the Leap, so the ruled criterion survives as a consequence.

## TIER 4 — two foundational gaps, each with a fix that composes on existing primitives

Both single-source (rigour), both serious, both verified as real readings of the text.

**Configuring is located nowhere.** §1.5 forbids the ground to organize; §3.1 forbids
rendering to configure; the suite assigns configuring to *spooling*, which §9.3 defines as
**the ground's** supply. So either spooling's configuring is the ground organizing under
another name, or a third thing exists that was never introduced. The same gap shows as an
undefined distinction: spooling and a Gap are both passage from the ground, one benign and
one catastrophic, and nothing says what differs.
*Fix, composing:* §2.3 already grants the ground is "infinitely constituted"; §1.4 already
makes the limit originary and nobody's. **Configuring is passage through the limit** — so
spooling is that passage seen as supply, the contraction is the same passage seen as
structure, and a Gap is passage that bypasses the limit. This also makes "the contraction"
ongoing rather than eventive, which is what *originary* should mean.

**"The draw" is a dynamics that the suite calls a property of configuration-space** — and
the author's own Locked Zone ruling proves it must be. §3.4 states a *landscape* (which
arrangements are harmonious). That configurations *move toward* them is a second thing, a
law of motion, assumed and never named. A property of configuration-space cannot be
locally switched off; a property of threads can — and the ruling says "you have removed
the tending. The threads remain; the motion toward does not."
*Fix:* split them in §3.4. The futural-potential-legible is the landscape; the **draw** is
a substrate dynamic — a law, not an agent, so §1.5 and P-07 are untouched. A Locked Zone is
then "draw locally zero, landscape intact", which is what §8.5 already says. Mark
`RULINGS.md:92-94` superseded by the Locked Zone entry, which contradicts it.

## TIER 5 — the lineage accounting

Single-source (rigour), and it is the section whose whole thesis is that debts get named.

- **The as-structure is Heidegger's and is in no lineage row.** "Interpretation of
  something as something" is *Being and Time* §32's hermeneutic *as*. §3.1 carries
  Heidegger's structure on Husserl's vehicle ("consciousness", which Heidegger refuses as
  the locus) and patches the mismatch with a body — Merleau-Ponty, also unnamed. The debt
  *is* paid, by imbrication doing the fore-structure's job; it is simply not recorded.
- **Lacan's Real is not "lack"** — for Lacan it lacks nothing; lack comes from the
  symbolic. The real pivot (structural → ontological) is stated in the same paragraph and
  is sound. Strike "rather than lack".
- **"Empirico-transcendental doublet" is Foucault's**, used unnamed after D-11 chose naming
  over scrubbing — and it is his *diagnosis of an unstable figure*, not a position one
  adopts. The suite may disagree with him; that disagreement is the substance of §2.1 and
  should be stated as one.
- **Freud is credited with the account Freud rejected.** "The not-quite-alive" is Jentsch;
  Freud rejects it for the return of the repressed-familiar — a mirror, which §4.5
  explicitly refuses.
- **"Retention" means recollection in §6.7**, under §9.2's explicit claim that the
  technical sense is used throughout. The conclusion holds; the term is wrong.
- **"Pre-predicative" misnames the passive stratum** — Husserl's pre-predicative sphere
  already has objects; what §3.3 describes is the affective/hyletic level.
- **Derrida is decoration.** The trace is not §5.3's residue; the supplement's "reversal"
  does not occur in the body; an etymological convergence is offered as "evidence the
  structure is sound". One place uses him correctly — §1.2's quasi-transcendental.
- **Kierkegaard's *Repetition*** is certified "correctly used" and appears nowhere.
- **The Husserl row promises three pivots; §0.3 delivers one.**

## TIER 6 — the unnoticed commitment worth keeping

**Not a defect. A consequence of what I wrote yesterday that I did not see, and it is
better than what I wrote.**

§6.8 says magnitude divides a working's cost, and that magnitude is imbrication. §8.3 says
the Einhir lattice *distributes* a working's displacement across everyone bound into it.
**These are one mechanism described two ways.** A sensitive practitioner is a larger vessel
*because their kin, places and community are part of the vessel* — so what a large
practitioner does not bear, **those they are knotted to bear in fractions**, through the
very channels §7.3 names for propagation.

I wrote the lattice's version as distribution and the practitioner's as reduction. The
consistent reading is darker: sensitivity does not reduce the cost, it spreads it to the
people you love. That gives §7.3's "the practitioner is not the only one at risk" an actual
mechanism, and makes §6.8 and §8.3 one account.

One sentence in §6.8 lands it. Flagging rather than writing it, because it changes what
being a powerful practitioner *means* in the setting.

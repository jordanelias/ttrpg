## D-A. Past the crossing, does a configuration tend anywhere?

**Found independently by three of the four reviews.** The most bankable finding in the run.

The suite says both, in different places:

| | says | where |
|---|---|---|
| **tends elsewhere** | "Once the resting point has left the band, the human shape is no longer where that configuration tends." | `07_drift.md:129-130` |
| **tends nowhere** | "shapes standing in harmony with nothing around them, which the tendency therefore draws nowhere" | `07_drift.md:261-263` |
| **tends nowhere** | "holding a configuration standing in harmony with nothing around it. By §6.6 that is manipulative by definition — off the attractor" | `07_drift.md:378-379` |

My irreversibility argument needs the first. §7.5's reality-strain needs the second. They
cannot both stand.

**And under the second, the argument inverts.** §3.4 defines harmony as "when nothing in
the set requires sustaining to persist." A crossed being holding a non-human shape among
human surroundings requires sustaining; a human-configured being among them does not. So
restoring a crossed being toward human moves it **toward** harmony — restorative, not
manipulation. The opposite of what §7.1 now says.

### The options

**(a) It tends elsewhere — it has a basin of its own.** Past the crossing the being
settles into a non-human equilibrium at which it needs no holding.
- Irreversibility keeps my §7.1 account: human is no longer where it tends, so working it
  back restores a remembered state.
- **Cost:** §7.5's "manipulative by definition" becomes false for such beings, and
  reality-strain loses its ground. Mending a crossed being becomes possible and safe — it
  settles them *as other*. §7.6's four reach-gated outcomes would need rebuilding, since
  three of them describe a being that must hold itself continuously.

**(b) It tends nowhere — there is no basin, only holding.** The crossed being is off the
attractor and every moment of its persistence is held.
- **Reality-strain keeps its ground**, and §7.6's outcomes stand as written.
- Irreversibility needs a new basis, and there is a stronger one available in *your own*
  Locked Zone ruling: *"becoming has been precluded, so the threads are no longer moving
  towards an equilibrium."* A crossed being would be a Locked Zone of one — **there is no
  tending left to restart, and restoration is precisely the restarting of a tending.**
- **Cost:** strike "no longer where that configuration tends" from §7.1. Also repairs a
  separate defect: that sentence currently leans on §6.6's knife-edge, which is my
  elaboration and not your words (see D-C).

**(c) It is reach-gated, like everything else past the crossing.** Shallow reach is pushed
by the environment and holds nothing; relational reach is held by knots; structural and
full reach hold themselves. Three different answers to "does it tend anywhere", matching
§7.6's existing structure.
- **Cost:** the most work, and the most faithful to what §7.6 already describes.

**One further consequence, whichever you pick.** A reviewer noted that a plastic set *is*
a move in where the configuration tends — that is why rest fails to return it. So after
the **first** permanent set, inside the band, moving someone back already opposes their
current tending, which is what the knife-edge calls manipulation. The band does not rescue
this unless "tends toward" is coarser than "toward the resting point". The **basin** model
fixes it: the human band is a basin, resting points are minima within it, self-mending is
climbing toward the basin floor, and the crossing is leaving the basin.

## D-B. Is the substrate the condition of the ontological register?

The suite says both, and `RULINGS.md` records both.

- `02_threads.md:10-12` — threads are "**the condition of possibility** for being able to
  render being as being", and `02:35-37` "a deliberate collapse of the Kantian distinction
  … The framework should state it."
- `RULINGS.md:154-155` — "The alternative, owning the reification, was available and **not
  taken**."
- §9.1 diagnoses the source suite for exactly this move: treating the condition of
  possibility as the most fundamental *thing* is "precisely the move the borrowed
  distinction was formulated to diagnose" — and then §9.1 makes threads ontic while §2.1
  keeps them conditioning the ontological.

**(a) Own the reification.** Threads condition disclosure; this is a naturalism about
disclosure, stated as such. Strike D-4's "not taken."
**(b) Withdraw "condition of possibility" from §2.1.** Threads are substrate only, and
rendering is self-grounding.

Independent of which: `09_terminology.md:35-36` calls the substrate "deep" and
"inapparent", which reinstates the appearance/reality split §3.1 denies and §6.2
explicitly rejects. What the suite actually has is one ontic stuff, two modes of
givenness, and the fact of givenness. That is a correction either way.

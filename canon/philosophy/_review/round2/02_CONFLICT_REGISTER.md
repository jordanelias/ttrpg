## G · Conflicts with the sources the suite names

Single-source (the rigour review), and it is the section whose whole thesis is that debts get
named.

- **G-1 · The as-structure is Heidegger's and is in no lineage row.** "Interpretation of
  something as something" is *Being and Time* §32 (Auslegung von etwas als etwas). §3.1
  carries it on Husserl's vehicle — "consciousness", which Heidegger refuses as the locus —
  then patches the mismatch with a body, which is Merleau-Ponty, also unnamed. The debt *is*
  paid (imbrication does the fore-structure's job); it is not recorded.
- **G-2 · Lacan's Real is not "lack".** `00_standing.md:89-91` — "a Real that is plenitude
  rather than lack". For Lacan the Real lacks nothing; lack is introduced by the symbolic.
  The actual pivot (structural → ontological) is stated in the same paragraph and is sound.
- **G-3 · "The empirico-transcendental doublet" is Foucault's**, used unnamed at
  `02_threads.md:24-27` after D-11 chose naming over scrubbing — and it is his *diagnosis of
  an unstable figure*, not "a recognizable philosophical position" one adopts.
- **G-4 · Freud is credited with the account Freud rejected.** `00_standing.md:83` — "Freud /
  Jentsch — The uncanny as the not-quite-alive". That is Jentsch 1906; Freud's 1919 essay
  opens by rejecting it for the return of the repressed-familiar — a mirror, which
  `04_being_persistence.md:194-196` explicitly refuses.
- **G-5 · "Pre-predicative" misnames the passive stratum.** Husserl's pre-predicative sphere
  already constitutes typified objects; `03_rendering.md:79-82` describes the
  affective/hyletic level of passive synthesis.
- **G-6 · Derrida is decoration.** The trace is not §5.3's residue; the supplement's
  "reversal" does not occur in the body; `00_standing.md:129-132` offers an etymological
  convergence as "evidence the structure is sound". One correct use: §1.2's
  quasi-transcendental.

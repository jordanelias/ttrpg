## V · The sources (6)

Not about the game. About whether §0.3's ledger of debts is honest — which is the section
whose entire thesis is that debts get named.

- **S-22 · The as-structure is Heidegger's and is in no lineage row.** "Interpretation of
  something as something" is *Being and Time* §32. §3.1 carries his structure on Husserl's
  vehicle — "consciousness", which Heidegger refuses as the locus — then patches the mismatch
  with a body, which is Merleau-Ponty, also unnamed. **The debt is genuinely paid**:
  imbrication does the work Heidegger's fore-structure does. It is simply not recorded.
- **S-23 · Lacan's Real is not "lack".** For Lacan it lacks nothing; lack enters with the
  symbolic. The real pivot — structural to ontological — is stated in the same paragraph and
  is sound. Only the description of what was left behind is wrong.
- **S-24 · "Empirico-transcendental doublet" is Foucault's**, used unnamed, and it is his
  *diagnosis of an unstable figure* rather than a position anyone adopts. §2.1 is entitled to
  disagree with him — that disagreement is its whole substance — but should say so.
- **S-25 · Freud is credited with the account Freud rejected.** "The not-quite-alive" is
  Jentsch 1906; Freud's 1919 essay opens by rejecting it. And §4.5's uncanny is neither
  man's — it refuses the mirror, which is Freud's whole mechanism.
- **S-26 · "Pre-predicative" misnames the passive stratum.** Husserl's pre-predicative sphere
  already constitutes objects; §3.3 describes the affective/hyletic level below objectivation.
  The concept the suite needs exists and has a different name.
- **S-27 · Derrida is decoration.** The trace is not §5.3's residue; the supplement's
  "reversal" never occurs in the body; an etymological convergence is offered as "evidence
  the structure is sound". One correct use: §1.2's quasi-transcendental.

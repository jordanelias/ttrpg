## §2 The findings that matter most

Ranked by consequence, each with its home.

1. **Two of the three rulings change no behaviour at all.** TN is already 7 everywhere — `roll_pool`
   stores the parameter and never reads it — and a pool *document* trips nothing. Only Tree A's
   removal changes what the game computes, and it is blocked on a design question. — **01**
2. **The population of Valoria is zero.** 46 authored characters; `world.npcs == {}`; no module reads
   the registry. Five separate chains terminate here. — **02 §2.1**, **04 §1**
3. **T15 and T16 are mirror holes in two 16-member rosters.** A territory with no settlements, and a
   settlement in a province that does not exist — owned by a faction that does not exist. Matching
   counts mean no count check can see it. — **02 §1**
4. **Seven of the thirteen convictions in use are silent no-ops**, including the most common one
   (Authority, 14 characters), because the code ships the roster canon declares superseded. Twelve
   characters cannot be scarred on anything. — **02 §2.3**
5. **The settlement memory layer has zero callers.** Precedent, Grudge, Debt, Reputation, Leverage —
   designed, complete, cited, unreachable. — **02 §3.2**, **04 §3.3**
6. **The Mandate penalty is permanent and says it is temporary.** A live balance defect that compounds
   across a campaign. — **04 §3.2**
7. **The Treaty system is wholly inert, and could not work if wired** — its no-rng fallback makes
   lapse impossible, and its comment says the opposite of its code. — **04 §3.1**
8. **A correction this session queued for CLAUDE.md would have put a false number in it.** — **03 §7.1**
9. **Six of sixteen vocabulary "conflicts" were overturned**, including the two most confident. — **03**
10. **Valoria is building the thing Prom Week's authors identified as their own failure**: outcomes
    from summed weights, with no legible cause. — **05 §1.2**

## §5 How to trust this

Three things a reader should know before relying on any number here.

**Everything was re-measured.** No figure in the suite was carried forward from an earlier session
document without re-deriving it from the tree. Several changed when re-measured, and those are marked.

**`grep` concluded nothing.** Rosters, call sites and reachability came from AST walks and structural
parses. Where the distinction mattered it mattered a lot — one count is 13 by grep, 0 by naive parse,
and 11 by structural attribution, and only the third is right (**03 §7.1**).

**The retractions are published.** Eighteen claims overturned, four narrowed, one framing inverted —
all in **06 §4**, each naming the reading that overturned it. A suite that reports only what survived
would be a less honest object than one that shows what did not.

**What is still unverified** is stated at the foot of each document. The main items: the `cells_float`
reproduction (read, not executed); the 40-seed `mass_seizure` unreachability measurement (carried as
quoted); a chronicle execution claim whose run artifact does not exist in the tree; and every external
precedent claim in **05**, which is tagged by evidence grade and is not about the Valoria tree at all.

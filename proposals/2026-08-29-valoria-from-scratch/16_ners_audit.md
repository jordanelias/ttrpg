# 16 — The Pessimistic Steelman NERS Audit

## Status: FILED (2026-08-29) — findings and dispositions. Nothing here ratifies on merge.
## Method: four read-only critics (`.claude/agents/valoria-critic.md` — Read/Grep/Glob only, so
## independence is structural rather than declared), one per slice, steelman-first then attack.
## Plus this synthesis, which corrects the critics where they were wrong and **retracts two of the
## orchestrator's own prior rulings** where they were wrong.

**How this audit differs from the one that produced the suite's evidence base.** Jordan ruled that
**elegance is a RATIO against necessity and robustness, not a fourth axis** — *"distill as much as
possible without losing emergent possibilities or robust choosing."* An audit that scores four axes
and averages them scores an amputated design as elegant. So the instruction to every critic was:
the highest-value finding you can return is a **false N-line** — an object whose claimed lost
possibility *actually survives the cut* because something else already provides it.

**Three were found, and the process worked exactly as intended on one of them:** two critics who
could not see each other's work independently reached the same verdict on Momentum. Independent
rediscovery is the strongest signal this process produces.

## 2. The three false N-lines

### 2.1 MOMENTUM — CUT. Found independently by two critics.

Its N-line claimed it was *"the only mechanical account of how the Restoration produces outcomes at
all"* and that without it *"playing to your values becomes pure flavour text."*

**Both are false.** The possibility survives twice over: a Conviction **is** a stance row (ruling
B-8), and stance rows already gate the willingness function, a negotiator's option set, formation
concord, and view salience. Convictions keep full resolver consequence with Momentum deleted.

**And the residue violates its own document's charter.** What remains after subtracting the
surviving possibility is *+1 die* — a flat pool bonus — where doc 02's opening paragraph reads:
*"nothing here is a modifier… a modifier is the one shape that cannot produce a politics."*

**And it does not survive its own engine.** It resets *per session* and is produced by *scene-level
detection* — frames that do not exist at auto fidelity, where the engine supplies the manoeuvre. So
either NPCs at auto fidelity never have Momentum, which is a pool asymmetry across fidelities in a
design whose central claim is that fidelity changes nothing but individuation depth, or "session"
needs an in-world definition nobody supplies.

### 2.2 THE COMMITMENT-DEGREE LICENCE COLUMN — CUT the column, keep the degree.

Its N-line: *"cut degree and everyone is in or out: no sympathiser slipping bread."* But the
requisition obstacle formula **on the same page** already prices asks continuously. The sympathiser
slipping bread survives on the formula alone: a low-degree low-burden ask clears, a low-degree
high-burden ask does not.

The discrete table re-enumerates what the formula prices, **and the two copies can disagree** — the
table forbids requisitioning a sympathiser outright while the formula would clear one at high
regard. That is the "second copy that can disagree with the first" the spine refuses. What is
genuinely discrete and survives: the top degree's *absent offer term*, and the avowal gate.

### 2.3 SHADOW STANDING — N-line false; the object is cheap enough to keep as a readout.

Its named behaviour — dual legitimacy, legalisation, a family becoming a house without changing
object type — is produced by capacity plus support-set compliance alone. The formal holder's
existential goes empty, and legalisation is an ordinary act his own decision function selects when
it becomes his cheapest option. No gate needed.

What shadow uniquely adds is a legibility readout, plus a *"repeat twice → legalisation"* rule that
is **asserted rather than derived** — who counts to two? Keep the subtraction as a published band;
delete the rule.

**⚠ Only one of the three was applied, and that is a process failure worth recording (noted
2026-08-30).** All three were found by this audit and written up above as decided. The correction pass
that followed applied **Momentum** (§2.1) and stopped there: the licence column (§2.2) and the shadow
trigger rule (§2.3) were never cut, so `07_alignment.md` went on shipping both objects while this
document said they were gone, and a later reader had no way to tell which of the two was current. The
contradiction was caught by the play-space coverage exercise's machine-view lane, which read `07` and
`16` against each other and found them asserting opposite things. Both cuts are now applied in `07`.
**Nothing about the design changed in the meantime and none of the three findings was wrong** — the
defect is entirely in the handoff between finding and application. A finding recorded as decided is not
decided until it is applied, and a §2 that says CUT is evidence of nothing while the document it names
still carries the object.

**Two N-lines were softened rather than cut**, because they named something real and claimed too
much: the Knot's (*"the only channel by which a person holding no post receives news"* — ties and
dispensations already reach the unposted; what is unique is the *unbidden, speakerless, undistorted*
deposit), and Composure's (multi-exchange structure is owned by the exchange loop, which survives
the cut; what actually dies is attrition-to-concession).

## 3. The orchestrator's own rulings, retracted

Both were made in this session, in the adjudication register, and both are wrong in the same way —
they were *half right and stated as complete*.

### 3.1 A-1 is RETRACTED IN PART. Splitting needs by source was right; the classification was wrong.

A-1 ruled that subsistence and standing read the **world** while commitment and exposure read the
**view**, on the reasoning that *"their faces are in front of you."*

That licenses reading **expressed** regard. But the standing formula reads *peers' stance rows
directly* — **concealed contempt included** — so a secretly hostile burgher moves your need before
any act has expressed it. Its input is another agent's interior, which is neither the world (the
resolver's domain) nor the observer's view.

It also falsifies doc 02's own claim that counsel extraction is *"the only direct read of another
person's interior anywhere in the design."*

**Corrected:** standing reads only regard the person holds a claim about. The three-way distinction
the register should have drawn is **world / view / another agent's interior**, and the third is
readable by nobody except through a claim.

### 3.2 A-6 is RETRACTED IN PART. It fixed half of the objection and said it kept both.

A-6 ruled that firsthand claims get a salience floor and told/inferred claims keep the clamp,
and called this "the resolution keeps both."

**It does not.** Doc 09's stated need was that *a sixty-year-old revelation must move people who do
not want to be moved* — and a revelation arrives as **testimony**, which the ruling left clamped. I
fixed "walk into the room and see it" and left the case that motivated the objection unfixed.

**And the gloss is unsatisfiable.** *"Always makes your working set"* cannot hold under a finite
claim budget for a person holding more than that many recent high-confidence firsthand claims —
which is precisely the investigator this game is about. The formula (a floor on salience) and the
gloss (guaranteed inclusion) diverge exactly where the game is most active.

**Held open, not patched.** The honest state is that the testimony half needs a mechanism — most
likely that a claim's *corroboration* can lift it past a hostile stance weight, since that is what
actually distinguishes a revelation from a rumour — and this audit is not the place to invent it.

## 4. R-failures: structural dominance

### 4.1 Open-low dominance in the stasis ladder — FIXED

Concession scaled with rungs descended, and a concession was written to the record **only on a
descent**. So opening at the second rung and holding it wrote nothing conceded and paid nothing —
while being *driven* to the second rung paid a quarter concession plus a permanent contradiction
hazard, **at the same final rung with the same substantive position.**

Opening at the rung you will hold therefore strictly dominated opening higher: identical best case,
no record cost, less fault surface. **The strongest rung was a trap option, and the ladder's whole
"terrain, not a menu" claim failed on it.**

Fixed by one rule: opening at a rung writes every rung above it into the record as conceded, exactly
as descending would.

### 4.2 The grievance-capital fix is real but the respondent controls its faucet — SHARPENED

The up-stroke found its own dominance failure (petitioning dominated by silence, gain decaying while
cost compounds) and fixed it structurally: refusal produces grievance capital, so
`value = P·U + (1−P)·G`. **G is genuinely consumed** — it feeds commitment, carriage mass and
dormant rows — so the fix is not a restatement.

But the deposit fires only *when a backer learns*, and a **silent drop suppresses learning**. The
real form is `P·U + (1−P)·p(learn)·G`, and `p(learn)` is partly the refuser's choice. A respondent
who refuses quietly every time re-creates the decaying sum. The fix holds at campaign scale and at
public refusals; it does not hold for the lone supplicant refused in private, and the document now
says so.

### 4.3 The drop's counterweight is elected-seat-shaped — OPEN

A perpetual dropper was said to lose his seat at the next contest. But the worked example holds a
**Crown post**, allocated by the Crown, whose judging set the petitioners cannot reach. For
appointed office, dropping has no seat cost — only the grievance channel. The counterweight as
stated does not bind the case it is illustrated with.

### 4.4 Freshness is a one-act tax, not a gate — ACCEPTED AS DESIGNED, claim corrected

The coupling chain runs through shared subjects, and an office-holder shares a subject with every
stake he administers — so any old grievance against anyone who touches the prize is permanently
ripe, and against anyone else, one staged public occurrence mints the subject for the cost of one
act. The mechanism does avoid a decay timer, which was the point. But the claim that *a
thirty-season-old grievance is not as motionable as yesterday's* is false in effect, and is now
stated as *it needs a hook, and a hook is cheap.*

## 5. Leaks of true state into decisions — the A-1 class, five new instances

The register's own note after A-1 was that *"the type discipline is necessary and not sufficient, and
every derived quantity has to be audited for the same leak."* Five more were found, which vindicates
the note and indicts the first sweep.

| leak | what it does | disposition |
|---|---|---|
| the **standing** need | reads peers' true stance rows, concealed contempt included | fixed — reads expressed regard only (§3.1) |
| the **container norm** fallback | computes a true aggregate stance and deposits it with **no source constructor**, violating "no null source" | fixed — must be an inference from claims the person holds, with a real root |
| **rarity** roll-up | rolls up true practice holdings; got no true/estimated split when the faction profile did | fixed — same split, or resolve-side only |
| **`firsthand_via_knot`** | a *fourth* source constructor outside the corroboration proof's enumeration, so the proof's load-bearing sentence — *"no operation mints a root token except witness"* — is false | fixed — a Knot deposit reuses the originating event's id, so one event reaching several partners yields **one** root. Without it, Knot-laundered corroboration was possible |
| the **cohort channel** | deposits **one claim** into a cohort, inherited identically by individuating members — one value, same sign and magnitude, into hundreds of people | fixed — a cohort claim carries a *distribution* of construals, and an individuating member draws their own. This one matters most: it laundered consensus broadcast through the one path the type system does not cover, in a design whose code-shape document calls that a type error |

**The honest generalisation:** the signature rule stops a decision function from *taking* the world.
It does not stop a helper from *computing* over it, and it does not stop a type whose whole purpose
is compression from erasing divergence. Both are discipline, not structure, and the design should
stop claiming otherwise.

## 6. The one genuine dilemma, unresolved

**Conferral is defined on persons, and the Crown needs it defined on offices.**

Person-rooted, the first monarch and every dead intermediate terminate conferral paths at corpses —
so the sovereignty query that all five factions' victory conditions operate on is **undefined across
most of the graph**, and a praefect becomes as irrevocable to the next king as a duchy is. The Crown
cannot be played across a succession.

Office-rooted, the graph resolves — but an institution is then performing the most consequential act
in the game, which ruling B-11 says must never be expressible.

**The suite currently asserts both and resolves neither.** The design's own evidence that it needs
the office-rooted version is the military order sworn *"to the Crown as institution, not the
bloodline"* — a warrant that is meaningless if conferral is personal.

This is a live design choice with two defensible answers leading to materially different games, and
it is not one an audit should decide. **It is the first thing to settle before any of this is
built.**

## 7. Emergence: what the design produces without being told to

Credited specifically, because an audit that only finds defects mis-reports the thing it audited.

- **A caste-breaking law is evadable through the terms it does not name.** Raising the work
  coefficient at an admission gate changes nobody's stance, so a committee routes the same exclusion
  through sponsorship and personal dislike.
- **Catechesis produces canon's sensitivity map as an output** — and reverses under an anti-caste
  duke's programme, which he did not intend either.
- **A Crown levy cannot refill a serjeant where a caste-open order can**, so breaking the caste
  system makes a duchy's levies more resilient. The political fight acquires a military payoff with
  no rule naming caste.
- **The levy is the generator of what it suppresses** — a hearth pays in hands, so next season's
  larder is short by exactly the men taken.
- **You cannot march through the territory you liberate**, because foraging is force against named
  hearths.
- **Passing degrades the capacity to pass**, from two mechanisms meeting.
- **Prejudice is the literal default of an empty ledger** — and is deposited with a root, so it is
  refutable.
- **A mutiny with no mutiny mechanic**: arrears, recomputed need, re-ranked options.
- **The Consecration Crisis is a capacity conflict** — two acts asking for the same three men in the
  same month — and a custody indeterminacy that stops any claimant reaching the proof floor.
- **A dynastic claimant's optimal strategy is waiting for a war's witnesses to die.**

## 8. What the audit says is most likely to be wrong

Each critic was asked for one. They did not converge, which is itself informative.

1. **That filtering is wholly a person's valuation.** Two seams leak: agenda composition had no
   person in it at all (now fixed with a convener), and the view budget's relevance term is an
   engine-computed importance score inside every decision. The suite's central boast survives at the
   drop and weakens one layer below it.
2. **The closed predicate vocabulary.** It declared twelve forms and used a thirteenth three
   sections later, and cannot express a claim about another person's interior — which three
   mechanisms require. Now fourteen, and the next reader should expect pressure for a fifteenth.
3. **The Crown conferral dilemma** (§6).
4. **That fidelity changes nothing but individuation depth.** Three independent leaks — Momentum
   absent at auto fidelity, `order refused` missing from the event-class parity list, and the cohort
   channel deposit — all point one way: the design is honest about losing third moments and quietly
   loses *social texture* at the fidelity where most of the world runs.

## 9. What this audit did not do

- **It did not run the game.** Every claim here is about whether the design can express a
  throughline, not whether the resulting game is good. No campaign was simulated because there is
  nothing to simulate — this suite is fourteen documents and touches no executable code.
- **It did not check the setting's canon for accuracy**, only that the design names real content.
- **It did not audit doc 13's slice at critic depth** in the politics pass, or docs 02/03/07 in the
  churn pass; each critic recorded its coverage, and those notes are preserved in the findings.
- **It found no R-failure in the epistemic core**, which is a weaker result than it sounds: that
  slice has fewer player-facing forks than the others, so there was less to fail.

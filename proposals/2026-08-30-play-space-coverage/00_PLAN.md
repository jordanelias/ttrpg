# The Play-Space Coverage Instrument — plan

## Status: PLAN (2026-08-30) — orchestration, not design. Nothing here ratifies on merge.
## Planned and adjudicated by Fable 5 (read-only review); executed and written by Sonnet and Opus lanes.
## Reads: `proposals/2026-08-29-valoria-from-scratch/` (the merged suite, 17 documents) and `canon/`.

**The end goal, in Jordan's words:** *"see all the different permutations and combinations available
regarding the different types of play so that we can identify gaps and weaknesses."*

So this is **not documentation of the design.** It is an instrument for finding where the design has
nothing for a character to do. A view that only shows the machine working is a brochure. **Every view
below is built so that an empty cell is visible.**

---

## 1. The method: seasons as probes

The unit is **"A Season in the Life"** — one named character, one season, traced through the seven
phases of the tick, ending in a verdict about what that character could actually *do*.

A season is not a story. It is a **sample point in the play-space**, and the characters are chosen —
and grouped into lanes — by **which cells of the matrix they probe**, never alphabetically. Two
dukes probe one cell; a duke and a queen with no office probe two very different ones.

**Every season must end with a diagnostic, not a flourish:**

| verdict | meaning |
|---|---|
| **RICH** | several live options, with materially different consequences |
| **THIN** | options exist but one dominates, or the differences are cosmetic |
| **BLOCKED** | the character wants something the design gives them no act to pursue |
| **SPECTATOR** | the character has no meaningful act this season at all |

A SPECTATOR or BLOCKED verdict on a *named faction leader* is a serious finding and must be reported
as one, not written around. **Lanes are explicitly forbidden from inventing an act to rescue a
character from a SPECTATOR verdict.** If the design cannot give the Queen of Valoria something to do,
that is the result.

## 2. The play-space axes

The permutations are the product of these. Every season records its coordinates.

**A · CONTAINMENT RUNG** — where the character stands
`Individual · Hearth · Community · Settlement · Territory · Province/Duchy · Realm · Off-board`

**B · OFFICE** — whether their decisions bind others
`none · binds members-by-admission (a guild warden, a cell) · binds persons-by-presence (a praefect, a duke) · office cluster (a Dicastery)`

**C · ALIGNMENT** — commitment depth, and whether it is known
degree `none · sympathy · sympathiser · member · sworn · constitutive`
× avowal `avowed · private · covert`

**D · MARKS** — what is read off them before they speak
`caste-advantaged · caste-excluded` × `Thread-sensitive · non-sensitive` × `house name / guild grade / Church standing / none`

**E · MODE OF PLAY** — the kind of thing they can do. This is the axis gaps hide in.

| mode | the acts |
|---|---|
| **Material** | larder, holdings, production, trade, smuggling, seizure |
| **Epistemic** | examine, interview, research, surveil, reconstruct, Thread-Read, tell, lie, conceal |
| **Political-up** | petition, back, carry, amend, bundle, drop |
| **Political-down** | issue, publish, comply, evade, refract |
| **Argument** | plead, press, descend, produce, object to venue, yield, negotiate |
| **Coercive** | force, levy, muster, arrest, violence, suppress |
| **Relational** | form/strain/rupture a Knot, requisition kin, marry, foster, admit, exclude |
| **Institutional** | confer, revoke, convene, appoint, charter |

**The matrix that matters is E × A** — mode of play against rung — because that is where a hole means
*a character at this level has no way to do this kind of thing.* B, C and D are the cross-cuts that
say *for whom* the hole opens.

## 4. What each season must contain

Fixed shape, so that eighteen seasons are comparable rather than eighteen essays.

1. **Coordinates** — the character's position on axes A–D, stated as a header line.
2. **Opening state** — the six person fields. What is in their ledger, and **what is conspicuously not**.
3. **Computed needs** — with the arithmetic, and which are read from the world versus from their view.
4. **The view** — their working set this season, and **at least one claim they hold that does not surface**.
5. **The option set** — every act available to them, and why each is legal. This is the load-bearing
   section: *the gaps live here.*
6. **The choice and its resolution** — through the seven phases.
7. **What propagates** — up-stroke, down-stroke, or neither; and how far.
8. **The diagnostic** — RICH / THIN / BLOCKED / SPECTATOR, with the reason, plus the R-check: does any
   option dominate by shape of gain against shape of cost?
9. **Cells populated** — which matrix coordinates this season demonstrates as live.

## 5. Discipline binding every lane

- **The merged suite is the authority.** Compose on it; do not redesign it. If a season cannot be
  written because the design lacks a mechanism, **that is the finding** — write the gap, do not patch it.
- **Canon is the source for who these people are.** Where canon is silent, say SILENT rather than
  inventing a personality. A character's *wants* may be derived from their stated goals; their
  *biography* may not be invented.
- **No new mechanisms.** Not one. Any lane that finds itself designing has left the task.
- **Name the real content** — Dicasteries, the Masterpiece Examination, Southern Einhir, the Baralta
  Crown Claim, Knots, the Löwenritter oath.
- **Do not rescue a character.** A SPECTATOR verdict on a faction leader is the most valuable output
  this exercise can produce.
- **Two known-open items may be hit and must be reported, not resolved:** the conferral dilemma
  (person-rooted vs office-rooted), and the testimony half of the salience floor.

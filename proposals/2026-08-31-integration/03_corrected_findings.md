## C. CORRECTIONS THAT CHANGE A PROPOSAL

**C-1 · The world-substrate object must be PLACE-SCOPED, and needs two amendments nobody noted.**
It clears the forbidden list — it is *primary physical state*, not a derived aggregate, so the
dead-state objection does not bite. But:
  (a) the ownership table says a container holds *"its stake, its judging set, its standing dates.
      **Nothing else**"* — this needs an **ownership-table amendment**, or a ruling that substrate
      condition is part of the material stake;
  (b) the settle phase is closed to *"matter, bodies, and the confidence of a memory"* — admitting a
      substrate that decays on a clock requires **ruling the Thread substrate is matter**.
  And decisively: **a global scalar re-runs the design's own anti-scalar argument in physical form.
  Place-scoped, or it is the refused shape with a new subject.**

**C-2 · The act-economy fix's "the coverage exercise already ran the experiment" is CONFOUNDED.**
It uses the King's THIN verdict to falsify a rival reading — while the matrix records that **his
verdict flips with the branch each lane declared**. The establishment correlate was extracted post
hoc from n ≈ 25. *The conclusion may still be right; the experiment claim is not available as support.*

**C-3 · E1 is a real, bounded compliance regression — and it is repairable at type level.**
Widening a petition's respondent to a venue moves B-11's price **from a type into prose**, which is
exactly the class #342 exists to forbid: *"a property enforced by naming can be spelled around; a
property enforced by a type cannot."* **Mitigation available and not yet taken: the venue tuple
already carries a `convener` field — make it REQUIRED and NON-OPTIONAL and the invariant is restored
at type level.**

**C-4 · The convergence measure is compliant IF AND ONLY IF it stays an offline instrument** — a
seeded-campaign tool, same class as the kept balance oracle, whose subject is the game. **The
in-engine reading would be an authored trigger**, the exact object the design refuses.

**C-5 · The characterisation adjudication is principled but was applied prematurely.** *"Apply the R
test at seats a player can occupy"* follows from Jordan's criterion — but crown playability is listed
as undecided, and **the same undecidedness applies to the Confessor's seat, whose row I nonetheless
settled.** Under its own rule, only the avowal row was decidable.

**C-6 · The blocked-cores arithmetic double-counts** — one character appears both among the ten
ACCEPTs and among the three answered by standing rulings; the totals do not sum to nineteen.

**C-7 · The conferral sweep is incomplete.** The universal holds within the range swept and no arc in
it needs person-rooted — but "needs" does double duty (structural necessity versus a drama argument),
and another lane's succession material was never tested. **The evidence points one way; the sweep does
not close.**

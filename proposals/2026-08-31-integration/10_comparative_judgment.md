acts, and a stated goal or defining arc with no act whose object it is"* — is **an
outcome-prerequisite test run per seat.** So (a) is a hybrid and the domains **overlap**: seat-wants
are outcomes, and outcome-prerequisites include actors — which is how the pure-outcome instrument
found a missing **seat class** (the off-board polity).

  *doubly* blind there.

  probe asks what an act degrades in common; **the coverage matrix contains no cell for it.**

**RULING: the combination is non-redundant at the unit layer — and shares its worst blind spot at the
summary layer.** Both instruments' **verdicting layers manufactured their own results**: (a) defined

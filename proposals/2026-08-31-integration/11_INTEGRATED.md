### 0.1 The instruments' domains — and the correction that matters

A coverage instrument over seats enumerates *affordances at a position*. It detects option-set defects
local to a seat, and it is structurally blind to anything requiring traversal of time: closure, tempo,
causal chains, world state no single season reads. A reachability test over outcomes enumerates
*prerequisites of endings*. It detects missing world objects, missing actors, missing forcing
mechanisms and closure structure, and it is structurally blind to the quality of any seat its fiction
never visits.

**That dichotomy is an overstatement in one direction, and the correction is load-bearing on
everything below.** Body (a) is **not a pure seat instrument.** Its central invention, the **blocked
core** — *"live acts, and a stated goal or defining arc with no act whose object it is"*
(`09_GAP_REPORT.md:23`, roster enumerated at `08_coverage_matrix.md:174`) — is an
**outcome-prerequisite test run per seat.** Body (a) is therefore a hybrid, and the two domains
**overlap**: seat-wants are outcomes, and outcome-prerequisites include actors, which is how the pure
outcome instrument found a missing *seat class* — the off-board polity, still open at
`00_INDEX.md:112-113` (*"An off-board polity acting without a person to carry it. Altonia and
Schoenland exert real pressure from off the map."*).

**Where the derivation nevertheless holds, decisively: each body's best finding sits in the other's
blind region.**

- **(a) alone found the floor** (D-1, `09_GAP_REPORT.md:72-79`). No arc in an elite canon-derived
  corpus runs through the unmarked, postless, unaligned person — and body (b) shares body (a)'s elite
  bias, so (b) is doubly blind there.
- **(b) alone found the world-substrate hole, the closure axis, and the closure limit.** No seat probe
  asks what an act degrades *in common*; the coverage matrix contains no cell for it.

## §1 · How they function for emergent narrative

This is the question the other two serve. Stated as play, not as documents.

### 1.1 What body (a) shows the design can and cannot produce: the floor

**The season of a person with no mark, no post and no alignment is empty, for two mechanical
reasons** (`09_GAP_REPORT.md:73-79`, quoted): *"Ordinary capability is an empty verb set, not a
smaller pool — verbs gate on a practice at rank 3+, so a person holding none gets no acts, rather than
worse odds at the same acts. And `mark_salience` = 1.0 makes an unmarked person **inaudible in both
directions**: their acts do not propagate and news does not seek them."*

**In play, that is not a balance note. It is the absence of a story class.** The journeyman who
notices something, the widow who wants a hearing, the fisher who tries anyway — the design's own T1
promise, restated at `10:33` (*"an untrained attempt is always legal (T1: a person with no office, and
no training, can still try)"*) — either has no verb to attempt with, or attempts one nobody hears.
Both halves have to fail for the seat to be empty, and body (a)'s finding is that both do.

**Why this survives when most of body (a)'s headline did not.** It rests on formula evidence rather
than on the exercise's control character: the rank-3 gate is an undeclared second class gate in a
document declaring one; the same gate makes the advancement rule unreachable below rank 3; and
`mark_salience` reads one referent kind of the four `02:227` carries (`03_corrected_findings.md` §B,
sustained). It is control-independent.

⚠ **The diagnosis survived; the repair did not.** Lane (a)'s I-1 — delete the rank-gated verb clause —
fell on three of its four legs (`06` W-1) and survives narrowed to *a 3→5 advancement gap for
rank-5-only verbs.* **So the floor is currently a stated defect with no surviving fix in this
exercise.** Anyone reading §4's table should read that row as an open wound, not a closed one.

### 1.2 What body (b) shows: stories end at sittings, or they do not end

**The closure axis** (`01_arcs_01_18.md:33`, the 18-arc band, counted as documents): **13** arcs ended
at a *scheduled sitting* and their closure is preserved; **3** ended at *a counter reaching a number
with nobody deciding* and lose their ending; 1 ended on a faction's decision and is transformed; 1 has
no closure by design.

**In play:** the design is good at the kind of story that ends in a room on a date, with a named
person who could have decided otherwise. It cannot produce the kind that ends because a number
arrived. The three that lose their ending do not become worse stories; they become stories that do not
stop.

**The design's own answer to those three is correct and unstated.** *Somebody must convene it.* That
answer has a floor under it, which body (b) proposes to write down as a stated limit (I-A-3): **the
design cannot resolve an argument whose premise is that nobody wants it resolved.** The only
position-forcing object is the standing date, and a standing date has a named convener who orders its
items (`05_up_stroke.md:197-200`). Where every person with power to convene prefers the matter
unresolved, it stays unresolved. Body (b)'s antagonist **ran** the falsifier rather than accepting it —
five candidate forcing mechanisms checked, none forces (`07` §D) — so the limit is true of the suite
as filed.

### 1.3 What body (b) shows: nothing anyone does degrades anything held in common

**No act any person performs degrades anything the world holds in common.** `Thread-Read(place)`
already takes a place and already yields *"prior configurations at a place"* (`03:531`) — a getter
with no field behind it. The suite charges for Thread operations it never defines: `02:4` defers to
*"doc 04 (Thread)"* and `02:435` writes *"Thread ops per doc 04"*, while `00_INDEX.md:31` names doc 04
*The Hearth and the Community*.

**In play, the missing story is the commons:** many individually rational private acts making
everyone's practice harder, including the actor's. The arc corpus names it directly — arc 48, *"The
Practitioner Economy … Individual optimisation degrades shared infrastructure; the degradation raises
the difficulty, which raises the failure rate, which accelerates the degradation"*
(`03_arcs_41_55_and_emergent.md:254-256`), verdicted LOST on *"there is no world-substrate state that
any person's act can degrade."*

The N-line for this survived a hostile sweep **wider than its proposer's**: the granary is allocated
by a named office-holder at a standing date (somebody's decision, the opposite shape); the larder is
hearth-private; ore grade and siltation are place-held but clock-driven and no player act accelerates
them; and the critic additionally tested `forestall`, `granary_stock` and `season_factor` and all
three fail the shape (`07` §D). **Nothing in the suite is simultaneously shared, place-held,
persistent and act-degraded.**

### 1.4 The joint picture, stated so it can be falsified

**Entry and exit.** Body (a)'s floor is a defect at the *entry* to a story — who is eligible to be in
one. Body (b)'s closure axis and closure limit are defects at the *exit* — how a story stops. They are
the same shape at opposite ends: **a person with no verbs never enters; a matter no convener wants
never leaves.**

**What would falsify that framing.** It would be false if body (a)'s surviving set were confined to
entry, or body (b)'s to exit. **It is not, and I state the exception rather than smoothing it:** body
(a)'s blocked core is an *exit* finding (a want with no act whose object it is), which is exactly the
hybrid of §0.1. So the framing is a description of each body's **best** finding, not of its domain.
Read it that way or discard it.

**The second falsifier, and it is the one to try first.** The floor is a claim about play derived from
two formulas; nothing has been executed. Construct the ordinary person's season against the suite as
filed and enumerate the acts available to them. If the set is non-empty and their acts propagate, D-1
is wrong.

### PART 4 — THE SHARPEST THING I FOUND

**R-5's falsifier fires, and the property it fires on is *ending*.** The ruling's own falsifier reads: "any faction property not derivable from the proposition plus the commit set. The candidate is stored institutional memory, which this shape homes in Records at a Rung." That candidate is answered — but it was the wrong candidate. The underivable property is **how a faction stops**.

Walk the types. A faction is an immutable Proposition plus `commit` edges (`02` §3.1). The Proposition is "fixed at utterance, never destroyed" (§3). `hold` permits a **Proposition subject** over a Site or Rung (§4.1) — the suite needs this so that a *faction*, not a person, can hold an annexed territory (§4.6 calls annexation exactly this). Now let every member decommit — the case §1.1 celebrates: "a faction collapses when people leave… there is nothing left to be the faction." But there is: an undestroyable Proposition holding `hold` Tenures that only `revoke` ends, where revocation names "who may revoke, and at what venue" (§2.3) and every path to a venue runs through persons the faction no longer has. Death ends a person's tenures via the MATTER-class `until` write (`04` §3, rule 1); no analogous producer exists for a memberless proposition — I searched the suite for one. The result is a ghost polity: territory legally held by a banner nobody carries, unaddressable at any venue, uncontestable because the conflict rule needs a second act and the holder can never supply one. The suite's own worked aesthetic — "the village empties and still legally exists until an office strikes it from the roll" — is *deliberate* for villages and *unhandled* for factions, because for villages an office exists to do the striking.

What makes this sharp rather than pedantic is that the archive spent two ratified rulings on precisely this surface, and the suite discarded both inside the sentence "nothing is lost." ED-675 answers *what happens to the holdings* (territories→Uncontrolled, i.e., holds end and the vacancy machinery — which the suite has, and which is one of its best mechanisms — takes over). ED-881 answers *what observable conditions end a movement*, with an 89-case empirical grounding Jordan explicitly chose over a model that could only escalate — and "sponsor withdrawal starves legitimacy even with territory intact" is, translated into the shape's vocabulary, a statement about `commit` edges lapsing when the claims that sustained them decay: derivable, elegant, and *unwritten*. The fix is one rule composing on existing primitives — a `hold` whose subject-proposition has no live commits becomes contestable as vacant custody at the venue that would hear its revocation — plus a params-grade port of ED-881's conditions into whatever produces commit lapse. Zero new objects, which is the suite's own standard. But until it is written, R-5's confident "CONFIDENCE: HIGH… nothing is lost" is the suite doing the thing its §0 forbids: closing a hole by declaring it closed.

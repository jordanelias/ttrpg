## §1 · THE CHARTER, AND THE ONE RULE THAT MAKES IT WORK

```
N  NECESSARY   the thing cannot be removed without affecting EMERGENCE
E  ELEGANT     as distilled as possible WITHOUT AFFECTING FUNCTION
R  ROBUST      offers CHOICES WITH REAL IMPACTS for the player
S  SMOOTH      integrates well and PROPAGATES AS REQUIRED ACROSS SCALES
```

> **RULE 1 — E IS NOT AN INDEPENDENT AXIS. IT IS A RATIO AGAINST N AND R.**
> *Distil as much as possible without losing emergent possibilities or robust choosing for the player.*
> **An audit that scores four axes and averages them rates an amputated design as elegant**, and that is
> the only reason this rule exists.

> **RULE 2 — S IS T5 AND T6 RESTATED, NOT A STYLISTIC JUDGMENT.**
> **S-UP:** can a demand travel up the ladder and be **filtered by a named person at a rung**?
> **S-DOWN:** can an opportunity travel down and reach **a person who holds no post**?

> **RULE 3 — R BINDS AT SEATS A PLAYER CAN OCCUPY. Everywhere else a dominant act is a PORTRAIT.**
> And this cuts both ways: it makes ***"is this seat playable?"*** a load-bearing question that must be
> answered **per seat** before R can be scored at all.

> **AND THE META-RULE: A FIX THAT ADDS A SYSTEM HAS FAILED.** The standard is the best repair this
> corpus produced — **three edits, two of them deletions, and the vocabulary got shorter.**

## §3 · THE FALSE N-LINES — the highest-value finding a NERS pass can produce

> **An object whose claimed lost possibility ACTUALLY SURVIVES THE CUT, because something already ruled
> in provides it.** Six fired. **Every one was proposed by a competent pass with a plausible argument.**

| # | cut | its claim | why the possibility survives |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | an `information` gauge | *inquiry needs a target* | knowledge stored on the thing known — **two owners, no knower.** The ledger and the per-observer estimate already do it, **and only they can be planted or refuted** |
| 2 | `credence` deposits | *holding-something-true needs a carrier* | `Claim.confidence` **is** the carrier |
| 3 | an auto-declared uprising kind | *the only route by which control changes with no post-holder* | **survives** — raisable force against coercive presence, with suppression scars, **and the alternative needed a registry table plus a check that does not exist** |
| 4 | a chartering act creating a faction | *a schism needs a founding act* | **survives** — a faction is a Proposition plus commitments, so founding is `commit` migration |
| 5 | an `allegiance` edge with a stored gauge | *person-to-faction feeling needs a home* | commitment **degree** plus a **stance row** already carry it |
| 6 | `forecast_mass` | *depth needs a forecast term* | **it has no producer anywhere in the corpus.** An object with no producer **cannot have an N-line** |

**The pattern is uniform, and it is the useful part:** a mechanism was named, a **store** was proposed
for it, and **the store's job was already being done by an object the design had ruled in.**

## §4 · E AS A RATIO — the distillation, and where it is watched

**What went in:** roughly twenty-two proposed objects across four incompatible primitive sets.
**What came out:** **four carriers · one immutable record · one edge · one change grid · two epistemic
types · two moral types · three signatures.**

**And the biggest moves are DELETIONS, which is what the meta-rule asks for:** the Faction carrier ·
`seat_items` · an `avowed?` boolean over a three-state domain · two verbs (`annex`, `secede`) · a fifth
claim source · a rank gate on verbs · six false-N objects · a forecast term.

**The vocabulary got SHORTER.** Every act formerly *added at rank 3* folded back into its base act as a
declared standard.

## §6 · S — the one axis this shape passes structurally

**S-UP: PASSES.** A demand is a real object — a Petition — carried by a **named person** who spends an
act, placed on a **dated docket**, and **droppable by a convener who pays for it in grievance when the
backers learn.** *Filtering is a real act by a real person at a rung, not a threshold.*

**S-DOWN: PASSES, with a falsifier that runs.** A dispensation is published as a telling, distorts in
transit, and reaches a **postless** person through **their own** `opening_set`. **Nobody authors an
opportunity for anybody**, and `12_TESTS.md` T3 is the falsifier.

> ⚠ **AND THE EXECUTING TREE FAILS BOTH TODAY, for one reason.** There is **no demand object** that
> travels up, and **no opportunity can reach a person who holds no post because there are no persons at
> all.** The shape's S is structural; **the tree's S is zero**, and `13_EXECUTION.md` step 3 is the
> whole difference.

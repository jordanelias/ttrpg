# PART A · WHAT A VERB IS

## §A.1 · A verb is a declaration, not a function

> **A verb declares five things and nothing else: who may attempt it, what must hold, what it writes,
> what it emits on success, and what it emits on REFUSAL.**

**Why a declaration rather than code.** One resolver reads the table. A verb whose behaviour cannot
be expressed in those five columns is not a special case to be coded beside the resolver — **a
per-verb body IS a second resolver**, and the second resolver is the refusal the whole seam exists to
protect. A verb that needs more columns is a verb the design has not finished thinking about, and
its honest state is *declared missing*, never *implemented specially*.

## §A.2 · Eligibility is never capability — and this is a derivation, not a preference

> **`AX-1` says a person acts. It does not say a GOOD person acts.**

Skill decides how well a thing goes, never whether it may be attempted. Four eligibility kinds:
**your own standing to act · a seat's warrant · custody of a thing · presence at a place.** Rank
supplies dice and gates nothing.

⚠ **`if skill < N: return []` inside an option set re-implements the gate the design deleted**, and
it deletes the best thing about the option set: **a person who tries something they are bad at is the
engine working.** An incompetent attempt is a story; an unavailable option is a menu.

## §A.3 · A seat adds no verb and no modifier

A seat makes **ordinary** acts eligible where they otherwise are not, and substitutes the pool
source. **No verb exists only for seat-holders, and no seat carries a bonus.**

> **Why this is load-bearing rather than tidy:** the moment a seat carries a modifier, the seat is a
> stat, and taking the seat becomes an optimisation rather than a political act. **Authority should
> change WHAT YOU MAY DO, never HOW WELL YOU DO IT** — and that is `AX-1` again, since a modifier is
> the institution acting through the person.

## §A.4 · Every opener declares its terms — `T-n` applied to the verb table

From Stage 1: *an end that is not the owner's discretion is declared by the act that opened it.*

**So a verb that opens anything — a relation, a term of service, a case, a wardship — carries the
end condition in its declaration.** The clerk's filing sets the term; MATTER matures what was set.
**Nothing ripens that nobody wound**, and every ripening cites the act that wound it.

**This is what gives an institution's patience handles**: bribe the clerk who set the term, burn the
record that carries it, or reach the person who must renew it. **A process nobody authored has none
of those, which is the definition of a GM.**

# PART B · CONSEQUENCE

## §B.1 · Effect and consequence are different things, and the design needs both words

> **The EFFECT is the state change. The CONSEQUENCE is what people come to believe happened.**

They are produced by different steps, carried by different objects, and can disagree — which is not a
defect but the entire epistemic layer. **A design with only effects has no politics**, because
nothing can be denied, misreported, concealed, or believed by the wrong people.

## §B.2 · Three kinds of consequence, and the third is the one designs forget

| | when | why it must emit |
|---|---|---|
| **success** | the act did what it declared | ordinary |
| **REFUSAL** | a precondition failed against the world its predecessors left | **§B.3 — this is the scarcity channel** |
| **a crossing** | a quantity passed a declared edge | `T-b` — it changes what may be chosen, never what happens |

## §B.3 · A refusal must emit, and this is where scarcity lives

> **If a failed precondition raises instead of emitting, there is no Event, no witness, and no arc.
> Scarcity stops being something the world does and becomes something the engine forbids.**

**The worked shape:** two people reach for one granary. Acts resolve as an ordered fold, so the second
sees the world the first left, finds it empty, **and gets a different Event.** Neither act knows the
other existed; no verb mentions contention; no branch appears in the resolver.

> ### **THAT IS THE SAME PROPERTY AS OBSTRUCTION-WITHOUT-A-VERB, AT THE LEVEL OF THINGS RATHER THAN
> SEATS** — and both come from `AX-4`: one owner, one writer, and the fold makes the order real.

⚠ **AND IT IS WHY `ID-9` IS THE IDIOM IT IS.** A verb that emits success while writing nothing is the
exact inverse of this: **a consequence with no effect**, which teaches every witness something false.
The gate applies the write, or the emission is a lie the whole epistemic layer then propagates.

## §B.4 · Every consequence names its antecedent

**An emission carries the ids of what caused it, non-empty, always** — and where there genuinely is
no antecedent it says so explicitly rather than leaving the field empty. **An empty list must be
unrepresentable, not merely discouraged.**

> **This is not an audit trail. It is the narrative layer.** An arc *is* a chain of these links, so a
> design that leaves them empty has not lost its logs — **it has lost its stories** and cannot tell
> that anything was ever about anything.

# PART C · A SLICE OF PLAY

**Nothing in the chain says what one is. Here it is, derived.**

## §C.1 · The derivation

A slice of play is bounded by three things at once, and they close on a single object:

1. **One person's attention.** By `AX-1` only a person acts, and by `AX-2` they act on what they
   hold — so a slice is *somebody's*, never the world's.
2. **One occasion.** A slice has a beginning because something raised it: a date came due, a claim
   landed, a need crossed, or a standing commitment is still standing.
3. **One place.** Presence decides who can witness, so a slice that spans places has no witness set.

> ### **A SLICE OF PLAY IS A SCENE: ONE PERSON'S ATTENTION, AT ONE OCCASION, IN ONE PLACE.**
> It contains one or more interactions; it is the unit a season's budget counts; it is what a contest
> expands.

## §C.2 · The ladder of play, and each rung is bounded by something different

| unit | bounded by | who bounds it |
|---|---|---|
| **interaction** | one exchange resolving | the verb |
| **SCENE** | attention · occasion · place | **the budget** — ~5 per character per season |
| **season** | the six steps completing | the loop |
| **campaign** | a fixed point, or somebody's ending | nobody — and §D.4 |

⚠ **THE BUDGET COUNTS SCENES, NOT INTERACTIONS, AND THAT IS THE WHOLE OF WHY IT MATTERS.** At one
scene per person a King's scarcity is identical to a copyist's, and **nobody ever chooses what to
leave undone** — which is most of what high office is. **Triage is the mechanism the budget exists to
create**, and it only exists above one.

## §C.3 · Spending a whole season on one thing is the mechanism, not an exploit

A character who spends every scene petitioning **has chosen to leave four other things undone.** No
cap, no dedup, no per-venue limit, no *already-before-this-body* rule — **each of those would be the
engine deciding a person's options**, which is `AX-1`.

⚠ **AND IF THAT IS AN ATTRACTIVE WAY TO SPEND A SEASON, THE ACT VOCABULARY IS THIN.** That is a real
signal and it is a signal about the vocabulary — **not a reason to cap the one act that is well
specified.**

## §C.4 · Fidelity — the same slice, rendered at different depths

A scene is a slice **whatever depth it is played at**: summarised in a line, narrated, or expanded
into its own contest. **The slice is the same object; only its rendering changes.**

> **This is what stops fidelity becoming a second resolver.** If a played scene and a summarised one
> were different objects, they would need to agree *on average* — which is the twenty-year complaint
> against every auto-resolve in the genre. **They agree exactly, because they are the same slice.**

# PART D · THE SEASON LOOP

## §D.1 · Six steps, four barriers, and what each is for

```
CALENDAR    occasions fire · nothing is decided
MATTER      the world moves by its three motions · the world FREEZES at the end
DELIBERATE  every person decides, from what they hold · a map, not a barrier
RESOLVE     acts apply, as an ordered fold · the only writing step for acts
WITNESS     one global fan-out · then each person deposits into their OWN ledger
CENSUS      shares WITNESS's join · demand-driven individuation
```

**The order is not arbitrary and each adjacency carries an argument:**

| adjacency | why |
|---|---|
| CALENDAR before MATTER | a fact becomes an **occasion** before the world moves on it |
| MATTER before DELIBERATE | people decide about a world that has already moved, **and it is frozen while they do** |
| DELIBERATE before RESOLVE | everyone chooses **simultaneously**; nobody reacts to a same-season act |
| RESOLVE before WITNESS | you cannot witness what has not happened |
| WITNESS before CENSUS | the population reconciles against **post-eviction** ledgers |

## §D.2 · The frozen world is the load-bearing property

**Between the end of MATTER and the start of RESOLVE nothing changes.** That is what makes
deliberation a pure map — parallel, order-independent, and reasoning about one world rather than a
moving one.

> **`AX-2` does not catch a moving world.** A person reading a world mid-motion is not omniscient —
> **they are reading a world at an undeclared time**, which is a different and worse failure, because
> nothing in the epistemic layer can express it.

## §D.3 · What partitions, and what cannot

> **Every step's body partitions by the owner of what it writes. Every step's boundary is global.**
> **Two bodies do not partition: the ordered fold, which needs ONE order, and the witness fan-out,
> which is one pass over presence.**

⚠ **Do not shard either.** A per-container fold has no order to fold in, and a sharded fan-out is
what made an earlier loop's parallelism claim **unsound rather than merely unproven.**

## §D.4 · No container gets a clock — from Stage 2, not from the barriers

By §B.2 of Stage 2, **process-nesting requires a caller-supplied cap.** A container that schedules
itself has no caller, so it is precisely the unbounded form the seam refused, arriving without the
argument that made the seam safe.

**And it costs three properties independently:** the frozen world is void; there is no single act
order to fold; and antecedent chains can no longer be ordered in time.

> **The parallelism a clock would buy is already available.** Deliberation is a pure map. **The clock
> buys nothing and costs three invariants.**

# PART E · WHERE A CONTEST RE-ENTERS

## §E.1 · One place, and only one

> **A contest attaches at RESOLVE, where a conflict subdivides the tick and runs the same steps over
> a smaller person set on a shorter clock.**

**A battle, a hearing, an examination and two siblings arguing over a barn are the same call with
different vocabularies.** Any part of that needing a second story is a defect.

## §E.2 · Four lines cross the boundary; a fifth is a leak

| direction | what crosses |
|---|---|
| loop → subsystem | **the call** — when two acts contend for one subject, or an act names a contest |
| subsystem → loop | **Events**, into the same log, naming the acts that caused them |
| loop → subsystem | **the persons** — read, never written |
| subsystem → loop | **the outcome's degree**, from the one ladder |

**The leaks, each of which is tempting:** a state write from inside a contest bypasses the write
rules, the witness layer and the log at once · a second resolver · **a faction as a combatant, which
deletes `AX-1` at the seam** · a subsystem-specific event family, when a contest's outcome is an
Event like any other.

## §E.3 · One ladder, and variation by declared extension

**One degree ladder for every scale of the game**, read off the margin. A duel, a debate, a siege and
an examination all use it.

> **A subsystem varies it by passing a declared extension that can only narrow an outcome, never
> widen one — injected by the wrapper, never resolved by the engine, because the engine does not know
> which subsystems exist.**
>
> **Design the type first, then the hook.** An extension that returns a yes-or-no has no signature by
> which it could promote a band or re-derive the ladder. **The constraint is structural rather than
> asserted** — and a variation needing a richer return is an amendment to the one owner, made once,
> never a parallel ladder in a subsystem.

⚠ **AND A SUBSYSTEM THAT RETURNS A WINNER RATHER THAN A MARGIN HAS NOT MET THE CONTRACT.** A winner
is not a degree, and nothing may mint the missing bands on its behalf: **an undecided outcome is a
legitimate result**, and inventing a tiebreak to fill the contract is how a refusal becomes a
fabrication.

# PART F · WHAT STAGE 4 INHERITS FROM ALL THREE

1. **Six axioms**, two of them found by adversarial passes rather than by drafting.
2. **Closure is ownership** (`T-m`), **terms are declared by their opener** (`T-n`) — together making
   open-without-close unspellable rather than merely detectable.
3. **There are no institutional relations** — only people's, read in aggregate.
4. **One hierarchy, one graph, one ordering**; only places nest; process nests separately and needs a
   cap.
5. **A slice of play is a scene**, the budget counts scenes, and fidelity does not fork the object.
6. **A refusal emits** — scarcity is a consequence, not a prohibition.
7. **Fourteen idioms**, of which `ID-9`, `ID-10`, `ID-13` and `ID-14` exist to make the measured
   defect class hard to write.

## §F.1 · The one thing all three stages refused to do

**None of them added a primitive.** Every question was answered by an edge that was already rostered,
a Query that was already licensed, or a field that turned out to belong somewhere else — and the two
schema changes proposed (splitting a symmetric bond, re-subjecting a succession) both **remove** an
exception rather than adding a mechanism.

> **That is the test an idealized shape has to pass.** A meta-architecture that answers questions by
> growing the vocabulary has not found the shape — **it has renamed the problem.**

# §1 · Slices — two different cuts, often confused

**The unit of ATTENTION is a SCENE:** one person's attention, one occasion, one place. Three bounds
closing on one object. `interaction < SCENE < season < campaign`.

**The budget counts scenes, not interactions** — which is the whole of why ~5 matters. **At one,
a King's scarcity is identical to a copyist's and nobody ever chooses what to leave undone.**

**Fidelity does not fork the slice.** Summarised in a line, narrated, or expanded into its own
contest — same object, different rendering. **That is what stops a played scene and a summarised one
having to agree merely *on average*,** which is the twenty-year complaint against every auto-resolve
in the genre.

**The unit of PARALLELISM is an owner.** Each step's body slices by the owner of what it writes; every
step's boundary is global. ⚠ **Two bodies do not slice:** the ordered fold needs one order, and the
witness fan-out is one pass. **Shard either and the parallelism claim becomes unsound rather than
unproven.**

# §2 · Graphs — several, and confusing them is a design error

| graph | shape | reachable from a decision? |
|---|---|---|
| **containment** | tree, single parent | **yes** — an aggregate over descendants, on demand |
| **subordination** | sworn person-edges, **no root** | no — resolver-side |
| **the lateral web** — ties, knots, commitments, distant holds | spans rungs freely | **no** |
| **causation** — `causes[]` | append-only, acyclic in time | resolver-side |
| **claim citation** | **cyclic on purpose** — rumour citing rumour | per-holder ledgers |
| ~~**touch graph**~~ | **REJECTED** | contests are *declared*, never inferred from write collisions |

⚠ **That rejection matters.** Inferring contests from collisions is the keyword router in new dress —
it hands an undeclared contest to every pair of `work` acts on one site.

> **The split between the first three IS the design.** An aggregate over descendants is licensed and
> cheap; following a tie, a commitment or a distant holding **leaves the subtree** and becomes
> resolver-side, therefore invisible to a person deciding. **That is why nobody can know their
> faction's true strength — only what they claim about it.**

# §3 · Chains — four, each doing different work

**`causes[]` is the NARRATIVE LAYER, not an audit trail.** An arc *is* a chain of these links.
Required non-empty; `[ROOT]` where there genuinely is no antecedent, so the empty list is
**unrepresentable rather than discouraged**. ⚠ **Leave them empty and you have not lost logs — you
have lost the ability to say that anything was ever ABOUT anything.**

**The `tell` chain is the only transport.** No news service, no rumour tick, no ambient diffusion. **If
a thing is known two duchies away, a named person carried it and spent a scene doing so.**

**Petition → `carry` → docket → date → sitting** is demands rising. Every arrow is a person's act or a
calendar fact. **No automatic promotion, no queue drain, no priority function** — the filter is a
person and the person pays, **which is why it produces politics rather than a work queue.**

**The ordered fold** is contention: each act sees the world its predecessors left. Scarcity falls out
free — the second claimant on an emptied granary gets a *different Event* — **and no act ever knows
another existed.**

# §4 · Loops — and the one that matters has never run

**The season loop:** `CALENDAR → MATTER → DELIBERATE → RESOLVE → WITNESS → CENSUS`. Six steps, four
barriers, the world frozen between MATTER and RESOLVE.

**The contest nest:** the same loop, smaller person set, shorter clock, entered at exactly one place —
RESOLVE — bounded by a caller-supplied cap.

> ### **AND THE ONE THAT IS ACTUALLY THE GAME:**
> a claim lands in someone's ledger → **raises a question** → forms a candidate → **becomes an act**
> → emits an Event → is witnessed → **deposits as a claim in SOMEONE ELSE'S ledger** → raises *their*
> question.

**Measured today: ZERO instances.** No Event anywhere has a cause that is an act by a different
person. Nothing moves a Record to a second person, so the second person's question never fires.

> **The clock ticks, the fold folds, the world wears — and no story has ever crossed between two
> people.** Every graph in §2 exists; the one chain that would connect them has zero links.

⚠ **And the open debt above all of it: SPIRALS.** Four arcs plus the King are self-feeding loops, and
**nothing bounds one across seasons.** The contest cap bounds within a tick; nothing bounds
season-to-season feedback.

⚠ **CORRECTED 2026-09-03 — this sentence ended *"and no stage pretends otherwise"*, and a stage now
does.** Stage 1's `ID-16` states the missing axis — *a design enumerates its loops and signs each
one* — and says why a design made almost entirely of refusals converges: every refusal is a damping
term. **The debt is not closed and its shape has changed**: what is missing is no longer a doctrine
but a **representation**, and `ID-16` names the blocker rather than papering it — the enumeration is
a cycle over *what a verb writes* × *what its precondition reads*, and `requires` is prose in all 32
rows (`F.24`). **So the honest state has two halves.** The **declared** signed list is buildable now — a `LOOP` row
kind with a `sign` column in `hole_register.yaml`, the closed-set form `G.3.1` puts first — and it is
what `ID-16` owes. ⚠ **It is not yet a mechanism**: nothing reads a `sign` column until the derived
check does, and by `ID-13` a column no resolver consults does not exist. The
**derived** check that recomputes the cycles from `writes` × typed `requires` and compares them to
the declared list is blocked until `requires` is typed. **What would be reference rather than
mechanism is a table of loops written into a markdown file**, which is a narrower thing than *the
enumeration is unbuildable*, and Stage 1 records the correction.

# §5 · Echo — an Event does not travel. A Claim does.

**The Event is a fact in one log. What propagates is per-person BELIEF about it.** Propagation here is
epistemic, not mechanical — so two people present at one event end up holding different, both-honest,
incompatible claims, and **nothing reconciles them.**

**Three carriers, and nothing else:**

| carrier | reaches you because | what you learn |
|---|---|---|
| **presence** | you were there | what changed **and who did it** |
| **a document** | you hold the Record | **only that it changed** — no attribution |
| **a telling** | someone told you | whatever they said, at their confidence |

**Because each mints a DIFFERENT claim**, the document-holder and the eyewitness are not two copies of
one message. **Which is what makes covert action expressible without a flag:** an act nobody was
present for produces no first-hand attribution anywhere — not because it was marked hidden, but
because the observer set was empty.

# §6 · Upward and downward

**UPWARD is aggregation, computed and never pushed.** Demands rise as *acts*. And the numeric half
composes over the containment subtree, on demand, cached at a barrier, discarded there. **A count over
ENDED edges is refused, because it can only climb.**

**DOWNWARD is refraction — and every instinct here is wrong**, because every instinct says tree-walk.

> **A dispensation travels by being NOTICED, not down a chain of posts.** Publishing is a `tell`, so
> it **distorts in transit**. **Scope enumerates EXECUTORS, not places.** It does not apply — it lands
> as a compliance contest, per rung.

**Four forbidden implementations, each tempting:** broadcasting to descendants (deletes distortion and
per-witness belief at once) · applying it as a state write (deletes the compliance contest, and with
it all political friction) · a scope over places (cannot express a body with no containment node) ·
**assuming delivery**, which erases *never received* versus *received and refused* — and that
distinction is the whole of enforcement drama.

**Then nothing further is needed.** The recipient's own need plus their new claim yields an opening
through the same option set every act comes through. **Nobody authored an opportunity for anybody.**

# §7 · Decay, and the flood that proved the bound

**Claim confidence fades on its own** — one of only three things the world does unasked — and eviction
ranks on **confidence × recency, never on salience.** A ledger has a cap.

**So influence is not taken away; it lapses.** *He loses the town by being forgotten* is not a mechanic
anyone wrote — it is what happens when nobody spends a scene renewing the claim.

⚠ **The measured lesson.** When MATTER began emitting per write, events went **207 → 896 → 3,389** over
two, three and four seasons, every ledger pinned at its cap, and the test suite stopped finishing. The
fix was not a smaller cap — it was **channel predicates**, dropping deposits **678 → 68** over three
seasons.

> **Unbounded echo is the failure mode, and the correct bound is *who could have perceived this*,
> never *how important it is*.** Bound it by importance and you have built salience-ranked memory,
> which is a narrator deciding what matters — **the actor the design exists to refuse.**

---

# §8 · What must not propagate

No pushed aggregate. No broadcast. **No cascade** — destroying a thing sets `until` on the tenures
naming it and touches nothing else. **No within-season reaction at person scale:** *you anticipated,
or you are late.* No signals, no subscription table.

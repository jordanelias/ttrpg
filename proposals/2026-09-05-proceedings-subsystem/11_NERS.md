# N — NECESSARY

> *Unable to be removed without worsening the gameplay experience · makes the game more robust and
> elegant · smooth integration with existing play · supports a cohesive experience **from all
> directions**.*

**N is defined THROUGH the other three and is tested from all six directions. An N-line holding in
exactly one direction is a NARROWED pass, not a passing one.**

| direction | the attack | outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **top-down** — does the strategic layer need it? | *"Territory, faction and war already exist. Delete proceedings and the strategic layer still runs."* | ⚠ **THE ATTACK PARTLY LANDS.** The strategic layer runs. What it loses is **every non-violent route to a disposition** — a title conferred, a duty imposed, a person censured — so the game becomes one in which **the only way to change who holds what is force.** *Narrowed pass:* necessary for the game's stated shape, not for its execution |
| **bottom-up** — does the person layer need it? | *"A person can already `utter`, `commit`, `tell`. Proceedings add nothing they cannot do in a room."* | **FAILS.** Without a bench, `utter` and `commit` change nothing anybody must respond to. **`determine` is the only verb in the table that disposes of a matter, and it has been dead since it was written.** The person layer has speech and no consequence |
| **vertical** — does it hold across scales? | *"A hearth argument and a realm parliament cannot be the same call."* | **FAILS as an attack** — the venue is an ordinal and nothing branches on it (`03_PARAMETERS.md` §C.1). ⚠ **Untested, because nothing runs** |
| **diagonal** — does it connect layers that do not touch? | *"A proceeding is a person-scale event with no strategic consequence."* | **FAILS.** `disposes` writes a Tenure — a seat, a duty, a severance — which is a strategic object. **A proceeding is the one mechanism that converts speech into holdings** |
| **lateral** — does it interfere with siblings? | *"It duplicates combat: two subsystems that both resolve a conflict."* | **FAILS.** Both go through **one** `seam.contest`, **one** ladder, **one** log. The prize differs; the machinery does not |
| **horizontal** — is it coherent with itself across its own instances? | *"Twelve games is twelve special cases wearing a schema."* | ⚠ **UNRESOLVED, and it is the design's central claim.** `03_PARAMETERS.md` §F.3 states the falsifier; **`09_IMPOSSIBILITIES.md` row 13 grades its enforcement CONVENTION + a scan.** *Not a pass; a hypothesis with a stated test* |

> ### **N: NARROWED PASS.** Necessary bottom-up, diagonally and laterally, on failed attacks. **Weak
> top-down** (the strategic layer survives without it) and **untested horizontally** (which is the
> claim the whole design rests on).

# R — ROBUST

> *Strategic thinking · customization · creativity and variety in approach and resolution · players
> feel important to and impactful on the world · **emergent hooks and scenarios WITHOUT player
> involvement** · mechanics **fully formed, error-free and complete***

| clause | the attack | outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **strategic thinking** | *"The player picks the best argument. That is not strategy."* | **FAILS.** The four decisions (`07_THE_GAME.md` PART A) have no dominant option: descending trades an irrecoverable concession for a lower obstacle; piling on has **opposite correct answers** depending on how hard the room is thinking; and the entry decision costs a scene out of five |
| **customization** | *"Everyone plays a proceeding the same way."* | **FAILS.** Convictions weight the axes; stances differ; **what a person holds is different for each of them and may be false.** Two characters at the same hearing form different Candidate sets |
| **variety in resolution** | *"Every proceeding ends in a verdict."* | ⚠ **PARTLY LANDS.** Four bands, plus refusal, plus lapse, plus nobody attending — **seven endings.** But `disposal: mutual` and `disposal: none` games have no verdict at all, so the attack is answered. **What it exposes is that the four band NAMES are this design's** — ⚠ **and the clause that stood here, *"`P-01` leaves their edges unruled"*, is withdrawn (2026-09-06): the edges are ruled and golden-pinned; only the naming is local** |
| **players feel important** | *"A single speech cannot matter against a bench of five."* | **FAILS.** ⭐ *A speaker with no standing may still place an argument that outlives the discount on its source* — **the deposit does not depend on the degree**, so the lowest-standing person in the room changes what everyone knows |
| ⭐ **hooks WITHOUT the player** | *"Nothing happens unless a player convenes something."* | **FAILS.** Dates fire; benches resolve; NPCs travel or do not; matters are pressed, descended, determined and appealed **through the same acts a player takes.** ⚠ **Untested — nothing runs** |
| ⚠ **fully formed, error-free, COMPLETE** | *"Name one place it breaks at the extremes."* | ### **THE ATTACK LANDS, REPEATEDLY, AND THIS IS THE HONEST RESULT.** ~~**`P-01`** — no band edges, so nothing can resolve~~ ⚠ **STRUCK 2026-09-06, DISSOLVED — and its removal does not soften the verdict, because four rows remain** · **`P-05`** — **no act can deposit what it produced**, `tell` included, so a proof told at a hearing changes nobody's ledger · **`P-21`** — **nothing in this design moves a conviction**, so `AX-3`'s normative half has no producer in the one place it belongs · **`P-03`** — `Act` has no `via`, so an adjudicator cannot act *as* a seat · **`P-04`** — no `term`, so no summons has a return day |

> ### **R: FAIL ON COMPLETENESS, PASS ON THE REST.**
> **Five clauses survive named attacks. The sixth does not, and it is not close.** The design is a
> shape with five holes in its executable path, three of which are `absent` with no default. **A
> reader who takes this as buildable has misread it: it is a derivation with a register attached, and
> `§0.2` says done means it RUNS.**

# S — SMOOTH

> *Integrates cleanly without friction · mechanics interact cleanly with interdependent mechanics ·
> **zooms out and in across scales** · transitions and sequences cleanly · **pauses correctly** when
> other systems or scales are called for · **calculations consistent in methodology** with other
> mechanics · a unified mechanical approach*

| clause | the attack | outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **no friction points** | *"It needs a new seam."* | **FAILS.** It uses the existing `seam.contest`, the existing log, the existing ladder. ⚠ **It does need a manifest row the chain has not built** (`08_SEAM.md` §B) |
| **interacts cleanly with siblings** | *"It will fight with the propagation loop."* | ⚠ **PARTLY LANDS.** A proceeding feeds `H-102` harder than anything else in the game — one occasion, many witnesses, many deposits. **This design adds no bound and says so** (`L-3`). *Recorded, not solved* |
| **zooms across scales** | *"A hearth argument and a realm assize need different code."* | **FAILS** — the venue is an ordinal |
| **pauses correctly** | *"A proceeding that reaches a physical fight has to stop."* | **FAILS.** A contest inside a contest is process-nesting with the caller's cap. **The nesting is the pause** |
| ⚠ **calculations consistent in methodology** | *"You have invented a second way to grade an outcome."* | ### **THE ATTACK LANDS PARTIALLY, AND IT IS THE SHARPEST FINDING IN THIS PASS.** The design reads its degree off a **margin**, while personal combat reads its degree off a **scene** — *"the degree is READ OFF the subsystem, never mapped onto it."* **Two subsystems, two degree SOURCES, one ladder.** That is licensed by the 2026-09-03 ruling and it is *"two ladders for one quantity is an S defect even when each is individually correct"* running very close to the line. **Answered, not dismissed:** `T-k` is about the **ladder**, and there is one; the *sources* differ because the subsystems genuinely distinguish different things. ⚠ **But `H-119` already records the combat seam's two degree surfaces disagreeing on ~5% of decided fights**, which is what this failure mode looks like when it is real |
| **a unified approach** | *"Twelve games is twelve approaches."* | **FAILS** if the closure claim holds; **UNTESTED**, per `N`/horizontal |

> ### **S: PASS, with one partial landing** — the two degree sources — **and one unbounded interaction
> with the propagation loop, recorded rather than fixed.**

# E — SCORED LAST, AS A RATIO

> *Logically simple · clear approach · **no unnecessary overhead** · easy to understand · **allows the
> player to intuit complex outcomes from simple choices**.*
>
> ⚠ **E is never scored as an independent axis. Scored alone it is satisfiable by amputation: cut
> enough and what remains is simple, clear and cheap.** So it is scored **against what N and R
> found**, and the ratio is the score.

**What N and R found:** the design is necessary bottom-up and diagonally, robust on five of six
clauses, and **incomplete on the sixth in five named places.**

| E's two tests — and they are different | verdict |
|---|---|
| **no unnecessary overhead** | ⭐ **STRONG, and it is the design's best result.** **Zero new carriers. Zero new edge kinds. Zero invented verb names.** One contested field, which turns out to be a field the architecture already has and was about to delete for want of a reader. **And the ratio is what makes this count: the overhead is near zero while N and R are non-trivial** — cut anything and R falls further, which is the test amputation fails |
| **intuit complex outcomes from simple choices** | ⚠ **THE WEAKER HALF, AND IT IS WEAKER THAN THE FIRST BY A LOT.** ⑴ The dilution rule has **opposite correct answers** by elaboration, and the player is told the arrangement but never told which regime they are in. ⑵ `Read` and `Misread` are **deliberately indistinguishable** — correct by `AX-2` and hostile to intuition. ⑶ **The player is never shown the odds** (`07_THE_GAME.md` PART D), which is `§C.11` obeyed and is the design's largest experiential risk |

> ### **E: PASS ON OVERHEAD, QUALIFIED ON LEGIBILITY.**
> **And the qualification is not a defect to be fixed** — every one of the three is an axiom being
> honoured. **The honest statement is that this design buys its N and R with legibility**, and whether
> that trade is right is a play question that no amount of derivation settles. `07_THE_GAME.md` §D.1
> states it as the open risk it is rather than arguing it away.

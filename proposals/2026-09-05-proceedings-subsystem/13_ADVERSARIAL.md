# PART A · THE METHOD, AND WHY IT WAS RUN THIS WAY

**Producing and checking are different jobs, and the division is STRUCTURAL** (`§G.4.2`): a critic
declared `tools: Read, Grep, Glob` **cannot write, whatever its prompt says.** Both passes ran under
`.claude/agents/valoria-critic.md`, received the producer's **output** and never its reasoning, and
were charged with all three directions of `§G.4.3`:

> *did the producer **invent**?* · *did the producer **refuse what the design permits**?* ·
> **and *did the producer grade something backwards*?** — the third being the one an author cannot ask
> of themselves, *because the misgrading and the confidence come from the same place.*

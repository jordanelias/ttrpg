## FIGURE 11 · Recoverability — 23 atoms, COST 14 (PERCEPTION 6)

**THE CONSTRAINT SET.** Three classes of failure. "TERMINAL … → no later step repairs these → and NO TRADITION CAN WRITE A RULE FOR THEM" (`FIG:495-502`). "COSTLY … → recoverable at a visible price → and THIS IS WHERE THE WRITTEN CORPUS CONCENTRATES" (`FIG:504-507`). "EFFORT BELONGS AT THE TERMINAL STEPS" (`FIG:514`).

**SUCCESS.** This figure does not define success of a move; it grades the *severity of a Failure*. Its one positive statement is the FREE class: devices not spent are hoarded.

**THEREFORE FAILURE.** DIRECT — the figure *is* a taxonomy of failures by consequence. What it adds to every other figure: Failure comes in three grades, and the terminal grade is where "no rule can be written".

**THE FOUR BANDS** (a move read through recoverability — and see §D.2 on the mismatch with a single-Failure ladder).
- **Overwhelming** — the move lands **without spending**: the proof unused, the objection unspent, the construction unneeded — the actor exits with reserves intact (`FIG:509-511`). This is Fig 11's genuine contribution: Overwhelming is winning cheaply.
- **Success** — lands at a visible price paid and recovered (the costly class, spent and repaired).

Weak as an *outcome* — it is a pre-move routing rather than something the room does with a failed frankness.
- **Failure** — read as "an attack", "a standing contest", "a characterization", "self-interest" (`FIG:1459-1497`), in the reading's own word. Still playing, and this is the figure's own fail-forward: "priced as one" (`FIG:1447`) — an attack is a move with its own ladder (Fig 10 P4/P5), so the actor is now in an attack contest he did not choose.

**CONFIDENCE.** High for Failure; medium-low for Partial. The design keys this figure as a "demote-only" veto (`RES:241`); the corpus keys it as a *reading* — "the hearer will choose the reading that costs him least" — which is a price, not a gate (`FLAT:123`). See §A and §D.

## FIGURE 27 · Dissolving a prejudicial attack — 19 atoms, ACT 7 (PERCEPTION 6)

**THE CONSTRAINT SET.** Six methods "ordered by what they concede" (`VII:112`): DENY THE FACT — "nothing"; CITE SOMETHING INCOMPATIBLE — "nothing — but shifts to character … works only where the record is already there (A2, Fig. 3)"; IMPUGN THE ACCUSER'S MOTIVE — "nothing on the fact — but signals that one cannot … does not answer the charge, and is the only method that works when nothing else does"; CONCEDE THE FACT, DENY THE HARM — "the fact"; CONCEDE THE HARM, DENY THE INTENT — "fact and harm"; HAND THE ERROR BACK TO THE AUDIENCE — "everything except the relation to the room … costly, and the only method that can turn the room against the accuser rather than merely away from him" (`FIG:1535-1569`). "The ladder is descended only when forced, because every descent is VISIBLE and the room reads it as a concession that was not announced. A speaker who begins at 'it did no harm' has conceded the fact before anyone asked him to" (`FIG:1571-1577`). ⚠ "brevity is chosen for how it makes the speaker look, not for what it does to what the room remembers" (`FIG:1589-1590`). "CAPACITY IMPLIED: Recognizing which rung one is standing on before speaking, and not descending without deciding to" (`FIG:1592-1595`).

**SUCCESS.** The charge dissolved at the highest rung the record supports, with no unannounced descent.

**THEREFORE FAILURE.** BY SUCCESS DEFINING FAILURE — the unannounced descent (`FIG:1573-1577`); DIRECT at the ⚠ — the long denial that "reads as anxiety and cedes the floor to the accuser's framing" (`FIG:1588`).

**THE FOUR BANDS.**
- **Overwhelming** — the room is turned *against the accuser*: "hand the error back to the audience … the only method that can turn the room against the accuser rather than merely away from him" (`FIG:1563-1568`). Note it is the *bottom* rung that produces the top band, "costly" and highest return — the same shape as Fig 9 Q4's joke.
- **Success** — dissolved at the chosen rung; nothing conceded beyond it.
- **Partial** — "IMPUGN THE ACCUSER'S MOTIVE … does not answer the charge … signals that one cannot" (`FIG:1547-1553`). The charge stands unanswered; the matter is held; the docket grows with **the accuser's motive as a counter-charge**. This method's *native* band is Partial — it cannot produce Success on the original matter by construction.
- **Failure** — the unannounced descent: "conceded the fact before anyone asked him to" (`FIG:1576-1577`); or the long defence that cedes the floor. The stance written is the concession itself.

**CONFIDENCE.** High.

## B · The recurring shapes, ranked

1. **Failure is the move read as a signal about the mover** (15 figures). The move itself becomes evidence against the one who made it: "the presumption is itself the evidence against you" (`FIG:1011`); "the asking is itself evidence" (`III:30`); "signals that you do not think the document sufficient" (`II:99`); "signals evasion" (`FIG:201`); "conceding its relevance" (`FIG:375`); "signals that one cannot" (`FIG:1552`); "concedes the fact by doing so" (`FIG:1285`); "a concession not announced" (`FIG:225`, `FIG:1574`); "a long defence reads as anxiety" (`FIG:364`). This is the single shape the ladder's Failure write to `Person.stance` should be modelled on: **the adverse write is what the move was taken to mean about the speaker**, never a penalty applied to him.

2. **Overshoot, not deficiency** (13 figures; S3 is corpus-heavy, `FIG:1066-1069`). Elaborating past the proof, answering more than asked, doing it twice, too fast, too elaborately worked up, visible effort, performing above one's rank, directness, invective where wit would serve. Fig 12 sharpens it: "the perception is available and does not govern the action" (`FIG:580`). **A Failure in this game is almost never "you did not see it"; it is "you saw it and pressed."** The ladder's tuning follows: Failure should cluster just past Success, not far below it — the actor who overshot was *close*.

3. **The band is what the room takes the move as, and the room chooses** (PERCEPTION, 155 atoms, 26 of 27 figures). "the hearer will choose the reading that costs him least" (`FIG:1461-1462`); Fig 8's whole right-hand column; "The protection is a property of the audience's disposition, not of the utterance" (`FIG:458-459`). This is why `FLAT:123` says the licence must price rather than refuse: nothing in the corpus refuses a move; the room *reads* it.

4. **Terminal = being seen at it** (7 figures). Every terminal fault is a visibility event: "the DISCOVERED lie — not the lie" (`FIG:135`); "visible effort once seen" (`FIG:497`); "it need only be visible that you know" (`FIG:1006`); "the false flower once exposed" (`FIG:499`); "as legible as a plain statement" (`FIG:969`). The costly class is *effect* faults; the terminal class is *exposure* faults. That is a clean rule for grading a Failure's severity without a coefficient.

5. **Reception is a function of standing, not of the act** (Figs 3, 19, 25; Mi Zixia, `FIG:1037-1044`). The same move gets a different band from a different position. The design carries this as `reception` in the obstacle (`RES:231`); the corpus says it is stronger than a modifier — it can flip the band's sign.

6. **The descent** (Figs 5, 26, 27; "the three ladders", `FIG:696`). One shape shared across three figures: "descended only when forced, and each descent concedes something the room can see". The Partial in all three is *the rung itself becoming the question*.

7. **Relocation and proxy are the corpus's fail-forward** (Figs 1, 2, 9, 10, 24, 26). Every costly Failure has a named next move: the sponsor sought (`FIG:66`), the room changed (`FIG:417`), the proxy who can be disavowed (`FIG:441`), the advocate defeated instead (`FIG:399`), the stock filled later (`FIG:28`). The corpus supplies the "still playing" reading unprompted in every case but one (§D.1).

8. **The inverted bands** (Figs 9, 18, 19, 21, 27). Four places the ladder's order is not the corpus's: the bottom rung produces Overwhelming (Fig 27 hand-back; Fig 9 Q4 joke; Fig 9 Q6 anger ⚠); Overwhelming carries the matter and writes *no credit* to the actor (Figs 18, 19 — the ruler adopts it as his own); the matter's Overwhelming *is* the step's Failure at row 14 ("Winning, and being repaid later", `III:43`); and Overwhelming is structurally absent for counsel (Fig 14). These are not noise; they are where the corpus's ladder and the design's single ladder disagree.

## D · Where the derivation failed, and why

1. **The inverse scale does not reconcile with fail-forward.** "whoever touches it is killed" (`FIG:1020`); "TERMINAL, and unrecoverable by any later step" (`FIG:1021`). Every other terminal fault has a corpus-supplied relocation. This one does not, and the figure's own box says the step "is a guess" when no third party will name it (`FIG:1027-1029`). Either the design treats Fig 19's inverse scale as the one place the ladder's Failure is *not* the outcome (the outcome is the actor's removal from the world, which is outside the proceeding subsystem), or it softens the corpus. I hand it forward unsoftened.

2. **The corpus grades Failure three ways; the ladder grades it once.** Figs 11 and 21 (46 atoms, COST-dominant) exist to say that a Failure at row 5 and a Failure at row 4 are different *kinds* of thing. The design collapsed this into `stakes_grade`, already "the most likely deletion" (`FLAT:98`). Shape 4 above (terminal = exposure) offers a coefficient-free replacement: the severity of a Failure is whether the fault was *seen*, and that is a `visibility` field the Claim already has.

3. **Row 14 inverts the ladder.** "Winning, and being repaid later" (`III:43`): the matter's Overwhelming is the close's Failure. A single `speak` contest cannot express "carried the matter, lost the relationship". Either the close is its own contest with its own bands (Fig 21's Partial — the joint fiction — is a good one), or `matter.carried` must be able to co-emit an adverse relationship write.

4. **The Partial is native in twelve figures and manufactured in six.** I have marked each. The manufactured ones (1, 3, 4, 19, 22, 26) should not be cited as corpus warrant for the docket-grows mechanic; the twelve native ones (5, 6, 7, 9, 10, 17, 18, 21, 23, 24, 25, 27) can be. The strongest four are 7 (the loop), 9 Q6 (relocation), 24 (defeat absorbed → concession pending), and 27 (impugn).

5. **Two branches where the corpus's band and the evidence's band disagree**, and both are in Fig 9. Q1-unprovable: the corpus says brief denial (Failure = detailed denial); the evidence says "detailed corrections outperform brief ones" (`FIG:1585-1586`) and preserves only the *standing* ground. Q6: the corpus says anger forfeits standing; the evidence says it extracts concessions from a counterpart who cannot afford impasse (`S:162`), so controlled release can be Overwhelming. The design must pick per branch, and the modern evidence for Q6 is `[UNVERIFIED]` (`VII:130`).

6. **Fig 13 has no carrier.** How legibly the band reaches the actor differs by genre — announced with reasons (theatre), announced without (court: "without deliberating and without giving reasons", `FIG:599-600`), never announced (statecraft). The design emits `matter.carried` etc. uniformly. A court should emit the band and withhold the reasons; a sovereign's audience may emit nothing for a season.

7. **Fig 24 hides bands rather than changing them.** Under a document, "the counsellor is not seen to be overruled, the sovereign not seen to be persuaded" (`FIG:1331-1332`). The corpus's interposition does not alter the outcome; it alters *who witnesses it*. The design's fan-out ("`matter.turned` fans out to every ledger in the room", `VERBS:253`) is exactly what a document interposition is built to prevent. That is a design decision, not a derivation.

8. **Fig 4's polarity.** The design carries latitude as an Ob term (`RES:230`). The corpus's stated effect of low latitude is a shift in *which band is modal* — "impasse rate rises … the deal is likelier not to happen", *and* "price obtained rises" (`FIG:183-184`). That is not "harder"; it is "more Partial, and a bigger Overwhelming when it lands". An Ob term cannot express a distribution reshaped in two directions at once.

9. **Fig 26 is a price in the corpus and a veto in the design.** "the hearer will choose the reading that costs him least" (`FIG:1461-1462`) is a *reading*, and `FLAT:123` already says so. A demote-only veto (`RES:241`) is the refusing polarity the census argued against. The four "FAILS AS" readings are four Failure *narrations*, not four gates.

10. **The Overwhelming-without-credit problem.** Before a superior (Figs 18, 19) the corpus's top outcome is that the ruler adopts the correction as his own, and the counsellor is *not* credited — indeed the more visibly he is credited, the closer he is to "the plan he wants as his own" (`FIG:996-997`). The `speak` row writes `Person.stance` on Overwhelming (`VERBS:69`). In the audience-with-a-sovereign genre that write should be to the *sovereign's* stance, not the speaker's, or the ladder rewards exactly what the corpus says is fatal there.

11. **Three figures are taxonomies dressed as rules.** Fig 12 (capacities), Fig 22 (canons), Fig 25 (routes) look like constraint sets and are inventories. I derived bands from 22 and 25 through one rule each (Dispositio; erode-by) and declined for 12. If the write-up cites 12 for a band, it is over-reading.

12. **Fig 2's superseded status.** I derived its bands at the phase level. The study itself says "go to Part III for what each step requires" (`FIG:58`). Anything cited to Fig 2 should be re-cited to the requirement row (`III:29-43`).

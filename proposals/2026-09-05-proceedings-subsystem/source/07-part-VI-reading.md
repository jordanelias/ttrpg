# PART VI — Reading

Primary texts by tradition, then the modern works, each with the finding it supports in brackets.

---

## Primary

**Greece.** Gorgias, *Encomium of Helen*. Plato, *Gorgias*; *Phaedrus* 259e–274b. Isocrates, *Against the Sophists*; *Antidosis*. Aristotle, *Rhetoric*; *Topics* VIII; *Sophistical Refutations*. [Anaximenes], *Rhetoric to Alexander*. Lysias. Demosthenes, *On the Crown*, with Aeschines, *Against Ctesiphon* — read as a pair. Thucydides: the Mytilenean debate (III.36–50) and the Melian dialogue (V.84–116).

**Rome.** *Rhetorica ad Herennium* — for I.11 above all. Cicero, *De inventione*; *De oratore*; *Orator*; *Brutus*; *De officiis*; and the letters to Atticus for the practice as against the precept. Seneca the Elder, *Controversiae* and *Suasoriae*. Quintilian, *Institutio oratoria*. Tacitus, *Dialogus de oratoribus*; the *Annals* for speech under an autocrat. Plutarch, *How to Tell a Flatterer from a Friend*; *Precepts of Statecraft*. Philodemus, *On Frank Criticism*. Demetrius, *On Style*.

**Byzantium.** Constantine VII, *De ceremoniis*; the *Excerpta de legationibus*. [Maurice], *Strategikon* XI. Liudprand of Cremona, *Antapodosis* and *Relatio de legatione Constantinopolitana* — Squatriti's *Complete Works* is the current English translation; Sutherland, *Traditio* 31 (1975), on the limits of the *Relatio* as evidence. Kekaumenos, *Strategikon*. Psellos, *Chronographia*. Anna Komnene, *Alexiad*.

**The Carolingian West.** Hincmar, *De ordine palatii* — MacLean's edition and study. Alcuin, *Letters*. Dhuoda, *Liber manualis*. Sedulius Scottus, *De rectoribus christianis*. Jonas of Orléans, *De institutione regia*. Smaragdus, *Via regia*. Einhard, *Vita Karoli*; Notker, *Gesta Karoli*. Nithard, *Histories* III.5.

**Persia and the Islamic world.** *Letter of Tansar*; *Testament of Ardashir*. Ibn al-Muqaffaʿ, *Kalīla wa Dimna*; *Risāla fi'l-sahāba*. Al-Jāhiz, *al-Bayān wa'l-tabyīn*. Al-Māwardī, *Adab al-Wazīr*; *Naṣīḥat al-Mulūk*. Kai Kāvus, *Qābūs-nāma*. Nizām al-Mulk, *Siyāsat-nāma*, Darke translation. Naṣīr al-Dīn Ṭūsī, *Akhlāq-i Nāṣirī*. Ibn Khaldūn, *Muqaddima*.

**The Ottoman court.** Lütfi Pasha, *Āsafnāme* — Kütükoğlu's edition. Kınalızāde Ali Çelebi, *Ahlāk-ı ʿAlāʾī*. Mustafa ʿĀlī, *Nushatü's-selātīn* — Tietze's edition and translation. Hasan Kāfī al-Aqhisārī, *Usūl al-hikam*. Koçi Bey, *Risāle*. For orientation across the genre: Sariyannis, *A History of Ottoman Political Thought up to the Early Nineteenth Century* (2018) — secondary, and the standard survey.

**China.** *Zuo zhuan* — Durrant, Li and Schaberg translation. *Guoyu*. *Analects* 1.3, 13.5. *Mencius* 1A.7, 3B.9. *Xunzi*. *Han Feizi* chapters 3 and 12 — Liao's translation for chapter 3. *Guiguzi*. *Zhanguo ce*. Sunzi, chapters 3, 7, 13. Liu Xie, *Wenxin diaolong* ch. 18. Wu Jing, *Zhenguan zhengyao*.

**Japan.** *Seventeen Article Constitution*. *Goseibai Shikimoku*. Hōjō Shigetoki, *Gokurakuji-dono goshōsoku*. Imagawa Ryōshun, *Imagawa-jō*. Zeami — Rimer and Yamazaki, *On the Art of the Nō Drama*, is the standard English collection.

**India.** Kautilya, *Arthaśāstra* Book I, on envoys and on the fourfold means.

**The Latin Renaissance.** Machiavelli, *Il Principe*; *Discorsi*; *Advice to Raffaello Girolami*. Guicciardini, *Ricordi*. Castiglione, *Il Cortegiano*. Erasmus, *Institutio principis christiani*; *De copia*; *Lingua*. Commynes, *Mémoires*. Barbaro, *De officio legati* — Branca's edition. Gentili, *De legationibus libri tres*. Hotman, *L'Ambassadeur*. Bacon, *Essays* — "Of Negotiating," "Of Cunning," "Of Simulation and Dissimulation." Sir Thomas Smith, *De Republica Anglorum*. Botero, *Della ragion di stato*. Lipsius, *Politica*. Accetto, *Della dissimulazione onesta*. Gracián, *Oráculo manual*. Callières, *De la manière de négocier avec les souverains* (1716).

## Modern

These are not part of the corpus. They are the outcome literature the corpus lacks.

**On individual differences and their measurement.** Elfenbein, Curhan, Eisenkraft, Shirako & Baccaro, "Are some negotiators better than others?", *Journal of Research in Personality* 42 (2008): 1463–1475 [S1, Part III]. Elfenbein et al., "Why are some negotiators better than others? Opening the black box of bargaining behaviors" [S1]. Elfenbein, Eisenkraft, Curhan & DiLalla, *Journal of Applied Psychology* 103 (2018): 88–96 [S1]. Sharma, Bottom & Elfenbein, *Organizational Psychology Review* 3 (2013): 293–336 [S1, 0.6]. Ferris et al., *Journal of Management* 31 (2005): 126–152; Munyon, Summers, Thompson & Ferris, *Personnel Psychology* 68 (2015): 143–184 [S1, as the null the pincer explains].

**On latitude and leader effects.** Jones & Olken, *Quarterly Journal of Economics* 120 (2005): 835–864 [S2, D4]. Crossland & Hambrick, *Strategic Management Journal* 32 (2011): 797–819, and their 2007 study of United States, German and Japanese firms [S2]. Quigley, Chirico & Baù (2022) on private against public firms [S2]. Bazerman, Neale, Valley, Zajac & Kim (1992) on agents and impasse [S2].

**On persuasion and message design.** O'Keefe, "Standpoint explicitness and persuasive effect," *Argumentation and Advocacy* 34 (1997): 1–12, with the 1998 companion on justification explicitness [S3]. Kumkale & Albarracín, *Psychological Bulletin* 130 (2004): 143–172 [II.1].

**On correction and denial.** Swire, Ecker & Lewandowsky (2017); Ecker, Lewandowsky & Chadwick (2020); Swire-Thompson, DeGutis & Lazer (2020); Ecker, Sharkey & Swire-Thompson, *PLOS ONE* 18 (2023): e0281140 [S4].

**On emotion in negotiation.** Van Kleef, De Dreu & Manstead, *JPSP* 87 (2004): 510–528; Van Kleef et al., *EJSP* 36 (2006): 557–581; Wang, Northcraft & Van Kleef, *OBHDP* 119 (2012): 54–63 [S4].

**On reading others.** Eyal, Steffel & Epley, "Perspective mistaking," *JPSP* 114 (2018): 547–571 [S5].

**On deference and on expertise.** Cheng, Tracy, Foulsham, Kingstone & Henrich, *JPSP* 104 (2013): 103–125 [II.1, D1]. Kahneman & Klein, *American Psychologist* 64 (2009): 515–526 [S6, D3]. Antonakis, Fenley & Liechti, "Can charisma be taught?", *Academy of Management Learning & Education* 10 (2011): 374–396 [S4, S6, D3]. Mellers, Stone, Murray et al., *Perspectives on Psychological Science* 10 (2015): 267–281 [S5, S6, 0.6, D3].

**On group decision.** Stasser & Titus, *JPSP* 48 (1985): 1467–1478; Nemeth et al., *EJSP* 31 (2001); Schulz-Hardt et al. (2006) [D4].

**On evidence and on distributions.** National Research Council, *Identifying the Culprit* (2014) [S8, II.3]. O'Boyle & Aguinis, *Personnel Psychology* 65 (2012): 79–119, with the contesting responses of Beck, Beatty & Sackett and of Spain, Credé & Harms [0.6, mentioned and not relied on]. Denrell (2003) on inference from samples that undersample failure [0.4].

**On the contested category.** Brown & Levinson, *Politeness* (1987); Matsumoto (1988); Ide (1989); Gu (1990); Mao (1994); Eelen, *A Critique of Politeness Theories* (2001); Watts, *Politeness* (2003); Hu (1944) on the two Chinese terms [0.3].

**On the classical reading of figured speech.** Ahl, "The art of safe criticism in Greece and Rome," *American Journal of Philology* 105 (1984): 174–208; Bartsch, *Actors in the Audience* (1994) [S3].

**Cited but not retrieved for this study.** A paper in *Group Decision and Negotiation* (2022) reporting that within-person variability in personality predicts negotiation gains and satisfaction better than average trait levels; bibliographic details unconfirmed and logged as a gap [S6, D3].

### VII.1c Third pass — the reorganization around findings

The seven corpus-doctrines that organized the previous version were arrived at by filtering for recurrence, and recurrence is a scenario filter: structures recur across traditions, persons do not. The findings that emerged once outcome evidence and the step-level interrogation were brought in could not be hosted by that organization. The crosswalk at the end of Part I shows where each old doctrine now sits. Nothing is lost; everything is subordinated.

Claims withdrawn in this pass, beyond those above:

| Withdrawn | Replaced by |
|---|---|
| That the scarce faculty is perception | S5 and S4: a portmanteau covering calibration, elicitation, control of display and allocation of attention |
| That the social-astuteness null showed reading people does not matter | S1: a self-report, post hoc, in a low-latitude setting, and exactly the null the pincer predicts |
| That two independent literatures agreed on situationism | S1: one literature and one design artefact |
| That the corpus records no failures | S3 and 0.4: types exhaustively, frequencies not at all |
| That the entry decision is a binary between direct and oblique | S3: seven registers, each with its own misreading |
| That the master sequence is a sequence of moves | Part III: a requirement analysis |
| That the institutional designs are a crutch for those who failed to train | S6: the correct response to a low-validity environment |
| That a sequence is what a domain produces when it cannot produce expertise | S6: true but incomplete, since the validity of a domain can be changed |
| That building the other party an exit is one doctrine among seven | S7: the device that keeps a costly failure from becoming terminal, which is a different order of thing |

A further five, identified in the same audit as defensible but of lower value, were subsequently built, bringing the appendix to twenty-seven. Three of them turned out to carry more than the audit expected.

| Figure | What it turned out to carry |
|---|---|
| The genre triangle | The three species with their topics, the characteristic fault of each, and the three cases where the scheme bends — a trial that is really about standing, an assembly asked to judge a past act, and amplification working inside the other two |
| The interposition taxonomy | Six kinds, and for each what it **can** absorb against what it **cannot**. The second column is new: an advocate absorbs defeat but not a discovered lie; a ceremony absorbs a contest over standing but not a substantive concession; and no interposition of any kind absorbs a terminal failure |

Four candidates were considered and declined. The crosswalk, the overshoot vocabulary and the inventory of fault catalogues are already tables and a table is the right form for each; the three grounds of credit are three terms and need no box. Drawing them would have been volume rather than clarity.

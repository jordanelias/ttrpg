## Conventions used throughout

▣ marks an evidential note: modern work bounds, reconciles or explains a
claim of the corpus. ⚠ marks a point at which modern work contradicts the
corpus's stated reasoning. Absence of either means no evidence was located,
not that the claim is sound.

Nothing here is written in the imperative. The figures are of three kinds and
each says which it is: an **anatomy** decomposes a thing into its parts; a
**reconstruction** compresses what the corpus prescribes into its decision
structure, to be read as prefixed by *the corpus holds that*; a **fault
table** records what the corpus names as going wrong at a point, with the
source in every cell, and the capacity that failure implies. Part 0.5 states
why.

No figure carries a probability, a base rate or an expected value. The corpus
supplies none and no located modern work supplies them for these steps. A
decision tree with likelihoods on its branches would be invention, and the
absence is the reason Part III is a requirement analysis rather than a
decision procedure.

# `source/` — the study this directory mechanises, vendored so the design is checkable

## Status: **SOURCE MATERIAL. Not this repository's work, not editable here, and not ratified by anything.**

**What this is.** *"Standing, Persuasion and the Actor — eight findings from setting the pre-modern
literature of political, forensic and diplomatic proceeding against modern evidence on what a person
can do"*, assembled September 2026, 33,395 words in ten files. It was supplied to the 2026-09-05
session as the substantive input for the proceedings design, as a zip archive.

**Why it is vendored rather than cited.** The design's central claim is that **the study's
twenty-seven figures ARE the parameter space** (`03_PARAMETERS.md` PART A) — so every parameter,
ladder and roster in this directory is a claim *about a specific figure*. **Without the figures in
the tree, none of that is auditable**, and `01_THE_STUDY.md`'s `[READ:]` trail is an assertion nobody
can check. **A read-only pass has already found three places where the mapping flattened a figure**
(`13_ADVERSARIAL.md` finding 17), which is exactly the class of error that is invisible when the
source lives in an upload.

## ⚠ THE FOUR THINGS A READER OF THIS DIRECTORY MUST TAKE FROM THE SOURCE AND NOT FROM THE DESIGN

**1 · The conventions are load-bearing and the design inherits them.**
> **▣** marks an evidential note: modern work bounds, reconciles or explains a claim of the corpus.
> **⚠** marks a point at which modern work **contradicts** the corpus's stated reasoning.
> **Absence of either means no evidence was located, not that the claim is sound.**

**2 · Nothing in it is written in the imperative, and the figures are not instructions.** *"The
figures are of three kinds and each says which it is: an **anatomy** decomposes a thing into its
parts; a **reconstruction** compresses what the corpus prescribes into its decision structure, **to be
read as prefixed by *the corpus holds that***; a **fault table** records what the corpus names as
going wrong at a point."*

**3 · ⭐ IT CONTAINS NO NUMBERS, AND SAYS SO.** *"No figure carries a probability, a base rate or an
expected value. The corpus supplies none and no located modern work supplies them for these steps. A
decision tree with likelihoods on its branches would be invention, and the absence is the reason Part
III is a requirement analysis rather than a decision procedure."* **Every threshold, weight, band edge
and magnitude in this directory is therefore either derived from a structure the study states, or is
`assumption`-grade with an injection site and a sweep (`ID-6`) — and is marked as such at the point of
use.** A number presented as the study's would be a fabrication.

**4 · Its own confidence register governs.** `08-part-VII-apparatus.md` §VII.3 grades which of its
modern claims were verified against published abstracts and which were adopted from an external
critique and marked `[UNVERIFIED]`; §VII.2 lists its `[GAP:]` and `[NULL:]` results. **This directory
inherits those grades and upgrades none of them** (`01_THE_STUDY.md`, closing block).

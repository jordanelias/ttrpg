## §5 · THE THREE GENERATORS

Stated because the individual corrections are symptoms and a v3 should not need writing.

**1 · The pass was graded on refusals, so it produced refusals.** `skills/ners/SKILL.md:41-43` — *"the
product of the pass… is the cuts that turned out to be free"* — and v1 reported *"Twenty-seven free
cuts"* as its yield. A pass told its product is free cuts will find them; the reverse cut, *add it and
name what the game can now do*, was never run on any of the 76 rows or the 18 proposals.

**2 · Disqualifier 2 was applied backwards.** `SKILL.md:239` asks *"who writes this, by what act?"* of a
**proposed object**. v1 asked it of **the tree** and treated the tree's missing producer as the idea's
defect. Run that way it reads *nothing new can ever be necessary* — and v1's thesis sentence, *"adopting
one adds a second empty carrier beside a first"*, is that reading stated outright. **The ideas propose
producers.**

**3 · The grading was asymmetric.** Candidates were graded `paper` for not already running in this tree
(*"GRADE: `paper` for every primitive and proposal in all seven documents"*), while the tree's own unreached
code was described as shipping. `paper`/`runs` describes **a design under audit**, not an external
candidate; every idea is paper until built.

# The flattened aptitude list (from `2026-10-16-attribute-derivation.md` §1)

## Status: PROPOSED (2026-10-16). An extract of the head's §1, for showing on its own. The evidence is in that proposal's §3–§5; the clusters and the attribute recommendation are in its `_part3`.

**Key.**

1. An **aptitude** is a capacity underlying learning and doing. One row per aptitude, ordered by total draws (an item requiring it), with 19 practice domains, 65 verbs (35 graded) and 10 subsystems interrogated. Ties go to more NAMED draws.
2. Columns 3–5 count the practice domains (P), verbs (#, with distinct resolvers in brackets: the seven thread operations share one pool, and `fight` and `accept` one bout) and subsystems (S) that draw it. In parentheses are up to three ids, NAMED and highest tier first.
3. **named Tn** means a source states it (T1 loop code, T2 stub code, T3 ratified intent, T4 design only). **overridden** means named at T4 but overridden for dice, mostly by the 2026-09-05 ruling. **inferred** means this proposal's reading.
4. The last column maps to the registry's nine only where a name, an alias or a source's use says so; the list was NOT bent toward the nine.

# The aptitude list, interrogated by etymology, synonym and domain/type, with the equivalences that follow

## Status: PROPOSED (2026-10-16). A reading of the 46 aptitudes in `2026-10-16-attribute-derivation_aptitudes.md`. Not ratified; merge ratifies nothing unless Jordan so rules.
## Method note. [UNVERIFIED: every etymology below is from memory and was not checked against a dictionary this session; the ones I am least sure of are marked ‡.] Synonyms are ordinary English, not the repo's registry aliases (those are in the aptitude list's last column). Domain and type are my classification, and a judgment. The equivalence classes in §2 are a **semantic** reading only: they say which words name one capacity, not that the game's resolvers treat them alike. §3 gives the test that would show that.

## 0. How to read the columns

- **Domain**: the faculty the aptitude lives in. P physical · C cognitive · Pc perceptual · V volitional (will, nerve) · S social.
- **Type**: what kind of thing it is. **cap** capacity (underlying, scales with the person) · **skl** a learned skill that the word names in ordinary English (so a practice, not an aptitude) · **dis** a disposition or temperament (a lean) · **lim** a limit on how much of something a person can hold · **act** the exercise of a faculty rather than the faculty (an act word).
- **Class**: the equivalence class of §2.

## 2. The equivalences

Relations used: **synonym** (one capacity under two names); **same capacity, different load** (the underlying capacity is the same, the thing it is exercised against differs); **same capacity, different object** (as before, for perceptual and social aptitudes); **faculty and act** (one is the capacity, the other its exercise); **not an aptitude** (a learned skill, a lean, or a limit on holding).

**A. Holding up under load** (the word-families *endure*, *patience*, *steadfast*, *tough*, *hardy*, *stamina* all mean *to last*; I read this as one capacity differing by the load).
- **A1 body-load: Stamina, Toughness, Hardiness.** Same capacity, different load: exertion over time, injury, exposure and sickness. Sources tie all three to END.
- **A2 will-load: Contact endurance, Self-coherence, Patience** (and Concentration (steadiness), Steadfastness, Will, Discipline, Devotion, which are class B and H). Same capacity, different load: thread contact, erosion of self, tedium. These three are *thread-specific or time-specific loads on one will-capacity*, and A2 sits next to B.

**B. Holding the will to its purpose** (Will, Concentration (steadiness), Steadfastness, Discipline).
- Will ≈ Steadfastness: **near-synonyms** (willpower vs constancy; *steadfast* is 'fixed in place', i.e. will that does not move).
- Concentration (steadiness) ≈ Will directed at attention: **same capacity, different load** (strain and distraction against sustained attention). The combat code already makes it one tracker (3·Focus + 2·Spirit).
- Discipline is etymologically *learning*, and in the sources it is flavour text for a Focus bonus; I treat it as **not an aptitude** (a practice habit) unless Jordan names it.
- Devotion (depth of investment in a person or ideal) is a **disposition** that WIL-style checks read (Resolve cap = Spirit × 1); it is H below.

**C. Perceiving the world** (Observation, Land sense, Situational awareness, Spatial sense).
- All four are **same capacity, different object**: physical detail; weather, terrain and soil; many moving parts; place and position. Sources tie all four to Cognition (ACU). Land sense (a rural trade's skill) and Spatial sense (a map-reading skill) lean **skl**.

**D. Perceiving persons and what is coming** (Reading people, Empathy, Discernment, Anticipation, Tactical read).
- Reading people ≈ Empathy: **same capacity, different object-in-the-person**: Reading people takes in state, intent and lies; Empathy adds feeling *with*. The sources name Appraise as *"empathetic perception"* (SCV:147), a single roll: **synonym** at the resolver.
- Discernment: **same capacity, different object**: how far to credit what is said or read (calibrated trust; Attunement in the sources).
- Anticipation ≈ Tactical read: **same capacity, different object**: predict a person (an opponent's next move) vs read a field.
- D is the perceptual twin of C: C's object is a thing, D's a person or an intention.

**E. Reasoning** (Reasoning, Inference, Judgment, Planning, Quick thinking, Numeracy).
- Reasoning ≈ Inference: **faculty and act** (Inference is Reasoning applied to partial evidence).
- Judgment: **faculty and act** (Reasoning under uncertainty and pressure); Planning: **faculty and act** (Reasoning toward a goal in time); Quick thinking: **same capacity, different load** (Reasoning under time pressure).
- Numeracy: **not an aptitude** (the word is a 1959 coinage for an *educational achievement*; a learned skill that Reasoning supports).

**F. Taking in and holding what is learned** (Memory, Learning, Breadth, Practice ceiling).
- Memory ≈ Learning: **faculty and act in sequence** (uptake, then retention); the registry's *Recall* spans both.
- Breadth and Practice ceiling: **not an aptitude** in the sense of a rolled capacity: both are **limits on holding** (how many practices; how deep each), the very function the 2026-08-15 census gives Recall as a *capacity that is never rolled*.

**G. Moving others** (Presence, Leadership, Persuasiveness, Verbal fluency, Deception, Intimidation).
- Presence ≈ Leadership: **faculty and act** (bearing, then bearing used to get others to follow); the Command derivation takes Charisma at 2 : 1.
- Persuasiveness, Verbal fluency, Deception, Intimidation: **same capacity, different method** (appeal, words, lie, threat). Deception and Intimidation are named as **learned skills** in the sources (HIST:339, :349) and have no source-named draw of their own: **not aptitudes**. Verbal fluency is the capacity beneath all four of the method words and is all inferred.

**H. Bonds** (Relational depth, Sincerity, Devotion).
- Relational depth ≈ Devotion: **same capacity, different object** (persons vs persons, places and ideals). Sincerity: **same capacity, other side** (being genuine vs forming deep ties); the registry already treats Sincerity and Affability as aliases of Bonds.

**K. The thread** (Thread sensitivity) stands alone: perceptual, named by sources, no synonym in the list. It is the only aptitude that no ordinary-English word covers (a coined term).

**M. Motor control** (Strength, Dexterity, Precision, Quickness, Reflexes, Balance).
- Dexterity ≈ Precision: **near-synonyms** (the first is *handedness*, the second *exactness*): fine control of the hands; Precision's name is in the sources only as a learned skill (*Precision Hands*).
- Quickness ≈ Reflexes: **same capacity, different load**: voluntary speed vs speed of reaction; the combat code gives them separate formulas, so the equivalence is semantic only.
- Balance: **same capacity, other face** (control, not speed).
- Strength stands apart (force, not control), but is **the only one of the six that names pure force**.

**T. Temperament** (Boldness): **not an aptitude** (a lean, TMP in the template; `disp` in combat).

**Count.** The 46 aptitudes fall into **12 classes**: A1 (3), A2 (3), B (4), C (4), D (5), E (6), F (4), G (6), H (3), K (1), M (6), T (1); 3+3+4+4+5+6+4+6+3+1+6+1 = 46. Seven rows are typed as not a capacity (**skl**, **dis** or **lim**: Numeracy, Deception, Intimidation, Boldness, Breadth, Practice ceiling, and Discipline, which §2 reads as a practice habit). Dropping T (Boldness), whose only member is not a capacity, leaves **eleven** classes. §4 folds A2 into B to get **ten** families; that fold is a judgment, not a count.

## 3. What the equivalences do and do not show

1. **They are semantic.** Two words that name one capacity may still be two things in a resolver. The test for each equivalence: *two persons that differ in only one of the two aptitudes should produce different outcomes in some resolver, at fixed seeds, with a named control.* If no resolver separates them, they are one aptitude; if a resolver does (combat's Quickness and Reflexes have separate formulas), they stay two inside the class and the class is a *group*, not a *merge*.
2. **The equivalence classes map to the registry's nine unevenly.** A1 → END. B → FOC and WIL. C and E → ACU. D → ATT (and ACU for Anticipation and Tactical read). G → CHA. H → BON. M → STR (Strength) and AGI (the rest). F, K, A2's thread-specific rows and the whole of E's Numeracy have **no home among the nine**: F is Recall; K is TS; A2's Contact endurance and Self-coherence are RES.
3. **Three clean equivalences and the two that are not.** Clean: Stamina/Toughness/Hardiness (A1); Observation and its three objects (C); Reading people/Empathy (D). Not clean: Will/Concentration/Steadfastness (B) because Concentration is Focus in the combat code and Will is Spirit, two registry attributes; and Memory/Learning (F) because the registry has no Recall to hold them.
4. **Words that point at learning rather than capacity.** *Discipline* (from the word for *pupil*), *numeracy* (a school achievement), *deception*, *intimidation*, *precision* and *land sense* (all named as learned skills in the sources) are best read as **practice** (the thing that grows by doing), which the flattened list's rule already flags.
5. **Ten of the 46 are all-inferred** in the evidence; this analysis does not alter that. The etymology adds no source, and a word's origin is not evidence that the game needs it.

## 4. What this suggests for the attribute question (secondary, a judgment)

Reading only the classes with a capacity at the centre, **ten families** result: body-load (A1), will-load (A2+B+H's Devotion), world-perception (C), person-perception and foresight (D), reasoning (E), retention and learning (F), influence (G), bonds (H), thread (K), motor control (M, which has two natural halves: force and control). That is close to the registry's nine, with RES and REC and TS and the thread-specific will-load falling where the nine are silent. It is a **reading to test against the resolvers**, not a count Jordan's ten attributes are bound to. Jordan's J-T1 and J-T2 are unchanged.

## 5. Limits

- The relation labels in §2 are judgments; the test in §3 item 1 is what would settle each.

# The 46 demands, read by etymology, synonym and faculty family, with the relations between them

## Status: PROPOSED (2026-10-16). A word study of the 46 demands in `2026-10-16-attribute-derivation_aptitudes.md`. Not ratified; merging ratifies nothing unless Jordan so rules. Secondary to the head and `_part3`.
## Method note. Etymologies are from memory, not checked against a dictionary in this session; each carries a confidence (high, medium, low). Synonyms are ordinary English, not the registry's aliases. **The grouping is not this file's**: it is the derivation's one grouping (`_part3` §8.1), each demand under the recommended primary that expresses it. This file adds the words, and the relations between members of a group.

## 0. How to read the columns

- **Faculty family** (renamed from *Domain*, which collided with the derivation's practice domains
  P01–P19): P physical · C cognitive · Pc perceptual · V volitional · S social. A reading of the word,
  not evidence.
- **Kind**: the head's §1.5 kind, independent of any roster: CAPACITY, COMPUTED QUANTITY, PRACTICE,
  LIMIT, TEMPERAMENT. It replaces this file's former *type* column (cap / skl / dis / lim / act).
- **Group**: the recommended primary that expresses the demand, or *uncovered*, *temperament*,
  *practice* (`_part3` §8.1). It replaces this file's former twelve *equivalence classes* and ten
  *families*, and the derivation's former sixteen clusters: one grouping, not three.

## 2. The relations inside each group

**Relation labels.** Two are symmetric: **synonym** (one capacity, two names) and **same capacity,
different load or object** (the capacity is the same; what it works against differs). Three are
**not symmetric**, and are written with an arrow: **faculty → act** (B is A exercised); **ingredient
→ formula** (the source computes B from A, today); **skill on →** (B is a learned skill that adds to
A's roll).

- **AGI: Quickness, Reflexes, Balance, Dexterity.** Agility → Tempo (quickness); Agility → Reflex,
  with Attunement (reflexes); Agility → Balance, with Strength (balance). Quickness and Reflexes are
  **two formulas over one ingredient**, not two capacities (F01, F02). Dexterity reaches Agility only
  by the registry's alias, and every draw is inferred.
- **END: Stamina, Toughness, Hardiness.** Same capacity, different load: exertion, wounds, exposure.
  Endurance → Stamina reserve (with Spirit) and → Durability (with Spirit and Strength); Hardiness is
  Endurance rolled directly (FW:239).
- **FOC: Concentration.** Focus → the Concentration tracker, `3·Focus + 2·Spirit` (F14). **Not "same
  capacity as Will":** the tracker blends two ingredients, and the source separates them (*"Spirit 6
  / Focus 2 … endurance without skill. Spirit 2 / Focus 6 … skill without staying power"*,
  `derived_stats_v30.md:267`).
- **ACU: Reasoning, Observation, Spatial sense, Land sense, Anticipation.** Reasoning, observation and
  spatial sense: same capacity, different object (structure, detail, place). Land sense is skill on →
  Cognition (Seasons Reader, HIST:314). Anticipation is Cognition → Reading, with Attunement at half
  weight (F06): Cognition-primary.
- **WIL: Will, Steadfastness, Sincerity, Devotion, Contact endurance.** Will ≈ steadfastness: near
  synonyms (steadfast is will that does not move), rolled on Spirit in five statements and on
  Cognition in one (S-defect 4). Sincerity: the sources roll Spirit (FW:430) and ED-503 assigns the
  Sincerity Gate to Spirit (FW:822); the registry's alias on Bonds (`descriptor_registry.yaml:48`) is
  the outlier (S-defect 2). Devotion: Spirit → the Inspiration cap, `Spirit × 1`
  (derived_stats_v30:557). Contact endurance: Spirit → Thread Fatigue threshold, `Spirit × 5`
  (threadwork_v30:195).
- **ATT: Reading people, Empathy, Discernment.** Same capacity, different object: state and intent,
  feeling, credibility. SCV:147 calls Appraise *"empathetic perception"*: one roll.
- **CHA: Presence, Persuasiveness, Judgment, Leadership, Situational awareness, Tactical read.**
  Presence and persuasiveness: Charisma rolled directly (FW:98, :409); not learned skills, since no
  source names either as a skill. Judgment: Hold Court rolls Charisma or Cognition (U15). Leadership,
  situational awareness and tactical read are all Charisma and Cognition → Command (F18): **tactical
  read reads a field, not a person**, so it is not a perception of persons; it is a use of Command.
- **REC: Memory, Inference, Learning, Breadth, Practice ceiling.** Memory → inference: faculty → act
  (FW:306 rolls Recall to assemble evidence). Recall → spark dice (learning, F38), → equip slots
  (breadth, F39), → depth ceiling (practice ceiling, F40). Breadth and practice ceiling are limits.
- **STR, BON, TS: one member each.** Strength stands alone (force, not control). Relational depth is
  Bonds. Thread sensitivity is TS, and the only demand no ordinary English word covers.
- **uncovered: Self-coherence.** resilience → Coherence-cost resistance (F34), dark; canon says it is
  *"how resilient their spirit is"* (operations.py:144).
- **temperament: Boldness, Patience.** Leans, not capacities. Boldness is `disp` (F17); patience has
  no source.
- **practice: Numeracy, Verbal fluency, Precision, Deception, Planning, Intimidation, Quick thinking,
  Discipline.** Each grows by use within a domain, and every draw is inferred. **Deception** meets the
  sources only as the skills *Smooth Liar* (HIST:339) and *Intimidation* (HIST:349), both on the
  contest's Obscuring move; no source names "Deception". **Precision**'s one source is craft practice
  (HIST:241); *Precision Hands* (HIST:350) is a perceptual examination, +1D examining crafted objects,
  and is no evidence for precision. **Numeracy** is practice because no source names it as a person's
  capacity, not because of its 1959 coinage. **Discipline** appears only as flavour beside a retired
  Focus rule (HIST:293).

## 3. What the word study does and does not show

1. **The relations are semantic; the test of a group is in the code.** ⚠ **Members of a group that
   the sources compute from one ingredient are identical by code**: no resolver can hold `quickness`
   fixed and vary `reflexes`, because both are computed from `agi` (`combat_systems.py:113`, `:172`).
   A test of two such words at fixed seeds cannot observe a difference, so it cannot fail. **The
   divergence test therefore applies to candidate primaries and to the formulas over them, not to
   words**: two persons differing only in one primary should produce different outcomes in some
   resolver, with a named control.
2. **The group map is the derivation's coverage map.** Every demand of kind CAPACITY, COMPUTED
   QUANTITY or LIMIT has a recommended primary except self-coherence (`_part3` §7.3).
3. **Clean relations**: stamina, toughness and hardiness; reasoning, observation and spatial sense;
   reading people and empathy. **Not clean**: will and steadfastness (two ladders, S-defect 4);
   sincerity (registry alias against the rolls, S-defect 2); concentration (a blend, not a synonym of
   will).
4. **Etymology is not evidence.** No group or kind here rests on a word's origin.

## 4. Limits

- Etymologies are from memory; the confidence column marks the least sure (*plan*, *learn*, *empathy*,
  *discipline*, *persuade*). None changes a group or a kind.
- The relation labels are judgments; §3 item 1 says what would test each.
- The groups follow `_part3` §8.1 and change if the recommended set changes; under the smaller set,
  concentration, relational depth and the five Recall demands have no group until a rewrite is chosen.

## Parts, in reading order: [head](2026-10-16-attribute-derivation.md) (words, §1 the demands, the candidate pool, the source formulas, the selection's evidence, the command) · **this file** (§2–§5, the evidence) · [`_part3`](2026-10-16-attribute-derivation_part3.md) (§6–§13: principles, selection, coverage, the one grouping, NERS, limits, both critic reconciliations).

Words, tiers, the abbreviated sources and the draw format are the head's. **A draw is written
`` `term` (roles, [key] basis: locator or reason) ``.** Roles are one or more of odds,
difficulty/cost, eligibility, effect size, resists another, joined by ` + `. The basis is NAMED Tn;
NAMED T4, overridden for dice by the kernel, the 2026-09-05 gloss or the T3 row; or INFERRED. **A key
is one source statement or one inference.** It is counted once per term, at its best basis (named
before overridden before inferred). A key should carry one basis everywhere; the one that does not,
`[fw-cover-identity]`, differs by item for a stated reason (#52), and the head's command prints every
mixed key so a second one cannot hide. An item may draw one term by more than one key.

**Every demand is classified in the head (§1.5) by what kind of thing it is, independent of any
attribute roster.** A cell says *a use of Command* (the source rolls its Command formula) or *a
sourced use of Agility* (the source names Agility alone) where that matters to the coding. Under the
head's rule, when a source names only an ingredient (Agility, Spirit …) or a formula (Command …) and
not the function, the term is INFERRED here, and the ingredient or formula is still NAMED in the
head's tables (§1.2, §1.4). **No cell here calls anything "derived"**: what is derived from what is
decided only after primaries are chosen (head, Words; `_part3` §7).

**Fixed form.** For each domain: (a) what doing it well requires **physically**; (b) **cognitively**;
(c) **perceptually**; (d) **socially or volitionally**; (e) the draws by basis, as a count of draw
entries (named / overridden / inferred). Each cell gives the term, its roles, its key and its basis.

**Notes that bind the table.**

ignore all prior rulings for this exercise"*). Notes 1–3 record what the tree currently says, because
the *overridden* tags come from them; the selection in `_part3` §7 counts every overridden statement as

## 4. STAGE 2b: WHAT EACH VERB REQUIRES (all 65 rows of the template's master verb table)

**Fixed form, per verb.**

- **shape**: one of three.
  - ***graded***: the act has, or when complete would have, a draw, a degree, an obstacle, a gate or
    an effect size that a person's quality could move.
  - ***choice***: the act has no degree. Its quality is the choice itself, which the chooser makes
    from values, stance and held claims. Choosing well is one requirement, counted once, at S10.
  - ***book***: bookkeeping. A record or tenure move with no skill in it.
- **ACT**: what performing the act well requires of the actor, by family (physical, cognitive,
  perceptual, social, volitional). This is the only column counted as verb draws.
- **DOMAIN**: the practice domains the act draws (§3).
- **resolver already names**: what a T1/T2/T3 resolver or stub reads off a person today.

⚠ **The ACT column does repeat DOMAIN draws.** For combat (#18, #20 against P03 and S01), threadwork
(#58–#64 against P05 and S05) and the contest (#50 against P02 and S03) the verb rows carry the same
keys as the domain and the subsystem. The first pass said they were *"not re-counted"*; that was
false. **Draw entries re-count by construction. The head's measure is the distinct statement (the
key), counted once per term**, and that is what orders §1.5 and what §7 weighs.

**How a verb's requirement was decided.** A *graded* verb's ACT draws come from: (1) what its
resolver or stub names (T1/T2/T3); (2) what a design names for the act (T4, or overridden); (3)
failing both, what the act's outcome plainly turns on (INFERRED, with the reason in the cell). A
design that names a **different act sharing the verb's word**, or the same act at **scene scale**, is
an analogue, and an analogue is INFERRED under its own key: #12's price, #24's travel leg, #56's
interrogation.

No term was added to a *choice* or *book* verb. **Rows 58–64 share one resolver** (the thread pool),
as do **rows 18 and 20** (one bout); the head's command counts distinct resolvers in brackets.

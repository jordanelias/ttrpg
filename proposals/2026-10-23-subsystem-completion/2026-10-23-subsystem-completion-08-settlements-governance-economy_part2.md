**R.** R-WORLD (the half with no player): the spine G1 → G3 → G9 runs unwatched once CP-01, RB-5 and CP-06/07 are on — a hungry town works less, starves faster, grows restless and yields less to its takers; a dead lord's levy is no longer asked of him. Succession's unwatched half arrives with CP-T02-01/-02 and IN-61. R-PLAYER (seats a player can occupy, through IN-47): the rate, whether to spend an act collecting, the payment, where to stand, where to trade, whether to open a market, whether to grant. Completeness at the extremes: zero hands yield zero (shipped off for that reason); a levy is capped by the stock `requisition` finds; `craft` with short inputs refuses whole; a settlement may not be endowed with nothing (CP-02's loader rule). Residual: a vectorless cohort's choices are draws (ED-IN-0296 (2)), and no one resists a levy by an act yet (the defiance demand, §5).

**S.** S-UP: labour → yield → stores → hunger (REM-50) → unrest → what the seat above can take. S-DOWN: a seat's edict → its holder's `requisition` → the town's stores → the next draw. Lateral: CP-02/05 between settlements. Pauses: the MATTER items (CP-01's factor, CP-03's spoilage) sit inside the one barrier at named steps and read no social quantity (S25); every governance move is an act at RESOLVE. No item adds a clock: CP-03 is AX-5's matter motion; CP-07's question moves nothing until the holder acts. ## 7 Risks, and what this deliberately does not do

**Risks.**
- **Stacked movers.** CP-01 and CP-03 change MATTER's output on top of RB-5, and CP-06/07 change what every requisition takes. Their arms must be read together: a sweep run per item would measure each against a world the others have already changed.
- **The populace is a draw.** Cohorts have no pursuit vector (ED-IN-0296 (2)); the populace's choices (whether to move, whether to resist) are draws. The mechanical couplings (labour by presence, compliance by band) bind without a preference; the choices do not.
- **A levy costs acts.** Collection by act means a seat-holder spends budget each season per edict; with the scene ceiling (T08 §0), a lord with many edicts collects few. That is F.18's price and may leave levies uncollected unwatched.
- **Claim volume.** `tax.levied` is chronicle-broadcast, into ledgers 13 of 74 of which are already at cap (overview §3). The chronicle itself is slated for deletion (SC-01 step 13; H-214 holds it); if it goes, `tax.levied` needs the publication route GV M-EDICT designs.
- **Inferred geography.** The geography file's own header calls 21 settlements' coordinates "inferred, not hand-tuned" (`systems/settlements/valoria_geography_v30.yaml:244-245`); CP-02's ground class reads those coordinates, so some endowments will be artefacts of the inference.
- **Markets need an act nobody forms yet.** CP-05's `offer` is minted by `create_record`, which computed play mints only as `text` (H-165).
- **`[UNVERIFIED]`:** whether `Office.upkeep` is in any hold snapshot (CP-08); which claim carries a rung's stock to its governor (CP-07); whether the populace present at its rung holds a claim of a `requisition.taken` whose subject is that rung (CP-06); `world_q` calling the person-side `stability` with a cohort (permitted by direction, not checked against `04`'s import rules).

**What it does not do.** No births, growth or immigration (Γ14); no elections or councils (Γ18); no hiring, credit or bankruptcy (Γ19, Γ20); no rationing (Γ21); no holder-skill output (Γ12, JQ-2); no succession (Γ10: CP-T02-01/-02 and IN-61); no ending rows (Γ16, factions' surface); no distance or logistics cost (WT); no crime quantity (Γ23); no standing orders or delegation (Γ15, IN-48); no levy at MATTER; no resistance verb (social contest's); no storehouse; no screens or UI. It re-specifies none of RB-5, REM-22, REM-25, REM-2B, IN-61, CP-T02-01/-02 or B-R's verbs; it composes on them.

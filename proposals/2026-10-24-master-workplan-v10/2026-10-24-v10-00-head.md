## 7. Risks of this plan

1. **184 positions in 20 batches (B10-01 to B10-19 and B10-Z) is a queue, not a forecast.** Batches are sized by file affinity (the verb cluster serialises), not by effort; none is estimated. B10-05 to B10-18 are the long serial spine.
2. **Premises older than HEAD.** The re-base rule (V1) is the mitigation; its cost is a base reading per batch.
3. **Hash churn.** B10-05 to B10-10 are declared movers; every seeded pin in `test_season_weather.py`, `test_season_shape.py` and the goldens re-records, each old → new in its commit body.
4. **Two design clusters wait on Jordan** (the character and capability source; what a raised troop is). B10-11's two JQ-2 items and B10-12's raise-a-force item wait with them; CP-T03-07 and CP-T03-08 wait on M6 through REM-52. The thrust layer (CL-THRUST) waits on M20 as well, because it contravenes four texts, each resting on a ruling or on ratified Layer 1 (`-05` M20); if it is released, its pieces each wait on their own needs (only B4.x and B5.2 fall after B10-17) and its ladders need content only Jordan can author (`-04` CL-THRUST).

## 8. Definitions coined here (`CLAUDE.md` §4)

- **Question M1 to M21 and milestone M1 to M3**: two unrelated numberings. `M1`…`M21` in `-05` and its citations are Jordan-queue questions; a milestone is always written 'milestone M1' (`CLAUDE.md` §0.2).
- **Re-base**: re-deriving a position's sites and its base reading on the batch's base commit before building (rule V1).
- **Home**: the one file that holds a position's full spec; a plan row names it.
- **Carried entry**: a v9 position copied into `-04` in full because its v9 home is deleted on adoption.
- **R8**: a still-held item stays in `B10-Z` until ruled, then slots into the next unopened batch of its cluster, else runs as its own sub-batch (v9 `_part3` §B.0).
- **Admission decision**: one call of the fold's `_admits` (eligibility, `requires`, counterparty) for one act, in the fold or the contest branch of `resolve`.
- **Invisible refusal**: an admission refusal for an act that never enters `driver.resolved`, so `aperture` and `corpus_run.attribute` do not count it.

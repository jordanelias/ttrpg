## 0. Verdict

**The engine is a deterministic, well-tested simulator of a lawful society, and it is not yet a game.** It runs: three pytest suites pass except three tests, the milestone's four-row gate passes three of four rows, and a season is byte-reproducible. What it does when it runs is mostly refuse. Over three seeds of four seasons, 67% of resolved acts end in a refusal event and no success (73.6% of all attempts once the refusals the instruments do not count, §5, are added), 16 of the 48 verbs that were attempted never once executed (a 17th, `march`, was declared 59 times and every declared march was then also refused: `march.declared` and `march.refused` both appear), and at seed 0 half of the people can neither tell nor argue because they know no one. PR #506's gap register measured the same finding independently, over five seeds of four seasons and at a different definition: 12,372 of 16,116 attempts refused by the driver's own rule (76.8%), 73.9% with graded losses excluded (`proposals/2026-10-25-thrust-layer/01_MISSING_IN_CODE.md`, P1 and P10). The causal chains that make the engine look emergent (over the four storybar seeds, depth up to 15 and 30% of acts answering another person's act) are not mostly refusals, since half of the cross-person edges at seed 0 start from a success, but the deepest chain at seed 0 runs through nine refusals and three successes. There is no text layer; the only rendering is one line per act.

Ranked, worst first:

1. **Formation outruns feasibility.** The chooser forms acts the world cannot satisfy. 3,620 of 7,570 refusal events (47.8%) end on a read that finds no referent of the kind the verb needs: 3,137 are `exists:<Kind> = 0` (130 of them `DocketItem`, an empty docket rather than a wrong kind) and 483 are `work` acts whose subject is not a Site. The count takes the 60 most frequent reads per seed, so it is a lower bound. A further 2081 admitted acts changed nothing when their effect ran (1240 of them are migrations into a rung at capacity). (§6, §8)
2. **The instruments undercount.** 1,899 of 5,103 admission refusals (37.2%) belong to contested acts that never enter `driver.resolved`, so `aperture` and `corpus_run.attribute` do not see them; they read "never attempted" for verbs that were attempted and refused hundreds of times. (§5, §6)
3. **Information does not circulate, and memory is a fixed window.** At build every person knows no one and holds no claim; after four seasons at seed 0 40 of 80 still know no one, a person's own telling is rare (at most 4 claims anywhere carry a `Claim.chain`, which only a spoken `tell` builds), and the ledgers fill to the 200-claim cap (all of them at seeds 0 and 2; at seed 1 the smallest holds 176). Of 236 `tell` attempts over three seeds, 54 won, 113 lost the contest and 69 were refused. (§10)
4. **Eight carrier fields end every run empty, and 62 of 146 declared Event kinds are never emitted.** Each empty field already has a reason on the record (four `Rung` containers that ratified Layer 1 declares and nothing maintains; `Record.ttl` with no reader or writer; `Person.capability` waiting on J-13's remainder; `Office.upkeep` read but declared by no office; `Claim.chain` empty at the end because only a spoken `tell` builds it and `tell` rarely wins), so none is a new defect; the never-emitted kinds are the larger signal. (§9)
5. **Stability, endings and the strategic scale do not reach play.** `stability` reads 0 at every office rung in every reading; no ending is reachable in computed play; the realm fights no field. These are already registered holes (H-224, H-176, H-175); this report only confirms them at the current HEAD. (§11)
6. **Three tests fail in this container.** Two are the known shallow-clone failures; one is a 1-unit-in-the-last-place drift in a combat pin that CI does not show. (§3)
7. **Much of the content an emergence reading needs is absent, and what is present but shipped off adds volume, not variety.** Equipment, martial traditions and troop types have no carrier in the season loop; a faction is a view with no subfaction; bonds have a carrier (`tie` and `knot`) whose verb is declined for want of a partner operand; no event deck exists; capability is empty and its vocabulary is one vocation. Turning on the content that exists raised event volume by 7% to 19% with all four arms on (single arms: −1.2% to +11.8%) and moved the number of effective verbs by at most 1.0. (§14)

## 5. The instruments undercount contested refusals

A contested act (one whose row declares `contests:` or routes through a graded inquiry) is admitted or refused in the contest branch of `resolve`, which `continue`s before `_fold` runs. `_fold`'s first statement is the only place an act is appended to `driver.resolved`, and `driver.resolved` is what `aperture` and `corpus_run.attribute` iterate. A contested act refused at admission is therefore logged as an Event and absent from the funnel.

1,899 of 5,103 admission refusals (37.2%) belong to acts that never enter `driver.resolved`, so `corpus_run.attribute` and `aperture` do not count them: `research` 710, `examine` 319, `surveil` 230, `accept` 220, `speak` 118, `fight` 107, `argue` 100, `tell` 69, `determine` 23, `march` 3

This is the defect REM-10 and REM-11 of the v9 remediation proposal describe (their scan found 984 refusal events in the realm against 761 counted by the aperture). It is reproduced here at the current HEAD, independently, with a different instrument. Per seed, refusal Events outnumber the funnel's refused acts by about a third (2,520 against 1,892 at seed 0).

## 6. Refusals

## 7. Read failures

- **UNKNOWN verdicts**: 1,089 of 9,780 admission decisions (11.1%), listed above. What this means for the game: the engine's non-omniscience (R-07, structurally sound) has a fixed-size memory and a social graph that does not form by itself. People who know no one cannot gossip, petition-by-name, or be told anything; what they know is what they saw in the room.

## 12. Limits of this report

### 14.4 What this licenses

It licenses claims about propagation, refusal structure, formation feasibility and determinism, which do not need content. It does not license any claim about variety of approach in combat, war, economy or bonds, about narrative hooks without the player (no deck; births are ruled out by ED-WR-0011), or "no dominant option" at a seat (an upper bound only).

## 0. How to read

- **Order.** Batches run in the spine order of the head (§4). Inside a batch the order line is the orchestrator's merge of the order each member's home declares; **where a home states a different order, the home governs.**
- **Entry gate.** Every batch begins with the re-base step (rule V1): re-derive each site by symbol, re-take the batch's base reading on its base commit, compare with the figure the home cites.
- **Exit.** The instrument and reading that close the batch, and its hash expectation (rule P14). A batch's finished positions leave this file in one net-deleting commit and the run stops for a cleared window (P3).
- **Members.** One table per batch: the state index. Homes: `rem-0N-…` is `proposals/2026-10-21-v9-remediation/2026-10-21-v9-remediation-0N-<name>.md` (the home of REM items; 02 holds REM-01 to REM-32, 03 holds REM-40 to REM-52); `cp-<name>` is `proposals/2026-10-23-subsystem-completion/2026-10-23-subsystem-completion-NN-<name>.md`; a `:n-m` suffix is the line range of the item in its home as the extraction lane recorded it (line numbers drift: re-derive by symbol). The *hash* column reads E (equal / none declared), M (a declared mover), ? (read the home); *needs* lists ids the item's own gate names.

**The definition of refusal used throughout is the driver's own**, not a suffix rule: an act is REFUSED when any Event it emitted is one of its verb row's `emits_on_refusal` kinds; EXECUTED when it emitted at least one Event and none of them is a refusal kind; NULL when it emitted nothing (`loop/driver.py:489-505`; the union roster is `REFUSAL_KINDS` at `data/verbs.py:1403`, not `:1073` as the brief says). ⚠ **Two things this definition includes or omits, stated because the headline depends on them (v2):** (1) it counts a **graded loss** as refused — a kind in both `emits` and `emits_on_refusal` (`tell`'s `news.untold`, `finding.none` on `examine`/`research`) — which `corpus_run.attribute` does not (G-03); P10 reports the share. (2) the fallback kinds `act.ineligible`/`act.refused` are in no row's `emits_on_refusal`, so an act emitting only those counts EXECUTED: 6 of 681 executed acts (P12, seeds 0–1, 2 seasons), so the executed figures are overstated by under 1%.

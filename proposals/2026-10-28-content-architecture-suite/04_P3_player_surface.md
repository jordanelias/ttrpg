## 1. Ground truth (measured this session)

- **What a rung 1–2 seat can do.** Klapp (NPC-039, Cardinal of Temperance, depth 1) and the Lord Steward (Gerik Strand, NPC-009, depth 2) are each eligible for 45 of 56 rows (`aperture 2 0`): the 41 every person has, plus 4 seat rows (`issue`, `covenant`, `requisition`, `seize`). 40 of the 45 resolve. Neither can `confer`, `revoke` or `dispatch`. **For these two seats the management menu therefore holds four orders.** Which orders a seat offers depends on its remit list, not its rung: the Confessor, also a rung-1 seat, holds `issue`, `determine`, `confer`, `revoke` and `convene` (`engine/season/offices.yaml:364-369`), and `dispatch` appears in no authored domestic seat's remit but survives on loop-built seats through `remit_unruled` (`offices.yaml:141`). A console with the Koei or CK3 feel therefore needs a starting seat chosen for its remit; a Confessor is the cheapest. (An earlier analyst count, that an unseated person already has 45 eligible rows, is superseded by this measurement: 41 every person has, plus the seat's own.)

**The combat window (all three; Jordan's Fire Emblem picture, without the forecast).**
1. **Before the fight: facts only.** Shown: the opponent's visible weapon, armour and condition; the claims in the character's own ledger about him (reputation, past fights); the character's own loadout and wounds. No probability. The player learns how fights go by playing them [FROM MEMORY: Obra Dinn, Dwarf Fortress, Total War].
2. **The exchange window.** The trace plays back event by event, with callouts available today: STOP-HIT, DISENGAGE, READ WON/LOST, BIND, RIPOSTE, DISARM / THROW / PIN, YIELD, FELLED. "Technique fired" callouts (Indes, mezzo tempo) need a trace field listing the ability factors that departed from 1.0 at that beat. That edit adds no rng draw and no state change (`wrapper.py:10-16`): **NEW position, PC lane**.
3. **The result.** The gate writes wounds, standing and surrender, which appear as new claims.

B10-S ships 1 to 3 as a **viewer of auto-resolved fights**: no new resolver, no hash moved (`test_combat_trace_returned.py`). REM-31 adds intent at the six moments (measure, reopen, commit, mode, counter, contact). A loadout that changes the fight needs `Person.kit` (J-T5) and a kit field in the input.

**Army surface (all three).** Commander card (RTK): the person, seat and command; no stat is invented while `capability` is empty. Unit card (Total War): type, count, morale, discipline, upkeep, reinforcement. Without M3 (A), a unit card shows persons at weight (REM-32). Choices: raise (`levy` D), assign a commander (D; there is no force object, and the army is whoever lives at home, `loop/sides.py:122`), replenish (regrowth D), merge or disband (M), march orders (`march` B), battle plan (`marshal` D). **All are orders (O). Only contact, a general's duel, is a scene.**

**Loadout surface (all three).** Slots: main hand, off hand, armour (4 tiers). The weapon card shows physics *contributions* (leverage, agility, heft, armour defeat, reach) from `weapon_physics`/`combat_systems`. There is **no fixed weapon triangle**: matchups emerge from bind, reach and armour, shown as the character's familiarity, learned by fighting. Proficiency = tradition plus invested ability levels (`combatant.equipped`, ED-PC-0024). Choices: equip / swap (M), repair (M, no durability), buy (`exchange` D), craft (M), issue to troops (`kind_for_kit` D, needs M3). **Loadout is a console screen (O) and takes effect only inside a fight.**

## 3. Session shape (shared skeleton)
- **First hour.** Pick one of 4–6 cast persons at rung 1–2 (Klapp, the Lord Steward, a Riskbreaker, a guild leader). Read one sheet and the opening question. Play about 4 seasons.
- **A season.** CALENDAR/MATTER notices → the management pass (free) → up to the budget in scenes → the season account.
- **A campaign.** 30–50 seasons. Death re-binds the player to the heir, then to a sworn tie (M7 b). The client ends the game; the engine halts on nothing.
- **Per proposal:** A, about 10 minutes a season, mostly reading. B, one console pass, then 2–4 scenes. C, the map first; it reads best from a seat with a wide remit.

**The Klapp hook** (directive 2). The player is a person at the cathedral (for example a Riskbreaker) whose ledger receives the seeded archive claim before Klapp's does. Choices: `tell` Klapp (B), keep silent, `conceal` (B); `destroy_record` is withheld for everyone (`engine/season/data/fixtures.py:958`, `withheld_verbs`). Klapp's `research` stays his own choice. Needs the scenario-bundle ruling (part 01 §8; §11 ruling 5) and J-T2; until then it plays with no Thread payoff.

## 4. The proposals

### P3-A — Ledger and menu
**PREMISE.** A text client where the seated person's real option set *is* the interface. Every subsystem is a line on a menu followed by an account of the result. **SPECIFIES.** One card per candidate: verb, subject, operands, the row's writes at each degree, the odds band (iii) for uncontested rolled acts, and the refusal it will meet (from `requires` and the drop reason). Example: `issue → Linder · Success 41% · may refuse: issue.unauthorized` (illustrative until `band_odds` exists). Management = several seat orders typed in one turn. **RESOLVES** M12 (the account is the person's own ledger delta), M1 (the shown odds are true odds). **RULINGS:** none new. **L-1**, no forecast in combat, is Jordan's own directive this session ("we wouldn't really forecast"); it limits v10 part14:282 (the Act interface shows true odds) to acts other than combat. Record it as a GO/PC ledger row citing his words; do not ask again. **PLAN:** B10-S = REM-30 text client plus the drop-reason trace, placed after B10-10, so the seated person's attributes exist (part 01 §1). **INSTANCE CELLS:** 51 verb-card templates (agent-drafted), 0 per person. **ARCS:** about 17, the plan's count; the surface adds no mechanism. **RISKS:** spreadsheet play; 28–63 lines per question need grouping. **FALSIFIER / CONTROL:** a scripted pick equal to the chooser's reproduces `content_hash`: done here, equal. **PRECEDENT:** Disco Elysium checks (D7a T05); Old World orders (T01).

### P3-B — Management console plus minigame scenes (recommended)
**PREMISE.** The home screen is the seat's holdings: roster, appointments, stores, forces, kit, orders. The world goes on in the background, and the player enters a *scene* when the loop releases one. **SPECIFIES.** Console tabs: Person (sheet, kit), Seat (the orders the seat's remit lists: 4 for Klapp's and the Lord Steward's seats; more for a seat like the Confessor's), Holdings (what the person's own ledger says of stores and records, each with source and date; a true stores figure is a resolver value and is ruling 12), Forces (unit and commander cards, read from the person's claims; unit counts need ruling 1), Court (persons the player holds claims about). Scenes: the fight window; a hearing (docket, proofs held, the room as the player's own claims); an inquiry over a case board; a working (once a provider exists); a battle plan with a general's duel. Legibility (ii): one word read from the character's own claims about an opponent or a matter, computed person-side. This is the believed-odds mode of CP-T01-03, which the ratified amendment AM-B-03 withdrew (`workplans/valoria_master_workplan_v10_part13.md:244-248`; `_part14.md:282`); it reopens only if AQ-X1 is ruled (b), so it is **ruling 11**, not a build. The default for P3-B is the true band on request (iii). An "odds on request" setting shows (iii), like RTK's switchable suggestions (T13:46). **RESOLVES** directive 3: orders cost no scene budget (ruling 10); where another person carries an order out, that person's own budget pays, and orders that write a holding directly cost what the verb costs (part 01 §13, weak reading, AX-1 unchanged). M4 (b): the scene accepts casualties on both sides. **RULINGS:** 10 (management reading and the budget), 12 (what the console shows), 11 only if you want the believed word; 1 (M3) before unit counts mean anything. L-1 is already yours. **PLAN:** B10-S = A's client, then the console as a second view over the same option set, then REM-31. **INSTANCE CELLS:** 5 scene framings (agent-drafted); 46 cast loadouts plus the arms inventory CP-T03-03 reads (Jordan's content); 8 tuning values for troops (Jordan). **ARCS:** about 17. The 9 venue arcs become playable by a human once a hearing scene exists (`determine` executes in 3 of 43). **RISKS:** with only 4 orders, the console feels empty from Klapp's or the Steward's seat, so offer, among the starting persons, a seat whose remit includes `confer` and `revoke`, for example the Confessor. **FALSIFIER / CONTROL:** a scripted order batch equal to the seat-holder chooser's own picks reproduces `content_hash`; and each order writes exactly what its verb row's `writes:` lists, no more. **PRECEDENT:** RTK XIV area overseers (T13:47); CK3 councillors (T01); Knights of Honor II governed 100% vs ungoverned a tenth (T13:251); JA2 auto / enter / retreat (T14:22).

### P3-C — Map-centred realm client
**PREMISE.** The peninsula map is home. Panels open from places and persons, management is a context menu, and scenes are overlays. **SPECIFIES.** The map renders **only what the seated person holds claims about** (the character's View; AX-2, and Layer 1 §C.11, `04:851-862`, forbid showing resolver-side values inside a decision): holder, stores and garrison appear as belief, with source and date. Unknown provinces stay blank, and that blank is what makes `survey` worth a scene. Place menu: move (B), migrate (T), march (B), requisition (B), survey (B). Person menu: tell, petition, challenge, covenant, issue. Legibility (i) throughout, with an odds-on-request setting. **RESOLVES** WT-C gap A, but only if CP-T10-01's `route()` prices the distance; otherwise the map promises a cost the engine does not charge. **RULINGS:** **L-3 (NEW)**: is a map of believed holdings acceptable when the player's map and the truth disagree (M12 a)? **PLAN:** CP-T10-01 moves ahead of the map client. The read-only map uses the 74 existing description strings. **INSTANCE CELLS:** 31 unnamed POIs, 37 settlement vignettes (agent-drafted). **ARCS:** about 17; with `foreign` on, +4. **RISKS:** a map promises realm control that a rung-1/2 seat lacks; the most art. **FALSIFIER / CONTROL:** every map cell traces to a ledger claim (`explain`); a person with an empty ledger sees a blank map. **PRECEDENT:** CK3, Koei province screens (T13), Pentiment's location lists.

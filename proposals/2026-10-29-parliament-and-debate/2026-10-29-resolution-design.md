# Resolution design — how debate and parliament come to change the world

## Status: **PROPOSED (2026-10-29). Design only. Nothing here runs. No head, roster, verb row, arrangement row or code moves; no `ED`/`PP` is allocated; no `CURRENT.md` row moves.**
## Held back from ED-1094's merge default: **§7's calls** (including a new verb row and an assembly's power to address a writ to an executor). Merging this file ratifies none of them.

> **What it answers.** The fit adjudication (`2026-10-29-debate-fit-adjudication.md`) said why persuasion and parliament cannot yet change the game. Jordan asked for a design that resolves *all* of it, built by an agonist–antagonist method with top-down and bottom-up handshakes. This is that design, after one round of attack at each stage.

**The relay's honest conclusion, before any detail:**

1. **The architecture is right and the seeded realm cannot exercise it.** A council seat with members by `oblige`, a motion keyed on a Proposition, a counted vote, a writ the executor must answer by an act — all lawful, all mapped to existing primitives. But the bottom-up baseline (`build_realm(0)`, two seasons) shows the realm's debate chain is dead at every link: every computed `utter` is an empty Proposition (44 of 44), `commit` is refused 63 of 63, nothing is determined, and `give`, `oblige` and `comply` form zero times. The one mechanism proposed as "a full chain, end to end, in the existing realm" was not. (§2, row 1.)
2. **Three structural repairs were forced by the attack**, and all three converge on one design move: **a counted vote must be its own act, not a dice-rolled `determine`** (§3, I7).
3. **What this delivers is a proven mechanism, not yet a played feature.** Played debate that arises from the world needs a *motion source* that does not exist (§3, I1; §6, J-11). The design says so plainly and builds the mechanism so that source can plug in.

## 3. THE RESOLVED DESIGN, LINK BY LINK

Chain: **I1** world→topic · **I2** topic→room · **I3** summons · **I4** room→bench · **I5** speech→hearer · **I6** hearer→vote · **I7** vote→declaration · **I8** declaration→document · **I9** document→executor · **I10** executor act→world · **I11** world→memory · **I12** stability. Labels: **[MATCH]** contract and code agree · **[EXTEND]** code grows · **[RELAX]** contract weakened · **[JORDAN]** not mine · **[OPEN]** unsettled.

**I1 World → topic · [RELAX → M2].** *Contract:* a motion's operands come from the utterer's own claims whose provenance ends at a world Event. *Supply:* every computed `utter` is empty; the only operand-bearing Propositions are the 21 seeded goal/creed rows. *Resolution:* M1 takes a **goal row's typed fields** as the motion's operands (subject = a rung or office, value = an organisation, plus a pursuit map) — a worldly subject retrieved from the loop, though *authored at world-build*, so **AP-1 is unmet in M1 and stated so**. The tabling precondition adds **"not already satisfied"** (a goal whose object is already in the state it asks for is refused). M2 (J-11) is a *motion source*: a data roster of motion kinds, each naming a strain query (`vacancies`, `uncontrolled`, `hunger`, `at_war`, rival HOLDS), a predicate verb and an executing verb, **refused at load without both**, with `utter` binding `subject` and `value` from the question's two classed referents (a vacant seat and a candidate; an ungoverned rung and a proposed holder). Whether an existing operand form can bind a second referent is the **grammar question H-94** and is settled by a spike, not by argument. *Magnitudes* (40 vs 25 grain) stay out: named-object motions come first. *Still true after the fix:* a strain band raises a Question; it never produces the motion (T-b).

**I2 Topic → room · [EXTEND] + [RELAX].** The docket item gains **`DOCKET_MOTION`** (the Proposition id) and **`DOCKET_FILE`** (the case file) — each with a write-matrix row, by the `TERM_KEY` precedent. The *matter stays a person* (for a Rung/Office subject, its holder via `hold_force`) so `slot_for`, `sittings_before` and `may_determine` keep working. Identity is `(matter, motion)`. **Motion items fill only the slot of a Date whose holder is a council seat; ordinary items skip those slots** (the reverse also holds: no judge's case fills a council's slot). Tabling needs `remit:determine` today; a seatless person's route is `carry`, and a computed motion's tabler is a seat-holder. **[RELAX] one live motion per party** (several need a `determine` on a Proposition subject — a new typed cell). `open_case`'s decline of an arrangement whose `disposes:` is not a Tenure kind (`effects_information.py:334-337`) is lifted for the council row.

**I3 Summons and attendance · [MATCH] + [RELAX].** `convene` is a `binding_decision`, so `_ch_chronicle` already tells everyone alive `date.scheduled` (a realm-wide summons exists). `sittings_before` widens to *the bench or the sworn, non-imposed members of the Date's holder seat*, admitted by a `date.fired` claim on the firing tick **or a `date.scheduled` claim matched by Date id** that landed before `due_at`; hearsay of a *firing* is still not admitted. The sitting question carries the **motion** as a referent and rides as a **stamped standing occasion**, not tier 4 — the motion must not be permanently out-ranked by `need` (a person answers one question per round and only if it sorts first). **[RELAX]** no per-member summons writ in M1. **[OPEN]** *distance costs no vote*: `argue` needs co-presence, `commit` does not, so debate is among those at the venue and absent members vote on what they have heard. Measured by probes P2/P3.

**I4 Room → bench · [EXTEND].** *One council seat per rung, members by their own `oblige`* (AX E.2.5, ratified). A new `binds` basis **`council`** (*the joiner's act admits him; his oath brings the seat no ground*), whose rule sits at one site: `purview_reaches` skips sworn edges to a `council` seat, as it does for `imposed` — so a King who joins hands the council no purview over his subtree. The roster note requires the rule to land with the basis in `_req_oblige`; the `test_obligees.py` pin on `BINDS_BASES` moves in the same commit. Seats and members are **data** in `offices.yaml` (`obligees:` = the ladder title-holders inside the venue, CR-38's default; precedent NPC-070 on `off_npc_020`): that is how a member learns the seat exists (M-2) — by seeding at build, later members by a deposit naming the seat. `upkeep: 0` removes the purse lever (**[JORDAN] J-membership**). The chair is the seat's single `hold` and holds **`convene`, not `determine`**: he judges nothing. *Who counts* and *who declares* are **two Queries with two owners**: the count is the distinct sworn, non-imposed members of the Date's holder seat, each with a live `commit` to `DOCKET_MOTION` made after tabling; the declarant is the holder of the Date-holder seat. The King's own realm-wide `determine` is unchanged by the council (lawful; whether the council restrains him is J-β). *Dominance left:* a member who never joins or never votes is silent (§8).

**I5 Speech → hearer · [EXTEND].** `argued_pursuits` gains `toward`: the argued goal's pursuit map **signed by the sign of the arguer's own motion term**, so a won argument moves only the pursuits the motion engages — toward it if he favours it, **away if he opposes**. A non-goal Proposition keeps today's behaviour. Clamp `consequence_pursuits` and the argue step to the authored range (loops L1, L2). *Not fixed here:* everyone argues at pool 2 against 2 (no `argue` capability row; 1 of 430 persons holds any capability) — persuasive skill varies by seed, not by person; `speak` still writes nothing (H-63).

**I6 Hearer → vote · [RELAX] + [EXTEND].** The content reader is **one general function** of the motion's typed operands, the voter's own tenures and pursuits, and an odds fixture, computed inside `choose` and discarded (per decision, per person; not a field and not an aggregate). It returns a *sign* (`aim`: pursuit alignment through the goal's typed `pursuits:`; `stake`: +1 committed to the goal's organisation, −1 a holder of the object not so committed) and enters through **`stance_term`'s hook** (`stance_toward(subject)`) at a fixture gain — **no fourth `score_terms` summand, so no amendment to `04 §F2`**. It reads *only* static goal rows and the voter's own tenures (AX-2: decision code imports only `person_q`), so `seat_holders` referents, which are a world read, give zero. Per-verb polarity for `commit +1`, `release −1`, `argue/speak = sign of own term`; the chair's act, the tabler's and the executor's are scored by the same reader against the motion (they were left at zero in the join, which made the term vanish where it mattered). **A "nay" is not a separate act in M1**: a vote against is the absence of `commit` or a `release`; a defeat is the lapse (I8), not a count. The adjudication's "first defector / social proof" fit is *forgone in M1* — a deliberate loss, because no term reads others' commits and so no herding loop is built. *Risk and horizon enter only here*, but the M1 horizon test has no instance (`delay` is 0 or 1) so appetite/horizon (**J-10**) and AP-8 are **deferred to M2**; where they come from is **[JORDAN]** (none of the seven axes carries either). **The `value` operand (40 vs 25) is not read in M1**; the writ carries it (I8) so nothing is lost on the way to M2. *Disqualifier to watch:* where `stake = 0` the term reduces to the `aim` that `attention_key` already weighs — a second thumb on one scale; if the probe shows this, `aim` is dropped from the vote and left to attention.

**I7 Vote → declaration · [EXTEND] — the central repair.** *A counted vote is its own act.* A **new verb row** (working name `enact`; the name is not ruled), declaring **no contest**, eligible by `remit:convene`, with one conjunct: *the docket item's Date holder is the acting seat* (`via`). Its writes are flat — `Record.exists` (the writ, I8) and clearing the item's matter — resolved at the full degree, **consuming no random draw**. Its precondition counts per I4 against the row's `quorum` (swept {1, 2, 3}); a below-count attempt is a precondition refusal that writes nothing and emits `enact.refused`. This one row retires, together: the leak of the chair onto every realm bench, the dice on a carried count (B-10: a motion that carried must not lose on the declarant's roll), the receipt convention (one `determine` row cannot carry two receipts), and the branch on `disposal` that would have been undeclared special-casing. `parliamentary_debate` becomes `disposal: declared`. **Cost and its N-line:** a new verb row, against the proceedings design's "zero new verbs" bar (`README`, `F.1`), TD invariant 20, and `04`'s verb-count discipline. *Without it:* either the chair judges everything, or a counted vote rolls dice — each fails a ratified rule. This is **[JORDAN] J-verb**; the alternative (an arm inside `determine`) was attacked and rejected by two critics. `enact` also fills the Record-disposal site that CR-36 plans, so CR-36's `resolution` kind is unnecessary here (a writ is distinguished by its terms, K-11/K-54).

**I8 Declaration → document · [EXTEND], with one [OPEN].** A carried count mints a **`dispensation`** (no new record kind) with `terms = {subject, value}` — *the value travels with the writ* — and `to = [the holder of the subject]`, held by the declarant (ED-IN-0292 (1): nothing is minted into the addressee's hand). **Lapse:** an item is live while its `DOCKET_FILE`'s declared stages have not all matured **and** the file's holder is alive; the tabling act sets the term **explicitly** (swept {2, 3} seasons, not the default `t+1` first-stage maturation), and one helper `live_item` is read by `docketed`, `open_slots` and `sittings_before`. The existing `term.matured` emission (`matter.py:351-354`) is real but is heard through `document_key` **by the file's holder, not by the room**, and never fires if the winder is dead. **[OPEN: D-lapse]** the contract (TD AP-5a) is that *a motion that ends unmet leaves an event the venue can witness, carrying the state of the count*. Two routes: (a) a lapse that CALENDAR processes on the fired Date it already owns, emitting `docket.lapsed` with the venue as subject (needs no winder, is witnessed at the venue, reads as a declared handle since the convener wound the Date — but it is a rule about when a sitting closes); (b) `term.matured` with a venue-reaching channel. (a) is preferred; the choice is an architecture reading of **T-c**, to be confirmed. *A defeat therefore leaves a lapse event, not a recorded division* (no stored record: invariant T-a).

**I9 Document → executor · [EXTEND] + [JORDAN].** The writ stamps on the chair as a `Record`-classed occasion (`writs_to_deliver`, beside `writs_held`); a roster entry `held_sourced: {give: [dispensation]}` lets `give` form on it, so the writ reaches the holder by an act that exists; the executor's performing act is `give` of the rung, its `to` **sourced from the writ's value** (not a fan over everyone known); his answer is `comply` + that act, or `evade / defy`, both witnessed. **Liveness:** a writ carries a term; `writs_held` drops a matured writ; the performing `give` consumes it; an unperformed writ matures with an emission heard by the issuing seat's obligees (the council's members; the `post_remit` channel's reach for `compliance.withheld` is unverified). A `comply` alone writes nothing and does *not* close the writ, so a sham `comply` is harmless but not free. `evade / defy` is re-typed (IN-09's form) **only in the commit that supplies its occasion**, and that step does not merge until a real-chooser run shows executions > 0 (it was reverted once for exactly this). **[JORDAN] J-β:** this *is* an assembly addressing a writ to an executor, built as a disposal effect so the council seat needs no `remit:issue`. It answers goal (2) most strongly and transfers coercive-adjacent power to a body (H-90 says the ruler has no monopoly; the adjudication judged negative concurrence — a ruler's own act admitted only on a held resolution — the lawful baseline and obligation a misfit). The honest weakness the attack found: *no M1 motion can check the King* (both throne goals have stance +1 and `give` cannot move a seat), so the writ's only live effect would land on a duke-tier holder — the inverse of H-90's purpose. A parliament that can check the ruler needs the `confer`/`revoke` arms (CR-36, CR-37) and the motion source (J-11).

**I10 Executor act → world · [MATCH].** The world-changing acts all exist — `give`+Rung (`rung.ceded`), `seize` (licensed by `warrant_sourced`), `confer`, `revoke`, `transfer`, `requisition`. No new gate basis, no assembly write. Enforcement is persons: `compliance.withheld` is the occasion for a council member whose seat grants `issue` to `issue` a warrant, and `seize` forms by `warrant_sourced` unchanged (`_seize_rung` licenses on occupation and muster, so enforcement is a muster of persons). **[JORDAN] J-γ** (a resolution licensing a *third* person's `seize`: legal expropriation by vote). T3a-style consent conjuncts for `confer`/`revoke` remain CR-36's and CR-37's.

**I11 World → witness → memory · [MATCH].** No stored tally or division record. `utter`/`commit`/`argue`/`open_case` are heard by those present or by obligees; `convene`, `enact`'s writ and `issue` are public. A vow-break (`release` ⇒ `commitment.ended`) is already priced.

**I12 Stability and the player · [EXTEND] + [OPEN].** Loops: L1 (judge self-reinforcement) and L2 (convergence) bounded by the clamps and the signed argue; L3 (herding) not built (no social-proof term); L4 (always agrees) and L5 (patron capture) **measured (P6), not prevented**; L6 (runaway) bounded because a re-tabling is a new Proposition with no inherited commits and each sitting costs the convener a Date. Scarcity: one slot per fired Date, one live motion per slot, `opportunity_key` bars a repeat. The human player's menu is the same Candidate list on the sitting question (`commit`/`release` the motion, `argue` a present member, `speak`, `open_case`/`carry`, `give`/`comply`/`evade / defy` a held writ). **R-CHOICE dominance remains and is not fixed here:** the proposer does nothing if the build commits already carry; the opposer, the swing member and the chair abstain at no cost; the holder `comply`s and never `give`s. Counting only post-tabling commits removes the first; the lapse event makes the others *visible* but does not make them cost. Whether abstention should cost (renewal by attending, TD's pick) is **J-membership**.

## 8. THE WEAKEST POINTS OF THIS DESIGN

1. **M1 does not make the realm debate.** It makes a lawful mechanism that hand-built worlds can exercise and the corpus can measure. Played debate waits on J-11 and on the H-94 spike, which neither designer could argue away.
2. **`enact` is a new verb row.** The argument for it is that three critics independently found every alternative leaks or rolls dice. It is still a new verb, against a bar the design has held to.
3. **The lapse (D-lapse) is not settled.** The contract that a defeat leaves a venue-witnessed event rests on an unconfirmed routing.
4. **The signed-stake reader is crude.** It sees +1 / −1 / 0 over a goal row's object, not a quantity; where `stake = 0` it may only duplicate salience.
5. **Dominance by silence is reduced, not removed.** The attack's R-CHOICE finding (proposer, opposer, swing member, chair, holder all dominated by doing nothing) is made *visible* by the lapse event, not made *costly*.
6. **A parliament that cannot check the ruler.** In M1 the only live writ lands on a duke-tier holder, which inverts H-90's purpose. Checking the ruler needs the CR-36/CR-37 arms, which this design leaves to the plan.
7. **Nothing here was run** beyond the bottom-up designer's baseline. Every hash, reach and formation claim is a prediction until its probe.

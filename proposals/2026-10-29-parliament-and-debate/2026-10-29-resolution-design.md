Rules used to settle each conflict: a ratified law is never relaxed to suit code (`CLAUDE.md` §0.05); a contract the code cannot meet is split into a milestone and the gap named; the smaller edit wins unless it fails a falsifier; anything that changes who holds power or overwrites a ratified ruling is Jordan's; a reader never lands without its producer in the same commit.

**The honest conclusion, before any detail:**

1. **The architecture holds; the seeded realm cannot exercise it.** The bottom-up baseline (`build_realm(0)`, two seasons, taken *before* REM-28) found every computed `utter` an empty Proposition (44 of 44), `commit` refused 63 of 63, nothing determined, and `give`, `oblige` and `comply` forming zero times; §2A re-measures on current `main`: sittings are now plentiful but empty, and no `commit` or `argue` forms. What this design delivers is a **proven mechanism**, not a played feature; played debate from the world needs a *motion source* that does not exist (RJ-11).
2. **A counted vote needs no declaring act.** Both blind passes, independently, found that the earlier design's new verb (`enact`) re-proposed exactly what `21_RECONCILIATION.md` C-7 / ED-SC-0034 rejected ("a `declare` verb, or a stored tally") and that it was a false N-line: a **licence on the owner's own act** does everything it did without a declaring act. Whether it also checks a *ruler* depends on a consent row on an act a ruler performs against another person's holding, and neither starting row does that today (I9, RJ-consent). Jordan ruled for the licence. §3 I7–I9 are rebuilt on it.
3. **Compulsion is gone, deliberately.** A parliament in this design is a **consent body**: it licenses and blocks; it does not order. That fits H-90, canon's no-confidence enforcement, and the history (Cortes, Joyeuse Entrée, the Crown "granting" what parliament petitioned), and it removes three unproven links (`give` of a held writ, `evade / defy`, the writ itself). The cost is stated in §6.

ED-SC-0034 (C-7) accepted only a two-entry cardinality widening (`21_RECONCILIATION.md:317-320`); the **filtered-cardinality form** needed here is the one that file says needs an eighth form and is "refused by default … argued as a design change, not smuggled" (`:684-691`). ## 3. THE RESOLVED DESIGN, LINK BY LINK

Chain: **I1** world→topic · **I2** topic→room · **I3** summons · **I4** room→members · **I5** speech→hearer · **I6** hearer→vote · **I7** the count · **I8** count→licence · **I9** licence→owner's act · **I10** act→world · **I11** world→memory · **I12** stability. Labels: **[MATCH]** · **[EXTEND]** code grows · **[RELAX]** contract weakened · **[JORDAN]** · **[OPEN]**.

**I1 World → topic · [RELAX → RM2].** *Contract:* a motion's operands come from the utterer's own claims, whose provenance ends at a world Event. *Supply:* every computed `utter` is empty; the only operand-bearing Propositions are the 21 seeded goal/creed rows. *Resolution:* milestone 1 (**RM1**, the mechanism slice RJ-0a to RJ-9) takes a **goal row's typed fields** as the operands (subject a rung or office, value an organisation, plus a pursuit map). **The motion's Proposition is that seeded goal's own id, not a fresh one**, so it carries the goal's standing commits; the post-tabling rule (I7) is what separates those from support given after tabling. That is a worldly subject retrieved from the loop but *authored at world-build*, so **AP-1 is unmet in RM1** and stated so. Tabling refuses a motion **already satisfied**. The seeded realm has two rung-goals: T13, Varfell's own (vacuous), and T15, the one territory no faction holds (`goals.yaml:337-346`), which is the only executable one. RM2 (RJ-11) is a **motion source**: a data roster of motion kinds, each naming a strain query, a predicate verb and an executing verb, refused at load without both, with `utter` binding `subject` and `to` from the question's two classed referents (a vacant seat and a candidate; an ungoverned place and a proposed holder). A motion binds `subject`, `to` and later `amount`; no new operand name (§7, H-94). Magnitudes (40 vs 25 grain) stay out; named-object motions come first. A strain band raises a Question; it never produces the motion (T-b).

**I2 Topic → room · [EXTEND] + [RELAX].** A **motion is a petition Record naming a Proposition**, tabled by **`carry`** into the nearest open slot of a Date convened at a council's rung (any `convene`-holder there may call it; the council seat itself is unheld). The docket item is **person-less**: `{date, matter: None, motion: <Proposition id>, file: <the petition Record>}`, with `DOCKET_MOTION` and `DOCKET_FILE` each given a write-matrix row (the `TERM_KEY` precedent). Consequences, all removals of earlier problems: no `determine` party conjunct; no `hold_force` substitution; no coupling to where a holder stands; no shielding of a holder from trial; `docketed(person)` ignores motion items; **the decline of a second motion is keyed on the Proposition's subject**. A motion item fills a slot only of a Date convened at a council's rung. `open_slots` treats any item with `matter is None` as an empty slot (`world_q.py:260`) and `slot.update` would overwrite it (`effects_information.py:349`), so both exclude motion items; ordinary items are **not** barred from such a Date's other slots (a realm- or duchy-tier Date could then never hold a trial), and the guarantee is only that a motion and a case never share one slot. `carry` today always appends a dateless item (B-4) and declines a matter already docketed (`:405`); routing it through `slot_for` for Dates at a council's rung is the one change to an existing act. Neither `carry` nor `open_case` back-fills a matter that already waits (`open_case` fills a slot only at creation, `:347-351`): that is a separate defect, in `2026-10-29-reasons-to-convene.md`. **[RELAX]** several live motions about one subject (needs a typed Proposition-subject cell) are out.

**I3 Summons and attendance · [MATCH-with-EXTEND] + [RELAX].** *Contract:* some channel that forms in computed play reaches every member. *Measured (§2A, current `main`):* `convene` is a `binding_decision`, so `_ch_chronicle` carries `date.scheduled` to about 85% of persons; first-hand hearing of a firing reaches only 6-11. The chronicle is built on the echo model R7 refused and is scheduled for deletion, so the design does not rest on it: a member *who did not hear* is simply not asked. The binding problem here is not reach but **meaning**: nearly every sitting is empty (`2026-10-29-reasons-to-convene.md`). The contract stays channel-agnostic so the `<rung kind>` reach channel slots in when it exists. `sittings_before` widens to *the bench or the sworn, non-imposed members of the council seat at the Date's rung*, admitted by a `date.fired` claim on the firing tick **or a `date.scheduled` claim matched by Date id** landed before `due_at` (the claim's subject is the Date or the convener, never the venue); hearsay of a firing is still refused. The sitting question carries the motion as a referent and rides as a **stamped standing occasion** (a person answers one question per round and only if it sorts first; tier 4 below `need` means a person with any ambition never sees it). **[RELAX]** no per-member summons writ. **[OPEN]** distance costs no vote (`argue` needs co-presence; `commit` does not). **Not fixed, and a real failure mode of real assemblies:** a ruler (or any `convene`-holder at that rung) who never convenes produces no sitting and no event. The history calls this the Personal Rule; it is a story, and the pressure against it must come from outside the room (grievance, petition, supply), not from a rule.

**I4 Room → members · [EXTEND].** *One council seat per rung, members by their own `oblige`* (AX E.2.5, for its **shape** — one seat, many holders, membership a Query; that axiom's semantics are delegation, "govern in his stead", and this design does not claim it settles what a parliament is). A new `binds` basis **`council`** (*the joiner's act admits him; his oath brings the seat no ground*), with its rule at **all three sites** that read sworn edges — `purview_reaches`, `guard_weight`, `admits_audience` limb 3 — beside the `imposed` skip. The rule lands with the basis in `_req_oblige`; the `test_obligees.py` pin on `BINDS_BASES` moves in the same commit. The seat is **data** in `offices.yaml` with `obligees:` = the ladder title-holders inside the venue (CR-38's default; precedent NPC-070 on `off_npc_020`), which is how a member learns the seat exists (M-2). The seat grants **neither `determine` nor `convene`** and is **unheld**, so it judges nothing and convenes nothing: there is **no chair and no declarant**; a sitting is called by any `convene`-holder at the council's rung. **An unheld seat is not buildable today:** `populated.py:1436` and `:1483` read `row['holder']` unconditionally and `:1427-1428` calls a holderless seat "a ghost", so RJ-2 changes both. And it routes no act: the obligee channel needs `Act.via` to be the seat (`epistemic.py:570-574`), so members learn of the seat by seeding and of a motion by presence at the sitting or by the sitting question (I3), never through the seat. The count (I7) is the only thing the room produces. **The joiner's term is a decision (RJ-membership):** a joiner's `oblige` otherwise gets `Term(tick+4)` renewed only by the seat-holder's `transfer` (an unheld seat has no purse, which is why the default below is no term), while seeded obligees get none; the default here is that a council join carries no term, as a data value. *Membership churns in computed play* (§2A: 31-48 `duty.taken` per four seasons). Before REM-28 it **bandwagoned onto one seat** (33-38 persons sworn to a single seat per seed); that concentration is **not re-measured** on current `main`. It is loop **LP7** (below), measured not prevented; whether a duke ever learns a *new* council seat is the second half of probe P3 and is still unmeasured. The verb allows an unheld seat (`_req_oblige` requires no holder); the seat builder does not yet (RJ-2).

**I5 Speech → hearer · [EXTEND].** `argued_pursuits` gains `toward`: the argued goal's pursuit map **signed by the sign of the arguer's own motion term**, so a won argument moves only the pursuits the motion engages — toward it if he favours it, **away if he opposes**. A non-goal Proposition keeps today's behaviour. Clamp `consequence_pursuits` and the argue step to the authored range (loops LP1, LP2). *Not fixed:* everyone argues at pool 2 against 2 (no `argue` capability row; 1 of 430 persons holds any capability), so persuasive skill varies by seed, not by person; `speak` writes nothing (H-63). *Caution from the pessimist:* re-weighting values from one motion homogenises the council; the clamp and the signed step bound it, and probe P6 measures pursuit variance.

**I6 Hearer → vote · [RELAX] + [EXTEND].** The content reader is **one general function** of the motion's typed operands, the voter's own tenures and pursuits, and an odds fixture, computed inside `choose` and discarded: a **sign** — `aim` (pursuit alignment through the goal's typed `pursuits:`) and `stake` (+1 committed to the goal's organisation, −1 a holder of the object not so committed). It reads only static goal rows and the voter's own tenures (AX-2: decision code imports only `person_q`), so `seat_holders` referents give zero. **Placement is a Layer-1 question, stated openly (RJ-layer1):** a fourth `score_terms` summand amends `architecture/ARCHITECTURE_V2.md` §F2 (D21); carrying it inside `stance_term`'s arms (gain `1+g`) avoids the amendment but makes "stance" mean two quantities, which the NERS pass called concealment. Per-verb polarity: `commit +1`, `release −1`, `argue`/`speak` = the sign of the arguer's own term. A vote *against* is the absence of `commit` or a `release`; there is no separate "nay" act in the first slice (a contrary motion needs RM2 operands). Social proof and the first defector are *forgone* (no term reads others' commits). Risk and horizon enter **only here**, but the first slice has no instance (`delay` is 0 or 1, so `h ≡ 1`), so **appetite and horizon (RJ-10) and AP-8 are deferred to RM2**; where they come from is deferred to CR-17 (ED-IN-0304; none of the seven axes carries either). *Disqualifier to watch:* where `stake = 0` the term reduces to the `aim` that `attention_key` already weighs; probe P6 checks and, if so, `aim` is dropped from the vote.

**I7 The count · [EXTEND] — one owner, one consumer.** The count is a **Query computed when read, stored nowhere**: the number of distinct **sworn, non-imposed members of the council seat** holding a live `commit` to `DOCKET_MOTION`, **in a later season than the season the motion was tabled in** (the one counting rule; the "post-tabling rule"), **as a share of the sworn membership** (fixture `council_share`, named apart from CR-34's absolute `quorum`; the loader test's own `quorum` fixture is 0.5, which is a loader fixture and no precedent for the value; swept {0.5, 0.6, 2/3}). An absent or abstaining member is not an aye, so a silent majority is not outvoted by an allied minority. It is the **single owner** of "how many support this" for a council and has **one consumer**, the licence conjunct below. It is its own Query, not CR-34's: CR-34's quorum counts distinct members of the bench (`judging_set`) holding a live `commit` to the party's OUGHT, with no time filter and an absolute threshold (`workplans/valoria_master_workplan_v10_part4.md:355`, `quorum: 2`, swept {1,2,3}); it stays CR-34's and is not routed through this Query. Nothing declares, nothing rolls, nothing is minted, **no verb is added** — C-7's "the declaring act is `determine`" is untouched because for a licence **no declaring act exists**. The rule's weaknesses are stated, not hidden: re-committing is free for loyalists, so it filters nothing for them, and it discards in-season debate. A by-round count is **not buildable** (a commit is a Tenure; `round` may go on a Claim alone; counting by round would read witnesses' ledgers, which I8 forbids). **The cardinality conjunct is not an existing idiom** (§2A: `confer` tests only that the holder has *a* commit), and it is **not** the widening ED-SC-0034 accepted: it restricts the count by a seat Query, the filtered-cardinality form `21_RECONCILIATION.md:684-691` refuses by default (§2A). It is argued here as one design change, taken once, and taken provisionally (§7, RJ-cardinality).

**I8 Count → licence · [EXTEND].** The **data owner of consent** is a roster row: *(executing verb, object kind, council seat, threshold)*, refused at load if the verb or the seat is unknown — the answer to "where does the consent discriminator live" (B-11) that an effect body would be scripting drift. A row says *this verb on this kind of object is admitted only while the count for a motion naming this object clears the threshold, and the motion's operands match the act* (motion subject = the act's object; motion `to` = the act's `to`), so a motion to keep a holding never licenses giving it away. Candidate rows: a holder's `give` of a rung that is his last territory (a new consent row; the prior design's last-territory protection, `parliamentary_transfer_v30.md:52`, blocked a parliament stripping a faction's last territory and runs the other way), a levy. No seeded motion reaches that row: the one executable goal motion names T15, which no faction holds (I1). **`_eff_give` declines unless the actor holds the rung** (`effects_information.py:840-842`), so that row stops a holder ceding his own land and checks no ruler. The Holy See's `revoke` of the throne (canon: the Parliamentary vote plus the Holy See) cannot form (`off_king` has `revocation: null`, `offices.yaml:189`) and CR-37 already plans that act behind a held resolution plus `joint_concurrence` (`workplans/valoria_master_workplan_v10_part4.md:394-396`); it is CR-37's, not a row here. `confer` of a nominated seat (CR-36) stays CR-36's held resolution, which is minted by `determine` under `parliamentary_debate`: **this design therefore keeps two parliaments** (the interim King/Regent bench mints CR-36's nominations and CR-37's deposition motion, both `determine` under `parliamentary_debate`; the council count gates only the consent rows, today one), kept apart for now (§7, RJ-parliaments). The conjunct reads **tenures** (commits), a world read CR-34's quorum conjunct also makes — not another person's ledger (F8). **A lapse:** a motion whose declared term matures without a licensed act lapses and is no longer live (I2); a commit made in the season after tabling counts before that lapse (RJ-3 declares a term long enough); the lapse event carries the count as it stood, and a lapsed motion licenses nothing; a re-tabling starts a new count (the post-tabling rule).

**I9 Licence → the owner's act · [MATCH] + [RELAX].** The owner's ordinary act (`confer`, `revoke`, `give`, `requisition`, `transfer`) carries the motion as a **typed operand chosen person-side** (the `terms_sourced` precedent); the conjunct refuses it without the count, emitting the verb's own refusal. **The history agrees with a licence:** parliament petitions and the Crown grants; a ruler who ignores it is unmoved, but cannot do the licensed thing without it. A licence checks the *owner's* act; it checks a *ruler* only where the licensed act is one a ruler performs against another person's holding, and today no row is (I8). **What this does not do, and the design does not pretend otherwise:** it cannot compel. A carried motion creates **no occasion** for the owner — he acts only if he wants to — and *announces nothing* until a lapse event or his act. Two consequences are accepted: (a) the strongest answer to "the outcome changes the world" is, in this design, *consent withheld or granted plus opinion*; (b) a motion that carried and was never acted on is visible only through the lapse event (I8's state-of-count claim) and through members' witnessed commits.

**I10 Act → world · [MATCH].** The world-changing acts all exist (`give`+Rung = `rung.ceded`; `confer`; `revoke`; `seize` licensed by `warrant_sourced`; `transfer`; `requisition`); no new gate basis, no assembly write. **Where a change happens that the owner did not choose**, it is **persons' own acts**: members committed to an OUGHT hold an ambition and may act on it with their own means — the commits *are* a faction (T-h) — so a parliament breeds coalitions, rivalries and, by the existing `issue` → `seize` machinery, enforcement by persons who hold the power to muster.

**I11 World → memory · [MATCH].** No stored tally or division record. `utter`/`commit`/`argue`/`carry` are heard by those present; obligees hear them only through an act whose `via` is the seat, and an unheld seat routes none (I4); `convene` and the lapse are public. A vow-break (`release` ⇒ `commitment.ended`) is already priced.

**I12 Stability and the player · [EXTEND] + [OPEN].** Loops: LP1 and LP2 bounded by the clamps and the signed argue; LP3 (herding) not built; LP4 (always agrees) and LP5 (capture by the convener, by silence and by the joiner's term) **measured (P6), not prevented**; LP6 (runaway) bounded because the post-tabling rule (I7) counts nothing made before a tabling, so a re-tabling starts a new count; **LP7 (bandwagon: persons swear to the seat everyone already swore to) measured, not prevented**; 33-38 oaths on one seat were seen before REM-28 and are not re-measured. Scarcity: one slot per fired Date, one live motion per subject, `opportunity_key` bars a repeat. The human player's menu is the same Candidate list on the sitting question (`commit`/`release`, `argue` a present member, `speak`, `carry`, the owner's licensed act). **R-CHOICE dominance remains** (§4).

## 4. R-CHOICE AND WHAT A PLAYER SEES

**The dominant act at each seat is silence, and the design reduces it without removing it.** Proposer: not tabling (no seeded motion gains him anything). Opposer: abstain — free; the share-of-house threshold turns abstention into a quiet "no". Swing member: abstain or commit nearly free. Convener: never convene — no Date, no lapse event, nothing to answer for (the Personal Rule). Owner: ignore the council. Whether abstention should cost (renewal by attending) is **RJ-membership**.

**What a player sees at a sitting:** the motion (the petition naming the Proposition), the commits he witnessed, the arguments he heard, and the arithmetic of his own holdings through `explain()`. **What he can say to a friend:** "it carried because enough of the council committed in a season after it was tabled, so the King was allowed to do it." **What he cannot predict:** why an ally's earlier vow did not count (the post-tabling rule); whether a defeat will be announced (it depends on someone convening and on the lapse's reach). *Verdict (NERS antagonist): a partial pass on legibility.*

## 8. THE WEAKEST POINTS OF THIS DESIGN

1. **The licence cannot compel, and in this slice it does not yet check a ruler.** If Jordan's goal (2) is read as "the parliament orders", this is the wrong design; the earlier writ route did that and was cut for failing N, for needing three never-observed links, and for overturning C-7. Checking a ruler waits on a consent row for an act a ruler performs against another's holding (CR-37).
2. **The slice does not make the realm debate.** It makes a lawful mechanism that hand-built worlds exercise and the corpus can measure. Played debate waits on RJ-11.
3. **The first-slice vote is crude.** It sees a sign over a goal row's object, not a quantity, and where `stake = 0` it may only duplicate salience. Free re-commits mean the post-tabling rule filters nothing for loyalists.
4. **Dominance by silence is reduced, not removed.** The lapse event makes some of it visible; the convener or ruler who never convenes leaves nothing.
5. **Membership may bandwagon.** Joining is live; concentration on one seat was seen before REM-28 and is not re-measured.
6. **Sittings have no matter.** Sittings are plentiful (21-23 a run) and reach ~85% of persons, but 11-17 are empty and 0-1 of 4-9 waiting matters is placed in a slot: `convene` forms on places-to-settle, not on a tier where a matter waits (`2026-10-29-reasons-to-convene.md`). The design's licence adds *demand*; it does not fix *meaning*.
7. **The design itself was never run.** Only §2A's seeded-engine readings were taken (harness outside the tree, so not reproducible from it). Every hash, reach and formation claim about the *design* is a prediction until its probe, and the two blind reviewers ran nothing.
8. **Alternatives this design does not compare, each cheaper on one axis (an altitude review, raised and not taken).** (a) *Motion as a docket matter:* `carry` already builds `{matter: <the petition's about-key>, petition: pid}` through `_docket_item` (`effects_information.py:162-168`, `:403-410`), so a petition whose terms name a Proposition gives `matter = <Proposition id>` with no new key; the gate `_is_person_id` at `world_q.py:276` and `:312` becomes a matter-kind test, and RJ-4's lapse writer is the slot-emptier. That would replace I2's person-less item, `DOCKET_FILE` (which duplicates `DOCKET_PETITION`, `world_q.py:227`) and the three exclusion patches. (b) *A held seat with an empty remit* (the tree has seats whose only remit is the unruled `dispatch`, `offices.yaml:107-114`) instead of an unheld seat: no `populated.py` change and a working obligee channel, at the cost of the seat's `vacancies()` behaviour when its holder dies. (c) *The CR-38 bench with CR-34's tally gating `determine`* (CR-36 and CR-37's held-resolution route), which would remove the two-parliaments split at the price of delivery by `give` (H-229). (d) *The `council` skip placed in `sworn_to`'s common source* rather than at three sites (`gate.py:423`, `world_q.py:1408`); `admits_audience` limb 3 (`world_q.py:1467`) scans `w.tenures` directly and would still need its own skip, and a skip inside `sworn_to` would also drop council members from `establishment_of`, which I7 needs. None was costed against this design's route.

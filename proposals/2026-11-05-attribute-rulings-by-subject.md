## 2. Strength

- **AR-10** `RULED` *"strength will involve the amount of damage a weapon can do (mitigated by the type of weapon -- a blunt weapon with a big head will be most positively affected but a very skinny piercing blade can slide in with little effort so strength doesn't impact as much), the ability to manage weapon heft, the ability to wear armour, the ability to leverage weapons, to push and force the opponent"* (F25)
- **AR-11** `RULED` *"strength also contributes to someone's health/wounds as increasing strength points towards more muscle mass, and that is relevant to health/vitality/constitution"* (F26)
- **AR-12** `RULED` *"strength will also impact how fluently you can wield weapons as per managing heft as well as weight itself"* (F25 item 2)
- **AR-13** `RULED` *"also, strength will impact the ability to stop momentum whether it be own weapon or other because that would involve counterbalancing the heft"* (F25 item 6)
- **AR-14** `RULED` *"weapons will have mass"* (F25)

## 3. Coordination

- **AR-15** `RULED` *"coordination concerns accuracy with weapons and stable footing and dodging without losing balance and precise manoeuvres"*
- **AR-16** `RULED` *"it'll also concern things like the quality of a forgery or ability to pick a lock"* (F20)
- **AR-17** `HEDGED` *"weapon accuracy as per the likelihood of hitting your target? that is supposed to be a thing. it'll be informed by skill and traditions, i am guessing, but coordination should also have a role in maintaining balance of weapon"* (F20)
- **AR-18** `RULED` *"the defender's read of the attack is also impacted by the suddenness and dexterity of the attack, which is a function of coordination"* (F20)
- **AR-19** `RULED` *"and even if you read the attack with perception, you need focus to be in the moment and coordination to physically respond properly"* (F22)

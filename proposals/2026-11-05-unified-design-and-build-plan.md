## PART 1. SYNTHESIS: the game Jordan has described

**The powers.** Four FACTIONS contend: **Valorsmark, Hafenmark, Varfell and the Church** (ED-IN-0339, ED-IN-0342). Valorsmark is the King's own nation and faction (canon's first of the three indigenous nations, `canon/03_canonical_timeline.md:76` `[UNVERIFIED]`); the King holds the Valorsmark duchy and other holdings and has VASSALS, the Duke of Varfell and the Duchess of Hafenmark; the Church owns its own territory and settlement (ED-IN-0342). The King is sovereign over the realm; each power has its own land.

**The arc.** Stakes RATCHET among the four (ED-IN-0339) until diplomacy and parliament cannot govern; then a civil war breaks out or a casus belli is formed; then factions war with one another **while parliament still sits**, because they dispute territory, influence and holdings but still want the nation to function. The arc is NOT scripted: it must be possible under the world's own rules and so strongly conditioned that it is ALMOST INEVITABLE (ED-IN-0341). The ratchet is carried by STORED CLOCKS AND COUNTERS that belong to the game, among them **Church Influence**, whose rise brings a theocratic attempted seizure after which the Church begins taking land (ED-IN-0343). Clocks are not contrary to emergence (ED-IN-0344): they trigger behaviours from an **accumulation of game-world events** (ED-IN-0345).

**Persons in the arc.** Holding an office binds the holder to a DUTY to the office's and faction's tenets and goals; a major betrayal (attacking a faction member, let alone the head) needs a significant series of events, opinions and circumstances (ED-IN-0335). A faction's creed, goals and duties condition its officers as a strife flow conditions everyone (ED-IN-0338). Fighting is EXCEPTIONAL: it belongs to civil war, criminal contexts and duels, is rare in civil society, is not simply chosen, and almost every pursuit-conviction profile finds it unwanted (ED-IN-0337, ED-IN-0340). A duel is typically to first wound (ED-IN-0336; RS-25 "a duel doesn't have to be lethal").

**What persons know.** Persons know one another by faction and by high status (ED-IN-0318); they remember for years (ED-IN-0319); a claim goes stale while the opinion of the deed stays (ED-IN-0320); one hearing gives less certainty, competing hearings are settled by trust or judgment (ED-IN-0321); only distinct origins corroborate (ED-IN-0322); belief weighs trust, count and fit with what one knows of the subject (ED-IN-0323); familiarity lets one intuit another's pursuits and obligations and, with context, adjudicate (ED-IN-0324, ED-IN-0325); Attunement judges persons and truthfulness, Reason judges offices and regimes (ED-IN-0326, ED-IN-0327); a person can know OF another by rung, allegiance and overlapping activities (ED-IN-0334).

**Persons' make.** Ten capability attributes and risk tolerance (ED-IN-0328); histories become bundles of learned skills that give life-path hooks (ED-IN-0330, ED-IN-0331); `lie` sits beside forgery (ED-IN-0329). The year is four seasons (ED-IN-0332).

**RULED versus HEDGED.** HEDGED, medium: ED-IN-0328 (the ten, "I think"; already folded as CR-84 with its own reverts) and ED-IN-0336 (duel-only, "I think … may need"). ED-IN-0337 and ED-IN-0340 are RULED and already decide most of what 0336 hedged: this design builds the ruled form (contexts: civil war, crime, duel) and leaves 0336's narrower "duels only" as the revert. Everything else above is RULED as intent; no row rules a mechanism, a value or a clock list (ED-IN-0343: "The mechanism, the values and the clock list are NOT ruled").

### J-E2-01 · May a march be refused for want of a claim to the territory, or is a claim only context? · FA, MB

**What the game does today.** A faction's person can form and fold a `march` onto a territory with no gate on why. Workplan v11 (CR-91 (3), with IN-36's interim cap) would add a gate: a march is admitted only if the marcher, his faction or his office holds a live claim on the target, or his faction is at civil war with the target's; the cap of one march per season into each territory stays until then.

**What you ruled.** ED-IN-0317 (2026-11-04): *"march can only happen if there is a casus belli ie claim to a territory...unless civil war breaks out. anyway, for testing purposes, once a season per territory cap."* And later (A-58, 2026-11-12): *"Usually there will be a casus belli or some other act, but generally, sending your military into another's territory is a pretty clear sign of aggression"*, which the suite reads as: a casus belli *"is context that bears on legitimacy and regard, not a precondition. No declaration field is required"*. A-68 adds that an unopposed entry onto held land is tolerated but disliked when parliament is functional and parties are at peace, which cannot happen if the march is refused first. A-74 lets a march be thought of on a claim or grievance.

**The conflict.** A gate on `march` for want of a claim (CR-91 (3)) refuses exactly the entries A-58 and A-68 describe. The suite's mass-battle and verbs proposals already keep `march` ungated (`26_MASS_BATTLE_aggression_and_land.md` §5: "a played or hand-built march on a neighbour needs only a Rung"), and `30_WR_A_the_era_is_a_named_period.md` line 65 still writes "a foreign march needs a claim (ED-IN-0317)".

**Option A.** Keep ED-IN-0317 as written: the gate is built (CR-91 (3)); a march without a claim or a civil war is refused; A-68's tolerated entry is then reachable only onto land the marcher may already claim, and A-58's "aggression is read from the act" applies to marches that pass the gate. Costs: the marcher with no claim cannot test the realm's patience; a hash mover (`march` admits fewer); `26_MASS_BATTLE_aggression_and_land.md` §5 and A-68's draw need a sentence each.

**Option B.** Read ED-IN-0317 as the *occasion* a person thinks of marching (a claim, a grievance or a known enemy, A-74) and not as an admission gate: no `requires_typed` conjunct; the claim carrier (CR-91 (1), (2), (4), (5)) is built as the occasion and as context for regard and legitimacy; IN-36's cap of one march per season per territory stays as the ruled test limit. Costs: a march with no claim is possible and the holder simply dislikes it (A-68); ED-IN-0317's "can only" is read as a tendency.

**What the lane recommends.** Option B is how the suite already behaves and costs no hash mover; the choice is yours because ED-IN-0317 is your explicit word and A-58 was given on a different question (what counts as aggression in a land-taking march), so neither strictly replaces the other.

**If you do not answer.** The v12 author builds CR-91a (the carrier) and IN-36's cap and leaves the gate unbuilt; nothing else waits on it.

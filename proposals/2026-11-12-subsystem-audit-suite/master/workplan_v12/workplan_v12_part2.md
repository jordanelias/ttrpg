## 0. How every table here was made

**Every table that can be generated is generated by a script from the returned parts, and says so in its heading.** The scripts are in `workplan_v12_part2_appendix.md`, verbatim, in the order they run; each reads only the parts, the structure files, `H2_DECISIONS.md` (the twenty recommended edges and the five advised against, typed into `graph.py` with their HD ids) and U3. Three tables could not be generated whole and are marked **hand-made from a script's dump**: the settings table (§4: the `DIALS AT CONTROL` lines are free prose; `dials.py` dumps every line with its part and line number, and each row cites it), the Layer 1 table (§7: copied from `unified/U3_LAYER1_AMENDMENTS.md` and U3 §1.4, opened), and the held table (§8: each trigger read from the intake's carry-forward block at the line the entry cites, by `held_extract.py` with the blocks opened by hand where the script found no trigger word). Nothing in this part was run against the engine: no pytest, no seeded run.

**What the parse found** (`parse_parts.py`, `summ.py`): 79 body files; **59 batch blocks** (the structure's 61 less B98 and B99, which lane A44 writes in part 44); **1,357 positions** in the position tables (every distinct `Bnn.mm` handle, `.99` included); 1,341 item headings; of the 16 positions without a heading, 15 are READING rows (no body, `V12_STRUCTURE_part2.md` §5.3) and one is a POINTER, B12b.06, which E-5 says gets a body of three to eight lines (§11 C-25). No handle is duplicated inside a batch; every item handle any `DEPENDS ON`, `GATE`, `TESTS`, `STOPS IF`, `INSTRUCTION` or `SOURCE` line cites exists in some position table (`xref.py`: 0 missing). Every batch has exactly one boundary row `| STOP. Batch <handle> closes here |`, exact (§2).

**Readings at HEAD `7257911`** used by the id table (§9): `references/id_reservations.yaml` `lane_ids.lanes.<LANE>.next_free` MB 82, PC 60, FI 10, SC 41, FA 40, WR 13, **IN 362**, GO 3, SE 57 (read by `yaml.safe_load`); the highest hole-register id in `engine/season/hole_register.yaml` H-362; the highest registry id in `references/npc_registry.yaml` NPC-090; `registers/editorial_ledger_in.jsonl` holds `ED-IN-0358` to `ED-IN-0361` (the Layer 1 applications, §7).

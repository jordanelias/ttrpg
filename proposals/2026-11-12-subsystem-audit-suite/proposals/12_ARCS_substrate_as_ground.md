## The resolution (A-40: ground; A-54: the four-band ladder)

1. `rosters.yaml`: a site kind for the substrate, the **Thread ground** (`thread_ground` `[ASSUMPTION: the id;
   the name is Jordan's]`), one per territory at world-gen, with `band_floors` of its own (`rosters.yaml:
   band_floors`, `:3538`). Its seasonal motion is **a drawn result on the four-band ladder** (A-54): pool =
   the place's endurance, obstacle = the season's hardship, a steward's skill bearing on it as named levels

   (`rosters.yaml:1864`) carries no fixed decrement for this kind.
2. `verb_table.yaml`: `restore` already repairs a Site; on the Thread ground it additionally requires the
   Thread-sensitivity gate at the `own_state_gate` use site (a `requires_typed` cell, as `thread_read` carries
   today; one of the gate's two sites, A-11). One new row for an operation on the ground, shaped as a task with
   nobody opposite: `contests: "a task"` with degree-keyed `writes:` (C3-L14; the loader edit of
   `18_DETERMINISM_task_draw_for_uncontested_verbs.md` item 4, C3-R1): every band below Success writes
   `Site.condition` down by the band's amount; the row's difficulty reads the ground's band, so a damaged
   ground makes the same operation fail more often. That loop (ARC-48, SCN-LOOP-A) is then three rows of data.
3. `write_matrix.yaml` / `loop/effects`: the same row's lower bands write `(Person, coherence)` for the
   operator, giving the row a producer; its bands cut the scene budget through `decision/budget.py`
   exactly as body bands do.
4. Perception, **drawn, not gated** (C3-C14): a band crossing of the Thread ground emits (as crossings do);
   `loop/witness.py` deposits the claim to every observer present, with a confidence drawn by a keyed draw

   site. Per-band perception beyond this is not proposed.

## Coherence at its floor (S-2, answered by A-43)

At zero Coherence a person keeps his seats, offices and goals (ARC-28, -29, -30 stand) and becomes unknowable:
others' readings of his reasons, his predictability, his regard and his speech scale down with his
legibility (`U5d_WR_zero_coherence_unknowable.md`). Item 3 gives the track a writer and bands; the floor's
meaning is that file's.

## Gate (execution artifact)

ARC-48 on the realm, seeds 0, 1, 2, eight seasons: the ground's condition falls faster in the arm where
a practitioner operates than in the baseline arm; after the first band crossing the operation's Failure
share rises against a control that pins the ground's condition. NPC-075: Orm's `restore` raises one
ground while the drawn motion lowers it; with him absent it only falls. Named tests (new,
`engine/season/tests/test_thread_ground.py`): `test_the_ground_falls_faster_where_a_practitioner_operates`;
`test_the_failure_share_rises_after_a_crossing_against_a_pinned_control`; `test_a_non_sensitive_observer_sometimes_perceives_a_crossing`
(over 200 seeds, a share strictly between 0 and 1, higher for a sensitive observer); `test_the_seasonal_motion_is_drawn_on_four_bands`.

## Falsifier

If the Failure share does not rise after a crossing, the floor does not bite and the loop is decoration.
If the ground's condition never crosses a band in eight seasons under any arm, the wear and the
operation's cost are too small to be a contest and the row is dead weight.

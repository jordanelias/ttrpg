# ARCS 03 — the world's substrate as ground: a Site kind, not a gauge

## Status: PROPOSED — held back for Jordan (audit §11, S-1 and S-2). Not ratified by merge.

Source: `audits/12_ARCS_adjudication.md` §4 (M-SUB, M-PERC), §5 stages 7 and 8, §6 row 18.

## What changes in `engine/season/` at runtime

Under Option A: one Site kind per territory with a condition that wears at MATTER; three verb-table rows
that read and write it; a writer for `(Person, coherence)`. Under Option B: nothing.

## The problem, with evidence

- 25 cases need a world quantity that acts degrade and that changes what can be done (58 core needs); 26
  need the person side (48 core needs). Code: no carrier for the first (grep, audit §12);
  `Person.thread_sensitivity` is set at build and written by nothing (`harness/loops.py:96`), read by one
  gate; `(Person, coherence)` is a matrix row with a gate branch and no writer (`state/world.py:1305`,
  H-47).
- Three reachability lanes (2026-08-30) reached the same absence and judged it an omission: the ratified
  shape refuses gauges on containers because they are social; a substrate's condition is material, the
  class of stores, harvests and bodies, which already move at MATTER.
- The engine already has the object's shape: `Site.condition` with per-kind `band_floors`, wear at
  MATTER whose band crossings emit and produce no outcome (`loop/matter.py:126-135`), and `restore`,
  `sabotage`, `work` rows gated on presence and on a floor.

## The two options (Jordan's)

**Option A — ground.**

1. `rosters.yaml`: a site kind for the substrate (name to be Jordan's; referred to here as the ground),
   one per territory at world-gen, with `band_floors` of its own and a baseline wear per season in the
   existing wear table.
2. `verb_table.yaml`: `restore` already repairs a Site; on the ground it additionally requires the
   Thread-sensitivity gate (a `requires_typed` cell, as `thread_read` carries today). One new contested
   row for an operation on the ground, with `writes_by_degree`: every band below Success writes
   `Site.condition` down by the band's amount; the row's floor is the ground's band, so a damaged ground
   makes the same operation fail more often. That loop (ARC-48, SCN-LOOP-A) is then three rows of data.
3. `write_matrix.yaml` / `loop/effects`: the same row's lower bands write `(Person, coherence)` for the
   operator, giving the row a producer; its bands cut the scene budget through `decision/budget.py`
   exactly as body bands do.
4. Perception: a band crossing of the ground emits (as crossings do); `loop/witness.py` deposits the
   claim only for observers at or above the sensitivity gate. Per-band perception beyond the gate is not
   proposed.
   `[UNVERIFIED: the wear table's and `band_floors`' exact homes; I read them through the CORE audit
   (`loop/matter.py:126-135`, `band_floors.dwelling`) and the requirement file, not at their rows.]`
5. Not in this option: operations beyond the one row, hazard entities, sensitivity that grows, the
   Forgetting. Each is design-first and waits on the first run of the loop above.

**Option B — not built.** The Thread remains perception and belief. The 25 M-SUB cases are recorded as
outside the game in their overlay files (`base: none`, with this ruling cited), and the 26 M-PERC cases
keep only what `thread_read`'s gate supports.

They differ as games: under A the realm can be lost by everyone at once and a practitioner's success is
never free; under B all stakes are political.

## A second decision carried with it (S-2)

What Coherence at its floor is: loss of agency (the reading the tree last adopted) or expansion with
continuity (the reading ARC-28, -29 and -30 open by asserting). Option A's item 3 gives the track a
writer and bands; it does not choose the floor's meaning, and no row is authored for the floor until
that is ruled.

## Gate (execution artifact), Option A

ARC-48 on the realm, seeds 0, 1, 2, eight seasons: the ground's condition falls faster in the arm where
a practitioner operates than in the baseline arm; after the first band crossing the operation's Failure
share rises against a control that pins the ground's condition. NPC-075: Orm's `restore` raises one
ground while wear lowers it; with him absent it only falls.

## Falsifier

If the Failure share does not rise after a crossing, the floor does not bite and the loop is decoration.
If the ground's condition never crosses a band in eight seasons under any arm, the wear and the
operation's cost are too small to be a contest and the row is dead weight.

## NERS reading (Option A)

**N**: tested top-down (the survival contest), bottom-up (one Site field), laterally (the same wear and
floor rules as a harbour or a seam): it holds from each; cut it and 25 cases and the game's second
contest go. **R**: strategy (when to operate, when to mend), a world that worsens with nobody watching,
completeness at the floor still unruled (S-2). **S**: one methodology with every other Site; pauses with
MATTER; zooms from one operator's act to a territory's ground. **E (last)**: "ground wears, work on it
can damage it, damaged ground makes work harder", a rule a player can say in one breath, against one new
verb row.

Design-first (a ruling), then a small build and data.

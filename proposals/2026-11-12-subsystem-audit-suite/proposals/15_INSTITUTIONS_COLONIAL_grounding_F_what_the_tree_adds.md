## The problem (evidence)

1. The colonial lane wrote the fief, the company, the hostage, the labour draft, settler society and
   distance from memory, and stated that the tree held no research on most of it. The statement was
   wrong (audit §2, withdrawn; map §0 item 2). The tree's sources describe mechanisms the design lacks.
2. The largest is in the oath. The design's `homage` binds the lord to forbear and to confirm (audit
   §4.3). The tree's one worked vassal contract has the lord owing **reward for service**, in two
   forms, confirmation and new grant, and records what happened when a won war left nothing to grant
   (`PHS:457-461`). Without that clause this design cannot produce the best-attested way a
   conquest-founded order decays: from the top, by an unpaid victory.
3. The tree states this lane's own distance mechanism with a third term the lane omitted: a decree
   lands by a roll "against its own disposition, distance, **and the presence of an enforcing agent**"
   (`PMP:491-494`).
4. On the hostage the tree says "the *cost* is the mechanism, not the threat" (`PMP:506`); the design's
   `wardship` costs the sender nothing after the ward leaves.

## The resolution, decided

### Edit 1 — the order's bearer (M-15) · composed; a gate and a report line; no code

Source: `PMP:491-494`; `PMP:661` ("an envoy who exceeds his mandate and is repudiated, or who refers
home and loses the moment").

No obstacle term is added. The enforcing agent is a person, and the engine already has what he does:

1. the issuer `issue`s the writ **in the room** to a sworn man of his own seat (at `distance` 0;
   `issue`'s row is `14_VERBS_BINDING_D`'s) and `give`s him the Record;
2. he `dispatch`es the man (CP-T09-08, as `14_VERBS_BINDING` adopts it); the journey is the man's own
   legs and scene budget (`loop/effects_migration.py::_relocate`);
3. arrived, the bearer `tell`s the writ's terms to the addressee. `tell` is a draw on the **bearer's**
   skill at `distance` 0. Under A-21 the terms also travel with the news of the issue; the bearer
   is one sure way the news arrives, and he is there to see the answer. A landed telling deposits the content claim, which is FA-P1's test for a
   writ being addressed to someone (COL-A edit 5: "he holds it, or his own ledger holds its content
   claim") `[UNVERIFIED: that a content claim received by `tell`, and not by the issue's own deposit,
   satisfies `writs_addressed`; if it does not, one clause there, owner FA-P1]`;
4. the bearer is present when the addressee answers, so the answer is witnessed first-hand by the
   issuer's own man: presence buys by cost what a far issuer otherwise learns only if someone travels to
   tell him.

What it costs: a person's seasons, the chance he does not arrive, and what he chooses to say. A bearer
who softens or sharpens the terms has made an ordinary `lie` or an ordinary true telling; the issuer
learns which only if someone tells him.

**The one edit** is to proposal E's report: a line per seed counting orders that went by bare `issue`
at `distance` ≥ 1 and orders that went by bearer, with each arm's band shares.

### Edit 2 — the lord's reward (M-18) · `instruments.yaml: forms.homage`; `data/instruments.py`; `queries/world_q.py::dues` · small build

Source: `PHS:457-458` (favour as confirmation or as new grant, against service); `PHS:461` and
`research/governance/conflicts_power_struggles.md` §Japan 3.2 (the unpaid bond); `FASE:138`
(confirmation at accession, for a fee: the other half, which `homage` already has).

**One new clause key, `after:`.** Today a `due` clause recurs by `every:` from the instrument's making.
A clause may instead carry:

```yaml
      - {by: grantor, kind: transfer.made, to: grantee_rung,
         after: {kind: field.won, of: grantee}, within: 4}
```

Reading, in `dues`' period test (COL-A edit 5: "inside the period `[made + k·every, …)`"): for a
clause with `after:`, a period opens at the tick of each Event of `after.kind` whose doer is the
`of:` party or a holder of a live sworn `oblige` to the `of:` party's seat, and closes `within`
seasons later. Inside it the clause binds exactly as a recurring one does: `dues` stamps the target on
the bound party's question; `kept` counts the period met or unmet from each reader's own ledger. The
amount is the instrument's `amounts` entry for that clause, as for a rent.
`[UNVERIFIED: who the doer of a `field.won` Event is. If it is the commander alone, `of: grantee` is
read as "the commander is the grantee or sworn to his seat", which is the same test one edge out.]`

Loader (`data/instruments.py`): `after.kind` a `DEED_KINDS` member; `after.of` in `{grantor, grantee}`
and not the clause's own `by:`; `within` a positive int; a clause carries `every:` or `after:`, never
both. `homage`'s row gains the clause above; `amounts` for it is content on the two seeded `homage`
instruments (SYNTHESIS-B §3; the Grand Master's oath has none) `[ASSUMPTION; 1 unit of the house's home
matter kind; sweep {0, 1, 2}; Jordan to correct]`.

**Why stores and not a grant of ground.** A clause names one deed kind. `transfer.made` makes the reward
cost the lord what he holds, so a war that took stores can pay its victors and a war that took nothing
cannot, which is the mechanism. A lord who grants ground instead (`land_grant`, `farm`) has done
something each vassal judges for himself through his own regard; the clause does not count it. That
loses the historical "new grant" as a way of *keeping* the clause, and is recorded as the price.

**Fallback if `after:` is refused:** `{by: grantor, kind: transfer.made, every: 4, to: grantee_rung}`,
a yearly gift owed whether or not anyone fought. It keeps "a lord who gives nothing loses his men" and
loses the tie to service, and with it the unpaid-victory story. It is the weaker form and is not taken
unless the key is refused.

## NERS reading (definitions CLAUDE.md §0.06; E last, as a ratio)

- **N.** Cut the reward clause and the oath binds the lord to nothing he must *do*: a conquest-founded
  order cannot decay from the top by an unpaid victory, which is the fixed point's own subject (rank by
  accomplishment implies accomplishment is owed something). It holds top-down (the lord's choice of
  whom to pay), bottom-up (each house's reading of `kept`), lateral (houses comparing what each was
  given) and across scales (a realm oath and a captain's). Cut the keep and a hostage is free to
  demand. Cut the commission's clauses and the far officer's war is news, not a breach. The farm's cell
  is **narrowed**: it bites only if a farm is ever renewed to the same holder, and gate 4 may show it
  inert.
- **R.** With no player: the houses that fought and were not paid; the host whose school of wards
  becomes a party; the officer recalled for a war that succeeded. Completeness: each clause is silent
  where its deed never occurs; none can strand a world.
- **S.** One clause key, read where every clause is read, on the season's own ticks. No second ledger
  of loyalty: `kept` remains each person's own count. The bearer is the existing `give`, `dispatch` and
  `tell`, so the order that travels pauses and resumes as a person does.
- **E (against what N and R found).** One small key, one enum value, four row edits, for five
  mechanisms; six stories for six folds in a report that already exists. The ratio would fall if
  `after:` needed a second meaning; it has one.

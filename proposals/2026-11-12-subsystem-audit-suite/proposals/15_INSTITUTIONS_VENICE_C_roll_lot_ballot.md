## The resolution, decided

### 1. A list is an establishment kept by warrant

`binds_bases` gains `members_by_warrant`. `_req_oblige` admits an `oblige` to a seat whose `binds` is that
value only if the joiner **holds a `dispensation` whose `terms` is that seat and of which he is an
addressee**, minted through that seat (the Record's rung is the seat's rung, as `_seat_rung` writes it).
The keeper edits the list with `issue` and `give` (to admit) and by not renewing (to drop): a member's
`oblige` carries a term and lapses at MATTER unless the seat renews it, which is D-5's own expulsion rule
(#453 `:2076`, "the seat withholding renewal"). A purge is therefore quiet, costs no accusation and is
visible only as a `tenure.closed` by term: the Florentine bag purge. Where the body, not the keeper,
admits, the keeper's `issue` follows a ballot on the candidate (edit 5) in which the candidate's deeds
are `proofs` levels (audit §5.3; C3-C10). Ladder step 4: the roster's own note says a second value "arrives
WITH its rule in `_req_oblige`".

### 2. Eligibility from a list

`rosters.yaml: tables.eligible_from` maps a seat id to the seat whose list fills it (no Seat field,
C3-L9; the table home of PAPACY-B §1). `state/gate.py::may_seat(w, off, person)` is the one reader: true
when the table names no list for the seat, or the person holds a live `oblige` to the named seat. Asked
by `_req_confer`, by CR-35's bench election and CR-36's nomination when they land, and by the lot. The
person-side decline (a conferrer who holds no claim that the candidate is enrolled does not form the act)
follows `confer`'s ban decline in `audits/14_VERBS_BINDING.md` §1.

### 3. The lot

The seat's basis stays `elected` (a lot is a way of electing), and `rosters.yaml: tables.selection` names
it `lot`. `conferral_bases` is **not** widened: its values are Jordan's (`rosters.yaml:3042-3056`,
`open: false`), so a fifth value is his to add (listed for Jordan: option A, add `lot` to the roster;
option B, taken, the table). `FILLED_BY` is unchanged (`elected` already admits `confer`,
`state/gate.py:697`). For a seat that `selection_of(off.id)` reads as `lot`, `_eff_confer` ignores the
act's `to` and **draws** the conferee with `H(w.world_seed, w.tick, off.id, "confer.lot.<n>")` (A-30; the
one draw owner) uniformly from the persons for whom `may_seat` holds and who are present at the seat's
rung or below it. A drawn person who is barred (edit 4's cooling-off; a live `ban` within the seat's purview) is
*seen and not seated*: the act emits `lot.seen` naming him, and the draw repeats over the remainder; an
empty bag leaves the seat vacant and emits `lot.empty`. The presiding seat (any seat for which `may_fill`
holds) chooses when to hold the draw and nothing else. The odds are the composition of the list, which
persons made (edit 1). A list of one is a certainty only in the sense that eligibility is: whoever
emptied the bag did it by acts others could see.

Venice's two-stage election needs no further mechanism: a nominating seat with `conferral: elected` and
`selection: lot`, `eligible_from` the body's list and `term: 1`, whose holder nominates under CR-36; the body confirms by
ballot (edit 5). Florence's is one `lot` seat. One alternation, not nine (audit §5.2).

### 4. Cooling-off and the audit at exit

One table and one seat key, beside FA-P3's `term`:

- `rosters.yaml: tables.cooling_off`: seat id → seasons (a positive int; refused for a seat whose row
  has no `term`). `may_seat` is false for a person whose most recent `hold` on this seat closed by term
  maturity fewer than that many ticks ago. Computed from the closed Tenure's `until`; stored nowhere.
- `audit_at_exit: <arrangement id> | null`, a seat-row key (A-13's exit audit; `R4_READ.md` 1.7). For any
  closed `hold` on the row's seat (X-12: any close, not only a matured term), `world_q.audits_due(w, p)`
  (SYN-A §4) **reads, when asked,** a classed referent `Party` naming the departed holder and the
  arrangement onto the question of every seat-holder who holds the `tenure.closed` claim and may open a
  case on that person (those for whom `may_fill` holds over the seat, and any seat of the same body whose
  remit carries `determine`) (`14_VERBS_BINDING_B_sittings.md`'s class). Nothing is written at MATTER:
  MATTER emits its `term.matured` only (C3-C7; the `vacancies` precedent, L-4). **Nothing is docketed by
  the engine.** A person opens the
  case or does not; subjects' told complaints are the evidence; the verdict is the bench's roll.

## NERS reading

- **N.** Top-down, a body's seats are filled from its own list. Bottom-up, a craftsman's enrolment is
  the first rung of every office above. Laterally, the peerage roll, the college of electors, the guild
  matricula and the company's register are one mechanism. Diagonally, a hearth's client packed onto a
  settlement's list moves a realm seat's election. Remove the list and capture has nowhere to happen;
  remove the lot and the list has no pay-off an election does not already give; remove the drawn ballot
  and every council in the game is arithmetic.
- **R.** Strategy at the list (enrol, lapse, challenge), at the calendar (when to hold the draw), at the
  room (sealed or open). Customisable per seat by five row keys. With no player: turnover, cooling-off
  gaps, audits and tied ballots that return next sitting. Extremes: an empty bag is a vacancy with an
  Event; a bench below quorum is a Partial, not a crash.
- **S.** The lot uses the one draw owner; the ballot the one roll owner and SC's one composer; the term
  the one clock. A ballot's Partial pauses the matter on the docket it was already on.
- **E, last.** One roster member, three tables and one row key, one gate predicate, one effect branch,
  one read-time referent. Against it stands every election, enrolment and audit in all the institution
  families.

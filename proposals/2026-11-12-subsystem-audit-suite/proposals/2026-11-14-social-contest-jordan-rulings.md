# Social contest and proceedings: Jordan's rulings of 2026-11-13 and 2026-11-14, verbatim

## Status: PROPOSED (reference: the words Jordan gave this session, collected in one place for the redesign; not canon until folded and ratified; resolves nothing at runtime, CLAUDE.md §0.05; deleted when v12 is adopted)

Source: the session's messages. Each ruling is quoted as given; the line after it is the plain reading the orchestrator took (marked `[reading]`), to be corrected by Jordan.

| # | Jordan's words | `[reading]` |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | "proceedings/social contests should be calling in focus as staying on topic, not being pulled away from argument into something irrelevant; reason as constructing the argument/speech, making it logical and reasonable and evidentiary with ability to account for opponent's factual circumstances like resources office influence etc; attunement as understanding what is most likely to rattle your opponent or reading their intent, being able to read the room, identifying what kind of argumentation/speech is most effective for audience adjudicator opponent; charisma as the ability to be listened to and be compelling with your delivery; grace as acting in the correct manner for the venue and occasion, as being seen as respectable, for remaining composed in face of difficulties and challenges; will as the ability to keep arguing your case, to believe in yourself and remain confident, to not get rattled in first place, that \"x factor\" that supports you" | six attributes, six manners (Focus, Reason, Attunement, Presence/charisma, Grace, Spirit/will) |
| R2 | "the subsystem needs to be using all of these attributes in the manner that I specified" | a non-zero slope per key is not enough; each attribute acts in its stated manner, with its own mechanism and test |
| R3 | "argue/speech/etc verbs should all be calling the social contest/proceedings subsystem as they all concern influencing people" | every influence verb reaches the subsystem |
| R4 | "proceedings, as I understand them to be designed, have different types based upon what is at stake, what factions are involved, what the venue is, who the adjudicators are, who the audience is, how and what kind of arguments/speeches are presented and in what order, what what system is used to render a verdict, etc; from heresy trial to parliamentary debate to private negotiation to rallying a crowd etc" | typed proceedings, with parameters (stakes, factions, venue, adjudicators, audience, speech kinds and order, verdict system) |

Written by the re-grounding lane under Jordan's direction of 2026-11-12: *"Research should have been found
in /research or /proposals."* It records, for the Byzantine and papacy families, which files of
`research/`, `proposals/`, `.audit/` and `.designs/` treat which institution, to what depth, and with what
caveat of their own. It explains a judgment about two artifacts that already exist
(`audits/15_INSTITUTIONS_BYZANTINE.md`, `audits/15_INSTITUTIONS_PAPACY.md`); it queues nothing.

**How the list was made.** `grep -rIil` for the nineteen terms of the work order over `research proposals`
(277 files, the suite itself excluded) and over `.designs .audit` (97 files); each hit ranked by its count of
Byzantine terms and of papal terms; then read. **Depth marks**: `FULL` read end to end; `SECTION` the named
sections read in full; `LINES` every matching line read with its paragraph (the file's lines are
paragraph-length, so this is the passage, not a snippet); `COUNT` only counted (the term is used in
passing or in another sense, e.g. "cardinal" as an adjective, "themes" as "topics"). Grade of the research
itself is its own: all of it is tier 3 (`research/00_INDEX.md` §1), informs and never settles.

## 0. The two claims this map corrects, exactly

1. **"The tree's research omits Byzantium."** Half true, and the true half has a line number. The
   three-theme governance corpus says of itself: *"Byzantine Empire is **omitted from this pass** (its
   research agent was interrupted; the design lead opted to proceed with the eight completed civilizations
   and add Byzantium later if wanted)"* (`research/governance/00_MANIFEST.md:28-29`; repeated in the head of
   each of its three documents at `:5`). That corpus does not treat Byzantium. **The tree does**, in six
   other places, listed in §1. The original audit used two of them
   (`.audit/2026-07-09-comparative-governance-research/` and three lines of `research/`) and marked the
   rest `[MEMORY]`.
2. **"Papal history is memory-grade."** The original audit cited the governance corpus for the
   Investiture quarrel, the conclave, the Schism and the Papal States, and wrote the curia, canon
   procedure, the sanctions, the orders and the inquisition from memory. The tree holds a sourced
   stage-by-stage account of inquisitorial procedure with its crown entanglements and its own nulls
   (`research/social_contest_procedures/02_inquisition_heresy_procedure.md`), the papal-election and
   council procedures as catalogue rows (`…/04_decision_procedure_catalog.md`), the Venetian Interdict
   as a procedure (`…/01_venice_procedure.md`), advowson and interdict as a parish-scale theme
   (`research/fa_se_historical_precedent_research_v1.md`), the orders, the venal curia
   and the cardinal-nephew as mechanisms (`research/proactive_governance_scale_research_v1.md`,
   `research/rise_to_power_roster_system_research_v1.md`), and an archived design of the Church's own
   ladder and elections (`.designs/systems/factions/reference/faction_politics_v30.md`, intent only).

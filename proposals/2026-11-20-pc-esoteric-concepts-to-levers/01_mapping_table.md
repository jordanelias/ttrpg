# Part 1 — The mapping table

## Status: PROPOSED. Reference only. Every row's six-step derivation chain is in `01_mapping_table_part2.md` under the row's id; a row without one there is not accepted. Words are defined in `00_head.md`.

**How to read a row.** *Engine handle* names the quantity the derivation found, with the consumer site opened in this session (`file:line` against the tree at this branch). *Direction and form* says which way it moves and what stays neutral. *Cost side* is the other end of the trade **as the tradition states it**, and where that end sits in the engine; "none named" means the tradition names no in-fight cost, and the row says so rather than inventing one. Status codes:

| status | meaning |
|---|---|
| **EXISTS** | a live lever, attribute, axis or rule already does what the derivation found; no build |
| **EXISTS-PHYS** | the engine already does it through weapon physics or the contest structure, for everyone; it is not a technique at all |
| **NEEDS-LEVER** | a pure function already computes the right quantity; one `ability_factor`/`ability_bonus` read there would carry it (named) |
| **NEEDS-GATE** | an existing `if` would have to widen (one of the jobs set's four proposed gates, M-1, or a new one, named) |
| **NEEDS-BEHAVIOUR** | a granted behaviour or a member of a choice set, or a branch keyed on a state the engine computes |
| **NO-HANDLE** | the situation the concept addresses does not arise in the engine, or the effect is a forbidden target (agency proposal §5 Class A); Part 6 holds the smallest addition |

Grades and tags are the research sheets' own (`scratchpad/research/esoteric/<cluster>.md`); a tag in square brackets is copied from the sheet entry named. **UBR** = the choice between the two nearest quantities is UNVERIFIED BY RUN; the worked examples (Part 7) and the discriminating runs in `examples.py` are the rows that were run.

# Part 4 — Stages of mastery, against the growth path of the jobs set

## Status: PROPOSED. Reference only. The growth path mapped onto is the jobs set's (`proposals/2026-11-20-pc-tradition-jobs-growth/04_acquisition_and_growth.md`): **technique levels 0-8, raised only by instruction**; **attention from Concentration, raised by fighting and sparring (PC-22)**; the `arms` skill score (live); and the derived **growth stage** (novice, practised, veteran: one, two, three slots per board column), all host-side. Nothing here reaches `modules/`.

## 4.2 What each ladder's rungs are, in the engine's terms

The derivation asks what changes between rungs, in the tradition's own account, then where that change would sit.

| what changes between rungs (ladders that say so) | where it sits in the growth path | fit |
|---|---|---|
| **More techniques known, then held well** (Munenori's first chapters; Meyer's phases; Fiore's guard → remedy → counter; kalari's weapon classes; Taiji's "familiar forms") | technique levels (taught) and the board's slots (growth stage) | **fits** the jobs set as written |
| **Technique stops costing thought** ("with successful training, training falls away"; Chōzan's technique → *ki*; Takuan's return; Cook Ding no longer sees the whole ox) | the jobs set's rule that an entry at level 6 or more costs one attention less ("what is drilled no longer takes attention", Part 2 §2.3.3) | **fits, and supports a rule the jobs set marked [ASSUMPTION; low]** with four independent period or later-classic sources |
| **Attunement to the opponent** (the cat's grey cat, *wa*; Takuan's "your opponent's weapon will become your weapon"; Musashi's "become the enemy") | familiarity, today a fixed table by tradition pair (`traditions.py:27-48`); PC-22 would make it pairwise and write it from sparring (jobs set Part 4 §4.2, item 5) | **fits** PC-22's pairwise familiarity; the cat ladder's third rung is a growth the season, not a teacher, would write |
| **The mind no longer stops** (the cat's old cat; Takuan; *mushin*; Tesshu after the vow) | Concentration (PC-22: fight experience writes it) | **fits** the 2026-11-07 ruling exactly (experience writes composure, "not … technique") — but see 4.4: the engine's Concentration hardly acts |
| **Endurance proved** (Tesshu's 200/600/1,400 matches; Sinfjötli's gloves; the agoge) | Concentration from fights fought (the vow is many fights) | fits as breadth, never depth |
| **A door opens that did not exist before** (Fiore: the counter master, then the counter-counter; Meyer: provoke, take, hit; Silver: only with the governors can one "fight safe") | a **set's opened gate** (jobs set Part 3 §3.3), or a stage that opens one | **fits a stage-gate the jobs set does not yet have**: see 4.3 |
| **Weapon classes opened by initiation** (kalari) | a `requires` keyed on a weapon class, host-side (the jobs set's board never names a weapon, Part 2 §2.1) | fits as authoring data; no engine change |
| **One teacher, then several, then none** (*shu-ha-ri*, MODERN-SYNCRETIC) | teacher-gated levels (G-1); the second tradition after a threshold; the one personal set | fits the path's three moves, under a modern label |
| **Admission conditions** (Vadi: no "thick-brained, pusillanimous and cowardly"; Liechtenauer v.20; the Dhanurveda's tested pupil) | a host-side `requires` on Spirit or on a social fact | fits (free gate) |
| **The unteachable last step** (Takuan's "special transmission outside the scriptures"; Chōzan's "drawing but not shooting"; *malícia* "cannot be taught"; Taiji's "listening" that "cannot be conveyed in words") | the growth that has **no teacher**: Concentration and pairwise familiarity, written by fighting and sparring | **fits G-1's split** (taught depth, fought breadth) better than the jobs set claims for itself: four traditions say the top of the ladder is fought, not taught |

**One canon caution.** P-13 ("ontological knowledge is not transmissible as propositional content; what transmits is the form of attention") resembles the unteachable last step. It is about **thread-level** knowledge (`canon/philosophy/10_constraints.md`, P-13) and says nothing about swordsmanship; it must not be cited as the reason a martial rung is untaught. The martial sources carry that claim on their own.

## 4.3 What a stage unlocks, and what it costs

| stage (jobs set) | what the ladders say it should unlock | what it costs, in the traditions' accounts | engine |
|---|---|---|---|
| novice (one slot a column) | the guards and the basic actions (Fiore's first masters; kalari's body forms; Meyer's correct cuts) | time with a teacher; an admission test | levels from `train`; nothing module-side |
| practised (two) | the remedy and the counter (Fiore's second and third masters); understanding jin (Taiji); "let the cuts fly" (Meyer) | years; Takuan's warning that this is the stage of "debating stances" — more held, less free | a second slot per column; a set's gate once members reach level 3 |
| veteran (three) | the counter-counter (Fiore); "training falls away"; the master's return to the beginner | Tesshu's vow, Katori's blood oath, Ekalavya's thumb: an oath, an ordeal, a fee — paid **before** the rung, not in the bout | a third slot; drilled entries cost no attention |

**A stage gate the jobs set lacks.** Fiore's ladder (and Meyer's) makes the *counter* a later rung than the guard, and the *counter to the counter* later still. The jobs set opens doors only through sets. Three ladders say a stage itself opens a door. The smallest form: a growth stage that opens one of the four M-1 gates for its holder (the practised fighter gets `counter_shallow`), host-side, with no new module edit beyond M-1. **This is offered as a variant, not a recommendation**: it duplicates what a set does, and Part 7 §7.1 shows the counter door matters a great deal more than the jobs set assumed (owned by workplan v12 B15.04, Part 2 §2.4).

**Fiore's counter-counter has no handle.** A landed counter becomes a riposte (`wrapper.py:361-362`); the original aggressor has no answer to it in the same beat. N-13 in Part 6.

## 4.4 Two findings that bear on the growth path

1. **The top rung of almost every ladder lands on Concentration, and Concentration barely acts.** The mind that does not stop, *mushin*, the old cat, Tesshu after the vow: all are Concentration under the 2026-11-07 ruling, and Part 7 §7.3 measures the engine's Concentration as near-inert in the bout (its read effect is gated by stamina fatigue; its lapse term is ±0.012 σ at the extremes). A growth path that sells a master's composure must first make composure do something. N-8 in Part 6.
2. **The counter rungs are behind a nearly shut door.** Fiore's third master, Indes, *kiri-otoshi*, *mezzo tempo*: all are counter techniques, and in a closed exchange the door opens on about 1% of commits (Part 7 §7.1). A curriculum built on them, as the jobs set's German, Italian, Japanese and Chinese lines partly are, sells levels that do almost nothing until a gate opens — or until workplan v12 B15.04 lands and "deep" follows the window (Part 2 §2.4).

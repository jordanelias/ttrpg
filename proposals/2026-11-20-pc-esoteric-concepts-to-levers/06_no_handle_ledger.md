## 6.3 Reading the ranking

- **N-1 and N-8 between them carry 12 of the 75 rows**, and they are the two families (F3, F8) with the most traditions behind them. N-1 is new state; N-8 is a change of form at a function that already exists.
- **N-4 is worthless without N-8**: it would drain a reserve that does not act.
- **N-9 and N-14 are both D3, and both already have an owner**: B16.08's conserved transfer prices the lean (N-9 withdrawn), and B16's round-choice content is where a "hold" member would live (N-14).
- **N-8 has an owner too**: `config.py:119` records that "PC-22 retires this key [LAPSE_K] into one Concentration function"; N-8 is evidence for that function's form (it must act on a fresh body), not a separate build.
- **Two additions of the ledger are not additions at all but existing jobs-set items**: N-5 is the jobs set's deferred `branch` relation, and N-12 is one more value of its `withdraw_band` gate.

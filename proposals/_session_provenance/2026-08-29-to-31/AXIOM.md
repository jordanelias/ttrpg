# JORDAN'S BINDING DESIGN AXIOM (2026-08-29, mid-session, verbatim intent)

## A-1 THE CONTAINMENT LADDER HAS FOUR RUNGS BELOW SETTLEMENT
Individual -> Family -> Community -> Settlement. (And upward from settlement, whatever the
world layer needs.) Family and Community are NEW rungs, first-class, not flavour.

## A-2 A FACTION IS ANY AGGREGATE OF INDIVIDUALS AT ANY SCALE
A faction is NOT a tier. It is a set of individuals. Its scale is DERIVED, not declared.
A faction may be two brothers or a national church. Same object, same mechanics.

## A-3 SIBLING COEXISTENCE IS THE POINT OF EACH RUNG
- Families alongside each other IN a community -> noble houses, cadet branches competing
- Communities alongside each other IN a settlement -> guilds, caste dynamics, ethnic groups
The rung exists so that PEERS CAN COMPETE INSIDE A SHARED CONTAINER.

## A-4 FACTIONS SCALE CONTINUOUSLY
A faction aggregates into increasingly large containers, or shrinks. Growth and decline are
the SAME mechanism run in two directions. No discontinuity between "small faction" and
"national faction".

## CONSEQUENCES I MUST HOLD THE DESIGN TO
1. Containment (where you are / what holds you) and Alignment (who you act with) are TWO
   DIFFERENT AXES. v2 conflated them by making faction a tier.
2. T5 upward aggregation now has real rungs to climb and real filters between them.
3. T6 downward ripple now has real rungs to descend and real refraction at each.
4. Canon's community level (settlement_layer_v30.md:171) is VINDICATED, and J-2 is ANSWERED
   BY JORDAN: community is a required rung. Family goes further than J-2 asked.
5. Cadet branches / caste / guild dynamics are the setting content that v1 was blind to.
   This axiom makes them mechanical rather than decorative.

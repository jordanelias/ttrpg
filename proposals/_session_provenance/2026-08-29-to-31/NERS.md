# THE NERS CHARTER (Jordan, 2026-08-29, binding on the audit stage)

N NECESSARY  the thing cannot be removed without affecting EMERGENCE
E ELEGANT    the thing is as distilled as possible WITHOUT AFFECTING FUNCTION
R ROBUST     the thing offers CHOICES WITH REAL IMPACTS for the player
S SMOOTH     it integrates well and PROPAGATES AS REQUIRED ACROSS SCALES

## RULE 1 — E IS NOT AN INDEPENDENT AXIS. IT IS A RATIO AGAINST N AND R.
Jordan's elegance ruling: "distill as much as possible without losing emergent possibilities
or robust choosing for the player" = E CONSTRAINED BY N AND BY R, in one sentence.
=> The audit MAY NOT score four axes independently and average. It must test E as a ratio.
=> This is THE ONLY WAY OVER-DISTILLATION BECOMES DETECTABLE. An audit that averages will
   score an amputated design as elegant.
=> Per object, the N test is written as: "state the emergent possibility lost if this is cut."
   An object that cannot name one is E-surplus. An object whose loss is named and real is
   N-necessary and MAY NOT be distilled away no matter how much simpler the result.

## RULE 2 — S IS T5 AND T6 RESTATED. IT IS NOT A STYLISTIC JUDGMENT.
"Propagates as required across scales" IS the up-stroke and the down-stroke.
=> The S verdict is decided by exactly two questions:
   S-UP   can a DEMAND travel up the ladder, and be filtered by someone at a rung?
   S-DOWN can an OPPORTUNITY travel down and reach a person WHO HOLDS NO POST?
=> v2 failed both structurally: down-stroke terminated in place-owned gauges; up-stroke had
   NO DEMAND OBJECT AT ALL. The new design must not inherit either failure.

## RULE 3 — R ADJUDICATES THE PLAYING-SURFACE BUDGET.
"Choices with real impacts" is a STRUCTURAL test, not a balance note.
=> A STRUCTURALLY DOMINANT OPTION IS AN R FAILURE.
=> Worked case Jordan named: v2's Comply/Defy fork. Defy's gain decays geometrically while
   its costs are durable and compound into next season's obstacle. Comply is dominant.
   That is an R FAILURE OF THE DESIGN, not something to tune later.
=> Therefore: for every player fork in the new suite, the audit must check the SHAPE of gain
   vs the SHAPE of cost over time. Decaying gain against compounding cost = dominance = fail.

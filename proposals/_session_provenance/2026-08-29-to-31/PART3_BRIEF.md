## What the two bodies ARE (state this correctly or the comparison is void)

  blocked-core, and a gap report. It asks: *given the design, what play exists at each seat?*
- **(b) The arcs** — `proposals/2026-08-30-arc-reachability/`. A **reachability test**: 83 arcs from

  remain reachable?*

**They are not the same instrument pointed at two corpora.** One enumerates *seats and their
affordances*; the other enumerates *outcomes and their prerequisites*. That difference is the whole
subject of Part 3 — where it makes them complementary, where it makes them redundant, and where it
makes them contradict.

# SETTING CONTENT — the design MUST name this (F-18 setting-blindness was a diagnosed failure)

## CASTE — two-tier, INFORMAL (not codified in law), radiating geographically
Northern/central Einhir (dominant) vs SOUTHERN EINHIR (stigmatized). Southern Einhir have HIGHER
baseline Thread Sensitivity and LOWER Church penetration. Structurally excluded from the post-war
settlement. Origin: the Einhir Catastrophe's site-network was concentrated in the south (Lupicco);
blame fell on southern practitioners; hardened by the south's exclusion from the Secession War
coalition leadership. Concentrations: Grauwald, Stillhelm, Oastad + western-fjord pockets.
CASTE IS ENFORCED BY RANK-GATING, PER INSTITUTION, NOT BY LAW:
  Crown  gates Standing 3+ behind public deeds / inner-circle sponsorship
  Church strongly gated - a Southern Einhir Canon is "a scandal"
  Guilds variable - documented bias in the Masterpiece Examination (the Free Master gate)
  Lowenritter and Niflhel are the two caste-OPEN institutions (Niflhel by design: waterfront and
    covert work needs Southern Einhir members)
=> DESIGN NOTE: caste discrimination is reproduced BY INSTITUTIONS, not by individual malice.
   That is explicitly the intent. It is a per-institution gate, which means it is a per-rung gate.

## INSTITUTIONS

Adjudication / Temporal Affairs / Doctrine and Archives). Confessor Arne Himlensendt - "sincerely
devout, completely wrong; pastoral compassion and ethnic suppression are the same act". Mission:
Solmundan Orthodoxy across the peninsula. Built over 200 years INSIDE an Altonian colonial
containment grant (cathedral city Himmelenger, tax exemption, education/archival monopoly) -
"the cage became a school". Canonically the UNWITTING suppressor of Thread Sensitivity: its
essentialist theology forecloses the perceptual preconditions for sensitivity. EMERGENT, NOT
DESIGNED - this is the model for how an institution causes harm nobody intends.

/Intel + PRESENCE MARKERS + "Community Weaving". Rejects formal sovereignty and alliances by
ideology. Horizontal/anti-hierarchical. Leader Yrsa Vossen. Linked to Southern Einhir grievance.
CAN EMERGE SPONTANEOUSLY from territorial neglect (Revolt -> Insurgency -> Faction pipeline).
=> This is the game's existing proof that a faction can be born from below. A-2/A-4 generalize it.
CROWN. Kingdom of Valoria, 3 duchies (Valorsmark/Almud, Hafenmark/Baralta, Varfell/Vaynard).

## KNOTS
A thread-substrate binding between two characters at Disposition +5. CONSTITUTIVE, NOT
CONTRACTUAL. Requires Disposition +5, TS>=30 either side, Bonds>=5, free slot (max floor(Bonds/2)+1).
Two tiers: Distant (-2..+5 strain) and Close (-5..+5, buffered). Bidirectional strain gauge.
Strain accrues from five uses: remote Thread-Read, Composure-buffering in social contests, counsel
extraction, contested Thread ops, sustained low Disposition. Decays -1/season on sustained
investment. RUPTURE at +5 strain or on 5 triggers: public betrayal of extracted counsel, partner's
death, Fell/Dissolution targeting the partner, opposing-Conviction shift, player choice. Breaking
costs Disposition -> -3, mutual Composure damage, Coherence -1, and for Close Knots a CONVICTION
SCAR. Knots mechanize relational contagion of Coherence-drift.

## THE PERSON
NINE attributes (1-7), a TENTH is ruled to exist and is deliberately UNNAMED:
  Body: Strength Endurance Agility | Mind: Focus Acuity Will | Social: Attunement Charisma Bonds
THREAD SENSITIVITY (TS) 0-100+, Thread Pool = floor(TS/10).
DISPOSITION: flat -5..+5 relationship quality. NOT capped by Bonds (Bonds gates Knot eligibility).
COHERENCE: integrity of layer-two self-rendering, ORTHOGONAL to TS. 10->0 countdown, bands
  Dissonant/Fragmented/Fractured/Severed with escalating social and Thread penalties.
CONVICTIONS: THIRTEEN canonical - Faith Authority Order Scholastic Utility Equity Liberty
  Precedent Community Identity Warden Virtue Honor. Structured concentration on a PRIMARY 1-3 plus
  distributed cultural weight. (Source says "1-3", not a hard cap of exactly 3.)
BELIEFS are SEPARATE from Convictions: player-authored personal goals (Burning Wheel style).
  DUTIES are a third thing: faction/cultural obligation.
MOMENTUM: +1 for pursuing a Conviction in-scene; scale 0-4, resets per session; spend for +1D.
MEMORY-PULLING is possible but messy, costly, DETECTABLE - produces a perceptible ORPHANED
  CONFIGURATION, never a clean erase.

## PLACES
Country -> Duchy (3) -> Province (conditional/emergent) -> Territory -> Settlement.
35 settlements / 14 provinces / 3 duchies, +2 special cases (Himmelenger church city-state,
Schoenland foreign island) = 37 march targets.
Settlement types: Seat City Town Fortress Port Cathedral Mine Outpost. Each has a "natural manager"
faction and 3 core stats Prosperity/Defense/Order (0-5).
BELOW SETTLEMENT: a DISTRICT sub-feature tier exists (e.g. Valorsplatz Palace/Riverside/Cathedral).
  *** "COMMUNITY" IS **NOT** A FORMALIZED RUNG IN CANON. It is used loosely for the Restoration
      Movement's operating level only. THE FAMILY RUNG DOES NOT EXIST AT ALL. Jordan's axiom
      CREATES both. This is a design contribution, not a restoration. ***
An older flat 17-territory scheme (T1-T17) coexists unreconciled with the new ladder.

## PHILOSOPHICAL FOUNDATIONS - the four that bind this design
P-01 INSEPARABILITY: every thread op moves all three dimensions (temporal/epistemic/actual) at
     once. NO ISOLATED EFFECTS. Binds propagation.
P-03 RENDERING is consciousness-performed; INFORMATION ASYMMETRY BETWEEN SENSITIVE AND
     NON-SENSITIVE IS THE CORE MECHANIC. *** This is T4 stated as canon. ***
P-08 The epistemic barrier is INACCESSIBILITY, NOT SUPPRESSION. Knowledge transmission is
     "religious poetry"; study alone cannot cross it.
P-09 Memory-pulling is messy, costly, detectable; never a clean eraser.
P-12 Coherence-drift propagates tridimensionally THROUGH KNOTS.
P-13 Forgetting is rendering failure; Southernmost knowledge is mechanically untransmittable to
     non-sensitives.
Others: P-02/P-04 monstrosity is ontological not moral (no alignment system); P-05 three emergence
modes must be mechanically distinct; P-06 Threadcut beings have no layer 2; P-07 the Calamity is
rendered-side, the Ein Sof never responds; P-10 Coherence indexes commensurability, not virtue;
P-11 Temporal Disjunction is universal; P-14 every play mode expresses P-01; P-15 three-layer
being-persistence, the "Leap" suspends layer 2.

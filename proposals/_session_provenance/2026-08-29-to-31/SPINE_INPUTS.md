# FAILURE-MODE EXTRACT — what the nine findings prove about DESIGN SHAPE
(stripped of all wiring detail; these are lessons, NOT a work list)

F-1  STORE WITHOUT PRODUCER. Belief, legitimating.*, forecast_mass, route_cut: designed stores
     whose writers never existed. LESSON: introduce every object BY ITS PRODUCER. A design that
     specifies schemas first and wiring later reliably ships unproduced stores. "A row that
     cannot bind looks like coverage."
F-2  NO PERSON LAYER AT STRATEGIC SCALE (the organizing finding). If the strategic world is an
     aggregate, EVERY seam needing a person fails at once -- combat, knots, NPC generation,
     investigation. One absence explained four separate stubs.
F-3  OMNISCIENT DECIDERS ARE A SIGNATURE PROBLEM. Every v2 decision function had the shape
     choose(actor, world). Omniscience is then STRUCTURAL and no amount of belief modelling
     fixes it. LESSON: the signature decides the throughline.
F-4  NO UPWARD DEMAND OBJECT. T5 failed because there was nothing to aggregate. Filtering by
     nonexistence. "Nothing between silence and revolt."
F-5  DOWN-STROKE TERMINATES ABOVE THE PERSON. A person holding no post could not receive an
     opportunity. The last hop was unfilled schema.
F-6  NO TRANSMISSION ACT. Lying was representable as a row and unperformable as an act. No
     agent could put a belief into another agent's head.
F-7  CAPPED-BIAS TRAP. A cap on the belief term means false belief decides only near-ties.
     "A lie that cannot change any outcome is flavour text with provenance." Under Jordan's R
     definition this is an R FAILURE, not a balance choice.
F-8  CONSENSUS BROADCAST. The one belief writer deposited the SAME sign and magnitude to every
     witness -- the exact opposite of divergent perspectives.
F-9  OVERLOADED CARRIER WITH UNOWNED NAMESPACE. One tag kind doing institutional memory +
     machine trigger + cross-document transport, with no registry owning the key vocabulary.
F-10 CONTAINMENT AND ALIGNMENT CONFLATED. Faction was a TIER. That is why it could not scale.
F-11 NO DEBATE, ONLY A VOTE. The argument mechanic was a hole with a roll in it. A motion had
     no proposition and nothing the opposition could attack.
F-12 NO NEGOTIATION. The counterparty existed only as an obstacle number.
F-13 DEAD STATE. Four clocks initialised and never written. Anything that has no writer is
     decoration that reads as mechanism.
F-14 CORROBORATION FAILED OPEN. Independence measured by disjoint ancestry, and EMPTY
     ancestries are pairwise disjoint -- so one rumour retold three times counted as three
     independent supports. Correlated rumour laundered into corroboration.
F-15 KNOWLEDGE STORED AS A PROPERTY OF THE THING KNOWN. An `information` gauge on the TARGET,
     with no knower, so A's inquiry raised B's and the player's knowledge too.
F-16 GRANULARITY GAP. "Torben was at the mill (season 12)" and "Torben was at the mill" hash to
     two ids with no entailment relation, so A-asserts / B-denies is not a detected disagreement.
F-17 SCALE-BLIND PRIMITIVES. A mechanism designed at one rung (blocs, elite-only by construction)
     could not express the same dynamic at another rung (populations).
F-18 SETTING-BLINDNESS (diagnosed at v1, never fully fixed). Caste, heritage, Church, Restoration
     Movement, Knots appeared zero times in 3,793 lines of design. A design that cannot name the
     game's actual content is not a design of that game.

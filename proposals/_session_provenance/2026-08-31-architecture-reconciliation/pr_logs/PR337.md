## 1. What the PR is, in 5 sentences

PR #337 lands two large FILED-reference research documents (not canon, per CLAUDE.md §0.05) that
inventory almost the entire strategic/political/personnel half of Valoria's design-vs-code gap: a
4-part **Systems Integration Master** (collate → slice → flatten → within-system analysis →
cross-category comparison → four rival integration proposals) and a 2-part **Game Precedent
Companion** (what other games did, and where it broke). It also carries a 14-line fix to an
already-landed file (`research/cross_scale_action_catalogue_v1.md`, itself landed by the prior PR
#336, not by #337) correcting a self-made error: the fifth `ledger.TAG_KINDS` family is `Leverage`,
not `Compact`, and a Compact is a `Debt` subtype per ED-IN-0046 D3. It appends one dated section to
`registers/handoffs/HANDOFF_IN.md` (65 inserted lines) recording the landing, two "instrument rules"
for future sessions, and next actions ordered by adversarial (NERS) attacks that changed the
proposals during authoring. The documents are explicitly not canon and ratify nothing (loudly called
out per ED-1094): they measure the working tree at commit `f567c13`/2026-08-28 and record, for ten
gameplay systems, 338 classified architectural things with a status distribution (32% BUILT, 30%
DESIGNED, 15% PROPOSED, 13% INERT, 6% RULED-UNEXECUTED, 4% built-with-defect) and the finding that
"the code exists, is correct, and nothing calls it" (INERT/dormant) is as large a bucket as the
entire unratified proposal corpus. The headline architectural claim threading every part is that the
game currently resolves without any people in it (`world.npcs` is empty in every seeded campaign),
which the PR identifies (and a later PR, #338, formally rules via ED-IN-0201) as the single
highest-fan-out blocker in the tree.

**Provenance note for this log.** Of the 8 changed files, `research/cross_scale_action_catalogue_v1.md`
was **landed by the prior PR #336**, not by #337 — PR #337 only carries a 14-line diff to it (the
Compact→Leverage/Debt-subtype fix). I read the whole file for context (it is the action census the
integration master builds on) and log its diff in full plus a representative sample of its
pre-existing architecture content, marked `[pre-existing, PR #336]` in the Where column. The other 7
files (the two HANDOFF_IN sub-sections that are in-scope, the 4-part integration master, the 2-part
precedent companion) are new content genuinely authored by this PR.

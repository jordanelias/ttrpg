## 1. What the PR is, in 5 sentences

This PR lands Parts 3–8 of `research/valoria_game_precedent_companion_v1*.md` — a precedent-genre analysis (Total War, CK3, Victoria 3, JA2/JA3, Burning Wheel, RoTK, Dwarf Fortress, Mount & Blade, Kremlin, John Company, Republic of Rome, Venice, HoMM, etc.) that judges each of Valoria's eleven design systems against what the genre treats as table stakes, then reconciles 24 candidate mechanical imports down to four unified moves (U-1 Disclosure Contract, U-2 Accrual/Ledger/Budget, U-3 person-as-relationship-ledger, U-4 couple-the-scales). It commits the full evidence trail behind that document and its sibling `valoria_systems_integration_master_v1*.md`: `research/provenance/2026-08-28-systems-integration-harvest/`, containing 1,079 structured extraction records from 11 parallel "harvest lanes" (H1–H9, H7A/H7B, H8A/H8B) that read the design corpus, an adversarial "Gate A" pass that found and corrected several errors (most notably a settled ruling — ED-IN-0046 D3, Compact-as-Debt-subtype — that six lanes had independently mis-reported as still open), three synthesis-stage documents that flattened the corpus into per-system tables of primitives/derivatives/mechanics/processes annotated BUILT/INERT/DESIGNED/PROPOSED, and the raw JSON/YAML data artifacts plus extraction scripts. It also files and records execution of **ED-IN-0201**, a real Jordan ruling made in the authoring session: no faction action, settlement governance, or battle may execute without a person (leader/governor/commander) who also determines *which* choice is made from the same option set — which, combined with the measured fact that `world.npcs` is empty in every seeded campaign, means a campaign that obeys the ruling as written performs **zero faction actions** until an NPC loader lands. The document is explicitly `PROPOSED`/reference-only under CLAUDE.md §0.05 (prose is not mechanism) and the provenance directory is explicitly append-only, not-to-be-reasoned-from, containing pre-Gate-A errors.

## 3. THE SUBSTRATE (from 01_substrate.md) — primitives named exactly, quoted

**The single structural idea (quoted):** "There is exactly one kind of actor — a person — and exactly two relations laid over the set of persons: **containment**, a strict tree that answers *where you stand and whose jurisdiction your voice falls into by default*, and **alignment**, an unconstrained set-system that answers *who you have thrown in with*."

### 1. The two structures
- **1.1 Containment** — nodes: `Person → Hearth → Community → Settlement → Territory → Province → Realm`, single-parent, extended upward/sideways as needed. A person's **address** is the path to root. Derivation for single-parent: multi-parent would let divided loyalty evaporate as a set membership; forcing the second belonging into alignment makes it a commitment that "can be concealed, betrayed, and punished." Closed loop: written by birth/marriage/admission/migration/death; carried on the person; read by every aggregation/judging set/stake allocation. N-line: lose "one rung up," and with it jurisdiction/outsider meaning.
- **1.2 Alignment** — a **faction** = a proposition + a map from persons to a degree of commitment. "Not a level, a rank, or a class of thing." Membership per-person, may be secret, held at a degree.
- **1.3 Scale is derived, a profile not a number** — `presence(f,n)`, `density(f,n)`, `footprint(f)` computed by rolling member addresses up the containment tree. Rejects "scale = lowest spanning containment node" (brittle; one member abroad promotes a village conspiracy to realm scale). "No act is unlocked or forbidden by a faction's size, and no roll takes it as a term." BUT: revolt is a comparison between committed density and coercive-apparatus capacity — a fact about the world, not anyone's estimate. **Two profiles**: the **true profile** (from actual memberships, readable by NOBODY — bookkeeping/tests only) and the **estimated profile** (per-observer, from that observer's own claim ledger only). This split closes a "banned object" back door — rolling up true membership for perception/threat-assessment would be a true-state derivation reaching a decision without passing through `witness`.
- **1.4 Growth/shrink is one operation** — `commit(person, faction, Δdegree)`. Degree→0 is departure. No merge/split/promote/found ops. Schism = subset migrating to a rival proposition; merger = members of A committing to B.

### 2. The person is the only actor
Six fields, each with a derivation for why it can't be cut: **Address**, **Marks** (ascribed public attributes), **Capability** (Attributes+Practices, the resolver's pool), **Stance** (one table: referent→attitude, feelings and beliefs fused), **Memory** (the claim ledger, owned by §3), **Ties** (ordinary contacts + Knots, TS-gated deep bindings). Personality = exactly two scalars inside the stance table: `credulity`, `obstinacy`. **Needs are not a field** — computed each tick, but split by what they read: subsistence/standing read **the world**; commitment/exposure read **the view** — "needs are never stale relative to the person's view, and are supposed to be stale relative to the world." A person with no office can still act — office changes only whether a decision binds others. **Populations are persons at coarse fidelity**: cohorts (one record, weight, evaluated once) that **individuate** (split into person + smaller cohort) on naming/spread/etc, and — the substrate's own addition beyond the initial six-field draft — **individuation has an inverse**: "A person persists exactly as long as somebody remembers them," reference-counting the world's memory rather than an authoring budget.

### 3. The signature rule
```
choose(person, view)    -> act      # agents. no world argument. ever.
resolve(act, world)     -> event    # the world. no agent argument.
witness(person, event)  -> claim    # the only bridge, and it is per-person
```
- **3.1 View is assembled, not filtered.** Memory = ledger of claims `(subject, predicate, value, when, source, confidence)`. Absence of a claim = absence in view (never a blur). View budget: at most K claims by `salience = recency × confidence × relevance × stance weight` — motivated reasoning "falling out of the ranking for free."
- **3.2 Belief is not capped.** "If the Praefect believes the levy was paid, then for the purpose of his choice it was paid; there is no −2 and no cap." Correction comes from collision with the world via `witness`, moderated by obstinacy.
- **3.3 Three corollaries**: **Divergent witnessing** (witness takes person first, no consensus deposit possible by signature); **Telling is an act** (`tell(speaker, hearer, claim, as_asserted)`, the gap between asserted and held IS the lie); **Corroboration fails closed** (every claim source is `firsthand`, `told_by`, or `inferred`, no null source, independence measured by differing firsthand roots, synthetic root shared by all retellings of a rootless rumour). **Claim identity is a tuple never a string** — `when` is mandatory, so assertion and denial collide computationally.

### 4. The rungs, and what each owns
A rung = "a role, not a class" — any containment node with a shared stake, a judging set, addressability. **Individual** — owns the only write path. **Hearth (Family)** — owns transmission across time: **larder**, **succession pointer**, **obligation edge**. Cadet branches "fall out" of a succession pointer leading nowhere, not authored. **Community** — owns peer judgment and the admission gate: **judging set** and **admission**; holds NO state of its own — "a norm is the aggregate of member stances on a proposition, computed on demand." Test for containment-vs-alignment: "A cell people live in is a community; a cell people belong to while living elsewhere is a faction." "The Church of Solmund is a faction; a parish is a community; a Dicastery is neither — it is an office cluster" (delegated to doc 07). **Settlement** — owns the first genuinely zero-sum stake and the first **office** — "a mark plus a binding power, held by a person, revocable."
- **4.1 Sibling competition is one function**: `contest(container, prize, claimants)`, claimants are factions (need not be tree-siblings), prizes ∈ {the stake, regard, offices}.

### 5. Up-stroke and down-stroke
Two objects, one transport (claims/tellings from §3). **5.1 Petition (up)**: `Petition(petitioner, proposition, respondent_container, backing)`. `carry(person, petition)` at each rung; carrier may **forward/amend/bundle/drop**. Dropping is "an act, by a named person, at a named time." Deposits grievance into backers if/when they learn of the drop. Revolt = commitment density crossing coercive-apparatus capacity, never a meter. **5.2 Dispensation (down)**: `Dispensation(issuer, proposition, scope, terms)`. Travels by being **noticed** — publication is a telling that deposits claims by presence/channel, never by post. Opening = computed via `EV(act|current_claims(person))`, never stored/authored. **5.3 Standing dates**: containers carry scheduled contestable moments making propositions contestable.

### 6. What is refused, under E-as-a-ratio (explicit list)
Faction tier/level/scale field; faction-wide reputation scalar; settlement unrest/loyalty/morale gauge; a "known %" on a secret; a world event log agents can read; any broadcast depositing the same value into many persons; a separate population/mass model; a confidence cap on belief; faction merge/split/promote/found-at-size ops; a quest/opportunity object; container-level memory/ledgers/councils; a personality trait vector (beyond credulity/obstinacy); a grievance-to-revolt threshold.

### 7. One trace, both directions
Worked trace of Greta (Southern Einhir, Restoration sympathiser), a dispensation (salt blockade), a petition, a drop, a commitment cascade — "Nothing in that paragraph was authored."

## 4. THE CODE SHAPE (from 11_code_shape.md) — reproduced near-completely

**Method note (quoted):** "adjudicated WITHOUT REFERENCE TO ANY EXISTING CODE. No module, signature, registry or test in this repository was read, cited, or treated as a constraint."

**The adjudication in one sentence:** "The module hierarchy IS the containment ladder, the type system is the throughline enforcement mechanism, and every rule in this document exists because a design property that would otherwise be a discipline becomes, under it, something the compiler or the call site refuses to express."

### §1 Why the levels are the game's levels
```
world/
  rung/person/      rung/hearth/      rung/community/
  rung/settlement/  rung/territory/   rung/province/    rung/realm/
```
"Parent–child in the module tree means containment in the world." Table: inputs distributing downward at increasing granularity = **T6**; outputs aggregating upward = **T5**; every rung the same module type at a different level = T5/T6 at every rung, no elite-only mechanism. **§1.1 Alignment is not in the tree**: `faction/` sits BESIDE `world/`, never inside it — the moment a faction has a level it can't grow across one without an authoring act.

### §2 The three signatures, as types
```
choose  : (Person, View)   -> Act
resolve : (Act,    World)  -> [Event]
witness : (Person, Event)  -> [Claim]
```
`choose` has no `World` parameter — "not a masked world, not a read-only world, not a world behind an accessor." `resolve` has no `Person` parameter (keeps per-actor special cases out). `witness` takes person FIRST, per-person by type — "Consensus broadcast is a type error." **The honest-keeping rule**: `View` must be a distinct type from `World`, no coercion, no shared supertype, no field of `View` holding a `World`. **§2.1**: View is assembled from claim ledger by bounded query, "smaller than the truth ... not blurrier." Absence of a claim = absence in view, never a widened interval.

### §3 Ownership: who may write what
| owner | holds |
|---|---|
| Person | address, marks, capability, stance, claim ledger, ties |
| Container (a rung) | its stake, its judging set, its standing dates. Nothing else. |
| Faction | its proposition and its commitment map |
| Nobody | aggregates, norms, densities, needs, openings, scale, reputation |

"Every aggregate is a function, never a field." **R-1** (⚠ marked in-file as SUPERSEDED by adjudication C-1): rung may read own state + addressed messages; may NOT read sibling/descendant state directly; MAY compute a descendant aggregate on demand when asked; may NOT receive a pushed aggregate; may NOT store one. **R-2**: a rung module may write only its own state; upward influence = emitting an aggregate; downward = emitting a refraction; "No module reaches through another."

### §4 One resolver, three fidelities in code
```
played    : the player supplies the Act at each decision point
witnessed : the engine supplies the Act; the player observes the trace
auto      : the engine supplies the Act; no trace is retained
```
"Identical `resolve`, identical rolls, identical seeds." The test that matters is distribution-shape, not mean.

### §5 Determinism, seeding, replay
Per-attempt substreams derived from stable identity: seed = hash(world seed, tick, actor id, attempt discriminator). Order-independence property is the property to guard (its absence is invisible). Replay is a re-run, not a log. Corollary: "no decision function may read the event log."

### §6 Compute budget
Cohorts individuate on naming/spread. "This must be one type, not two." Generation is on demand, never on a clock.

### §7 What must NOT exist in the codebase — table (quoted verbatim, key rows)
| forbidden | because |
|---|---|
| a `World` parameter on any decision function | T4 collapses |
| a `view_of(world, person)` that masks rather than assembles | someone eventually masks nothing |
| any function taking `[Person]` and one `Event` | consensus broadcast |
| a deposit into a cohort that carries a VALUE rather than a DISTRIBUTION | consensus broadcast laundered through the cohort type — "this row exists because the defect passed every other row in this table" |
| a pushed aggregate, or a field one is stored in | needs a landing site = stored aggregate state |
| a stored aggregate, norm, density, unrest or reputation field | dead state that reads as mechanism |
| a knowledge value stored on the thing known | can't be planted or refuted |
| a second resolver, an auto-resolve formula, a fast path | 20-year unsolved genre divergence |
| a `tier`/`level`/`scale` field on a faction | growth becomes discontinuous |
| a flat additive modifier from a person onto a roll | worth X/(0.671·√Pool), helps weak pools more — backwards |
| a personal effect on a group that is not a fraction of that group | the only concrete anti-leverage rule the precedent corpus supplies |
| a scheduled recovery tick on standing | converts consequence into treadmill |
| a per-entity branch anywhere in the resolver | scripting drift |
| an authored per-person opportunity/quest object | churning world turns back into content |

### §8 What a test proves here — four falsifiable structural tests (quoted, condensed)
1. No decision function can see the world — assert by inspecting signatures, not grepping a string.
2. Two witnesses of one event can disagree — construct opposed stances, assert deposited claims differ.
3. A person with no office can act/petition/receive an opportunity — one fixture, three assertions.
4. Order independence — resolve a tick, permute actor order, assert identical outcomes.
"What must NOT be built: validators over the design documents, freshness checkers, guards on the guards, or any apparatus whose subject is the repository's own process rather than the game."

### §9 The honest limits of this adjudication
Written without reading existing code, by instruction. N=1 to N=1000+ leverage problem NOT solved, only bounded (fractions not flat amounts). Per-person claim ledger cost scales with tellings, not persons — "whether that holds at world scale is an empirical question this document cannot answer by reasoning."

## 5. THE RESOLUTION SURFACE (from 10_resolution_surface.md) — reproduced near-completely

**What this document owns (quoted):** specifies "what `resolve` does at the instant an act's outcome is uncertain." Two functions: `roll(pool) -> successes` and `obstacle(context) -> target`.

### §1 The core roll
- N ten-sided dice; 1–6 = 0 successes, 7–9 = 1 success, 10 = 2 successes. Per-die P(0)=0.6, P(1)=0.3, P(2)=0.1, mean 0.5, variance 0.45, σ≈0.671. Reasoning: pool > DC+mod because a target number must be **computed** not assigned by a nonexistent GM.
- **Pool assembly**: `Pool(person, practice) = Attribute[relevant](person) + Practice[practice](person)`. Attributes 1–7 (nine named + a ruled-but-unnamed tenth), Practice 0–7. Realistic pool 1–14.
- Probability table for pools 3–14 and the full distribution at Pool 8.
- **Closed loop / N-line** for `roll`: cut it and the bigger-Capability side always wins — collapses "even the skilled can fail" texture.

### §2 The obstacle, with one owner
```
obstacle(context):
    if context.opponent is a person:
        return OPPOSED    # both sides roll, live
    R = resistance_pool(context)
    if R <= 1:
        return 0          # uncontested — automatic Clean Success
    return round_half_up(R / 2)
```
"The obstacle formula is not a different idea from the roll — it is the roll's own expected value, E[roll(ResistancePool)], used deterministically because the resisting thing has no agency to vary." **Ceiling**: if Obstacle > 2×Pool, attempt is impossible and the resolver refuses to roll. **§2.2 worked case**: Masterpiece Examination obstacle computed on-demand from sitting masters' individual stances toward the candidate's marks — never a stored caste penalty.

### §3 Degrees of outcome
Margin = successes − Obstacle.
| Margin | Band |
|---|---|
| ≤ −2 | Disaster |
| −1 | Failure |
| 0 | Costed Success |
| +1, +2 | Clean Success |
| ≥ +3 | Overwhelming |
Costed Success table across pool/obstacle combos runs 14–28% everywhere. "Failure test run properly": even at Pool 14 vs Obstacle 2, Disaster is 0.08% — genuinely reachable, never zero except via the §2 ceiling.

### §4 Opposed contests
"Same function, live on both sides" — `obstacle` returns `OPPOSED`, both call `roll`. Win-probability table by pool gap; past a gap of ~6, underdog chance falls under ~11%. "The honest response is not to hide the mismatch ... it is to publish it" — publish inputs and band probabilities, never the trigger.

### §5 What a player chooses — manoeuvres (each must alter a primitive)
5.1 Reframe the pool source (free). 5.2 Contest the venue, not the fight (redirects to a different obstacle entirely). 5.3 Escalate the stake (Costed Success now counts as Failure; symmetric consequence swap). 5.4 Draw aid from a Knot (real sourced dice, +1 Strain, self-limiting via Rupture).

### §6 No flat modifiers from persons
"0.671 is the constant for this die" — a flat shift of X successes = `X / (0.671·√Pool)` in standardized terms, inversely proportional to √Pool. Aid survives because it is sourced (a real second pool). Marks survive because they change eligibility/obstacle composition, never a flat penalty. **Leader affecting a group is NOT owned here — doc 09 §6 owns it in full.** An earlier `ΔPool_group = f × Pool_group_base` form is explicitly WITHDRAWN (third leader formula, required a leader rating that doesn't exist).

### §7 Randomness, determinism, replay
`substream = hash(world_seed, actor_id, act_type, target_id, tick, sequence)`. Four guaranteed invariants with their own check method: Reproducibility, Order-independence, Preview-transparency (odds computed analytically, never calls `roll`), Fidelity-invariance.

### §8 Setting content kept
8.1 Thread Sensitivity/Thread Pool = `floor(TS/10)`, a second sourced pool through the identical `roll`. 8.3 Coherence bands 10→0 resize Thread Pool by fraction/step, never rolled itself; reachability check run to conclude Severed is reachable "inside the span of a single campaign," corrected from an erroneous "roughly a decade" (seasons≠years bug) to ten seasons = 2.5 years. 8.4 Composure: per-scene resource depleted by margin of loss in opposed social contests.

### §9 What is refused
Second resolver of any kind (strongest refusal in the corpus); critical-hit tables; per-skill subsystems; confidence cap on the roll; exploding/open-ended dice (breaks fixed substream-stepping); separate auto-resolve formula. **Momentum — CUT, explicit retraction** with three reasons: false N-line (Conviction IS a stance row per B-8), residue was a flat +1 die (the one shape §6 refuses), and it broke fidelity-invariance (scene/session frames don't exist at AUTO). "Two independent auditors reached the cut separately."

### §10 One attempt, three fidelities
Worked trace: Greta vs a Knight of the Peace, same `attempt_id`/substream across Played/Witnessed/Auto — "fidelity is a camera, not a second engine."

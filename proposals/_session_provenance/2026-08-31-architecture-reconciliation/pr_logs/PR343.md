folds everything the testing found into one 2,017-line superseding document,

design. The headline result: PR #342's core substrate (one actor/person, two structures
containment+alignment, three signatures `choose`/`resolve`/`witness`, one resolver/roll/obstacle,
fourteen forbidden shapes) survived almost unchanged; the corpus's failure mode is **termination**, not
generation — stories start easily and rarely end, because the design refuses thresholds and the only
forcing object is a standing date with a convener who can simply not convene. The fix is stated as
"four enlargements that are one structural promotion": the standing date becomes the design's spine,
via (1) convening conditions/"watches" that schedule dates without deciding anything, (2) petitions
retyped to address an office (never a mechanism) with expiry-by-supersession, (3) commons damage
modeled as option removal rather than an obstacle modifier, and (4) a new one-way `transfer` act that
lets goods move between unrelated persons. The whole PR runs under an explicit, heavily self-policed
adversarial-relay protocol (agonist writes, antagonist attacks blind, orchestrator adjudicates) that
itself becomes a subject of the document — round 1 found and deleted an entire invented mechanism built
on a refuted premise, round 2 found 9 of 16 challenges were regressions in round 1's own prose, and the

### §0.2 — the design in one page (verbatim primitive roster)
One actor (**a person**). Two structures: **containment** (strict single-parent tree) and
**alignment** (unconstrained set system; faction = proposition + commitment map at any scale). Three
signatures carry everything. One resolver, one roll, one obstacle. Containers hold a stake, judging
set, standing dates; offices hold a remit and their own dates; **nobody** holds an aggregate. Knowledge
lives only in ledgers.

**The organizing claim:** the four enlargements are ONE structural promotion seen from four sides — the
standing date stops being one object among many and becomes the design's spine. A convening condition
(§7) causes a date to exist. A petition (§8) addresses an office that will answer at one. A lost option
(§10) and an unpaid wage (§11) are what people convene *about*. Underneath all four: **there is no
fallback** (§13) — if no person acts, the thing does not occur.

### §1 — THE SUBSTRATE
- §1.1 **One actor.** Persons act; nothing else does. Containers/factions/settlements do not decide.
- §1.2 **Containment is a tree, single-parent on purpose.** `Person → Hearth → Community → Settlement →
  Territory → Province → Realm`, extended as needed. Every person exactly one parent hearth; every
  hearth exactly one community. Address = path to root. Multi-parent containment refused explicitly
  (Einhir smith counterexample) — "if a person can be contained twice, divided loyalty becomes a set
  membership and evaporates." Cut it and you lose: a defined "one rung up," jurisdiction, outsider-ness.
- §1.3 **Alignment is a set-system with no tier field.** Faction = proposition + map(person→degree of
  commitment). Two brothers = a faction; Church of Solmund = a faction. Membership per-person, may be
  secret, held at a degree. **Scale is derived and gates nothing** — `presence(f,n)`, `density(f,n)`,
  `footprint(f)` roll up from member addresses with no declaration. Capacity to act at a node routes
  through persons individually `eligible` (`07:180-182`). **Two profiles**: a **true** profile
  (computed from actual memberships, nobody may read it) and an **estimated** profile (computed from
  one person's own claim ledger, readable only by them). Underestimation is the default. **One
  membership operation:** `commit(person, faction, Δdegree)`. Degree to zero is departure. No merge,
  split, promote, found-at-size.
- §1.4 **The three signatures**, ruled as plural on `resolve`:
  ```
  choose  : (Person, View)   -> Act        # no world argument. ever.
  resolve : (Acts,  World)   -> [Event]    # no person argument.
  witness : (Person, Event)  -> [Claim]    # the only bridge, per-person
  ```
  `choose` has no `World` — omniscience unspellable, not just uncaught. `resolve` has no `Person` — no
  per-actor special cases. `witness` takes person first — no collection-of-persons signature exists;
  **consensus broadcast is a type error.** `View` must be a distinct type from `World`: no coercion, no
  shared supertype, no field of `View` holding a `World`. **`View` is assembled, not filtered** (§3.3):
  absence of a claim → absence in the view, never a widened interval (ignorance ≠ uncertainty).
  ⚠ Ruling C-note: #342 ships three spellings (`11:57-59` singular, `01:212-216` singular, `09:819-821`
  plural); this doc rules plural `resolve` because a season's acts resolve together and conflict between
  them is first-class (§6.3).

### §2 — THE PERSON
Six fields (derived by asking which property becomes unreachable without each): **Address**
(jurisdiction, aggregation), **Marks** (ascribed publicly-read attributes — heritage, caste standing,
guild grade, house name, Church standing, visible Thread sensitivity; "what make the same act by two
persons produce different results"), **Capability** (aptitudes/practices — the pool the resolver
rolls), **Stance** (one table, `referent → attitude`; referents = persons/factions/propositions/places),
**Memory** (the claim ledger, §3), **Ties** (ordinary contacts + **Knots** — a person's few deep
bindings, gated on Thread Sensitivity ≥ 30 both ends; roughly half the peninsula cannot form one — every
formal institution gates Southern Einhir out, the deepest informal channel gates them in — deliberate
asymmetry, "do not later fix it"). **Personality = two scalars inside the stance table**: credulity
(how far a telling moves me) and obstinacy (how hard my stance resists contradiction). No other traits.

**Needs are computed each tick, not a field**, and read differently:

| need | reads |
|---|---|
| subsistence | the world (larder vs mouths) |
| standing | the world (peers) |
| commitment | the view (must believe unmet) |
| exposure | the view (must have heard of it) |

So needs are never stale relative to view, but supposed to be stale relative to world (crier latency
produces distorted need computation).

**A person with no office can still act** — action eligibility never consults office; office changes
only whether a decision binds others; marks/regard change only odds. Untrained attempt legal, small
pool; practice rank *adds verbs*, doesn't constitute the list; postless fisher's shortfall arithmetic
identical to a Duke's. **The design's low end is right and this document does not touch it** (validated
by §17.1's testing summary — see below, this is a load-bearing survival claim later PARTLY REVERSED by
Part 3 reconciliation, §11 of this log discusses).

**Populations are persons at coarse fidelity** — a **cohort** (persons sharing address/marks/stance) is
one record, a weight, evaluated once, applied to all. Individuates when an event names a member or
stance spread exceeds honesty threshold. One type, not two (avoids elite-only-politics-by-accident).
**Individuation has an inverse**: re-merges when holding no Knot, no office, no live petition, and named
by no other ledger. *"A person persists exactly as long as somebody remembers them."*

### §3 — KNOWLEDGE, CLAIMS, AND THE VIEW
- §3.1 **The claim**: `Claim = (subject, predicate, value, when, source, confidence, visibility)`.
  `when` is a mandatory closed interval, universal not existential (asserts value held *throughout*).
  **Collision**: same subject/predicate-form/arguments, intersecting `when`, incompatible values —
  computed at deposit time, one ledger at a time. **Predicate vocabulary CLOSED; referent space OPEN.**
  Three ops only: collision, entailment, relevance, functions of predicate *form*. Subjects include
  other claims (`SAID(Aldwin, C, season 12)`) — makes a lie first-class.
- §3.2 **Corroboration fails closed.** Sources: `firsthand(event_id)`, `told_by(person, handle)`,
  `inferred(claim_id...)`, `firsthand_via_knot(event_id)`. No null source. `witness` is the only op that
  mints a root token; a Knot deposit *reuses* the originating event's id (five partners feeling one
  rupture = one token).
- §3.3 **View assembly and motivated reasoning**:
  ```
  view(person, question) -> at most K claims
  K = 7 + Focus + 2 per Knot consulted − Coherence penalty (Dissonant 1 … Severed 5)
  salience(c) = recency(c) × confidence_live(c) × relevance(c,q) × stanceweight(c,person)
  stanceweight(c) = clamp(1 + λ·agreement(c), 0.05, 2.0),   λ = obstinacy/5
  ```
  Floor 0.05 (not 0) so a devastating firsthand contradiction can still cross. **What's attenuated is
  retrieval, not value.** Ruling: `K = 7 + Focus` (doc 03) beats `09:63`'s constant 12 (§3.4 conflict
  table below).
- §3.4 **THE CONFLICT RULE**: where #342's docs disagree about an object, the doc whose *declared
  subject* is that object wins; a doc asserting a value it does not derive loses to the doc that derives
  it. Four rulings under this rule:
  | collision | ruled | ground |
  |---|---|---|
  | `K` (03:325-329 `7+Focus` vs 09:63 constant 12) | `7 + Focus` | doc 03's subject is view assembly |
  | practice rank (02:153 `0-5` vs 10:33 `0-7`) | **0-5**, pool 1-12 | rank is a field of doc 02's tuple; doc 02 uses it structurally (rank≥3, rank≥5 verb ladder) |
  | Thread term placement (02:197-200 inside pool vs 10:192 second pool) | **second pool through same `roll`** | same ground; alternative violates §14 row 10 in spirit |
  `exposure` explicitly NOT in the table — five senses, five objects, the rule selects nothing; scoped
  by fiat to §5.4's pre-roll odds preview sense only.

### §4 — THE RUNGS, AND THE FIVE OWNERS
- §4.1 What each rung owns. **Individual** = only write path (acts, claims, stance). **Hearth** =
  transmission across time — larder (§11.1), succession pointer, obligation edge (`requisition`).
  Cadet branches: not authored, just a hearth whose succession pointer misses the main line.
  **Community** = peer judgment + admission gate; judging set = who hears by default; admission = act by
  standing-holders changing another's address/mark; community holds no state of its own (norm =
  aggregate of member stances, computed on demand). "A cell people live in is a community; a cell people
  belong to while living elsewhere is a faction" — settles Church-vs-parish-vs-Dicastery. **Settlement**
  = first contested material stake + first office (granary exclusivity). **Sibling competition** is one
  function at every rung: `contest(container, prize, claimants)`, claimants are factions.
- §4.2 **Ownership table — FIVE rows** (amended from #342's four):
  | owner | holds |
  |---|---|
  | Person | address, marks, capability, stance, ledger, ties, `Holding` edges, commitment edges |
  | Container (a rung) | its stake(s) · judging set · standing dates (each with capacity + convening conditions) · **the matter it holds**: hearth `stores`, site `condition` (§10.2), transmission pointer. **No social aggregate, ever** |
  | Office | post, node, remit, conferral/revocation rules, establishment, `seat_items` (§4.3), upkeep, **own standing dates** |
  | Faction | proposition + commitment map |
  | Nobody | aggregates, norms, densities, needs, openings, scale, reputation |
  **The Office row** and **the Container matter clause** are both amendments, stated not smuggled. New
  general rule: **"A container may hold MATTER and DATES. It may never hold a SOCIAL AGGREGATE."** —
  provenance, not location, is the line. **An office is not a container** — no stake, no judging set,
  not in the containment tree; `Holding := (person, office, since, conferrer)` is an edge on the person.
  "Who holds the praefecture is a query, not a field. Nothing anywhere stores control."
  **R-1**: a rung module reads own state + addressed messages; may NOT read a sibling's/descendant's
  state directly; MAY compute an aggregate over descendants on demand; may NOT receive a pushed one or
  store one. "Compute-on-demand, never push, never store."
  **R-2**: writes only its own state. "The module tree IS the containment ladder." `faction/` sits
  beside `world/`, never inside it.
- §4.3 **The two capacity quantities**: `capacity(date)` (owned by the container/office holding the
  date — how many items a sitting hears; carried petitions may exceed it, convener chooses) and
  `seat_items(office)` (owned by the office, spent by its holder — how many things he can hear or carry
  in a sitting). `carry` spends ONE OF EACH. No third quantity. Cap on live convening conditions =
  `seat_items` (fired date consumes the convener's hours). ⚠ §17.4's original "missing capacity object"
  finding narrowed to specifically **the establishment** (named persons an office employs) — not
  capacity generally.
- §4.4 **Office, completely**:
  ```
  Office  := (post, node, remit, conferral, revocation, establishment, seat_items, upkeep, dates)
  remit   := (acts[], scope_node, binds)
  binds   ∈ { members-by-admission, persons-by-presence }
  ```
  `remit.acts` drawn from a **closed set of five**: **issue** (dispensation, §9), **determine** (one
  person's decision at a venue, §12), **confer/revoke**, **dispatch** (`requisition` on the
  establishment), **convene**. **`convene` names TWO distinct operations, each costing an act when
  performed**: setting a standing date, and ordering its items (`compose_agenda`, §8.4). **Office
  changes the option set and the pool source — never a modifier.** A flat shift is worth
  `X/(0.671·√Pool)`, helping weak sides more (backwards). When an act is performed *by remit*, the pool
  is drawn from the **establishment**, not the holder's own capability. Three costs: `seat_items`,
  **publicity** (every act by remit is public), **upkeep** (unpaid establishment becomes a faction,
  "treats plunder as wages").
- §4.5 **Conferral basis** — analyzed but NOT ruled (recommendation offered, withdrawn as a ruling per
  D-7). Recommendation: `confer` is performed BY REMIT, basis = the conferring OFFICE, act performed by
  whoever holds it. Argument: person-rooted conferral terminates the graph at every dead conferrer, so
  `sovereign_fraction(root)` is undefined across most of the graph and the Crown cannot be played across
  a succession; office-rooted, the graph resolves and root-plurality is a political condition not an
  invariant. Cost of the other answer: person-rooted makes a warrant a personal bond that dies with the
  man. Returned to §16 as a live choice for Jordan.

### §5 — THE RESOLUTION SURFACE
- §5.1 **One roll.** N ten-sided dice, 1-6 nothing, 7-9 one success, 10 two successes. Mean =
  Pool÷2, σ≈0.671/die. `Pool(person, practice) = Attribute[relevant] + Practice[practice]`. Attributes
  1-7 (nine named + ruled-but-unnamed tenth, formula never inspects name). Practice 0-5 (ruled), 0 =
  never trained but always legal. Realistic pool 1-12. Rank≥3 adds verbs, rank≥5 adds verbs unreachable
  below it. "A difficulty number must be decided by somebody, and that somebody is the GM this game does
  not have" — pool vs target-number-plus-modifier justification.
- §5.2 **One obstacle, one owner**:
  ```
  obstacle(context):
      if context.opponent is a person: return OPPOSED
      R = resistance_pool(context)
      if R <= 1: return 0                       # auto clean success, no roll
      return round_half_up(R / 2)
  ```
  `Obstacle > 2×Pool` ⇒ resolver refuses to roll (impossible). Masterpiece Examination = worked
  institutional case: resistance computed on demand from sitting masters' stances toward candidate's
  marks — nothing stored, no caste number.
- §5.3 **Degrees**: `Margin = successes − Obstacle`. ≤−2 Disaster · −1 Failure · 0 Costed Success · +1/+2
  Clean Success · ≥+3 Overwhelming. Costed Success runs 16.8%–28.1% at balanced pools (Pool ≥3);
  #342's own "14-28% in every row" gloss is FALSE against its own outmatched-row 10.6% figure (D-13
  correction). Overwhelming unreachable at Pool 1-2 (scoped correction, C-19). Opposed contest = same
  `roll` called twice, deterministic obstacle replaced by an actual draw; underdog collapses past pool
  gap ~6; design chooses to *publish* the mismatch rather than hide it.
- §5.4 **Pre-roll exposure/odds preview.** Resolver publishes both pool sizes + obstacle interpretation
  before any roll, "never calls roll." A publication-rule PARTITION (four rows), corrected from a
  round-1 claim of "self-enforcing" which was false:
  | resists | preview publishes | why |
  |---|---|---|
  | material/publicly inspectable | the scalar | anyone can look |
  | resistance composed from private stances (judging set) | a BAND, never scalar | §1.3 forbids true-profile reads — **change to #342, which published the composed scalar** |
  | hidden world state gated behind investigation (§10 site condition) | a band; scalar never an operand of any roll | enforced by construction |
  | opponent's pool in opposed contest | published deliberately | `10:132`'s stated policy — legibility chosen on purpose |
  Residual (§15.15): the partition is a rule, only its third row structurally enforced; a resistance
  neither plainly material nor plainly stance-composed (forged document quality, unseen fortification)
  has no ruled row.
- §5.5 **Determinism.** Per-attempt substreams from `hash(world seed, tick, actor id, attempt
  discriminator)`, never a shared sequence. Order independence is the property to guard. Replay is a
  re-run, not a log; **no decision function may read the event log**. Three fidelities (played,
  witnessed, auto) differ only in who is asked to choose, never in how outcome is computed — identical
  `resolve`, rolls, seeds.

### §6 — THE SEASON — SEVEN PHASES, THREE WRITE CLASSES
- §6.1 Tick = a season. **One discretionary act per person/cohort.** ⚠ **D-2, the act economy, is
  OPEN and the LARGEST open ruling in the design** — #342 asserts one act (`09:33`) then narrates a Duke
  taking seven verbs under a header claiming ten (`14:562`), wrong under every reading; `seat_items`
  (`14:91-92`) is already a fourth per-holder capacity against "three quantities and nothing else."
  Decides whether Duke play is a personnel game or a decree game. §16 carries it.
- §6.2 **Seven phases** (simultaneous within a phase):
  | | phase | what happens |
  |---|---|---|
  | P0 | CALENDAR | advance date · fire due standing dates into docket · **evaluate convening conditions, schedule the dates they name (§7)** · recompute option availability |
  | P1 | SETTLE | metabolism/nature only: larders vs mouths, production (`yield` rolled, `season_factor`+d10, §11.1), wounds close/fester, ageing, travel. No social quantity, no act effect, **no site `condition` write** (§10.4) |
  | P2 | NEEDS | computed from situation, pure/parallel, never stored |
  | P3 | VIEW | top-K claims by salience (§3.3); K=3 per cohort |
  | P4 | CHOOSE | `choose(person, view) -> act`, everyone, against frozen P1 snapshot |
  | P5 | RESOLVE | `resolve(acts, world) -> events` |
  | P6 | WITNESS | events fan out by presence/channel; `witness` per person |
  | P7 | RECKON | claim confidence decays; ledgers evict lowest salience; cohorts individuate/de-individuate |
  Reaction latency = one season, so surprise is structural. Contests subdivide the tick (nested loop).
- §6.3 **Three write classes, and no others may be added.**
  | class | phase | what may be written | licensed because |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | calendar | P0 | dates and dockets | a date is not an outcome, it's an occasion |
  | matter | P1 | larders, bodies, travel, `yield` roll | metabolism/nature |
  | acts | P5 | everything else, **including every condition delta an act caused** | a person did something |
  Convening conditions add nothing new — evaluated in P0, which already writes dates. A site's
  `condition` is written in P5 only, all deltas are act effects (§10.4) — self-correction from an
  earlier draft that put it in P1 (C-17).
- §6.4 **Conflict between acts.** Every act declares `touches: {(object, mode)}`, mode ∈
  `{read, alter, exclude}`. Conflict iff share an object and either mode is `exclude`, or both `alter`
  the same field. Conflicts route to `contest`. Ties break on `hash(act-id, world-seed)` — never rank,
  office or list position. Resolution strata: movement first, then binding decisions at docket dates,
  then contested physical acts, then uncontested material acts, then social acts last.

### §7 — THE CALENDAR, AND CONVENING CONDITIONS *(enlargement 1)*
- §7.1 The object:
  ```
  ConveningCondition := (holder, predicate, date_form, set_by, set_at)
     holder     ∈ Container | Office
     predicate  : pure over holder's own readable state, or the calendar. PUBLISHED AS A BAND.
     date_form  : (venue, horizon, convener office)

  ```
  **Named "convening condition," not "watch"** — #342 already uses "the watch" for soldiery
  (`12:37,99`); reusing the word would be the exact idempotent-vocabulary failure `CLAUDE.md` §4
  records. "Convening condition" is ordinary charter English.
- §7.2 **A new composition, ZERO exact shipped instances** (retraction of an earlier claim of "four

- §7.3 **The provenance rule** (C1-C5): C1 PROVENANCE — attaching is `convene`'s first operation

### §10 — MATTER, THE COMMONS, AND OPTION REMOVAL *(enlargement 3)*
- §10.1 **The inversion.** "Invert it. Damage to a commons REMOVES AN OPTION. It never adds
  difficulty." A silted harbour doesn't make sailing harder — it makes cargo impossible. Legible without
  a gauge. Second-order: political faction forms out of a physical fact with no authoring (§1.3's
  faction object).
- §10.2 **The object.** A **site** = matter the design already owns (holding, route, seam, channel,
  fishery). `condition(site) ∈ [0,1]`, touched via existing `alter`/`exclude` modes. **Cross-rung
  semantics** (three rules): primary state lives at the finest node the act names; coarser reads are
  computed on demand; no coarser rung stores one. Aggregation = draw-weighted mean:
  `condition(n) = Σ condition(c) × draw_share(c,n)`.
- §10.3 **The sizing rule**:
  ```
  Δcondition(site) = − condition(site) × f(degree) × share(actor, site)     -- P5 only
  f(Disaster)=f(Failure)=0, f(Costed)=1/16, f(Clean)=1/8, f(Overwhelming)=1/4
  share(actor, site) = actor's own draw ÷ site's total draw
  ```
  Bound: "you can never move more of a site than your own share of it, times a degree fraction." At a
  many-drawer commons, closure requires collective effort across many actors/seasons (the tragedy of
  the commons shape). ⚠ At a single-drawer site (`share=1`), one Overwhelming season moves 1/4 of
  condition — "correct behaviour, not leverage" (no group at share=1). Falsifier: fraction moved must
  fall as N (drawer count) rises. **Discrete destruction (`exclude`, e.g. burning a granary) is NOT
  cleared** against the anti-leverage row (`forestall` precedent doesn't transfer — it's a purchase with
  surviving goods, arson has no purchase price and destroys goods). Demoted to a stated limit (§15.17).
- §10.4 **Band gating**: `verbs(site,n) = {v : condition(n) ≥ floor(v)}`. Published in full with inputs,
  never with the trigger point. **`depletion` REDEFINED, after two attempts (first had a sign error
  producing enrichment; C-9 corrected round 1 then D-1 found ROUND 1's own fix relocated the units error
  by adding a dimensionless multiplier centered on 1.0 to a [0,1] quantity and inverting `13:70-71`'s
  permanence semantics).** Final corrected form: `base(H)` does not move; `condition` is an ACT-ONLY
  accumulator:
  ```
  yield(H, season) = base(H) × condition(site) × season_factor(territory) × (3+d10)/8.5
  condition(site) = clamp(condition(site) + Σ(this season's resolved condition deltas), 0, 1)   -- P5 only, ACTS ONLY
  ```
  Honest cost: a site that decays with nobody touching it cannot be modeled by this accumulator — must
  be a matter event under §10.6's three conditions (real narrowing, §15.19).
- §10.5 **Exposure, band-quantized.** The obstacle term derived from a site's condition is the band's
  representative value, never the scalar (§5.4's exposure rule; this is the case that forced it).
- §10.6 **The matter-channel licence, claimed under three conditions, all required**: (1) quantity
  crossed is matter or bodies, never social — the design admits exactly THREE clock-driven quantities:
  matter, bodies, confidence of a memory (`09:562-564`); (2) what changes at the edge is an OPTION SET,
  never a roll term or outcome; (3) the closure is an event, witnessable by presence. Under these, a
  band edge closing a verb is licensed — "a social threshold remains forbidden. A material one is the
  world having weather." NOTE: this refusal is NOT one of §14's fourteen forbidden rows (D-8 correction)
  — it's the design's own separately-stated refusal.

### §11 — THE TRANSFER ACT *(enlargement 4)*
- §11.1 **The larder, and the hole**:
  ```
  mouths(h) = Σ appetite(p)
  stores(h) += draw(h) − mouths(h)             -- may go negative (a debt)
  margin(h) = stores(h) / mouths(h)             -- seasons of cover, the only number read
  draw(h)   = Σ yield(H,season) − Σ levy(d,h)
  yield(H,season) = base(H) × condition(site(H)) × season_factor(territory) × (3+d10)/8.5
  ```
  `yield` IS A ROLL, load-bearing d10 term ranges 0.47×base to 1.53×base, mean 1.0. ⚠ An earlier draft
  DROPPED this term, making `stores` deterministic (D-2 correction, restored). Bands: Provisioned →
  Sufficient → Thin → Hungry → Failing. **The hole**: no act by which one person gives another anything
  unsolicited. `requisition` is a demand on kin, presupposes an undefined transfer verb. Doc 12
  (coercion) spends coin as live mechanism throughout while doc 13 (material) refuses a currency
  outright — "the coercion document's central fork rests on an object the material document refuses to
  model."
- §11.2 **The act**:
  ```
  transfer(giver, receiver, amount)          -- amount in the SAME `stores` scalar, mouth-seasons
     precondition: co-present, OR entrusted to a carrier act
     effect:  stores(hearth(giver)) −= amount ;  stores(hearth(receiver)) += amount
     witnessed: by presence, per person
  draw(h) = Σ yield(H,season) − Σ levy(d,h) + Σ transfers_in(h) − Σ transfers_out(h)   -- amends §11.1
  ```
  "One clean act plus one yield term. Not a currency." Opens: wage-labourer, bribe, purchase (a pair
  of transfers, §11.5), mercenary, corrupt official. ⚠ Overclaim withdrawn (C-14): does NOT open a
  material verb for a person who had none — §13 already gives a postless person 5 channels; the act
  changes what can REACH a postless person, not what they can do.
- §11.3 **Two attacks that fail, one overclaim that comes off.** Does not reopen the currency refusal
  (same `stores` scalar, no second unit). Does not abolish bribery (`witness` per-person, presence-
  based — a back-room transfer has exactly its two witnesses). Overclaim withdrawn: makes coercion
  arithmetic *expressible*, not *implementable* — re-denominating retinue costs/arrears/wages into
  mouth-seasons is unwritten work. ⚠ D-2 dependency: if a lord's transfer is his one act, spending his
  season on paying retinue is a real cost, only under one-act reading.
- §11.4 **Is `stores` the realm's denominator? — LIVE CHOICE, not an assumption.** Mouth-seasons are
  perishable/bulky. Option A: accept it as a feature (force is logistics-real). Option B: coin returns
  by the back door (a fungible transferable `stores` functions as money). "Jordan must see this." §16.
- §11.5 **The gift path constructs (traces every step to shipped mechanism); the market path fails as
  filed** (needs a price signal — the design refuses continuous markets by name — and reciprocity,
  needing a second transfer plus an agreement form). Stated as a limit (§15).

### §13 — NO FALLBACK · COINCIDENCE · THE EPISTEMIC PAYOFF
- §13.1 **THE RULE**: "If no person acts, the thing does not occur. No distribution just happens, no
  garrison is assumed paid, no repair is presumed made. The engine has no caretaker." Production =
  metabolism (no act required); distribution = politics (an act, always). Genuinely NEW ruling:

  NOTHING, and lapses. Not redistributed, not held over, not by seniority. "The famine writing itself: ...
  people starve inside a system that is working exactly as written. Nobody did anything wrong."
- §13.2 **The falsifier**: "Find a beneficial effect that no person's act produced." Exactly four
  licensed exceptions: (1) metabolism/nature (P1); (2) matter events (a storm, a silted channel, §10.6

  and convening-condition scheduling are NOT licensed by this row, they both trace to a person's act).
- §13.3 **Coincidence is what no-fallback PRODUCES**, not an exception to it — many acts by named
  persons, none of whom intended to help. Its hard prerequisite is §11 (the transfer act) — without it
  the world could harm you by omission but never save you by accident.
- §13.4 **The epistemic payoff**: "The knowledge layer was never the problem. It was already right...
  What it lacked was enough happening to disagree about." Table of six enlargements → what fact each

### §3 — Ownership: who may write what

| owner | holds |
|---|---|
| **Person** | address, marks, capability, stance, claim ledger, ties. Everything interior. |
| **Container (a rung)** | its stake, its judging set, its standing dates. **Nothing else.** |
| **Faction** | its proposition and its commitment map. |
| **Nobody** | aggregates, norms, densities, needs, openings, scale, reputation |

The last row is the important one. **Every aggregate is a function, never a field.** A norm is the
stances of the members, computed on demand. A faction's density is a roll-up computed on demand. A
person's needs are computed from their situation. An opening is computed from need plus capability plus
terms. Not a style preference: stored aggregates are how a design acquires dead state that reads as
mechanism — a value initialised once, never written, cited for seasons as though it meant something. If
the aggregate is a function, it cannot go stale, and cannot be initialised and forgotten, because there
is nothing to initialise.

**R-1** A rung module may read its OWN state and any message addressed to it. It may **not** read a
sibling's state and may **not** read a descendant's state directly. It **may COMPUTE an aggregate over
its descendants on demand**, when asked; it may **not** receive a pushed aggregate, and it may **not
store one**. ⚠ Self-correcting footnote in the file: an earlier draft said "it receives aggregates" —
retracted (adjudication C-1) because that wording forbade the very aggregations the suite computes
everywhere (a settlement's Order capacity, a faction's estimated profile, `carriage_mass`, `Hold`,
`concord`, `rarity`, `price`) AND simultaneously licensed the per-tick push flow that requires stored
landing-site state — the very thing §7's forbidden list bans three rows down. "Compute-on-demand, never
push, never store."

**R-2** A rung module may write only its own state, and a container's own state is only its stake, its
judging set and its standing dates. Everything else lives on persons. No module reaches through another
to write. A cross-rung read is the single easiest way to destroy T5/T6, because once the realm can read
a person directly there is no reason for the ladder to exist, and every intermediate rung quietly
becomes decoration.

### §2 — The three signatures, as types

```
choose  : (Person, View)   -> Act
resolve : (Act,    World)  -> [Event]
witness : (Person, Event)  -> [Claim]
```

Not conventions — "the enforcement mechanism for T4," working by what they omit. `choose` has no
`World` parameter — no masked world, no read-only world, no accessor — "omniscience is not something a
reviewer must catch — it is something an author cannot write." `resolve` has no `Person` parameter —
the world does not know who is asking, keeps the resolver from acquiring per-actor special cases.
`witness` takes the person FIRST — the only bridge from truth to interior, per-person by type;
"consensus broadcast is a type error," the structural answer to "the failure where one event deposited
the same sign and magnitude into every witness." **The rule that keeps this honest:** `View` must be a
distinct type from `World`, no coercion, no shared supertype, no field of `View` holding a `World`. "If
a `View` can be constructed from a `World` by masking, someone will eventually mask nothing." File ends
mid-section at `### 2.1 View is built, not filtered` (truncated at 94 lines — the section header is the
last line of the file, content not carried into this excerpt).

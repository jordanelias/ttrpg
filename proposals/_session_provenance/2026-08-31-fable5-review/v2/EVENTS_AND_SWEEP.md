## §A2 · HOW A CARD ENTERS PLAY AND RESOLVES

**Selection.** *"Every card is drawn by the same Π-weighted event-table engine"* (`:43`). The weight
function is not in this file; the governing substrate gives
`weight = base + Π-scaling + tag-modifiers`, **band-gated 0–2 / 3–7 / 8–10**
(`systems/_architecture/governance_ripple_substrate_v1.md:530`). Π maps to a **family band** — low Π to
Opportunity/Ambition (`:275`), high Π to Intrigue then Crisis, flipping at **Π 7→8** (`:45`).

**Trigger.** The band selects the family; the card's own predicate gates the specific card. Both must
hold. Tags bias the within-band draw (`:207`: *"her 'Benefactor' tag biases her future Ambition card's
draw-weight upward"*).

**Timing.** Thin, and the thinness matters. A **Thread** ticks per season (`:230`); effects schedule
forward (*"Π +1 deferred"*, `:103`; *"Debt(settlement) fires each future call"*, `:103`). **The file
never states which sub-phase of a season a draw occupies.**

**Who applies the impact.** The chosen branch does, through one of three resolvers (`:52-55`). For the
`deterministic_accounting` majority — *"most Opportunity/Ambition branches"* (`:54`) — **the impact is
a direct state write, no roll, no performer named.** The governor decides the branch; **nobody decides
the arrival.**

**Contested / refused / mitigated.**
- **Refused — always, and always priced.** *"Decline — refuse a perpetual obligation binding your
  successors"* (`:117`); *"Decline — keep your options open"* (`:170`); *"Decline — the coin is better
  spent on walls and grain"* (`:151`). Refusal never returns to neutral: it costs Disp, PS, or cedes
  the prize (`:151`: *"a RIVAL seat may redeem it instead"*).
- **Contested — only on `dice_pool` branches**: *"A branch that is itself a contest (Denounce→Tribunal,
  Verify→up-tier, a Parliament motion)"* (`:55`) — GEO-09, GOVFRIC-06, ECON-03/06.
- **Mitigated — branch 3 is consistently the hedge** that trades magnitude for a durable liability
  (`:103`, `:235`).
- **What cannot be refused: the draw.** No card has a "nothing happens" branch, and nothing lets a
  person prevent a card arriving.

# PART B — RECONCILIATION UNDER THE PARTITION

## §B6 · THE PARTITION AUDIT — EVERY IMPACT TYPE, AND EVERY CARD THAT CROSSES THE LINE

**This is the deliverable.** The test is not *which loop step does an event apply at*; it is
**is the subject of this state change peninsular human society?** If yes, no event may drive it.

### B6.0 · The analytical move that makes the audit tractable

A card produces **two distinct state changes**, and they have different drivers:

| | the change | its driver | partition applies to it? |
|---|---|---|---|

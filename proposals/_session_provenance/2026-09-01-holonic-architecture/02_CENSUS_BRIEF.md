# The census brief, and the test that did the work

## The instruction

Jordan, verbatim: *"please sweep #337 onwards and try to identify any dropped rulings, ideas, etc"* —
and *"the goal here is to make things work."*

## The definition

A thing is **dropped** if it was decided, ruled, endorsed or measured somewhere in #337–#352, and a
**later document in the chain** either (a) contradicts it without saying so, (b) restates the question
as open, (c) omits it while restating the surrounding material, or (d) promises to carry it and does
not.

## The field that made it a census rather than a list of suspicions

> **Field 4: NAME THE LATER FILE AND SECTION THAT RESTATES THE SURROUNDING MATERIAL WITHOUT THE THING.**
> **If you cannot name it, it is NOT dropped; discard it.**

**Sixteen candidates failed that test and are recorded as rejections**, because rejections are the
evidence a census is not noise. The most instructive: *"damage removes an option"* was **carried under
another name** — a band gate on `condition` **is** option removal — *"a rename, not an absence"*, which
is the repo's costliest recurring error class and is why the brief required grepping the **concept and
its synonyms**, never the original token.

## The exemplar the brief pointed at

The pattern was already documented in the tree: **two rulings given in the same conversation as a third
drew zero commits**, and a grep for their own words returned zero files. *"A ruling delivered alongside
another, and satisfied by the tree's current state, is the one that gets silently dropped."*

⚠ **That sentence was later found to be out-of-chain** (Lane A F3) and is withdrawn as a citation in
the census itself. **The pattern it names is still visible in the census's own rows**, which is the
only evidence the finding needs.

## What the census found, and the one structural conclusion

**27 items. 14 folded in, 3 refused, 1 struck, the rest recorded.** And the shape of the loss:

> **Most were lost at a section that RESTATES its neighbours.** A prose register is **re-typed**;
> rows are **inherited**. The clearest case: a register whose own heading reads *"stated so no later
> document can cite this one as though these were closed"* — **and five gaps fell out of it anyway.**

**That is the argument for graded rows over any prose list of open items** (`ARCHITECTURE.md` §42.2),
and it is the census's most useful output — more useful than any single item on it.

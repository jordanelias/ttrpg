## 0. First step, as the source orders: how `terms` is filled today

Read `decision/options.py::operands_for` (`:1265-1307`) through `operand_bags` (`:1310`), `_person_bags` (`:1368-1418`), `_operands` (`:1421-1477`) and `_derive_operand` (`:1093-1242`), and `held_record_claims` (`:970-993`). What the code does:

1. **`terms` is not an operand.** No cell binds a `terms`. A petition's `terms` is the act's `subject`: `_eff_petition` mints `{terms, to, from}` with `terms` the act's `subject` (`loop/effects_information.py:699-714`), and `carry` dockets that `terms` as the matter (`:455-461`).
2. **A computed petition's `subject` is its addressee.** `petition`'s typed cell binds `to` (`existence`, kind `Person`) and `from` (kind `Rung`) and no `subject` (`verb_table.yaml:1429-1436`); `_operands` adds `subject`/`to` where the form admits them (`_REFERENT_OPERANDS`, `options.py:1262`, `:1437-1439`), and `_derive_operand` answers both with the question's referent (`:1191-1192`, `:1217-1218`). The row's own note says so: *"a computed petition is addressed to the person it is about. That is `H-94`'s single-referent limit"* (`verb_table.yaml:1438`). `petition` is `referent_class: Person` (`:1424`), so the referent is a person.
3. **The one route by which a held thing re-aims `subject`** is IN-10's `held=`: a held Record's content claim answers first, `subject` included (`options.py:1183-1187`), and `opening_set`'s `terms_sourced` reader adds a held writ's `terms` as a referent (`:165-185`). Both read a `content:<kind>` claim through `rosters.yaml: record_sourced_operands` (`_content_answer`, `:939-956`). A Record kind is the only kind that map admits: `data/requires.py` refuses a non-`record_kinds` member (`rosters.yaml:2446-2449`), and `_load_terms_sourced` refuses a kind that is not `RECORD_REFERENT_KINDS | HOLDING_RECORD_KINDS` or whose `subject` the map does not answer (`data/verbs.py:1890-1913`). `terms_sourced` today reads `commit: [dispensation, petition]` (`rosters.yaml:2573`).
4. **A person holds no claim saying what a Proposition says.** The deposits of an `utter` name the Proposition and its actor and never its mood, predicate or value (`queries/world_q.py:5331-5333`; the holder's claim is `(prop, "proposition.uttered", True)`, `engine/season/tests/test_cr28_propositions_heard.py:36`). The maker holds his Proposition by a live `hold` (`_eff_utter`, `loop/effects_information.py:1212-1237`), and that is all his own state says of it. A computed `utter` carries no operands (untyped row, `verb_table.yaml:2367-2380`; `_operands`, `options.py:1429-1436`), so its mood is `OUGHT` (`_eff_utter`, `:1230`): **no computed act makes a `HOLDS` Proposition today.**
5. **Where the tree reads a held document's content to decide what a person is offered**, it reads it resolver-side and stamps a referent: `world_q.writs_held` (`:3182-3208`) reads `w.records[...]`'s kind and addressee for the Records on `p`'s own live `hold` edges, and `questions_for` adds the ids as classed referents; `propositions_heard` (CR-28, `:3324`) asks the World only which ids are Propositions. The person-side formation then reads the stamp.

## 1. Which row forms a petition from a held conclusion

**The choice the source gives:** (a) a second sourcing map keyed on held Propositions of mood `HOLDS`; (b) the accusation petition taking the uttered Proposition's id as its `terms` operand through the existing `record_sourced_operands` route.

**Taken: neither as given — a third option, (c) a referent stamp: the doer named by a held `HOLDS` conclusion is stamped as a `Person`-classed referent of the question the concluder answers, in `writs_held`'s occasion-stamp form. Ladder step 5 (what the architecture makes clearly right), with step 4's precedent fixing the form.** (c) shares (a)'s key (a held Proposition of mood `HOLDS`) and drops (a)'s second operand map; it shares (b)'s single route and drops (b)'s Proposition-as-`terms`. Steps 1 to 3 are silent: no later ruling decides the row, the subject is live, and `08_FI_03` §3 hands the row to this pass (*"the design pass decides the row"*).

(b) is rejected on two code facts, either sufficient:
- **The chain breaks at the bench.** With the Proposition's id as `terms`, `carry` dockets a Proposition (`_eff_carry`: the matter is the petition's `terms`, `loop/effects_information.py:455-461`), and `determine`'s `party` conjunct is `existence` of `subject`, kind `Person` (`verb_table.yaml:392-397`, `:391`: *"The subject must be a PERSON (`party`): the disposal is an edge he owns"*). The accusation could be filed and carried and never determined. Reading the doer off the Proposition inside `_eff_carry` would be a kind-keyed branch in a generic effect (scripting drift).
- **The route refuses the kind.** `record_sourced_operands` and `terms_sourced` admit Record kinds only (§0 point 3); a Proposition is not a Record (`state/carriers.py:974`, `:1080`). Admitting it means a content deposit for Propositions, which no channel writes (§0 point 4): a new deposit rule, not the existing route.

(c)'s form, from precedent (§0 point 5) and the H-94 limit (§0 point 2):
- **No second operand map is needed.** Because a computed petition's `subject` and `to` both bind its one Person referent, an accusation forms as soon as **the doer is a Person-classed referent of the question the concluder answers**: `terms` = `to` = the doer, `carry` dockets the doer, `determine`'s `party` admits him. A second map would separate `terms` from `to`, which is plan position `15c`'s job (`verb_table.yaml:1438`), not this one's. The accusation is therefore addressed to the accused, as every computed petition is addressed to the person it is about; that is H-94's limit, carried, not chosen here.
- **The stamp, resolver-side, `writs_held`'s shape:** `queries/world_q.py::conclusions_held(w, p) -> tuple` — sorted, the `value` of each Proposition on `p`'s own live `hold` edges whose `mood` is `HOLDS` and whose predicate stem is `concluded`, where that `value` is a person id in `w.persons` other than `p.id`. `questions_for` adds them as `Person`-classed referents through its `added` splice, the term list `propositions_heard` joined (CR-28, `engine/season/tests/test_cr28_propositions_heard.py:1-4`). It reads `p`'s own edges and, for their objects only, the Proposition store, as `writs_held` reads the Record store. A `Partial` conclusion (the hand withheld, 19_LACUNA R-19-04) has no person `value` and stamps nothing.
- **What it is keyed on is the commitment, not the inference.** The `concluded:` claim alone stamps nothing: the source's control (*"the same person without the Proposition forms none"*) is exactly the rule that a conclusion is acted on only once it is a committed assertion (19_LACUNA's title). A person who reasons and does not utter keeps his inference private.
- **Every `Person`-classed row forms on the stamped doer, not only `petition` — and only those.** A classed row forms on the referents the question carries as exactly its class, an unclassed row on the plain referents only (`data/verbs.py:267-271`, `VerbRow.referents_of`; `state/carriers.py:537-542`, `Question.referents_for`). The rows declaring `referent_class: Person` at `c21e451f` are `dispatch` (`verb_table.yaml:457`), `capture` (`:937`), `open_case` (`:1364`), `petition` (`:1424`) and `tend` (`:1768`). So a concluder also forms `capture` on the accused, `open_case` where he holds a seat with that remit, `dispatch` and `tend` on him, each scored by the chooser and admitted or refused by the fold as on any other Person referent. `challenge` is NOT reached (it is `Disputant`-classed, `:998`), nor `tell` or `forgive` (they declare no class, so a classed stamp does not reach them; `08_FI_03` §4's list of *"the ordinary rows"* does not hold for this stamp). That `tend` forms on the man one accuses is the price of a class-wide stamp; no row is named in the term, the classes decide, and a narrower stamp would name a verb (scripting drift). The build reads the Candidate set this widens (formed on the doer, by verb, seed 0, one season, with a planted `HOLDS` conclusion) and quotes it.

**Falsifier.** NEW `engine/season/tests/test_playable_inquiry.py::test_a_held_conclusion_forms_an_accusation` (game-state; lane: the FI subsystem): a hand-built world in which person P holds a `concluded:<kind>` claim naming D and a live `hold` on a `HOLDS` Proposition `(S, concluded:<kind>, D)`; `opening_set` on the question `questions_for` gives him forms a `petition` whose `to` (and so whose `terms`) is D, and folding it through the real driver mints a petition Record whose `terms` is D. **Control:** the same person, same claim, no Proposition (or the Proposition with mood `OUGHT`) forms no `petition` naming D. **Planted failure, run once and not kept:** `conclusions_held` stubbed to `()` reddens the first assertion. The source's other gate, `test_playable_inquiry.py::test_some_conclusion_names_a_non_actor` (`08_FI_03` §3, written by the second half's `08_FI_03` item), is copied here as this build's second gate: over `inquiry_world` seeds 0–19 at least one conclusion names someone who is not the Event's actor.

## 2. Whether `record` scores the concluder

**Taken: yes, behind `Fixtures record_concluders` (control `0`), and only for a holder who was TOLD the conclusion. Ladder step 3** — `19_LACUNA` R-19-05 decides the *whether* (*"a concluder who is not paired is a teller with a free pass, which makes concluding costless"*) and names the fixture and its control (edit 4(b)). **Step 5 fixes the *how*, because R-19-05's how does not run on the tree:**

- R-19-05 pairs *"the Proposition's cell `(subject, predicate, value)`"* for *"a holder `p` of a `HOLDS` Proposition"* in `record(p, u, fx)`. `record` is person-side (`options.py:1775-1815`; its sets are `p.ledger` only, `:1809-1810`), and no holder's ledger carries a Proposition's cell (§0 point 4). Reading `w.propositions` from `decision/` is the World read the AST guard refuses (`operands_for`'s docstring, `:1299-1301`).
- R-19-05 pairs it *"against `p`'s firsthand cells, by `_pair`"*. `record`'s own side is `p`'s firsthand claims on cell predicates (`_is_cell`, `:1810`); a `concluded:` claim is deposited `inferred`, never firsthand (FRES §7), so no firsthand mate on `(S, concluded:<kind>)` ever exists. What contradicts a conclusion is a deed the holder saw with another hand: `person_q.deed_doer` (`queries/person_q.py:329-374`) over his own untold deed claim of that kind on `S`.

So the build is: **(i)** the told side is what a holder was told — the concluder's `tell` of his `concluded:` claim lands as a told claim with `Claim.teller` the concluder (the told channel, `chain`, `state/carriers.py:351-363`). **`record`'s `told` set does NOT hold it today:** that set is `c.teller == teller and _is_cell(c)` (`options.py:1809`), and `concluded` is no cell stem (`CELL_STEMS = REQUIRES_STEMS - WORLD_ONLY_STEMS`, `data/requires.py:877`; its own note, `:873-876`, says a one-value slot outside `REQUIRES_STEMS` is never paired). **The build owes that widening:** at `record_concluders` 1 the `told` filter also admits a `concluded:` claim; at `0` it is today's `_is_cell` filter exactly. Scheduled in §4's §2 row. **(ii)** At `record_concluders` 1, a told `concluded:<kind>` claim on `S` pairs against the holder's own reading of who did the `<kind>` deed on `S`: for a `PAIRED_KINDS` kind, `deed_doer` over his untold deed claim on `S` (it answers that set only, `queries/person_q.py:357-358`); for an unpaired kind done to a person, `deed_counterparty` over his untold deed claim `(doer, <kind>)` naming `S` as the one acted on (it answers `PERSON_SUBJECT_KINDS` only, `:377-391`), the claim's subject being the doer (H-192's reading). Any other kind has no mate and is not paired. Agree if the reading names the same doer, disagree if another, nothing if it names nobody (both readers *"never guess"*).

**A departure from R-19-05, named.** R-19-05 scores a holder of the Proposition *"by WITNESS or by telling"* (`19_LACUNA_conclusion_is_a_committed_assertion.md:67-69`). This design scores the TOLD holder only: a witness of the bare utterance holds `(prop, proposition.uttered, True)` and nothing of what was asserted (§0 point 4), so there is nothing person-side to pair. Consequence: **a concluder who utters in public and never tells anyone is not scored by those who heard him utter.** Closing it needs WITNESS to deposit the uttered cell of a `HOLDS` Proposition to those present, as a told claim with the utterer as teller — a new deposit branch at `loop/witness.py`, which this design does not schedule (CR-83: compose on what exists). Taken as the lighter reading by ladder step 5; the B11 batch's close names the departure in its PR body so a reader of R-19-05 sees it. The pairing runs through `_pair` with a derived `own` set (one claim per resolved deed, `(S, concluded:<kind>, doer)`), so the agree/disagree rule stays the one owner (`_pair`, `options.py:1506-1535`); no second arithmetic. **[UNVERIFIED: that `person_q.said_of` will pick an `inferred` `concluded:` claim for a `tell`; the build reads `said_of` (`person_q.py:1701`) first and, if it declines the source, adds nothing to it and records the gap in its commit.]**

**Falsifier.** In `engine/season/tests/test_conclusion.py` (19_LACUNA's file, B11 second half), its named `test_record_re_grades_a_concluder_only_at_record_concluders_one`, read with this section's pairing: a holder told D's conclusion naming X, who later holds his own `seen`-paired deed naming Y on the same subject and kind, reads `record(p, D) < 1.0` at `record_concluders` 1 and `1.0` at `0` (control). **Second falsifier (19_LACUNA's third):** a holder who never held the conclusion reads `1.0` at both values, so the pairing reads no ledger it may not.

## 3. What a caught liar costs beyond the question, as CR-83 reads it

**Taken: nothing is stored. The price is the hearer's own reading of the liar, already live, sharpened by Attunement when CR-19's flip lands. Ladder step 3** (CR-83's entry: *"by composing on what exists and coining no carrier"*; CAT-3, *"store the teller and grade the claim WHEN READ"*, `teller_weight`'s docstring, `options.py:1834-1836`) **and step 4** (`record` is that grading's existing owner).

What the tree already charges, read off the code:
1. **The question** (`08_FI_03` §4, unbuilt): the sibling of `standing_crossed` (`queries/world_q.py:4013`), a question whose referent is the teller; the ordinary rows then form on him.
2. **His record with the catcher.** A caught lie is one disagreeing pair, so `record(p, liar)` falls (`options.py:1775-1815`; `record_gain` ships 0.5, `data/fixtures.py:854`), and `teller_weight` re-grades **every claim the liar ever passed to `p`**, past and future, at read (`:1818-1899`; `record` enters at `:1897-1899`). This is the cost CAT-3 rules: graded per hearer, never a flag on the liar. **What it cannot tell apart, stated:** a claim stores no time of observation, so a told cell that is TRUE NEWER NEWS, contradicting the hearer's older firsthand cell, is a disagreeing pair too (`options.py:1793-1795`, recorded at `H-183`; `engine/season/tests/test_one_reader_of_a_ledger.py:157-160`: each teller who contradicts a stale sighting *"earns `record` < 1"*). So the live `record` already charges an honest bearer of news as it charges a liar. That is the tree's shipped behaviour and H-183's, not this design's; file B item (5) keeps it out of anything Attunement sharpens.
3. **Only the catcher pays it out.** A third party re-grades the liar only when he holds his own pair (he was told the same lie and holds a firsthand contradiction) or when the catcher tells him the firsthand cell; nobody is told *"X lied"* (the deed lie, H-198 rule (ii), is unbuilt, `verb_table.yaml:601`). That is CR-83's reading: what a holder may read of another is what he himself holds of him.

What CR-83 adds (file B, item (5)): **Attunement sharpens the catch, not the sentence.** CR-19's competence factor (`options.py:1677-1716`) ships at gain 0 because a person-side reading cannot separate a lie from a truth (`:1698-1709`). A disagreeing pair in `record` is NOT by itself a separator (point 2: true newer news makes one too). The narrower thing that is one is **a pair whose firsthand side the hearer took at or after he was told** — he looked after hearing, and what he saw contradicts what he heard — and file B item (5) scales by Attunement only the balance of those pairs, never every told claim and never a pair whose firsthand side predates the telling. A liar caught by a later look then costs more with a perceptive hearer; an honest bearer of newer news costs nothing more with him than with anyone; an uncaught liar costs nothing anywhere. The residual, stated in file B: a teller whose own look was old when he told, contradicted by a later look after the world changed, is charged as caught. No stance row, no regard term and no reputation scalar is written for a lie.

**Falsifier.** A planted world (built in B11's second half beside `08_FI_03` §4's `test_a_contradicted_teller_becomes_a_question`): teller T tells hearer H a false cell value; H then holds his own firsthand cell with the true value. At `record_gain` 0.5, the weight `teller_weight(H)` gives a **second, unrelated** told claim from T is below what it was before the catch; at `record_gain` 0 (control) it is unchanged; a third person who holds neither the lie nor the contradiction reads T unchanged at both. If the third person's reading moves, a cost is being stored somewhere, and this section is wrong.

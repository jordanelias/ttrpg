### The event half (`IS-B2-FI-07` (1) to (3), `IS-B2:295`)

**(1) A hidden event is an unnoticed deed.** One the notice draw (`18_DETERMINISM_notice_is_a_draw`, built in B11's second half behind `notice_arm`) did not deposit to anyone present, or one done where nobody stood. No new carrier: the notice draw is the tree's one rule for who sees a deed (ladder step 4, the source's).
- Falsifier: `test_an_unnoticed_deed_is_found_only_by_inquiry_on_its_trace`, first half: a planted non-public deed with `notice_arm: on` and no co-located person but the doer; after WITNESS no ledger but the doer's holds a claim naming it.
- Control: `notice_arm: off` reproduces B11's pinned base hash (seed 0, one season).

**(2) Finding it is an inquiry on its traces.** `reconstruct` or `interview` whose subject is a trace or a witness `16_PESSIMIST_FI_C_what_a_deed_leaves_behind` leaves; its `finding.made` deposits a claim naming the deed (the source says "and, at Overwhelming, its doer"; CHANGED by (9) to the doer at Success, the band `finding_terms` already shows the hand at) in the finder's own ledger.
- Note, read against the tree: `reconstruct` carries a band since B07.05 (`rosters.yaml: graded_tasks` row `reconstruct`, `armed_by: task_draw_arm`, `engine/season/data/fixtures.py` `task_draw_arm="on"`), so its `finding.made` reaches `finding_terms`. `interview` is a prompt (K-36, `hole_register.yaml` at the `finding_terms` owner row): it carries no band on the asker, and what a witness knows reaches the asker through the witness's own `tell`. The graded route of this point is therefore `reconstruct` (and `examine` at the place, `16_PESSIMIST_FI_C` edit 2); the `interview` route is the witness's telling, which this design leaves as it is.
- Falsifier: the same test, second half: after a planted Success `reconstruct` on the deed's trace the finder holds a claim naming the deed.
- Control: `engine/season/tests/test_fi03_trail.py`'s hand-built trail still deposits (the instrument can see a find).

**(3) Where the doer conceals, the one opposed rule decides** (`29_DICE_one_opposed_rule_form_and_tuning`): the doer's `conceal` band stands against the finder's draw as the opposed side, as it already stands against the notice draw.
- **What is built.** The one form is `modules/social_contest/roll.py::obstacle` (`opposed_ob(opposition if a person opposes else pool_default, opposition_rate)` plus the instance terms). The cover carries its band: `loop/effects_information.py::_eff_conceal` writes `forge_quality_at(res.degree)` (`rosters.yaml: forge_quality`: Overwhelming 1, Success 1, Partial 0) as the cover Record's `forgery_quality`. A doer's live cover is read by `state/attribution.py::live_cover` (a held `cover` whose `terms` names its holder, `:151-154`).
- **What does NOT reach the finder's roll today, and the build must add.** (i) The input builder passes a `forgery_quality` only from the Record that is the act's own subject (`seam/wrappers/sigma.py:529`, `:553`, the `examined=subject` of `:1112`); a doer's cover is never that Record, so nothing carries it to an inquiry about the deed. (ii) Opposition is read only from a person who is the act's subject (`sigma.py:541-542`, `_opposition_of` `:120-123`); the doer of a found deed is not the inquiry's subject. (iii) Were the doer read as the opponent, `seam/wrappers/pool.py::score` (`:167-179`) would give his score in the ROLLED verb's skill (`inquiry` for `reconstruct`, `examine`, `surveil`), not in concealing.
- **The build, therefore:** the input builder (`sigma.py::_read_social_roll`, for an inquiry) adds one obstacle term: the highest `forgery_quality` among the live covers (`live_cover`) of the doers of the trail Events `epistemic.trail_of` returns to this finder (up to one Event per `covert_kinds` member per side, `epistemic.py:365-375`), 0 where none, through the existing `forgery_quality` operand of `_frame` beside the examined Record's (the larger of the two, so a forged-and-covered trail counts its worst concealment once). One draw per act, as today. The doer is NOT read as the opposed side: his concealing score is `pool_default` for every person (`conceal`'s `capability: none`), which is exactly the `opposed_ob(pool_default)` the inquiry already stands against, so the source's "his skill score against the searcher's" reads the same today; CHANGED in form, declared in this part's head. A concealing skill, when one is defined, enters as an `opposes` row of the inquiry's capability site, not here.
- Falsifier: `test_a_concealed_deed_is_found_less_often`: 200 dice seeds, the doer with and without a live cover drawn at Success (`forgery_quality` 1), the find shares strictly ordered, `assert checked >= 200`.
- Control: a cover drawn at Partial (`forge_quality` Partial 0, `rosters.yaml: forge_quality`) gives a share equal to no cover at every seed.

## 2. The duty decline (charge (1))

**The rule.** In `_opening_set`, after the `own_stance_declines` reader and beside it, for a verb whose row `subject_is_opponent`:

```
F_p        = { org_faction("seat", S) : S an object of p's own live `hold` Tenures }  − {None}
officers_F = ∪_{S : org_faction("seat", S) == F}  holders_named(p.ledger, S)   ∪  holders_named(p.ledger, HEAD_SEAT[F])    (minus p himself)
bound(r)   = { r }  if r ∈ officers_F for some F ∈ F_p,  else ∅                               # persons only
keep (r, seat) iff  bound(r) is empty
               or seat is a seat of F (the candidate's `seat_of`, granted per the hold Tenure snapshot)
                  whose rank exceeds r's believed rank (§6's `station`)                       # the office acting down its chain
               or grievance_against(p, r, fx).total > fx.betrayal_band
```

Every term is read structurally: the actor's own `hold` Tenures and their grant snapshots, each seat's own `offices.yaml` row, the `head_seat` roster, his own ledger. **No seat, person or faction is named anywhere in the rule**, and no verb: the verbs are whatever `subject_is_opponent` returns. `HEAD_SEAT[F]` is united in although every shipped head seat is already an F seat (§1), so a future head seat authored under another body still binds. It narrows and widens nothing, as its siblings do; a declined referent is appended to `declined` with `unmet` naming the duty, so the player's surface says why (B34.02's shape).

**Two limits, stated (not filled):**
- **Seats founded in play are outside the duty.** `org_faction("seat", S)` reads the static `offices.yaml` rows only (`goals.py:313`, `SEAT_ROW_BY_ID`), and a founded seat (`loop/predicates.py:340-345`, `founded_office_id`) has no row, so it is in no `F_p`, binds no holder in (1), gives no faction in (2)'s creed term and no `office` in (3). The same holds for any seat with no row. Reading a founded seat's faction person-side needs a carrier the hold Tenure does not hold; nothing is coined.
- **`holders_named` is over-inclusive** (`person_q.py:2461-2465`): for a seat changed IN PLAY it names every subject of the Event, the conferrer beside the man conferred on. **At build it is clean:** `seed_seat_news` deposits `claim_subjects(..., "per_change")` with no actor (`boot/realm.py:848-852`, `:881-882`), the holder and the seat, and the seat is excluded. **In play** a conferrer of an F seat may be read as its holder; he is the holder of a seat whose grant carries `confer`, usually an F seat, so the over-inclusion mostly binds a man already bound; the residue (a conferrer of another faction, or a seatless witness of two seat changes in one lap) is a declared limit, erring toward loyalty, and is not filtered (a filter needs the World, which `decision/` does not hold).

**Three readings the ladder settled** (§10 gives each its step):

- **The opponent set is the rule, not the audit's list (and no roster).** A roster equal to a rule is a second copy of it; `VIOLENT_OPPONENT_VERBS` (`options.py:656`) is derived the same way. So `accept` is never declined here — it is priced (§6) — and `capture`, `challenge`, `succeed`, `establish`, `revoke` are in. A first draft bound the subject's believed holder; it was cut, because each case is already handled where it lives: a `march` into land the actor's own faction holds is unopposed by the fold (`loop/sides.py:78-90`: `subject` is `None` when the target's holder is the actor's faction), rung holds are not seeded into any ledger (`seed_seat_news` deposits only seats, `t.object in w.offices`, `boot/realm.py:879`; the territory holds of `:1911`, `:1918` reach no ledger), so `holders_named` would name a rung's holder only on a hand-planted ledger; `succeed` is narrowed by `own_title_declines` (`options.py:287-291`) and is refused at the fold on a held seat; a computed `revoke` stays refused at its precondition (`options.py:923-925`). - **The office acting down its own chain is not betrayal.** A candidate exercised THROUGH a seat of F (`seat_of`, `options.py:945-959`, whose grant is read person-side from the hold Tenure snapshot, `exercisable_seats` / `_granting_holds`, `:933-973`, never from `offices.yaml`, since remits are rewritten in play, `loop/effects_governance.py:273-280`) on a man whose believed station is BELOW that seat's rank is exempt: the King's arrest of a duke, a realm bench's `determine` on a territory officer. **Person-side, not World-side.** B21d.05 step 3 says "F's head read through `faction_q.head`"; that reader takes a World (`world_q.py:5157`, re-exported) and `decision/` holds none (AX-2, `04` §B.9). **`fx.betrayal_band`.** `None` is the CONTROL and the REVERT: the decline is off and reads nothing (the `hostile_stance_gain` precedent, `choose.py:117-127`: `None` is the revert). A number is the band, compared strictly (`>`). **Refused below 2.0 (and NaN)**: the stance term is capped at one step and the creed term is 0 or 1 (§3), so 2.0 is the most a person's build-time state can read; a band below it would let a build-time stance and a rival creed alone lift the decline, which the charge forbids. `+∞` is legal and is the decline at its tightest (nothing lifts it) — a falsifier arm, not the control. **Proposed value: `betrayal_band = 2.0` `[ASSUMPTION; medium; Jordan to correct; revert: None; swept None (control) / 2.0 (the floor, shipped) / 3.0 / +∞ (total)]`.** At 2.0 the band means "more than his build-time state can carry": with no grudge and no rival creed, more than two steps of wrong; with both, any wrong at all on top.

**Falsifier** (B21d.05, `engine/season/tests/test_cr88_duty_of_office.py`; game-state; lane: the season loop): a hand-built world, officer O on a seat of faction F, K on F's head seat, O's ledger holding the seat-news claims naming K; O answers a question carrying K, and every actor below holds a CR-87 violence context against K (a `challenge` Record between them, `options._duel_context`), so `fight` is formable and only the duty can remove it. At band 2.0 and an empty grievance, `fight` on K is not formed; parametrised over every `HEAD_SEAT` faction with the seat ids renamed, the result is the same (a decline keyed on a name fails here). In the same world: an F *member* holding no seat forms `fight` on K (the decline reads `hold`, not membership); an officer of another faction forms it; O with K's seat-news claims removed forms it (person-side); the exemption, over more than one verb: O on a realm-rank F seat whose hold Tenure's grant carries `dispatch` and `determine` forms `arrest`, `capture` and `interrogate` on an F officer his ledger names at territory rank, and none of them on K (equal rank, so up or across the chain); the same seat with `dispatch` dropped from the Tenure's grant snapshot (the offices.yaml row unchanged) no longer exempts `arrest` (the grant is read from the Tenure, not the row); `fight` on the territory officer stays declined (an `own` act is never exempt). **Control:** band `None` — every person's candidate set at seeds 0, 1, 2 after one season equal to the pre-item tree's, and the realm hash at seed 0 after one season equal (the batch gate's EQUAL arm).

## 3. The betrayal reading (charge (2))

**`person_q.grievance_against(p, q, fx=None) -> GrievanceParts`**, computed when read, stored nowhere, Person-side (no World, no `decision/` import). Three addends on **one unit, the stance row's valence step** (B10.09's `deed_steps` unit, `person_q.py:234-241`, so a deed and a row are commensurable), and their sum `total`, `regard_parts`' shape (`:733-766`):

```
deeds  = Σ over DISTINCT deed triples t = (c.subject, c.predicate, c.value) among attributed_deeds(p.ledger, q)
             of  max(0, −steps(t)) · support(t)
         support(t) = LedgerReader._support over t's claims, weigh(c) = min(1, c.confidence / confidence_default)
                      (a noisy-OR over DISTINCT origins `claim_origin(c.chain, p.id)`, each origin once at its highest weight;
                       `_support` groups by (predicate, value), so the build passes one triple's claims at a time)
         steps(c) = deed_steps(p, axis_w, c, sworn_bond, deed_counterparty)     with, for a paired deed whose patient is an
                    object of p's own live `hold`, the patient read as p himself (`_bond`'s own rule for the judge who is the counterparty)
stance = min(−MEMBER_BAND, max(0, −stance_toward(p, q)))          # 0 to 1 step
creed  = 1  if p and q share a faction F by seat (p holds an F seat; p's ledger names q holding one, §2's officers_F)
            and p holds a live `commit` to a faction Proposition (`FACTION_BY_PROP`) other than F;   else 0
total  = deeds + stance + creed
```

`axis_w`, `sworn_bond`, `deed_counterparty`, `scar_*` are read from `fx` exactly as `regard_parts` reads them (`:758-762`); with `fx` `None` the controls are read, as `regard`'s are.

- **"against his pursuits, kin and holdings".** Pursuits: `deed_steps`' cosine of his projected pursuits against the kind's row. Kin: `_bond`, the allegiance factor a paired deed (and, at `deed_counterparty: person_subject`, an unpaired one done to a person) already carries — a wrong to one he holds dear weighs up to two steps. Holdings: a deed suffered by a seat, rung or Record he holds is read with him as its patient, `_bond`'s existing self rule, so a condemned seizure of his seat weighs double and an approved one nothing. `deed_steps` gains one keyword (the patient, defaulting to `c.subject`), so `regard`, `reception` and `legitimacy` read exactly as today. **Against only:** a deed he approves adds nothing and cancels nothing (the charge: the deeds "that `p`'s `deeds_judged` scores against" him); his warmth toward `q` still reaches every choice through `regard` in the stance term.
- **Distinct origins only (ED-IN-0322) — corroboration firms ONE deed; it is not a series.** A teller repeating himself adds nothing (one origin counts once, `_support`'s rule); several origins of the same deed raise its support toward 1, which is the ruling's "counts as corroboration and carries more weight" — more weight of BELIEF that the deed was done, bounded by the deed's own step. A firsthand claim at `confidence_default` is already full support. A deed never counts for more than itself, so only DISTINCT deeds add up to a betrayal (see the observation below). The weigh is the claim's STORED deposit confidence, never its present (faded) confidence and never `teller_weight`: no recursion through `teller_weight`, the fold and the formation read one number from queries/ alone, and no fade is decided here (opinion persistence is CR-83's measurement, B11.02 §10 (1), unread at two seasons). **The limit, inherited:** two deeds of one kind by `q` are one triple (B11.02 §10 (4)), so the second is read as corroboration of the first, not as a second wrong.
- **OBSERVATION FOR JORDAN (recorded; settled by the ladder, nothing withheld).** ED-IN-0335 rules that a major betrayal needs "a significant series of events/opinions/circumstances" and must not occur in season 1 from a neutral state; ED-IN-0322 rules that several origins "count as corroboration and carry more weight". Read additively (one weight per `(deed, origin)`, this file's first draft), one deed told by three witnesses would lift the band at 2.0 — one event standing in for a series. **Settled, steps 2 and 4:** ED-IN-0322's own words are about corroboration of "the same thing", and the tree's owner of that ruling, `LedgerReader._support` (`person_q.py:1629-1665`, cited by the ruling itself), is a noisy-OR bounded at 1 per value; ED-IN-0335's "series" is a count of events. So corroboration raises a deed toward its full weight and stops there; the series is distinct deeds. If Jordan reads 0322 as additive, the revert is one line (sum over `(t, origin)` instead of `support(t)`), and the band would need raising for 0335 to hold.
- **"a build-time stance alone cannot clear the band" — structurally, not by value.** A stance row carries no provenance (§1), so the stance addend is capped at one step, `MEMBER_BAND`'s unit (CR-87 (5)'s bound for a build-time disaffection), and the band is refused below 2.0 (§2). Every build-time ingredient together (a grudge row of any size, a rival creed) reads at most 2.0 and never exceeds a legal band: a betrayal needs deeds. The price, stated: a large grudge earned in play (a lost field's row) counts one step too; deeds carry a betrayal, a grudge tilts it.
- **"or by F's officers acting through `q`'s seat" — NOT BUILT, the gap recorded.** No carrier says a deed was done through another man's seat: a claim carries no `via` (`state/carriers.py` `Claim`), `epistemic.Seen` carries `stratum, marks, who, why` only (`epistemic.py:936-939`), and delegation is a second seat, not an act through another's (`state/gate.py:329`). The one carrier that could connect an officer's deed to `q`'s order is `Seen.why` (the antecedent claim a witness connects, H-458), which ships at chance `0.0` (`data/fixtures.py:1518-1523`): no `why` is ever read in a realm, so a reader of it would have no input to read and no game-state test of it could pass except on a hand-planted ledger. That is why it is not built now, not any ban on carriers. **The reading, for whoever builds it after the `why` raise:** a deed claim of an F officer whose paired `seen` names a `why` that is itself a deed attributed to `q` counts toward `grievance_against(p, q)`. Until then the deed half reads `q`'s own deeds.
- **`inferred_pursuits` (B11.02 §9) is not read.** The charge scores deeds by `p`'s own pursuits; `inferred_pursuits` (a deed against a pursuit `p` inferred `q` holds — hypocrisy, a different reading) is unbuilt and would be a second owner of a deed's valence here. B11.02's line is a record, not a schedule; nothing is owed to it.

**Falsifier** (B21d.05's named EXIT tests, game-state, lane: the season loop): on a planted ledger whose holder's pursuits run opposite to the rows of three deed kinds (cosine near −1 each), **three distinct deeds of K's, each from its own origin at `confidence_default`**, read `deeds` above 2.0 and lift the decline at band 2.0 (`test_three_planted_deeds_by_the_king_from_three_origins_lift_the_decline`); **one deed from one origin three times** reads at most 1.0 and does not (`test_one_origin_three_times_does_not_lift_it`); **one deed from three origins** reads at most 1.0 and does not (corroboration is not a series, the observation above); one told deed at half `confidence_default` from one origin reads half its step, from two such origins three quarters; a deed he approves reads 0; a stance row of −4 toward K reads `stance` 1.0; a live commit to a rival creed reads `creed` 1.0; stance −25 plus a rival creed with no deed reads 2.0 and does not lift the decline at the floor (`test_a_build_time_stance_alone_does_not_clear_the_band`); a condemned `tenure.closed` on a seat he holds, paired to K, reads twice its cosine. **Control:** an empty ledger, no stance row, no rival creed: every part `0.0`; and `regard(p, q, fx)` and `holds_dispute`'s other readers unchanged by `deed_steps`' new keyword at its default (the seed-0 one-season hash equal with the decline off).

## 4. Duty's positive half (charge (3)), restated as B10.30's `aim` term

**It is that term, scoped as that term is, with its own gain for the office.** Duty's positive half is the `aim` dot — `damp · Σ_axis to_axes(goal.pursuits)[axis] · align(c.verb, axis) · odds_weight`, under `aim_term`'s three conditions (a `need` question, its `about` a `goals.yaml` row, a live `commit` to it) — weighted by `fx.duty_weight` where the goal's organization belongs to a faction whose seat `p` holds:

```
office(p, goal) = 1  if  org_faction(goal.org_kind, goal.org) ∈ F_p  (§2),  else 0
duty_goal(c)    = duty_weight · office(p, goal) · [the aim dot]           # 0.0 wherever aim_term's conditions fail
```

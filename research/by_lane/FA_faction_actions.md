### 1.3 Critical evaluation

1. **Method.** Three generations of method: memory-written theory (`FASE`), per-civ researcher-plus-verifier (`GOV`), and 31-agent find → fact-check → distil (`HCC`, `PGS`, `RTP`). Only `GOV` shows verifier text, and that text is truncated in all eight civ notes, so "adversarially verified" (`A9_2 §K1`) cannot be checked. `RTP` shows fact-check outcomes for six of ~94 entries. `FASE`, the most FA-specific source, has no verifier at all. [READER]
2. **Corrections not applied.** In `GOV` at least six verifier fixes are visibly not in the body text (`A9_1 §H`; `A9_2 §K2`). `CGR`'s Aragon correction is the only one found applied. The 00_MANIFEST says notes are "quoted inline", which records the correction without applying it. [READER]
3. **An over-generalization quoted in a register row.** The "kinship gets containment" generalization (`political_hierarchy_standing.md` L22, `A9_2 §T3` [READER]) has one counter-case in the same file: Liang Ji, a consort-clan head, disposed of by granted suicide, which the source frames on the same line as "preserving some family status/property versus outright execution" [CHECKED `:427`]. The extraction's second counter-case (zu extermination, `A9_2 §K6`) does not hold: the cases at `:429` are Dong Zhuo, a warlord, and the Ten Attendants, eunuchs, neither seated by kinship [CHECKED]. This is tension, not self-contradiction. H-100 quotes the generalization and records the research as tier 3 that "CANNOT SETTLE THIS" [CHECKED `hole_register.yaml:1945-1947, :1957-1959`].
4. **Counts that do not add up.** `CGR` 21 vs 23 queue items; `RTP` 94 vs "~96"; `PGS` 75 vs "~72"; `HCC` "~94" with repeats; Han "roughly 20 grades" with 11 listed (`A9_1 §H`, `R7 §K8`, `R6 §K1,§K7`, `A9_2 §K10`). [READER]
5. **Retired idiom.** Every hook in `GOV`, `RTP`, `PGS` and the compendium is phrased as Key emission, Directive/Need gap or echo (`A9_1 §V`, `A8_1 §H`) [READER]. The substrate is retired (CLAUDE.md §0, ED-IN-0232); none of these hooks can be adopted as written.
6. **Anachronism and setting.** Valoria's governance is late-medieval/Renaissance; `HCC`'s embargo and fiscal rows run to 1956 and Zimbabwe (`R6 §C HCC §2.9`); `RTP` uses Tammany Hall, NKVD and the Night of the Long Knives (`R7 §C`) [READER]. As shapes these are defensible; as grounding for a pre-firearms polity they are surface analogy.

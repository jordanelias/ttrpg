### 1.3 Critical evaluation

- **No research program was ever run for FI.** Every historical row above was produced for SC (proceedings), FA/SE (governance) or IN (verbs) and reaches FI only because VCD cited it as evidence for an act family. The fieldwork documents themselves cite no history and no research file [READER D3 G: "No external game/history corpus files cited"; U4 G].
- **Act-level grounding is three single rows**: coroner (`examine`), Walsingham (`research`), State Inquisitors (`surveil`, `[UNVERIFIED]`). Nothing historical stands behind `interview`, `reconstruct` or `thread_read`. The richer historical material — inquest, inquisitorial procedure, denunciation, informers, proof hierarchies — maps onto `open_case`, `utter`, `petition`, `determine` and `interrogate`, i.e. onto SC's proceedings, not onto the findings verbs.
- **An anachronism the corpus does not name.** The investigation design is shaped by modern detective fiction (crime scene, prints, CCTV, case board: VCD rows 2-5), while the setting's institutions are pre-modern. Pre-modern inquiry was overwhelmingly testimonial and procedural (oath, compurgation, denunciation, inquest jury), which the history rows show and the six-act design (four of six acts physical or archival) does not reflect.
- **Method defects carried in**: GOV's verifier fixes are truncated and partly unapplied (Council of Ten 1355 vs 1334); RHET's high-confidence headline conflicts with its own source-depth admission; RTP is tertiary-sourced and Key-shaped. None of these bears on a number FI uses, because FI uses none of them.
- **Surface-analogy hooks**: "Evidence clock 3/5/8 ≈ *positiones/articuli*" maps a design constant to a manuscript structure with no argument; Verres as "hidden Exposure" relabels an audit case as fieldwork.

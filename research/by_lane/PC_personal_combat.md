### 1.3 Critical evaluation

- **Method.** All four fact files share one directory date and come from one run. None has a Status or verifier line, and every one is self-authored (R9 K15) [READER]. They are honest about their limits: explicit disagreements, `sources_attempted_but_unreadable`, flagged nulls. They are then inconsistent about those same limits across sibling files. A source one file could not open is listed as consulted in another (K1, K2, K5), and one Silver text is rated "full text, high" in CD and "located but not read" in FS (K3). The reach mechanics' historical grounding therefore rests on the weakest-attested text.
- **Advocates' claims treated as fact, on both sides.** Silver wrote the *Paradoxes* to argue for English short weapons and the staff against the Italian rapier; Fiore's fol. 39r claim that off-line guards with short spear and sword are "enough" against any long weapon is likewise his own, "not independently verified" (R9:78) [READER]. The fact files record both as doctrine and mark neither as advocacy. They converge on the short weapon's mechanism (off-line step and beat, the long weapon's inertia behind the hands; R9 T3, :80, :91) [READER]. The tension is between Silver's crowning of the staff and Fiore's sufficiency claim. The engine carries both: it implements fol. 39r's step and beat (`combat_systems.py:409-410`; `wrapper.py:192`), and its arrest rework, grounded on physics ("damage is wound, not impulse") and the stop-thrust against a step-in, notes that the retired `recoil = K*damage` "stranded the staff Silver's Paradoxes crowns" (`wrapper.py:194-205`) [CHECKED]. Whether the reach excess (§0 item 5) follows from that ranking is not measured in any extraction [CONFIDENCE: low].
- **Anachronism and modern glosses.** The research separates period terms from modern labels well: larghissima/strettissima, "half-staffing" and the 1880s half-staff are all flagged (R9 CD F1, MG cons. #4; E2a H) [READER]. The biomechanics transfers in the other direction, from unarmed modern sport to armed medieval combat; the files mark these "inference", and the commitment optimum (C4) uses them anyway (E2a G) [READER].
- **What reached code.** The research reached `modules/combat` through hand-transcribed constants and comments. No exporter or registry carries a research value, and no `.audit` file is loaded by code (`git grep r0_research_` outside `.audit` finds only `research/00_INDEX.md`) [CHECKED]. This is lawful under CLAUDE.md §0.05: the constants are the mechanism and the research is reference. But no test fails if a constant drifts from its cited source, and the grounding marks are claims written next to a number.
- **What did not reach.** Most of it, for different reasons. Grappling escapes and counters were never extracted (GR G). The companion arm is unbuilt.

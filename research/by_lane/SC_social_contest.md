### 1.3 Critical evaluation of the historical research

1. **Three corpora, three standards, one ranking.** The vendored study (`PS/source/`) is the only historical source in this lane that separates verified from unverified modern work, publishes withdrawals, and refuses to turn its tables into procedures (`C1-4` H). `SC-SRC` tags each claim T0/T1/T2 and retracts its own fabricated matrix (`E3-2` H). `RHET` tags confidence but grades the repository's own design in a section it marks `[SELF-AUTHORED]` and then reports "confirmed well-founded" (RHET line 331, 338 [CHECKED]). `GOV` claims adversarial verification it cannot show: the verifier notes are truncated in the files, and fixes that are visible are not applied (`A9-2` K1-K2).

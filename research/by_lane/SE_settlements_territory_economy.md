### 1.3 Critical evaluation of the historical research for this lane

1. **Method is uneven but not the main defect.** HCC and PGS have a find → independent fact-check → synthesis design and list their corrections [READER R6 §FILES, §K14]; GOV has a per-civilization verifier whose notes are cut off mid-sentence in the files, with several visible fixes not applied to the body [READER A9_2 §K1-K2]; FASE, PW and PA are single-author passes with no verifier and no fetched sources [READER R6 §K14; R3 §K4]. Nothing in the SE research was fetched and checked against a primary text by a second party except where HCC/PGS say so. The scholarly attributions that this lane can judge from general knowledge (Thompson 1971, Scott 1976, Sen 1981, Olson 1993, Kantorowicz 1957, Will & Wong 1991, Joyeuse Entrée 1356, Quo Warranto 1278-94, Taikō kenchi 1582-98) are correct as cited [CONFIDENCE: medium — own knowledge, not fetched].

2. **The research grounds mechanisms and openly refuses to ground magnitudes** [CHECKED FASE L323; READER R6 §PGS "Numbers are deliberately absent"]. That is the correct posture, and it means every number in the derived SE documents (Ob = base + floor(age/8), Accord −10/−25, granary 0-3, AP = 2 + FacilityTier) is ungrounded by construction.

3. **It was written against a target that no longer exists.** FASE's scope is `sim/territory/registry.py` and the `settlement_layer_v30` / `governance_play_redesign_v1` pair [CHECKED FASE L9]; HCC's triggers and PGS's menus use `governance_play_redesign_v1` vocabulary (Π, Ledger tags, the eight AP verbs) [READER R6 §K2]; CGR's landing sites are the same documents [READER A9_1 §H]. That redesign is ruled TIER 4, "just for ideas. do not treat as required" [CHECKED `engine/season/rosters.yaml:79-80`]; the settlement sim it was written to was retired at plan position `29c` [CHECKED ED-SE-0007]; the design documents are quarantined under `.designs/` [CHECKED `CURRENT.md:25` names them; A2 §FILES banner]. Of the ED-SE docket items the research generated (ED-SE-0007…0024), 12 were superseded on 2026-10-06 as "authoring task[s] against … quarantined" documents (0007-0012, 0016, 0018-0020, 0022, 0024), 4 already carried `superseded` on their 2026-07-08 rows (0013-0015, 0017) and 2 remain `ratified` (0021, 0023) [CHECKED `registers/editorial_ledger_se_archive.jsonl:12-14, :16, :21, :23, :55-66`]; ED-SE-0008's row names IN-07 as heir [CHECKED :56].

4. **Convergence across the three FASE/HCC/PGS programs is weaker evidence than it looks.** They are one research program in three rounds by the same pipeline, cross-referenced (HCC §4 cross-references CGR's 44 proposals; PGS cites the HCC docket) [READER R6 §T, §X]. Their agreement on granary/buffer, frontier garrison-settlement, charter hardening and agent oversight is a real pattern, but it is not independent rediscovery in the sense `research/00_INDEX.md` §1 rule 3 requires.

### 1.3 Critical evaluation

- **Method.** HCC is the only WR-relevant historical pass with an independent check stage. Its corrections were applied where it records them (Water Board, Norse, figure exclusions; `R6 K14`). Its counts do not add (~94 rows include repeats; `R6 K1`). FASE and the world documents cite scholars and events from memory with no verifier.
- **What it was written against.** HCC's predicates (Π, Levy/Fortify/Develop verbs, Capital-Posture, Contagion Vector, Quarantine_Tier) belong to `governance_play_redesign_v1`, a proposal (`R6 K2`). That vocabulary is not the season loop's: the loop has verbs (`levy`, `build`, `restore`, `work`), site kinds, wear and band floors (`B1 F`, `B1 P`). Nothing translated HCC from the proposal's vocabulary into the loop's, so its cases now attach to no live predicate. A reader would have to re-derive each card.
- **The repository did reach HCC's central shape, but without citing it.** The forcing proposal's FORCE-WEATHER (a keyed seasonal draw on yield) and FORCE-HAZARD (a coupling rule in the wear loop, crossings emitted at band floors) are the same "crisis audits neglected fabric" structure HCC distils from Lisbon, the Fens and the granaries (`C7_2 F` FCH §4.1). The forcing proposal cites Jordan's nine drivers, the axioms and canon P-07, not HCC (`C7_2 G`). The convergence is real but undocumented, so it counts for nothing as evidence (index rule 3: convergence is the class that earns weight only when the two sources are independent and named).
- **Anachronism and register.** HCC spans Megarian Decree to Zimbabwe 2000s and the Berlin Blockade (`R6 C` §2.2, §2.9). The setting is pre-firearms (index `PFF` scope); modern cases are shape donors at best. HCC does not flag period fit.
- **Surface-analogy hooks.** The world documents' own groundings (Pax Romana for IP, Carolingian deed-monarchy, Raj containment, Bolshevik 1917 for RM founding) are one-line analogies that assert a mechanism without showing the case supports it (`A3 G`, `A4_1 G`). None is load-bearing on code.
- **Gap.** No historical research addresses threadwork, Mending, the Calamity or Locked Zones as mechanisms (by construction: they are invented metaphysics), and none addresses narrative emergence. Historical material on Thread's social consequences does exist (PA §4.3, `research/historical/precedents_analysis.md:199-218`, `:240` [CHECKED]: cultural-revival patterns applied to Thread revelation and the Forgetting); no extraction assigned to this lane read it, so the lane's evaluated history coverage is events-only, and that coverage is unreached by code.
- **One historical-grounded event artifact exists and it too is unreached.** `proposals/grounded_event_card_deck_v1.md` (PROPOSED 2026-07-11, "not yet Jordan-ratified") draws 33 of its 58 cards from HCC and names a historical case per card (Fuggerei 1521, Standon-Wadesmill turnpike 1663, Sainte-Chapelle relic, John Law 1716-20); only 9 card bodies are present in the 335-line file (`G1 FILES`, `G1` EV rows) [READER; line count CHECKED]. Its resolvers (ED-874 domain-action `d_sigma`, Π-band draw) belong to the retired campaign driver and the unratified governance redesign (`E4_4 F`).

## Net

The attachments' stated worry (20G:402, 9T:573) was right: one of their "unoccupied" cells (rumour as knowledge in transit) is occupied outright by Dwarf Fortress (and by Kingdom Come: Deliverance II's reputation diffusion), and two (populace × scarce named agents, zone × delegate) are occupied in part by Dwarf Fortress, Songs of Syx, RimWorld and Victoria 3, with the labour-routing delegate still absent. What survives untested is the rule-bound-automaton gap, the dual-scoring gap, the multiplayer-labour gap, and belief-with-fabricated-provenance. The four factual conflicts this record first listed are now settled (see section B): the Suzerain word count, the RimWorld mean-time-to-break, the Tropico 6 faction roster and the Manor Lords latest update. Of these, the attachments were wrong on three and right on one (Manor Lords Update #8).

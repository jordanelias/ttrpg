Mechanics worth knowing:
- Approval is a derived stat with named inputs (food variety, clothing, fuel, church level, tavern ale, public order, corpses, tax) [S2 ggrecon/gamespot; ggrecon opened: clothing, church level, housing, taxes]. Since Update 5 it has 7 tiers: Reviled 0-4, Despised 5-19, Disliked 20-44, Respected 45-54 (no effect), Liked 55-79, Admired 80-94, Revered 95-100 [S2 search summary of wiki.hoodedhorse.com/Manor_Lords/Approval; wiki typo "Admitted" vs patch-note "Admired" [V2 pcgameshardware 17 Feb 2026]]. Effects per tier: Revered +3 monthly migration, +1 passive income all plot levels, +20 Influence/month, +10% militia morale; Reviled -100% tax efficiency, -3 migration, -5 Influence/month, -10 public order, cannot form militia [S2 wiki]. Approval memories fade after 90 days instead of 365 [V2 thesixthaxis 19 Dec 2025].
- v1 thresholds (">=50 = 1 family/mo, >75 = 2, <25 leave") are Apr 2024 guide figures [V2 siliconera 12 Apr 2024] and were superseded by the 7-tier system; I found no source stating what happens at "<25" after Update 5 (Disliked has no migration penalty).
- Low approval and unemployment can lead to banditry [M; not re-verified]. Population converts into an adversary without a command (design point stands only if banditry mechanism holds `?`).
- Development points: 1 per settlement level; up to 6 per settlement [S2 digitaltrends]. Settlement level used to count burgage plots (e.g. Large Town 30 L1+/20 L2+/15 L3+ in the 0.7 build [V2 gamerant 9 May 2024]); since 0.8.065 it counts family levels, so all plot-count tables are outdated.
- Influence: bandit/raider kills (~20 per brigand per one guide), Church upgrades, tithes [S2]. Claim 1,000 / 2,000 rival-held [V2 gamenguide 13 Dec 2024, three press sources agree; one forum anecdote of ~1,400 or "set to 2,000" early game - cost may vary]. "~7,000 for 7 regions" = 8-region 2024 map x 1,000 [S2 search summary of game8].
- Militia: up to 36 men, fewer if weapons short [S2 search summary of windowscentral/wiki]; Retinue starts at 5, +1 per 50 treasury, base cap 12, Garrison Tower +12 [V2 steam 4358998644638175626 for 50; S2 for caps].
- Raiders now fill inventories before retreating, after "stealing about half of a settlement's available loot or burning about 40 percent" [V2 simulationdaily]. v1 phrasing "half stolen or 40% burned" is accurate to that sentence but reads as outcomes; it is the retreat trigger.
- Player places buildings and assigns families; villagers haul, queue, consume, emigrate. Update 7: unemployed family members do chores [V2].

Mechanics worth knowing:
- Laws are the main policy verb: nearly every law moves Hope and/or Discontent, many unlock buildings [V2 thegamer ; Wikipedia]. Directions are sourced; **per-law numeric Hope/Discontent deltas were NOT obtainable** (gamepressure, thegamer, gamingbolt give only "a bit", "slightly", "significantly").
- Law cooldown [CONTESTED]: thegamer says a global 24 h wait after signing any law [V2 thegamer.com/frostpunk-book-of-laws-guide/]; fandom snippets list per-law cooldowns (Patrol 36 h, Security Outposts 2 d, Emergency Shift 18 h, Child Labour-All Jobs 36 h) [S2 fandom]. v1's "36 h Patrol / 2 d Security Outposts" is therefore [S2], not [V]. Treat all as version-dependent.
- Exclusivity: Faith XOR Order; at the end of either path New Faith / New Order replaces Hope with Devotion / Obedience [V2 thegamer: "Hope bar will turn into Obedience"; Wikipedia: extreme Order/Faith "effectively replaces the hope/discontent system"].
- Concrete law numbers that ARE sourced: Triage heals 60% and 25% of the ill die; Care House: patients eat 50% less; Ceremonial Funerals: deaths no longer cost Hope [V2 gamepressure zdad7a]; Medical Post treats 5; Bunkhouse/Tent 10 citizens, Child Shelter 15 children; normal shift 8 h, Hard Work Rigor ~14 h, Emergency Shift 24 h [V2 gamingbolt]. "Radical Treatment leaves 30% amputees" [S2 search summary].
- Mutual-exclusion pairs are cheap, legible policy forks: Soup vs Food Additives, Child Shelters vs Child Labour, Medical vs Engineers Apprentices, Radical Treatment vs Sustain Life, Cemetery vs Corpse Disposal [V2 gamepressure zdad7a].
- Emergency Shift raises Discontent [V2]; v1's "can kill workers" not found in the pages I opened [unverified].
- Rations: 1 raw food -> 2 edible food [V2 gamingbolt]; "1 ration/person/day regardless of role" and "raw eating 1:1 raises illness" [M, fandom unreadable].
- FP2 differences: Council of 100 votes on laws; seats follow faction popularity [V2 keengamer]; a vote on a law and then a **10-week cooldown whether the law passed or failed** [V2 gamerguides.com/frostpunk-2/guide/communities-and-factions/laws 24 Sep 2024]; four law categories Survival/City/Society/Rule [V2 same]; districts; time in weeks; radicals grow inside factions [V2 Wikipedia Frostpunk_2]. Breach of Trust adds a periodic Vote of Trust checking approval [V2 gematsu].
- Player controls: laws, building, assignment, expeditions, crisis responses. Sim decides: who falls ill/dies/strikes, FP2 faction behaviour.

Mechanics worth knowing:
- Population is the resource: homeless villagers will not have children; "people will start freezing to death before they starve"; a no-intervention test lost five people to the first winter [V2 anandtech post-36118933, anecdotal].
- Starting families 6 / 5 / 4 by difficulty. Easy: clothing, food, firewood, materials, tools, built houses and storage, seeds and livestock. Moderate: food, firewood, tools, materials, a storage barn, some seeds. Hard: small amounts only, no seeds; seeds and livestock only after a Trading Post [V2 Getting_Started_Guide]. Correction to v1: clothing is not granted on moderate.
- Aging is stepped, not continuous: 5 ticks per year. Forum equivalent: "1.25 years per season" [V2 anandtech]. A child can only enrol at school at age 10; a child who becomes a laborer can never be educated; one teacher, max 20 students per school [V2 School page]. The wiki's School page says nothing about education lengthening life, so v1's "uneducated 45-60, educated 80+" is unverified (an 87-year-old old-age death exists in one anecdote).
- Wooden house: ~25 firewood in winter; stone uses about one third less and is warmer [V2 House].
- Disease: hospital uses herbs when available [V2 Hospital]. v1's "cures need old forests; nomads raise disease risk" was not on that page [downgraded to M].
- No policies: levers are building, job counts, trade, nomads. The mod kit (custom resources, auto-purchase example) is where extra levers would come from [V2 shiningrocksoftware.com/game/mods/].

## Cross-game takeaways for a faction/roster/policy design
- Manor Lords: policy is tax, tithe, development picks; unrest flows through approval into migration, income, Influence and militia morale via 7 tiers (Dec 2025). Real-time battles on the same map; no personal combat, investigation, or diplomacy beyond region claims. Settlement level moved from plot counts to family levels (Mar 2026): the roster (families) became the progression metric.
- Frostpunk: strongest law system of the three (two trees, exclusion pairs, promises, cooldowns); FP2 adds a 100-seat vote with 51 / two-thirds thresholds and faction radicals; DLC adds war on a neighbour city. No trade, no named NPCs.
- Banished: no governance. Valoria already exceeds all three in H, I, J and G; the gaps these games cover (C2-C6 spatial building, K3 survival, E1 stockpile flows) are where Valoria is probably thinnest.

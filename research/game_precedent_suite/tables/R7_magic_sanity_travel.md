Mechanics worth knowing (all [V2], DD2w pages above):
- Banter bands: stress 0-3 no negative banter (11% positive); 4-6 6% positive / 6% negative; 7-9 90% negative; 10 breach. Negative banter adds stress and removes affinity: one scalar feeds the relationship layer (banter chances are from in-game files, 2025-07-24 capture).
- Meltdown (80% base): HP to 10% (not if already <=10%), -15% Deathblow RES until next Inn, +40% Stress RES for 3 turns, -3 affinity with all, 40% negative quirk. Resolute (20%): heal 67% HP, +15% Deathblow RES until next Inn, +40% Stress RES and +50% Debuff RES for 3 turns, +2 affinity with all, 40% positive quirk. The stat is "Deathblow RES" (template DB2), not v1's "Death's Door resistance".
- The fork's odds are built by character state: Meltdown +20% Shattered Will, +15% Fragile / Weak Hold On Life, +10% Coward / Soft, +5% Nervous, +10% region Malaise; Resolute +20% Calm, +15% Ascetic / Death Seeker / Resilient, +10% region Praise the Sun.
- Toxic (Flagellant only): replaces both outcomes; Toxic token, -2 affinity with all, all cooldowns cleared, -10% Deathblow RES until next Inn, HP set to 30% (45% on Scourge path, which also gets +1 Blight dealt until end of combat). A cost turned into power, DD2's nearest J1 analogue.
- Flame: 0-100, starts at 100 each region, drains while travelling and when a fight stalls (no damaging skill used); Assistance Encounters give 15-30; "A Glimmer of Hope" item +15; at 0 Cultists ambush and beating them gives 40; below 30 a Shambler may ambush on a Road Battle. Five bands per Confession (e.g. Denial: Deathblow RES +6/+3/-3/-6/-9, lowest band +100% Enemy Advantage chance). v1's "some kills give +10" is not on the page: dropped.
- Stress falls by: Inn (usually -1, up to -3; The Tempting Goblet adds +2 on entering the region), Inn Items, ally skills, quirks (Death Seeker -10 on Death's Door, Resilient). Item sizes (Calming Incense -2, Book of Bawdy Tales -6) are [S2 siliconera.com/?p=1042126]. Whether Stress resets to 0 after Meltdown: not stated on any DD2w page; [S2 gamesradar.com/darkest-dungeon-2-tips] reportedly says the meter resets and repeat meltdowns in one battle are possible (article body not retrievable). Treat as unconfirmed.
- Affliction/Virtue are Darkest Dungeon 1 terms; DD2 replaces them with Meltdown/Resolute plus quirks (the DD2 page has no Affliction/Virtue text).
- Cost class: recovery always has a price or a location (Inn stop, item, ally action); breach leaves permanent quirks and affinity pips; Relationships expire each region, so the relational cost resets on a clock.

Mechanics worth knowing:
- Terror bands [V2 SSf Terror, 2024-03-11]: Serene 0; Wary 1-24; Nervous 25-49; Fearful 50-74; Haunted 75-99; Crazed 100. Gauge: orange dots +1 per 2 s, red dots +2 per 2 s, green dots -1 per 5 s (progress only, not whole points); crew below a quarter of capacity doubles dot gain. Gained by sailing without the lamp outside lit areas, not hugging shore, some enemy attacks, events. At 100 a mutiny fires; failing either the Hearts or the Iron challenge kills you and ends the game.
- Terror recovery menu [V2 SSf Terror, a player-edited page: "about" values]: lamp on saves about 5 Terror per fuel crate; any two of {lamp, light source, shore} turn the gauge green; killing enemies removes 10% of current Terror at once; Rosegate -3 for 12 Echoes; Port Carnelian -5 for 20 Echoes; Chelonate -15 for 30 Echoes and 1 crew unless Iron 167; Kingeater's Castle -25 for 1 fuel + 1 supplies (needs Terror 50+); Frostfound, Mangrove College -10 each, gated by "Something Awaits You" (SAY); London: carouse -3 or -5 for 10-30 Echoes once per SAY, family time -20 once per SAY, Rest -1 / -15 / -20 for 10 / 100 / 100 Echoes by lodging.
- Returning to London: Terror above 50 drops to 50 [V2 SSf Terror; also cliqist.com/2015/04/01/sunless-sea-beginners-game-guide, 2015-04-01]; the wiki says the excess is turned into nightmares ("Ouch"), a persistent cost. v1's recovery claims "ports, lit areas, items" were right but unnumbered.
- Hunger [V2 SSf Hunger, 2019-02-25]: +half the crew every 10 s unpaused (.5 rounds to even); at >= 50 one supply is eaten and Hunger resets to 0; crew 19-25 = 1 supply per 50 s (1.2/min), crew 50-98 = 20 s (3/min, no ship has quarters for it); if Hunger exceeds 50 and no supplies remain, "Mouths to Feed" fires; journal bands Replete 1, Fed 25, Peckish 50, Ravenous 75, Desperate 100. Both v1 figures confirmed.
- Fuel: no tiles; drain is time-based and scaled by Engine Power. Speed = engine power over total weight; "a 1000 weight ship with 1000 engine power moves about the same as a 2000 weight ship with 2000 engine power" but the latter burns twice the fuel [V2 SSf Engine_power, 2025-12-29]. Out of fuel = "drift helplessly" [V2 SSf Fuel, 2025-06-17]; the 2013 Failbetter prototype post said "abandon ship" (outdated). Prototype post also: full speed "burn more fuel but less food" [V2 failbettergames.com/how-will-sunless-sea-look, 30 Jul 2013]. Player tests (equal fuel per distance at gear 1 and 2, full power far worse) are [S2, steam threads unfetchable]. Fuel 8-10 Echoes/barrel by port [V2 cliqist 2015].
- [CONTESTED] lamp and Terror: cliqist (2015) says the deck light "doesn't help slow terror"; the 2024 wiki says lamp on saves about 5 Terror per fuel crate. v1's "dark sailing raises Terror" matches the wiki; the older guide predates patches [version-dependent].
- Game time [V2 SSf Time,_the_Healer, 2021-03-29]: the "Another Day" clock advances only when docking in London with SAY (+6 to +10), so story time counts completed voyages, not minutes.
- Cost class: Terror, Hunger, Fuel recoverable (priced in Echoes or gated per voyage); Terror 100, crew loss and ship loss are not; permadeath with heir. Design point: three clocks share cargo space, so a trip is one allocation decision.

Mechanics worth knowing:
- Threshold: below 50% the camera tilts and Sanity Effects begin; effects fire mostly on room entry (sometimes on picking items); lower meter on entry = more likely and more severe; most hinder or harm, some are cosmetic [V2 EDf Sanity, Sanity_Effects]. Empty meter: drain events hit Health instead [V2 EDf]. Pious Augustus has no meter ("courageous resolve").
- Examples [V2 EDf Sanity_Effects]: statue heads turn, bleeding walls, upside-down rooms, character sinks or explodes into a fake death, Recover spell may "explode" the caster as a fake death, inventory appears empty, "Are you sure you want to delete all of your Saved Games?" (saves are not deleted). v1's "Giant Bomb fake memory-card deletion" is confirmed by this and superjumpmagazine.com/the-dichotomy-of-eternal-darkness-sanitys-requiem ("save data being deleted").
- Restoration [V2 EDf Sanity]: a finishing blow on a downed enemy returns a portion, "usually the amount equal to how much was lost from their initial gaze" for Zombies, Bonethieves, Horrors; Xel'lotath-aligned Recover; items such as Liquid Courage. [V2 nintendoworldreport.com/review/3905] "can only be regained by finishing off the enemy (aside from magical solutions)"; [V2 gamerevolution.com/?p=35766] adds items. v1's "finishing is [M]" is now sourced.
- Recover costs [V2 EDf Magick]: restores 24 / 40 / 100 gauge points at Circle 3 / 5 / 7; Xel'lotath (sanity) costs 10 / 20 / 30 MP, Chattur'gha (health) 30 / 40 / 50, Mantorok (both) 40 / 50 / 60; Ulyaoth Recover only recoups its own MP. Sanity can therefore be bought with MP at a favourable rate, or earned free by finishing enemies.
- Cost class: recoverable; the "cost" is perceptual deception plus (at empty) Health damage. A no-GM engine can reproduce the damage tiers but the fake-UI effects need a display layer.
- Call of Cthulhu 2018 note (Cyanide, pre-release interviews only; shipped behaviour not re-checked): lead designer Romain Wiart: "your sanity will go down", "if your insanity grows you have less endings, less options because your character can't make rational choices"; phobias: "every setting of the area triggers one phobia at a time"; regaining sanity is not covered [V2 gamingbolt.com/call-of-cthulhu-developers-discuss-how-sanity-mechanic-and-phobias-work-in-the-game; wccftech.com/call-of-cthulhu-main-path-12-15-hours/amp/]. bit-tech.net preview: a phobia is gained at a sanity threshold and then drains sanity in confined spaces; zero sanity opens "another reality" [V2]. v1's "meter removed" and "about four endings" are NOT on any page I could open (rpgamer.com 403): dropped. Mechanics worth knowing:
- Clock [V2 PLw Time]: dawn 07:30; Day 1 = 4 real min per game hour (24 h = 100 min), shortened 4 min per day to Day 11 = 2.5 min/h (60 min); time stops in dialogue and most menus, not in looting or Alembic/Brewery; saves only at clocks; midnight: new districts infected, stores refresh and raise prices, children go home, bandits appear.
- Body [V2 PLw]: 6.5 h hunger, 16.5 h exhaustion, 5 h sleep to clear exhaustion; coffee cuts exhaustion at Health and hunger cost; Immunity regenerates to 50% of its current capacity (0 to 50% about 3.25 in-game h when uninfected); infection grows exponentially, shrinks Immunity capacity, and cannot be removed without a cure (antibiotics only manage it, with considerable Health loss); thirst capped at about 66% of the meter, pushes stamina capacity down.
- Correction: v1 attributed to the player "infection at midnight if hidden value (1-100) exceeds immunity; immunity 40-50% on Days 3-6 falling to 20-30%". That is the roll for **Bound NPCs** in infected districts: random immunity per day (Days 3-6 40-50%, 7-9 30-40%, 10-11 20-30%) vs a hidden infection value 1-100 assigned at 07:30, resolved at midnight; the player's prophylaxis (boosters, tinctures, Twyrine) raises it; the animation shows odds; reloading after 07:30 does not reroll; infected Bound die if their hidden death value is below infection at midnight [V2 PLw Bound_Infection_&_Death]. A seeded daily draw with player-side mitigation, close to Valoria's draw model.
- Correction: permanent per-death penalties belong to Pathologic 2, not Pathologic 1. Max Health drops at deaths 1, 2, 4, 5, 8, 9, 11, 13, 14, 15, 17-20 (all of them = -50%); max hunger at 4, 12, 21; max exhaustion at 6, 16; death 2 removes the ability to touch; after death 10 autopsies sometimes yield Cotton Wool or Buttons; at the cap further deaths cost only time. Removed only by a new save or the Fellow Traveler's deal [V2 PLw Death; Theatre]. The Pathologic 1 section lists no death penalty: `?`.
- Pathologic 1 (the same page): sleep only in beds of private homes, 1 game hour about 5 real minutes, failed quests make a Bound fall ill [V2 PLw]. Pathologic 3 (released 9 Jan 2026 per en.wikipedia, which also still says 2025 in its Development text): Apathy and Mania replace hunger, thirst and exhaustion; the emotional scale slides toward Apathy, maxed Mania = heart attack, maxed Apathy = suicide attempt with a 5 s talk-down; Amalgam funds time travel and survival of death, running out = "True Death", save deleted; Town Management decrees over twelve days [V2 PLw, section flagged MissingInfo: pre-release text, may not match the shipped game].
- Cost class: recoverable needs plus one permanent meta-level penalty (P2). Design point: survival costs sit on a shrinking clock, so travel is a time budget against deadlines.

| id | val | how |
|---|---|---|

Mechanics worth knowing:
- Recovery is cheap: "You can raise your Humanity like any other character stat, by spending experience" [V2 VTf tips]; Humanity is also gained in quests (e.g. persuading two rivals to reconcile, 6 XP plus Humanity, "speak ... again for another point") [V2 VTf The_Hitman_Impasse]; Masquerade points are reinstated "by performing actions to protect the Masquerade" [V2 VTf game page]. v1's "redemption missions [Gaming Nexus]" is consistent but the wiki gives no numbers per action.
- Humanity floor [CONTESTED within the wiki]: game page says no game over at the minimum ("3 by default") but frenzy near-certain when hungry or hurt; Character_Sheet says at zero "they go into a frenzy". The range itself (3 floor vs 0) is unresolved.
- Frenzy [V2 VTf Frenzy]: triggered by starvation, pain, public humiliation; Brujah -2, Gangrel -1 on frenzy checks; character histories can modify.
- Zones [V2 tips]: Elysium forbids attacks and Disciplines and draining mortals there costs Humanity; combat areas allow Disciplines with no Masquerade violation but may still cost Humanity; seducing a human lets you feed without violating the Masquerade.
- Cost class: power costs blood (recoverable by feeding); Humanity is a slow behaviour score, buyable back with XP; Masquerade is a 5-count lives meter that ends the run. Differs from Valoria: the power itself does not erode Humanity.

- Factions (10, era-gated): Crown + Revolutionaries (Colonial only); Communists, Capitalists, Religious, Militarists (from World Wars);
  Environmentalists, Industrialists (from Cold War); Intellectuals, Conservatives (Modern). Opposing pairs: Capitalists-Communists,
  Conservatives-Intellectuals, Religious-Militarists, Industrialists-Environmentalists [V2 gamepressure fractions; V2 jeu.video/article/effets-donnent-factions
  (French, calls Crown "Royalistes"); pairs S2 trueachievements.com]. Original file left this "?": FILLED. "Nationalists/Loyalists" do not appear.
- Constitution: unlocked at independence; "number increase with each era"; paragraphs can be changed only "once a few years" [V2 gamepressure politics].
  Count "4 per era, 2 unlocked at start, 2 need research" [S2 tropico.fandom.com/wiki/Constitution_(Tropico_6), search snippet; fetch 402]. Total not stated by any page I opened.
- Edicts: gamepressure lists them by era in a table (fetch-tool count 46: 10 Colonial, 14 WW, 11 Cold War, 11 Modern) [V2, count unverified]. Prices rarely numeric:
  Urban Development "expensive" activation, 5 years; Industrialization "high", 2 years; State Loans $50,000 credit; Nuclear Testing pays $100,000, once per game;
  Early Elections triggers an election one year after activation [V2 edicts page]. No T6 edict cost list in dollars exists in any page I could open: `?`.
- Election pressure is a lever you pull before a date: promises raise approval "a serious raise"; at 30-40% approval a speech or faking the vote helps [V2 politics + happiness pages].
  Forum: Audience and Tax Break used before elections; Martial Law + Right to Arms to avoid them [V2 steam .../598519066984987061; opinion, partly disputed in-thread].
- Governance verbs resolve deterministically in every source read; probability appears in effects (Detente 50%, Spelling Bee 5%), spy conversions, and coup risk [V2].
- Time: real time with pause [M]. Elections are optional from World Wars (hold regularly or skip); Early Elections edict forces one a year after activation [V2 gamepressure eras + edicts].

Mechanics worth knowing:
- HEADLINE ATTACK: no dice found, but governance actions DO fail, deterministically. Bills: "This bill will fail with only 90 votes unless you accept the alliance"; another is "guaranteed to pass" [V2 wiki Bill]. A persuasion decision: "He can either persuade Heron Garaci or he will fail to do so", success set by prior choices, "not chance" [V2 wiki Decision]. Absence of RNG is a negative: no page I read states it outright [V2 pages silent]. 
- Constitutional levers with numbers: limited veto needs 166/250 (two thirds) or 150/250 (three fifths) to override; impeachment needs 2/3 of the Assembly; electoral threshold 10%, options 3%/8%/15% [V2 wiki Reform].
- Decision gating is by stat thresholds: "only happen if Government Budget is -8 or lower", "Economy is less than 7 and Late Game Crisis is higher than 2" [V2 wiki Decision]. Hidden stats feed these.
- Three political paths per country: Sordland Autocrat / Dictator / Reformist (Dictator "most difficult ... most rewarding"); Rizia Absolutist / Reformist / Status Quo (Reformist "gain less authority") [V2 suzerain.wiki.gg/wiki/Political_path].
- Rizia decrees "consume precious authority and resources" and affect opinion and stability [V2 Xbox]; Rizia adds energy and authority resources [S2 Epic listing].
- Ironman: first playthrough has checkpoints only, manual saves unlock after finishing [V2 steam FAQ]. Endings "can be avoided with calculated action" [V2].

Mechanics worth knowing:
- Turn = three months; quarterly report; "You are not able to proceed to the next turn until outstanding dilemmas have been dealt with" [V2 mirror].
- Political capital is generated each turn from popularity, electoral majority, minister loyalty and whether emergency powers are active in a crisis; coalitions reduce it [V2 mirror]. The policy data carries separate capital prices for Introduce, Cancel, Raise, Lower [V2 positech mod_policies.html]. A country can multiply one: "EU countries now have a 4x multiplier ... to change immigration" (build 1.24, 2021-03-12) [V2 positech.co.uk/cliffsblog/2021/03/12/updated-democracy-4-to-build-1-24/]. Absolute capital numbers: not published on any page opened: `?`.
- CYNICISM [V2 mirror 2020-10-02]: tracked per voter group; "caused by making policy changes that appeal to voters just before an election", by "upsetting voters just after an election" and by "flip-flop" reversals; "eventually tails off and is forgotten"; shown on the group details and polls screens. Build 1.24: "Cynicism has a stronger effect now." Cliff (2022-01-09): "Perhaps cynicism is not a strong enough effect ... it clearly drops between terms 2 and 3!" [V2 blog].
- COMPLACENCY [V2 mirror]: per group, groups "initially happy" start to take pleasing policies for granted; "If support from that group falls low enough, complacency levels will drop away again"; the maximum perceived level rises over terms, so core supporters get harder to please. Cliff (2022-02-24): "If they are massively happy, then complacency creeps in", modelled from group happiness; a steady rise for the "everyone" group is "a bit hacky" [V2 blog]. Two inputs (term count 2020, happiness 2022): treat the exact rule as version-dependent [CONTESTED].
- HIDDEN CONTROL beneath the transparent model: "Winning is the value of a special hidden variable I use in the game to adjust difficulty." Win rates by term in v1.45: T1 54.19%, T2 79.23%, T3+ 83.97% (v1.46: 54.54 / 76.46 / 83.06); average election vote share ~74% vs an intended 52-58% [V2 blog 2022-01-09]. Late-game "everything gets solved", "the next win is pretty guaranteed" [V2 blog 2022-02-24].
- Assassination risk: angry pressure-group members "may, over time, become 'radicalized'" and join terrorist groups; strong military/intelligence policy "will reduce the likelihood" of a successful attack; FAQ: players "get assassinated when they try to change their country super-radically overnight" [V2 mirror; positech.co.uk/democracy4/faq.html]. This IS a chance event on governance consequences, not on the policy action.
- Voters join a group only when "greater than 50% invested" in an issue [V2 mirror].

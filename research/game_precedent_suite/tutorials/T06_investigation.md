### Return of the Obra Dinn - facts line
Lucas Pope / 3909 LLC; first-person deduction puzzle; PC/macOS 2018-10-17 or -18 [CONTESTED: Wikipedia infobox says 18th, Development text says 17th], consoles 2019-10-18 [V3 en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Return_of_the_Obra_Dinn raw 2026-10-22]. Rules relied on: the wiki General page rev 2025-10-10 (I did not look for patch notes). One mode, no difficulty setting. One sitting about 1-2 h [M]; whole game about 8-15 h [M, reviews vary].
Basis: wiki General/Endings/Pocketwatch/Epilogue pages and Game Informer tips are [V3]; the "what the first hour feels like" steps are partly [M] and say so.

#### 1. Setup choices
- There are none that change play. No character, difficulty, or map. The only pre-game options I found are display ones (1-bit filter styles, e.g. "Macintosh") [V3 obradinn.fandom.com/wiki/General rev 2025-10-10, FAQ].
- You are fixed as the East India Company's chief inspector, with a book (the catalogue) and one pocket watch (Memento Mortem). Your save is kept after you leave: reloading returns you to the ship just before you go back to the boatman [V3 wiki General].
- The task is fixed at 60 souls: for each, a name, a fate (cause of death, or where alive) and, if killed, the killer's name [V3 wiki General; Wikipedia].

#### 2. The loop
Clock: none. There is no time limit; the rain only signals that every memory has been seen [V3 wiki General "Ending the game"]. Exploration is free-movement first person.
1. Walk the Obra Dinn. Doors marked with a line in the keyhole open, a cross does not; new decks open as you complete memories ("check the stairwell hatches") [V3 wiki General].
2. Approach any remains. The watch comes out automatically when you are in range; interact once to enter that person's death memory, again while the hands spin to abort [V3 wiki General].
3. In the memory (a frozen scene, with an audio clip of the seconds before the death played first): move, zoom, read, look at faces, clothes, positions, accents. You cannot touch doors or use the watch on corpses seen there, at first [V3 wiki General].
4. After a time the screen fades; the page for this memory fills in with basic information and a field to enter the fate. If the memory contains another corpse the watch shakes, and you can chain into a follow-up memory [V3 wiki General].
5. Leave through a doorway that opens onto empty space, back on the real ship; the map now marks where the corpse lies [V3 wiki General].
6. Open the book (Tab), read the page and transcript, look at the crew sketches and manifest, then assign identities and fates by picking from lists. Revise freely.
7. Once three entries are fully correct the game locks them in: handwriting is replaced by print, and the names leave the picker. Wrong entries are never marked wrong; they stay handwritten, so a wrong one is revealed only by what did not lock [V3 wiki General; Wikipedia; v2 note on filmstories.co.uk, not re-opened].
8. Repeat until every memory is seen. Then it rains and the boatman calls; leave at any time (even with few fates solved), and the ending letter depends on the count [V3 wiki Endings, Epilogue].
Automated: filling in the page, the map update, chapter marks (a mark is made on the chapter's first page when all its memories are seen), face un-blurring, lock-in. Interrupts: none; control is never taken from you except the fade at the end of a first viewing.

#### 4. First hour, step by step
1. Climb the ladder (interact at the ladder), then go back down when the boatman says the box is too heavy and take the book and watch from the chest [V3 wiki General "Getting started"].
2. Open the book (Tab) once. [M] It is mostly empty at first, but it holds the ship's map and route, the crew sketches and a preface [V3 wiki General; Pocketwatch].
3. Walk the open deck, find a corpse, hold position until the watch appears, interact. [M] the first corpse you meet is a late-story death, since the story is told out of order.
4. Listen to the audio that plays before the scene: on a first viewing a written transcript follows and stays attached to the page; accents and names in it are clues [V3 wiki General; gameinformer.com 2018-10-18].
5. In the scene, ignore the victim for a moment; look at everyone in the room, their uniforms (rank), where they stand, their voices and accents, who is beside whom [V3 wiki Tips].
6. Zoom in on faces: a white outline appears; opening the book now jumps to that person's page or sketch [V3 wiki General].
7. Leave through the empty doorway, return to the book, read the page and the transcript.
8. Notice a face that is blurred: the game does not think you have enough information yet. The one-to-three triangles by a face show a fixed difficulty [V3 wiki General].
9. Resist guessing. Collect every chapter's first memory first; the tip is to look for the corpse with flies on each deck [V3 gameinformer.com 2018-10-18].
10. Follow a shaking watch to the follow-up death when one happens (a corpse inside the memory has its own memory) [V3 wiki General].
11. Use the sketches against the manifest (nationality, rank) to narrow names; assign the sure ones (one-triangle people whose name or nationality is spoken).
12. After a few correct entries, three lock in. That is your first feedback about whether you are reasoning correctly. If two or three sets pass without a lock, the advice is to revisit your reasoning [V3 gameinformer.com].
13. Return to open decks as hatches unlock. Re-enter old memories with new knowledge; the game expects heavy backtracking.

#### 5. What changes later
- No new mechanic is added. What grows is the number of linked pages: the chapter structure (ten chapters, I to X, with VIII reachable only after the epilogue) and cross-references between memories. Late people are the ones whose identity only follows from many others [V3 wiki General, Bargain].
- Verification tightens: sets of three, then for the last six fates discovered, sets of two, until 58 of 60 are logged [V3 en.wikipedia.org].
- Pacing is player-set; the only pressure is the book filling and your own certainty.

#### 6. Failure and ending
- You cannot fail. A wrong entry has no cost beyond being absent from the lock; changing someone's identity does NOT update the killer name already entered elsewhere, you must fix both pages by hand [V3 wiki General].
- Endings by fates solved: 0-27 (Dr Evans died regretting the choice), 30-56 (appreciation), 58 solvable (Evans returns the book and a monkey's hand; chapter VIII is then playable and all 60 fates can be completed) [V3 wiki Endings rev 2021-04-01; Epilogue rev 2026-06-03]. The wiki gives no tier for 28-29 or 57; treat as `?`.
- Leaving early is permitted. "Abandon Ship" is awarded for leaving without solving all available chapters [V3 wiki Achievements]. No save is lost: reload returns you to the ship [V3 wiki General].

#### 7. Consequential vs cosmetic choices
- Consequential: how many fates you complete (ending tier, access to chapter VIII) and which order you reason in (every identity depends on others).
- Cosmetic: the order you view memories beyond the game's own gating, display style, whether you guess. Ending letters differ in text only (letter, shelf, maid's line) [V3 wiki Endings].
- How a hypothesis is tested: you enter a candidate (name, means, killer) and nothing happens until three pending entries are fully right; then those lock. So confirmation arrives in bursts, and a wrong entry shows only by staying handwritten.
- Dominant answer: guessing is the only "cheat". The wiki says brute-forcing is possible, but "more than enough information" exists to finish without one guess [V3 wiki General]. A reviewer reportedly cycled three candidate names across three batches to find the wrong one (v2 note on filmstories.co.uk/?p=83249, not re-opened): the lock is a throttle, not a wall.
- Fixed difficulty: the triangle rating never changes with your progress [V3 wiki General].

### Shadows of Doubt - facts line
ColePowered Games / Fireshine Games; first-person procedural detective-stealth sim. Early access 2023-04-24; 1.0 = patch 39.07 on 2024-09-26 (Missing Persons update: kidnapping and cover-up cases, ragdoll bodies, 2 more pre-made cities); current patch read: 42.04 on 2026-09-16 [V3 api.steampowered.com ISteamNews appid 986130, 154 posts]. Tutorial assumes Sandbox on a Medium city after The Dead of Night. A murder case takes roughly 1-3 real hours [M]; a full run to retirement is tens of hours [M].
Basis: wiki pages via api.php (Resolution Form rev 2025-02-27, Cases rev 2026-09-10, Handcuffs rev 2024-03-13, Social Credit rev 2026-06-28), Steam patch notes, HackerNoon guide (2023-09-28, pre-1.0, no version given). The wiki Case Board page is a stub; board mechanics are from the guide and patch notes.

#### 1. Setup choices
- Mode: The Dead of Night (scripted tutorial murder, 1979 ex-cop who was evicted; afterwards continue or start Sandbox) or Sandbox (custom character, no script) [V3 wiki The Dead of Night rev 2024-03-07; Sandbox rev 2024-03-07].
- City: one of 3 pre-made (Charlotte Heights pop 326, Casablanca Quarter 368, New Lilly-Crofte 318, all Medium) or generate with name, size (Small 9 blocks ~300-350 pop; Medium 12 blocks ~350-450; Large 16 blocks ~450-550; Very Large 20 blocks ~550-700) and seed; optional City Editor [V3 wiki City rev 2025-02-27]. Seeds are not stable across game versions since 36.06.
- Game Length sets the Social Credit you need to retire: Short 5, Normal 8, Long 10 (default per The Fields page), Very Long 12; the table lists Very Short as 0 while The Fields page says 3-12 [CONTESTED within the wiki] [V3 wiki Social Credit rev 2026-06-28; The Fields rev 2026-09-10]. Perks cap at the chosen length.
- Toggles: individual Status Effects on/off; starting apartment on/off; Permadeath (added 2023-12-13); modifiers since 41.02 (2025-09-18): Film Noir, Ironman, Gambling Debt (loan shark, pay daily), House Arrest, Short-Sighted, Fame & Fortune, Snail Nemesis, Rat Detective, "many more" [V3 Steam 41.02 post]. Since 42.04 you can disable regular-murder, sniper or kidnapping case types for future cases [V3 Steam 42.04].
- Character details beyond name are `?` (not read).

#### 2. The loop
Clock: continuous in-game clock (digital watch, 24h shop hours, citizens follow daily schedules); opening the case board pauses the game, except while auto-travelling [V3 Steam 37.07 notes]; sitting lets you "pass time". Real-to-game ratio `?`.
1. A killer is active. A murder is reported over the police transmissions you carry, and you open a case; the case's questions appear as objectives on the right [V3 en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shadows_of_Doubt; Steam Dev Blog 46 2026-08-10].
2. Travel to the scene (plot a route on the map; Auto Travel walks it). Enforcers (1-3) search, cordon and guard it; entering unseen needs vents or distraction until Social Credit 7 lets you in legally [V3 wiki Enforcers rev 2026-06-28; Social Credit; Wikipedia main page].
3. Collect evidence: body, prints and footprints (Print Scanner, labelled A, B, ... AA), photos, documents, bullet holes, notes. Prints deteriorate ("a few in-game hours" per HackerNoon 2023); if you wait out the Enforcers, evidence deteriorates and a home scene is eventually cleared out [V3 wiki Print Scanner; Enforcers].
4. Pin it to the case board. Finding something usually draws a red string to its source and linked people; you add your own strings and notes [V3 hackernoon 2023].
5. Follow leads outward: name -> City Directory / City Hall database / address book / employee record -> address, workplace, phone, CCTV (24 h), V-mail, call logs, receipts.
6. Find, watch or intercept a suspect (routines are real; "pass time" to wait for them). Optionally question, bribe, accuse in dialogue, handcuff (2 h limit) [V3 wiki Handcuffs; Steam 35.04].
7. Go to City Hall, fill in the Resolution Form with the board's items, submit. Result: payout or fine; case ends; after "some time" the game picks the next killer [V3 en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shadows_of_Doubt].
Between steps the game runs the city: schedules, muggers (SC2+), Enforcers, security systems, hunger and fatigue. Automated: red-string creation, form validation, killer selection, new murders.

#### 4. First hour, step by step
1. Choose The Dead of Night. Your phone rings with silence; a note under the door names a citizen to find [V3 wiki The Dead of Night].
2. Find that citizen's home with the City Directory (search a name for an address; GPS can be enabled) [V3 gamepressure 2023-04-27].
3. Enter and find the body. Inspect it first: it starts a victim profile [V3 hackernoon 2023-09-28].
4. Scan prints and footprints, pick up documents; items appear on the board with strings. Photograph messages [V3 hackernoon].
5. When Enforcers arrive you must escape through the air ducts; afterwards you are directed to solve the murder [V3 wiki The Dead of Night]. Steps 3-4 are the wiki's "documenting several pieces of forensic evidence", filled in from the guide.
6. Follow name leads from the victim: workplace, apartment, phone, neighbours. Advice from guides is to learn about the victim first because the suspect is usually linked to them [S3 search summary of beginner guides, unverified].
7. If someone is out, check their workplace or wait for them to come back [V3 gamepressure.com 2023-04-27].
8. Match prints from the scene to a person: prints can be taken from things they touched, or directly from someone asleep, cuffed, knocked out or dead [V3 wiki Citizen Profile rev 2025-06-05].
9. Fill the Resolution Form at City Hall: the name is required; arrest, weapon, evidence and address are bonuses [V3 wiki Resolution Form].
10. Hand it in. The tutorial then evicts you and gives a small apartment; free play begins [V3 wiki The Dead of Night].
11. In Sandbox, read the bulletin board by a public phone for side jobs while you wait for a murder [V3 gamepressure].
12. When you can, buy tools (the guide says carry at least a camera and lockpicks) and install a SyncDisk; eat and sleep, and avoid trespass zones [V3 hackernoon; wiki SyncDisks].

#### 5. What changes later
- Social Credit unlocks (points 350, 750, 1250, 2000 for levels 2-5): apartments, fast travel, loitering, crime-scene access at 7, echelon access at 8, more tolerant citizens at 10. Muggers start at SC2 [V3 wiki Social Credit].
- New killer types (snipers leave no prints), then kidnap cases with a six-hour rescue window and cover-up jobs (late) [V3 wiki Cases, Resolution Form].
- What ramps: muggers from SC2, Enforcers who bar scenes until SC7, security in Echelon zones, and a fresh killer after each resolved case; killer types widened over time (snipers 2024-04-10, kidnappers 2024-09-26) [V3 Steam posts; wiki Social Credit].

#### 6. Failure and ending
- Wrong killer name: -100 credits, the real killer keeps killing [V3 wiki Resolution Form; Wikipedia]. Optional fields wrong cost nothing. A wrongly cuffed person is fined [S3 steam forum snippets; anecdotal]. Players report per-field right/wrong marks on a rejected form [V3 steamcommunity.com/app/986130/discussions/0/4349995545098337377 2024-06-21].
- Getting caught: fines. Death normally ends at a hospital; with the Ironman modifier you keep one save slot per character, and with Permadeath as well that slot is deleted when you die [V3 Steam 41.02 post].
- Win: reach the required SC, submit the Retirement form, nominate an heir, the game ends [V3 wiki The Fields]. Whether you may resubmit the same case after a wrong name: `?`.

#### 7. Consequential vs cosmetic choices
- Consequential: the killer name (the only graded field); speed (prints fade; scenes wiped); how you enter (trespass risk); whether you build chains from evidence.
- Bonus-only: arrest, weapon, evidence, address (+350/+300/+500/+50), no downside.
- Killer archetypes leave signatures (Ripper's note with the name's letters scrambled; Cutthroat's business card; Sniper no prints), so recognising the type narrows the field [V3 wiki Cases rev 2026-09-10].
- Cosmetic or taste: apartment decor, board colour codes (community convention, not game) [V3 Steam Investigations #16], most modifiers except Ironman/Permadeath/Gambling Debt.
- How a hypothesis is tested: you may stack guesses on the board freely; the first and only verdict is the form at City Hall (and, since 2023, a dialogue accusation whose result is the suspect's reaction). A wrong name costs 100 credits but the killer then kills again. One reviewer cites "lack of consequences" in the base game [V3 Wikipedia].

### Pentiment - facts line
Obsidian Entertainment / Xbox Game Studios; 2D narrative adventure-RPG (Josh Sawyer). Windows, Xbox 2022-11-15; PS4/PS5/Switch 2024-02-22 [V3 en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pentiment_(video_game) raw 2026-10-22]. No difficulty setting; assumes a first blind playthrough of Acts I-III on the current version (wiki revs 2022-2026; I did not look for patch notes). Whole game about 15-20 h [M]; one session is one or two "days" of the story, about 1-2 h [M].
Basis: wiki pages via api.php (Time rev 2023-07-07, Quests rev 2026-04-13, Character creation rev 2026-03-31, Dialogue system rev 2024-11-17, Persuasion rev 2023-07-07, Achievements rev 2026-04-13), Game Developer (2023-01-04), GameBanshee (2023-01-16), TheGamer (2022-11-19). The opening hour is from quest-stage text in the wiki, not from a played walkthrough; where I infer, I say so.

#### 1. Setup choices
- No difficulty setting that I found [M]. The game asks for biography choices, one set per act; each unlocks dialogue options and persuasion points, not stats [V3 wiki Character creation rev 2026-03-31].
- Act I (Andreas, 1518), chosen in the opening scenes: Wanderjahr area (Basel, Low Countries, Italy: languages and cultural references); Background (Hedonist, Craftsman, Bookworm, Rapscallion, Businessman); Master's degree (Theology, Imperial Law, Medicine); two Favorite Subjects of five (Latinist, Logician, Orator, Occultist, Heavens and Earth).
- Act II (1525): where Andreas worked for the past years: France, Poland, Aragon or England (more languages; different talk with some characters).
- Act III (1543, you play Magdalene): a trait from the book Andreas gave her in Act II (Empiricist, Vitae Sanctorum, Myths and Legends, Bibliophilia), a Work skill (Tinker, Domestics, Bookkeeping), a Reputation (Barbs, Flirt, Haggle, Snoop), a Reading Interest (Polyglot, Volksbucher, Reformation).
- Trade-offs are real: "reading French can help in some situations but prevents you from deciphering Italian texts" [V3 gamebanshee.com/9xhy8].

#### 2. The loop
Clock: story days divided by canonical hours (Lauds, Prime, Sext, Nones, Vespers, Compline, Matins) in Acts I-II; a 24-hour dial with calendar in Act III [V3 wiki Time]. Free movement and talking cost no time; "certain actions" (mostly major quest events) move the clock; the game warns you first (Andreas thinks "I'll lose some time tomorrow morning unless I turn back now"). At meals (Sext, Vespers) time only advances when you choose whom to eat with [V3 wiki Time].
1. Wake in the morning; a journal entry sets the day's stage (e.g. "get to the scriptorium").
2. Walk the town and abbey: people move on schedules and events take them away [V3 thegamer.com 2022-11-19].
3. Talk. Choose dialogue lines; some are marked "This will be remembered"; some conversations end in a persuasion check [V3 wiki Dialogue system].
4. Pick which investigation quest or lead to advance; the big ones advance the clock, which is why "you'll never be able to complete every single quest" [V3 wiki Quests].
5. Pick who to eat with; this decides whom you learn from.
6. Compline/Matins: sleep. The journal logs what you learned (every character is listed with a portrait).
7. A story gate ends the act: Act I the Archdeacon's interrogation; Act II soldiers surround the town and you "only have until the end of the day" [V3 wiki Soldiers Approach]. There you accuse. Control passes to the story; a cut shows the consequence; years pass.
Automated: time marking, journal entries, schedules, remembering flagged choices, checking persuasion (a threshold test, no dice).

#### 4. First hour, step by step
Built from wiki quest-stage text, so partly inferred; the order of days is the wiki's.
1. Start a new game; you open in the Memory Palace, which gives the first choices and shows the text style [V3 wiki Memory Palace rev 2024-08-13].
2. During "A Few More Pages" (the first in-game day) scripted conversations ask for the Wanderjahr area, background (Big Jorg asks), degree and two subjects; this quest "counts as character creation" [V3 wiki Character creation; Quests].
3. Walk to the abbey scriptorium; optional Gertners' Taxes is offered. Time passes after each stage [V3 wiki A Few More Pages].
4. Eat with Otto and Endris, work, then take supper at the abbot's table, where the baron and abbot quarrel [V3 same].
5. Day 2, "The Flood": the baron is found stabbed in the chapter house; Brother Piero kneels beside him; the abbot orders you out; the Druckers name four people with grudges (Lucky Steinauer, the Widow Kemperyn, Mother Cecilia, Prior Ferenc) [V3 wiki The Flood rev 2023-07-07].
6. Choose: join Florian's autopsy (throw a rock at the infirmary window) or follow other leads. Joining yields a bloody note from the "Thread-Puller" [V3 wiki Thread-Puller Notes].
7. Pick a supper partner; go to bed (or try the library).
8. Day 3: join the spinning bee to hear gossip (the baron's affair, the beaten Rannig) [V3 wiki The Spinning Bee].
9. Work the suspect quests: Occult Hands (Ferenc: charcoal rubbing, cipher, a disturbed grave), The Widow's Curse, A Stonemason's Anger, Notes on Sister Matilda [V3 wiki Quests; Occult Hands; Widow's Curse].
10. Notice the journal updates after each stage; read it when unsure.
11. The Archdeacon arrives; you are summoned to the chapter house at Nones [V3 wiki The Archdeacon's Investigation].
12. Persuade the Archdeacon: his check adds points such as Orator +3, "Gave your name politely" +2, "Made a pedantic point about corpus delicti" -2 [V3 wiki Jacob Estler]. In the interrogation you accuse one suspect; the four recorded outcomes are Matilda, Lucky, the Widow or Ferenc executed [V3 wiki Achievements rev 2026-04-13].

#### 5. What changes later
- Act II (1525) repeats the form with new suspects (Brother Guy, Hanna, Martin Bauer), different leads (Golden Hand inn, the miller, a yellow flower), and visible results of Act I: for example where the Widow lives depends on who was executed and whether you saved her home [V3 wiki Ottilia page rev 2024-08-29].
- Accusing in Act II is also a persuasion check: the wiki's "Accuse Martin" table scores Rapscallion +3, Orator +3, plus points for each fact you found (his missing scar, no sneezing, Jobst Faerber's name) [V3 wiki Martin Bauer rev 2026-05-14]. Naming Martin "will get him killed". The Act II culprit pool is Brother Guy, Hanna or Martin [V3 wiki Quests].
- Act III (1543, Magdalene) has no trial: historical research quests; your replies fill a four-part mural (Pagan times, Christian Tassing, Peasants' Revolt, Future) and the family-tree ending [V3 wiki Endings rev 2023-10-06]. Wikipedia's plot has the priest Thomas Sprecher revealed as the mastermind who orchestrated the murders and the attack on Claus.

#### 6. Failure and ending
- There is no fail state and no check of your answer. Acts end at the story gate; Act I has four execution outcomes (Matilda, Lucky, the Widow or Ferenc), which are mutually exclusive achievements [V3 wiki Achievements]. The abbot first blames Piero; the Archdeacon is biased toward the widow and against the monk [V3 Game Developer 2023-01-04; Wikipedia].
- Recovery: none in a save ("choices are permanent unless you start a new file", autosave) [V3 thegamer.com].
- Ending: the Act III mural and family tree; outcomes differ in text and fates of families, not in a win flag.

#### 7. Consequential vs cosmetic choices
- Consequential: who you accuse (a named person dies and later scenes change); which leads you spend hours on (you cannot do everything); whom you eat with; flagged "remembered" choices that add or remove persuasion points.
- Character background: opens options and +/- points (Orator +3 with the Archdeacon, Imperial Law spots the lease trick) but dialogue, not numbers on the world [V3 wiki Ottilia page; Jacob Estler].
- Not checked: guilt. Sawyer: "there cannot be a right answer", no culprit [V3 shacknews.com 2025-10-08]; most crimes have no canonical killer [V3 gamedeveloper.com 2023-01-04]. An alibi system was prototyped and cut because it only excludes suspects. GameBanshee: "you'll never know for sure if the people you accuse are the actual perpetrators".
- How a hypothesis is tested: it is not. You collect motive and means, then argue; the only feedback is the cut-scene consequence (and persuasion points shown as triangles at the moment of a check).
- Cosmetic: the Memory Palace thought bubbles (I found no mechanical effect), script fonts, Act III mural pieces beyond the epilogue.

### L.A. Noire - facts line
Team Bondi / Rockstar Games; third-person detective action-adventure with MotionScan faces. PS3/Xbox 360 May 2011, Windows November 2011 (Complete Edition, 8 Nov in North America); Remaster by Virtuos for Switch/PS4/Xbox One 2017-11-14; VR Case Files 2017-12-15 [V3 en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L.A._Noire raw 2026-10-22]. This tutorial assumes the Remaster's labels (Good Cop / Bad Cop / Accuse). No difficulty setting found; optional black-and-white mode. One case about 1-2 h [M]; 21 assigned cases plus 40 street crimes [V3 lanoire.fandom.com/wiki/Case rev 2024-07-07].
Basis: Wikipedia gameplay section, wiki Interrogation (rev 2024-11-12), Case rating (rev 2024-01-31), Intuition Points (rev 2020-07-23), Crime Scenes, Evidence, Truth/Doubt/Lie, Upon Reflection (rev 2025-06-18). I did not play-test; sound-cue and UI details are wiki text.

#### 1. Setup choices
- Almost none. You pick a save, and between cases you may replay any completed case from the case select screen; a replayed case starts you with the maximum five Intuition points [V3 wiki Intuition Points].
- Black-and-white film noir display is optional [V3 Wikipedia].
- Per case you may let your partner drive (skips travel; avoids vehicle damage, which counts against your rating; one case shows a missed call that blocks 5 stars) [V3 wiki Case rating].
- Labels differ by edition: PS3, Xbox 360 and Windows show Truth / Doubt / Lie; Switch, PS4, Xbox One and VR show Good Cop / Bad Cop / Accuse. Development names were Coax / Force / Lie [V3 Wikipedia footnote; wiki Interrogation].

#### 2. The loop
Clock: none in play. Cases run in a fixed order across desks; between cases free roam has no schedule. Each case is a short authored chain.
1. A cutscene shows the crime; the Captain briefs you; you and your partner go to the crime scene (drive yourself or "partner drives") [V3 wiki Case].
2. Search the scene on foot. Small audio "dings" sound near objects of interest; picked items go in the notebook; when all key clues are found the investigation music ends (a newspaper must be read first if present) [V3 wiki Evidence; Wikipedia].
3. Interview persons of interest: choose a question from the notebook list; the character answers on MotionScan faces; you pick Good Cop (believe), Bad Cop (doubt) or Accuse (call it a lie, which needs a notebook item as proof) [V3 wiki Interrogation].
4. The game responds at once: right choice advances the line and may add a clue or location; wrong choice may make the suspect stop talking and you lose that information [V3 wiki Interrogation]. Stingers mark correct and incorrect (wiki sound cues).
5. New locations appear in the notebook; repeat 2-4. Chases, brawls and shootouts interrupt; you can skip an action sequence after three failures [V3 Wikipedia].
6. Close: arrest, or at the station choose whom to charge when two suspects fit (Homicide has two such cases) [V3 wiki Homicide; Wikipedia].
7. Case report: 1-5 stars (Unbecoming, Routine, Meritorious, Distinguished, Valorous) from clues found, questions correctly branched, vehicle damage, city property damage, injuries inflicted; XP raises Rank [V3 wiki Case rating].
Automated: clue logging, partner calls, rating, Intuition regeneration (+1 per Rank, cap Rank 20).

#### 4. First hour, step by step
This is the Patrol desk tutorial case "Upon Reflection", then the move to detective work.
1. Start as a Patrol officer with partner Ralph Dunn; you take a radio call to a shooting at 6th and Industrial [V3 wiki Upon Reflection rev 2025-06-18].
2. At the alley, Detective Floyd Rose asks for an evidence search; walk and search; the first thing found is a newspaper, then blood on the door [V3 same].
3. Find the second clue: a windowpane reflecting a gun on the next roof; climb the drainpipe and collect a nickel Smith & Wesson Model 27 (serial S71893).
4. Open the notebook; it now lists clue, lead and objectives.
5. Ask Dunn about gun stores; drive (or let him drive) to Central Guns.
6. The shopkeeper shows his order book; match the serial to find the buyer, Errol Schroeder, 203 South Gless Street.
7. Go to the address and knock. Schroeder says his gun is in his drawer; it is missing, Phelps arrests him, a brawl follows, then you search the apartment and find a notebook listing Detective Rose's number, which Dunn tells you to leave where it lies [V3 wiki Upon Reflection]. This tutorial case has no graded interview.
8. The other Patrol cases (Armed and Dangerous, Warrants Outstanding, Buyer Beware) are tutorials for clue finding, shootouts, chases and interrogation and give no star rating [V3 wiki Case rating]. By the wiki's list only Buyer Beware of the four needs an interview, so the first real interview is probably there (my inference) [V3 wiki Interrogation trivia].
9. After Patrol you are promoted to detective (Traffic desk). The first Traffic case, The Driver's Seat, includes interviewing a suspect and an objective "Charge Frank Morgan or let him go" [V3 wiki The Driver's Seat rev 2026-10-07].
10. Watch the face while the answer plays: sweat, eyes, smirk; Cole's own face sometimes shows an obvious lie [V3 wiki Interrogation].
11. After each interview, the notebook updates; read it before the next question.

#### 5. What changes later
- Desks move Traffic, Homicide, Vice, Arson; Homicide gives the most two-suspect charges [V3 wiki Homicide]. Jack Kelso is played in some Arson cases.
- You can reach Rank 20 by the midpoint, after which XP stops counting and Intuition points stop regenerating, so they matter less as a crutch [V3 wiki Intuition Points]. The wiki's Ranks page instead says the pool is 5 per case and resets to 5 when you start or leave a case [CONTESTED in detail; same pool cap of 5].
- Interview trees get longer; some suspects need all three answers; a few cases need the same answer every time [V3 wiki Interrogation]. Five cases need no interview at all: Upon Reflection, Armed and Dangerous, Warrants Outstanding, The Quarter Moon Murders, A Different Kind of War [V3 wiki Interrogation].
- DLC cases are mandatory story in the Complete and Remaster editions.

#### 6. Failure and ending
- Wrong answers: the suspect clams up, a lead is lost, rating falls; near the end of a case failing the interview may fail the case [V3 wiki Interrogation]. Contaminating a crime scene (e.g. driving a car around it) can fail a case [V3 wiki Crime Scenes]. What the game shows when a case is failed, and whether you restart from a checkpoint: `?`.
- Wrong charge: lowers the star rating; the story is authored and continues [V3 Wikipedia].
- Ending: the story's last case; I found no permanent-loss state [M]. A 5-star on every story case gives an achievement ("Shamus to the Stars") [V3 wiki Case rating].

#### 7. Consequential vs cosmetic choices
- Consequential for the score: answering every question correctly (the only way to 5 stars, per the wiki "guess all questions correct"), finding all clues, damage to people and property.
- Consequential for what you see: missing clues removes Accuse options; a wrong branch can lose information.
- Dominant answer: each question has one fixed correct choice. If you know nothing, the wiki's totals (236 questions: 56 Truth, 106 Doubt, 74 Lie) make Doubt the best blind guess (45% vs 24% vs 31%) [V3 wiki Interrogation; percentages by my arithmetic from the wiki counts].
- How a hypothesis is tested: one statement at a time. You judge a single answer, and the game responds at once (the line advances or the suspect shuts down). There is no running theory board; the notebook only holds facts.
- Cosmetic: black-and-white mode; optional street crimes; label set (the relabel was meant to fix wording, not change answers; whether any keyed answer changed is `?`).

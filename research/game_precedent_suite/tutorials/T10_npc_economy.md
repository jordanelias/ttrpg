### The Sims 4 - facts line
Maxis / The Sims Studio (EA); life simulation sandbox. Base game 2014-09-02 [V2*]; now free to download, packs sold separately [V3 simscommunity.info/2023/07/04/how-to-play-sims-4-a-beginners-guide 2023-07-04].
Relied on: fan-wiki revisions 2025-12..2026-10-17, patch 132 (2022-07-26), Royalty & Legacy (21st expansion, 2026-02-12) [V3 sims.fandom.com/wiki/The_Sims_4:_Royalty_&_Legacy rev 2026-10-17].
Assumed: base game + R&L, Normal lifespan, free will on, one household played. One session = about 1-3 h real time; real minutes per Sim day: `?` (not found for Sims 4).
Mostly [V3] from the fan wiki and two beginner guides; the tutorial walk-through comes from a 2020 guide plus the wiki's list of tutorial milestones, so later tutorial changes are `?`.

#### 1. Setup choices
- **Start path**: the tutorial (a pre-made roommate household, ~20k simoleons, four tutorial aspirations, three random traits) or New Game with Create a Sim; Story Quiz can build a Sim from answers [V3 thegamer.com/sims-4-complete-beginners-starting-guide 2020-07-07; simscommunity 2023-07-04].
- **Per Sim**: appearance, gender and (since patch 132) orientation settings [V3 wiki Patch_132 rev 2025-08-15]; traits: adults up to 3, teens 2, children 1, toddlers/infants 1 from their own sets [V3 wiki Trait_(The_Sims_4) rev 2026-09-30]; aspiration (14 categories for teen+, "grow up" ones for children, none for toddlers) [V3 wiki Aspiration_(The_Sims_4) rev 2026-08-17].
- **Aspiration** gives a permanent bonus trait for its category when first chosen, can be changed at any time; finishing one adds a reward trait (stackable) [V3 same page].
- **Household**: size and funds (20,000 + 2,000 per extra Sim [V2*]); pets/other life states depend on packs owned.
- **Home and world**: buy a ready house or an empty lot with the starting budget in Willow Creek, Oasis Springs or Newcrest (empty) [V3 simscommunity 2023-07-04]; R&L adds Ondarion with three neighbourhoods and existing dynasties [V3 wiki R&L page].
- **Options that change play**: Free Will on/off (can be off for the selected Sim only) [V3 wiki Free_will rev 2026-06-07]; Wants and Fears on/off in Gameplay options [V3 wiki Patch_132]; lifespan Short/Normal/Long and who ages [V2*]; Neighborhood Stories [V2*]; start season if Seasons is installed [V3 wiki Time rev 2026-04-14].
- A fresh save starts on a Sunday at 08:00 [V3 wiki Time].

#### 2. The loop
Clock: real time with pause, three speeds plus Ultra; game time runs in every world at once; days have weekdays and bills land each Monday [V3 wiki Time; wiki Bill rev 2025-12-30]. With nobody awake on the lot the game fast-forwards [V3 wiki Time].
1. Look at the active Sim's six motives (hunger, bladder, energy, social, hygiene, fun) and the current emotion; low motives add moodlets such as Uncomfortable or Sad [V3 wiki Motive rev 2026-07-27].
2. Give orders by clicking an object or Sim and choosing from its menu; orders queue (about 8 [V2*]).
3. The game fills idle time: with Free Will on a Sim acts on needs, mood and traits when nothing is queued; the other household members and visitors act this way all the time [V3 wiki Free_will].
4. Three wants (long-term, short-term, reaction to place/needs) hang over the controlled Sim; completing them earns satisfaction points to spend in the rewards store (potions, bonus traits) [V3 wiki Wants_and_fears rev 2026-08-16].
5. Work or school: a career Sim leaves at set hours (an adult job can be a rabbit-hole, work-from-home, or an active career you direct on site) [V3 thegamer 2020-07-07].
6. Evening: socialise, practise skills, host guests; sleep ends the day.
7. Monday: pay bills by phone, computer or mailbox; unpaid for 48 h cuts power, 48 h more cuts water, then the repo man [V3 wiki Bill].
8. Birthdays advance the life stage; the stage lengths are fixed by the lifespan setting; death comes at the end of Elder or by accident.
Interrupts: fires, burglars, visitors, scandals (R&L), emotional deaths [V3 wiki The_Sims_4 line on emotions]; control stays with the player, pop-ups only ask.
Automated: need-seeking, social chatter between household members, the job commute, and (optionally) other households' careers, births and deaths [V2*].

#### 4. First hour, step by step
Source: the tutorial's milestone list in the wiki's aspiration page (rev 2026-08-17) and a 2020 guide [V3 thegamer 2020-07-07]; the tutorial was replaced in patch 73, 2018-11-13 [V3 wiki The_Sims_4 patch list].
1. Pick one of four tutorial aspirations (e.g. Trend-Setter, Painter); the game rolls three traits. 2. "Get to Know Roommate": talk to the assigned roommate (Alex).
3. Hover each need to see which object fills it; "Grab something to Eat" (fridge/oven). 4. "Interact with Fun Object" (TV, music, computer). 5. "Go to Sleep for the Night" (bed).
6. Day 2, "Prepare for Work": use the toilet and shower; "Make Coffee"; "Go to Work"; "Socialize with Alex" (any roommate counts).
7. After work, "Clean the Home", do a daily career task, improve a skill, earn a promotion. 8. "Take a Vacation Day", travel to a community venue, fish for an hour, meet new Sims.
9. Finishing gives the Over-Achiever trait (salary bonus, faster promotions and skills), the roommate moves out, and you choose your own aspiration [V3 wiki Aspiration].
10. Start a real game: Create a Sim, pick a lot, spend the budget on bed, toilet, shower, fridge and stove first, keep a reserve for bills [S3 search summary of simscommunity/godisageek guides].
11. Get a job by phone or computer; no job at start unless the Story Quiz gave one [V3 thegamer 2020-07-07]. 12. Sell paintings, writing or harvests by dragging them onto the register [V3 thegamer].
13. Meet neighbours and raise friendship, then invite them over; a Sim living alone fills Social this way [V3 thegamer].
What the game then shows: wants bubbles, a weekly bill, a career panel, and the aspiration progress panel.

#### 5. What changes later
- Life stages arrive on a schedule (Normal days: newborn 1, infant 5, toddler 7, child 14, teen 21, young adult 28, adult 42, elder 14+; Short and Long are half and 4x) [V3 wiki Lifespan rev 2026-05-31]. The table agrees with TheGamer's table, so "nearly eleven" Normal teen days in the 2023 prose is the outlier [V3 vs V2*].
- Household grows through marriage and children; more bars to tend; relationships get dynamics (friend, family, romantic) that change which autonomous interactions occur [V3 wiki Relationship rev 2026-08-09]; sentiments (up to four per pair, since patch 103) colour how a Sim feels when the other enters [V3 wiki Sentiment rev 2026-02-19].
- Fears appear after lived experience (13 base-game fears) and are overcome for points [V3 wiki Wants_and_fears].
- Packs add careers, life states (vampires, mermaids...) with their own motives [V3 wiki Motive]. R&L adds dynasties (founder becomes head, up to 9 relatives, 3 skills and 3 ideals, prestige and unity meters) and a noble ladder with one holder per rank [V3 wiki Dynasty rev 2026-09-25; Noble rev 2026-09-10].
- Pressure ramps through bills, aging deadlines and, for a dynasty, rivals and scandals; nothing scales into a larger economy.

#### 6. Failure and ending
No win or loss condition found [M, same as v2 M1]; play ends when you stop. Failure states: a Sim dies (old age, accident, extreme emotion); utilities cut and repossession for unpaid bills; a dynasty can lose prestige or have a member made an outcast [V3 wiki Dynasty]. An elder's death within days of the minimum Elder length is a clock [V2*]. Recovery: paying the bill ends the shutoff [M]; a dead Sim is not recoverable [M] (ghosts exist); a heir keeps the dynasty going [V3 wiki Dynasty "Continuing the Dynasty"].

#### 7. Consequential vs cosmetic choices
- **Traits**: they bias autonomous behaviour and which wants and fears roll [V3 wiki Free_will; Trait page line 25]. A mod page's complaint that vanilla toddler behaviour "isn't coded to traits, moods, relationships" shows the reverse case [V3 snootysims.com/wiki/sims-4/personality-please 2023-11-10].
- **Career and bills**: income against weekly bills sets how much you can build; skills and friends gate promotion [V3 wiki Career].
- **Aspiration**: low commitment (switchable anytime), but the first pick's bonus trait is permanent [V3 wiki Aspiration]; the reward store is the real payoff.
- **R&L heir and ideals**: head and heir produce most prestige; the heir designation is the one succession decision [V3 wiki Dynasty].
- **Cosmetic or near it**: Create a Sim looks, house decor, most skill choices (no counter-pressure found). I did not find a community-consensus dominant strategy for a standard sandbox; no balance data exists for a sandbox with no score `?`.

### Anno 1800 - facts line
Ubisoft Blue Byte (Anno Union community); city builder with sea trade and light naval combat; PC 2019-04-16, consoles 2023-03-16 [V2*]. Newest game update the fan wiki lists: GU 18.4, 2024-12-05; later updates `?` [V3 anno1800.fandom.com/wiki/Game_Updates rev 2024-12-07].
Assumed: Campaign Chapter 1 for the first hour, then Sandbox on Normal difficulty with two AI rivals, base game (Old World + New World). Real time with pause; one session = 2-4 h to reach Workers/Artisans [M].
All facts below are [V3] fan-wiki wikitext read via the Fandom API (revision dates given) unless tagged; the first-hour guide is two third-party guides, one German.

#### 1. Setup choices
- **Mode**: Campaign (Prologue on a tropical island, then Chapter 1 "A Tale of Two Brothers" in the Old World) or Sandbox [V3 wiki Campaign rev 2023-01-24]. Sandbox lets you pick "less or more guidance" (tutorial messages) [V3 wiki Sandbox_mode rev 2022-10-03].
- **Character**: portrait, company logo, name, colour (cosmetic) [V3 Sandbox_mode].
- **Difficulty presets**: Normal 75,000 starting coins, large world and islands, abundant minerals, high fertility, full construction refunds, free relocation; Advanced 50,000, average, half refunds, relocation costs; Expert 25,000, small, few minerals, low fertility, no refunds, no relocation [V3 same].
- **Custom**: 0-3 AI competitors (each rated easy/medium/hard and takes one small island), 0-2 pirates (Anne Harlow, Jean La Fortune), 1-4 neutral traders (Archibald Blake always) [V3 Sandbox_mode; wiki Characters rev 2023-03-01].
- **World**: seed, world size, island size and difficulty, fertility (plenty/medium/sparse), minerals, map type (random, archipelago, atoll, island arc, corners, snowflake) [V3 Sandbox_mode].
- **Features**: income level, inactive upkeep, refunds, relocation, trading-post restocks, city incidents (easy/medium/hard), quest frequency [V3 same].
- **Start and victory**: starting ships (none/flagship/trade fleet/war fleet), harbour, capital, revealed map; victory conditions Monuments, Investors, Target Population/Wealth/Revenue, Diplomatic, or none [V3 same]. DLC regions (Cape Trelawney, Arctic, Enbesa) have fixed island counts [V3 same].

#### 2. The loop
Clock: real time with pause and adjustable speed (keys for decrease/increase/pause exist [V3 wiki Keyboard_shortcuts rev 2025-08-30]); most quests give 30 minutes [V3 wiki Quests rev 2021-04-09]; money changes per minute as a "balance" [V3 wiki Coins rev 2022-07-14].
1. Place buildings (roads, houses, farms, workshops, public services); each costs coins and materials, and most carry a per-minute maintenance (marketplace 500 coins, upkeep 20) [V3 wiki Marketplace rev 2023-04-11].
2. Residents move in; the game sets house population from how many need-types are met (marketplace gives +5 residents per low-tier house in full range) [V3 Marketplace].
3. Goods flow by automatic carts between warehouses, producers and houses; island stock decides what houses get.
4. Check the balance and the finance tab: income from needs met, maintenance, ship upkeep, royal taxes [V3 Coins].
5. Upgrade houses when needs are met and the next tier's jobs/goods are ready; workforce is a pool per tier, so shortage cuts efficiency [V3 keengamer.com/articles/guides/anno-1800-four-tips-beginners-must-know 2019-12-20; V2*].
6. Expand: settle another island with a ship that carries the materials; each island needs a trading post, which costs coins, materials and influence [V3 wiki Trading_Post rev 2023-05-03].
7. Link islands with trade routes; ships run them unattended [V3 wiki Trade_routes rev 2025-06-15].
8. Handle quests, diplomacy, expeditions, rivals, pirates, incidents as they pop up.
Interrupts: quest offers, newspaper issues (cooldown 30-120 min by progress level), fires, riots, illness, pirate raids, war declarations [V3 wiki Game_progression rev 2021-04-22; V2*]. Control never leaves the player; the game pauses only if you pause.
Automated: carrying goods, consumption, tax income, passive trade, route ships, AI competitors' building.

#### 4. First hour, step by step
Sources: wiki Campaign list; PC Games guide, Chapter 1, 2023-03-16, German [V3 pcgames.de/Anno-1800-Spiel-61247/Tipps/Loesung-Guide-Kampagne-1279846]; KeenGamer 2019-12-20.
1. Prologue: collect dynamite, blow up fish shoals, trade 3 t of fish for a ticket to the Old World [V3 wiki Campaign].
2. Chapter 1: build a Marketplace and 10 Farmer houses; goal 50 farmers. Start with 4-6 houses and one marketplace, then wait for settlers [V3 pcgames; wiki Campaign].
3. Add a lumberjack, a warehouse and a sawmill for planks; clear ruined districts by clicking them [V3 pcgames].
4. Keep houses in a grid ending at the marketplace so its reach covers them [V3 pcgames; wiki Marketplace].
5. Build a Fishery (second need) and reach 100 farmers; leave room for the trading post [V3 wiki Campaign].
6. Schnapps: potato farm plus distillery; build a Pub; target 150 farmers; stockpile 4 schnapps for Edvard [V3 wiki Campaign; pcgames].
7. Work clothes: two sheep farms and a tailor; Edvard asks for 4 work clothes [V3 same].
8. Upgrade a few houses to Workers; each upgrade of 10 farmer houses turns 100 farmers into up to 200 workers, so keep farmers in surplus of jobs [V3 KeenGamer].
9. Watch the balance; maintenance of idle buildings eats coins [V3 Coins].
10. Rescue castaways, choose a side in the letter-or-Archibald choice, build a shipyard, finish the chapter [V3 pcgames].
11. Look at the minimap for the fertilities your island lacks, then sail the flagship to another island and place a trading post [V3 KeenGamer].
12. Reserve more housing space than you think; consider paved streets once Workers unlock (13 coins + a brick per tile vs 3 coins dirt) [V3 KeenGamer].
What the game shows: need bars in the marketplace menu, supply arrows on warehouses, quest pop-ups.

#### 5. What changes later
- Tiers: Farmers, Workers, Artisans, Engineers, Investors in the Old World (Tourists and Scholars with DLC); New World Jornaleros, Obreros (Artistas with DLC) [V3 wiki Population rev 2025-11-20].
- Progress levels (Early to End) unlock by pop: 35 Workers, 1 Artisan, 1 Engineer, 1 Investor, then 1000 Investors + 25000 pop; traders offer more goods and AI gets stronger warships [V3 wiki Game_progression].
- The first Artisans open the discovery expedition that unlocks the New World, whose job is to feed the Old World with materials [V3 wiki Expeditions; New_World].
- Influence grows with global population and Investors and caps how many ships, islands and optimisation buildings you hold [V3 wiki Influence].
- Royal taxes bite at 1000 residents of a tier on an island [V2*]. Rivals expand, pirates raid longer routes; DLC regions add Arctic, Cape Trelawney, Enbesa.

#### 6. Failure and ending
Loss: coins at -5000 (Archibald can gift 25,000 once) is bankruptcy; also island takeover by an AI or lost war [V3 Coins; wiki Trading_Post]. Win: Campaign chapters complete; Sandbox ends when the chosen victory conditions are met, or never if none are set [V3 Sandbox_mode]. Recovery: rioting or ruined houses are fixable by restoring needs; bankruptcy is not.

#### 7. Consequential vs cosmetic choices
- **Island choice and layout** (fertilities, space, road distance): decides what you can make and how cheaply; the strongest KeenGamer advice is to check fertilities first [V3 KeenGamer].
- **Jobs below workforce**: a plain rule from the same guide; breaking it cuts every workplace of that tier [V3 KeenGamer; V2*].
- **Trade-route structure and which AI you ally with**: the main mid-game levers; trading raises AI reputation by value traded [V3 wiki Trade rev 2025-01-24].
- **Difficulty and refund settings**: change risk of recovering from mistakes (refunds, relocation) [V3 Sandbox_mode].
- **Cosmetic**: portrait, logo, colour, most decoration; Attractiveness is the exception for palace/tourism content [V3 wiki Palace]. No poll of the community was found, so "dominant" answers rest on KeenGamer and PC Games only.

### Patrician IV - facts line
Gaming Minds Studios / Kalypso Media; trading and strategy sim in the Hanseatic League. PC release 2010-09-17 on Steam (Steam Special Edition adds alternate Counting House and Cog textures); Rise of a Dynasty expansion 2011-04-11 [V3 store.steampowered.com/api/appdetails?appids=57620 and 57730, read 2026-10-22]. Patch level used by the sources: `?`.
Assumed: Campaign start (home Lubeck, 20 cities, Normal difficulty), base game; Rise of a Dynasty features noted where they change play. Real time with variable speed; a first productive session is 2-3 h `?` (no source gives it).
Thin on first-party text: no manual reachable; the tutorial is the in-game "rich uncle" advice, known from a 2010 review. Most mechanics come from one long Steam guide (posted 2016-05-13, updated 2023-07-01, comments to 2025-11) plus the fan wiki (revisions 2021).

#### 1. Setup choices
- **Play option**: Campaign (preset: Lubeck, 20 cities, normal difficulty, historic production, 2,000 average starting population), Free Play (all adjustable), Scenarios (short goal games; harder ones unlock), Co-op (up to four, with the expansion) [V3 steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=682751098 guide; store 57730].
- **Home city**: 12 of the 40 cities can be chosen; 20 or 26 cities are present at start; 6 more can be founded through Alderman missions and 8 reached by opening land routes through a region's prince [V3 patrician4.fandom.com/wiki/Cities rev 2021-01-11].
- **Difficulty** (Normal / Advanced / Professional): changes the profit band of trade (wood one-diamond band 40-59 Normal, 40-52 Advanced, 36-46 Professional) and the AI; harder levels freeze some harbours in winter [V3 guide table and Play Options].
- **Production**: historic (each city makes its real goods) or random [V3 guide].
- **Pirate activity** (Options): the guide advises Low; commenters play on High [V3 guide].
- **Always the same start**: you begin at rank Grocer with one ship and one Counting House already built in the home city [V3 wiki Trader_Rank rev 2021-01-13; wiki Counting_house rev 2021-01-11]. Starting money: `?`.

#### 2. The loop
Clock: days with a running date; speed is adjustable, 0 pauses, and the guide advises holding space to advance only when you decide; combat cannot be paused except by ESC [V3 guide]. Production, consumption and costs are per day, and several reports use 10-day windows [V3 guide].
1. Select a convoy docked in a city's harbour; open the Market Hall; the market shows, per good, a four-diamond supply bar (red short, green surplus), quantity, price and your average cost [V3 guide].
2. Buy where the good is made and cheap, move the cargo on the world map by right-clicking the destination; a trip takes days, and random finds (lost cargo, survivors, treasure map pieces, pirate bases) appear near convoys [V3 guide].
3. Sell where it is short; every sale or purchase moves that city's stock and price, and a large sale equals many small ones [V3 guide].
4. Meanwhile the Counting House administrator trades at your set price and quantity rules, your businesses deliver goods to it, houses pay rent, wages and costs are paid [V3 guide; wiki Counting_house].
5. Check balance (10- and 30-day) and goods screens; shortages in the 10-day list stop production [V3 guide].
6. As ships multiply, set auto-trade routes of up to 18 stops with per-stop rules ("automatically trade" or "trade according to specific") [V3 guide].
7. Spend wealth on guild standing, buildings, ships and political office as ranks unlock.
Interrupts: pirates attack convoys; you choose manual fight, automatic, or flee; manual combat is real time with three ships a side [V3 guide]. Also famine, fire, disease, winter, ruler troops [V3 guide; store 57620]. Automated: the administrator, auto-trade captains, city consumption, AI traders.

#### 4. First hour, step by step
Source: the uncle advice [V3 gamingnexus.com/Article/2819 2010-11-15]; the guide [V3]; one commenter's remarks on the campaign [V3 guide comment 2024-10-20].
1. Choose Campaign; the rich uncle's pop-ups start with buying, selling and intercity trade. 2. Click your docked ship; open the Market Hall in Lubeck.
3. Read the diamonds and the "gear" mark for what a city produces; buy produced goods, sell to cities that lack them [V3 guide].
4. Drag the quantity bar to transfer; buying and selling are the transfer between city and your ship, and moves between ship, storage and wagon are free [V3 guide].
5. Right-click a neighbouring city on the world map and sail. 6. Sell at the shortage price; note the average price you paid. 7. Repeat on a loop; the review says money "starts to flow in after a few trips" [V3 gamingnexus].
8. Use the Counting House: hire an administrator and set one pair of rules; the game compares with strict < and >, so add or subtract one coin; a bad pair (buy at 50, sell at 40) burns cash [V3 guide].
9. Do not run into debt; with no cash auto-trade cannot buy [V3 guide]. 10. Build the second Counting House when the campaign asks (Aalborg is the campaign's mission; a commenter says other cities also work) [V3 guide comment].
11. Reach Trader at 80,000 wealth and a Counting House for Town Hall missions [V3 wiki Trader_Rank]. 12. Add ships and a captain, set the first auto-trade route [V3 gamingnexus; guide].
13. Join the Guild at Wholesaler to build houses and production; production costs below the market price [V3 guide table: wood costs 29 to make against a 40-59 band].
14. Save to two alternating files before risks [V3 guide].
What the game then shows: Balance screen, Goods screen, quest offers in taverns, pirate sightings.

#### 1. Setup choices
- **Campaign**: six Story campaigns (Age of Ulstryx 3 chapters vs Gorgons; All the Bones of Summer 5 vs Drauven; Eluna and the Moth 5 vs Thrixl; The Enduring War 5 vs Morthagi; Monarchs Under the Mountain 5 vs Deepists; The Sunswallower's Wake 3) or a Generic Campaign of randomised chapters (pick 3 or 5 chapters, optionally start with Legacy heroes) [V3 wiki Campaign rev 2024-03-16].
- **Two difficulties**, each Storyteller / Adventurer / Tragic Hero / Walking Lunch: combat difficulty (hero and monster health, armour, damage; start health 7 / 6 / 5 / 5, open-door chance 0 / 25 / 50 / 50 %) and overland difficulty (calamity timer 220-240 / 160-180 / 120-140 / 100-120, cards per timed draw 2 / 4 / 5 / 6, incursion pace and size, chapter time limits) [V3 wiki Difficulty rev 2023-04-18].
- **Heroes**: the starting party is three generated farmers, each with an upbringing (2-3 hooks and passive bonuses); the character sheet can be rerolled until you like it [V3 Steam guide 1926567284 rev 2023-01-10]. In Age of Ulstryx the class is not chosen: each farmer becomes warrior, hunter or mystic through an event [V3 wiki Hero rev 2025-05-21; wiki Age_of_Ulstryx rev 2025-07-12]; for generic campaigns `?`.
- **Relationships** between the first heroes (friends, lovers, rivals...) are picked in the opening events; **company name** at the end of the intro [V3 wiki Age_of_Ulstryx].
- **Legacy**: heroes finished in earlier campaigns can return as younger versions for extra Legacy Points [V3 wiki Hero]. Online play shares heroes between players [V3 wiki Campaign].

#### 2. The loop
Two scales. Overland: time passes in days while heroes travel, labour and heal (jobs are measured in hero-days; the wiki's example hero with recovery 25 heals 1 health per 4 days while resting) [V3 wiki Job rev 2023-01-22; Hero]. Tactical: a grid fight in turns where each hero spends action points to move and act [V3 wiki Stat rev 2024-06-01; Mission rev 2022-03-06]. Chapters (goals) are bridged by intervals of years [V3 wiki Chapter rev 2025-06-07].
1. A chapter opens with an Omen; new territory and a new town appear [V3 Chapter].
2. On the overland map you send heroes to jobs: Scout, Patrol, Assault, Secure, Build a station, Prepare Defense, Bridge, Pass, Recruit [V3 Job].
3. While they work, the game draws events matched to their personalities, hooks and the tile; you choose among comic-panel options [V3 wiki Event rev 2025-05-03; Steam guide].
4. At timed intervals a calamity card is drawn and an incursion horde marches on your sites; Legacy Points can cancel the card [V3 wiki Calamity rev 2022-02-20; Difficulty].
5. Fights (assault, defense, ambush, intercept, event): foes are drawn from the faction's calamity-track cards; hit chance is attacker accuracy minus the target's block, dodge and cover [V3 wiki Mission; Combat_mechanics rev 2023-06-28].
6. After a fight: XP (5 per monster card), loot, a calamity card (win or lose), +2 LP for a successful assault, defense or interception [V3 wiki Hero; Legacy_Point rev 2025-03-22].
7. Goals done: the interval pays resources, shows tidings, retires old heroes, lets a child join, lets you craft gear, removes some calamity cards [V3 Chapter].
Interrupts: events; ambush of under three heroes in an infested tile; incursions; Mortal Choice at 0 health. Automated: relationship changes, calamity draws, incursion movement, healing, what event fires [V3 wiki Relationship; Event].

#### 5. What changes later
- More chapters: bigger incursions (Adventurer: 3 sites in chapter 1 up to 6 by chapter 5), and a new monster per calamity threshold (Gorgons: Roe, Raccoon, Rager at 0; Bogmoor at 2; Gheist 4; True Gorgon 6; Bartoth 8) [V3 wiki Difficulty; Calamity_track rev 2022-03-05].

Per fight: a hero at 0 health gets a Mortal Choice; a second fall in a chapter is certain death ("Final Moment") [V3 Mortal_Choice]. Per campaign: no game-over screen documented in the pages read; defeat costs LP and calamity cards `?`. Win: finish the final chapter; heroes join the Legacy, with leftover LP converted to promotions [V3 Legacy_Point; Main Page]. Recovery: yes within a campaign; a maimed hero heals at a town and returns.

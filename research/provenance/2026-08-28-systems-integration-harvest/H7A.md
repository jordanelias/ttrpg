## Coverage notes

All 17 FULL-READ files were read completely, cover to cover, no sampling. `scale_transitions_v30.md` confirmed both baseline-flagged defects on disk (empty §3.3, literal "GM adjudication"/"GM recognises" text) exactly as the contract predicted, and I found a third empty section (§8) plus, via the companion infill file, that §3.3/§8 actually have unmerged prose content sitting in a grandfathered infill file — which downgrades those two from "no rule specified" to "rule specified but stale and unmerged," a real correction worth flagging (H7A-180, `conflicts_with` the earlier gap IDs).

The single most valuable non-full-read file was `_architecture_flow_skeleton_v1.md` — a code-verified, path:line-anchored trace that let me confirm with hard evidence (not design-doc assertion) that only 1 of the corpus's celebrated "eight handoffs" is production-reachable, that the Thread Domain Echo leg is dead code, and that `npc_ai.py` is a fully orphaned module bypassed by live dispatch. `engine_atlas_v1.md` supplied the aggregate-level confirmations (Church Tribunal implemented twice, LPS pipeline absent from accounting, a four-subsystem contract-layer cycle). These two files outrank most design prose in this lane for reliability per §0.05.

Three genuine territory/scale-count collisions surfaced independently across files that never cross-reference each other: `conflict_architecture_proposal.md`'s Peninsula/Province/Settlement, `session_a_spec_patches.md`'s PP-675 three-scale model, and the eventually-ruled five-tier Settlement→Territory→Province→Duchy→Country (`scale_hierarchy_v1.md`, cited via `ners_vsg_reconciliation_v1.md`) — none of the three documents that predate the ruling name the others. Similarly, "Mandate" resolves to at least three different formulas across the corpus (§1.8's retired `round(7T/(T+6))`, §1.5's `Σ settlement L/PS`, and an unrelated CI-driven parliamentary meter), and territory-value ranges appear in at least three incompatible scales (0-4 balanced-at-2, 0-5 settlement, 0-7 Legitimacy/PS).

A large fraction of this lane's non-priority files (`hybrid_gaps_v30`, `campaign_modes_v30`, their infills, `cogload_moderate_target`, `companion_app_design_note`) turned out to be pre-videogame, GM-dependent tabletop design explicitly superseded by `videogame_mode_spec.md`'s no-GM ruling — I gave each a single dense superseded record rather than exploding their content, per the anti-duplication instinct the contract asks for even though they aren't technically baseline documents.

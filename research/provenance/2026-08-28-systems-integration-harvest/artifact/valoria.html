That is the same size as the entire unbuilt proposal corpus, and roughly half the size of everything that actually runs. Valoria is not underdesigned — 30% of the corpus is ratified canon — and almost none of the inert code is defective. succeed_governor sweeps expired ledger tags and lets durable ones survive a handover, which is the subtle thing done right. §5.1 · F2 – F10What the comparison found

F2

The absent person is the highest-fan-out blocker in the tree

Six of ten systems name personnel-roster as their blocking dependency, and it is empty. In every seeded campaign the population of Valoria is zero. Settlements need a governor to appoint, parliament needs seat-holders for 74 authored ladder rungs, faction succession is unreachable by construction because Faction has no leader field, and derive_command — whose flag already defaults on — silently falls back to a hardcoded 4 because nothing supplies a commander's attributes.

The circularity is exact: nothing appoints a governor because there is nobody to appoint, and nobody is generated because no mechanic needs one. Meanwhile generate_npc is a complete two-tier generator with no call site, and 46 authored characters sit in a registry with zero runtime loaders. Deleting that file would change nothing the engine does.

F3

The primitive four systems are each asking for is already built, and inert

Faction has no grudge — parliamentary_action.py says so in its own comment. Parliament has no motion history. NPC.persistent_state is declared, serialised and never assigned. Across fifteen traced flow skeletons, Key-typed inputs were 7 of 165.

And ledger.py ships five tag families with dedupe, TTL sweep and succession survival — and zero production writers. A faction grudge and a settlement Grudge tag are the same primitive at two scales.

The enum is closed at five, and the fifth is Leverage. A recurring term-limited claim — a Compact — is a Debt subtype, ruled July 2026. Four fiscal proposals were adjudicated before that ruling reached the prose and are unjudged against the Debt form, one of them rated "ratify as-is".

F4

Every homeostatic loop is open at one end, or has no damper

Faction.standing is a bare int mutated at eleven sites outside the clamping method, feeding two pools whose outcomes write it back. Settlement pressure Π has a restoring term that saturates at ±1 while six ambition clocks supply up to +3.0/season — the ceiling is pinned arithmetically. Wealth has four write sites in the whole engine, all costs, no income anywhere. Turmoil, IP, PI and Strain are set at world-gen and never written again: four pressure gauges, all painted on. InsurgencyRecord.L is assigned 1.0 once and promotion needs 3 — the world's only mechanism for producing new political agents is a closed loop with the exit welded shut.

F5

Seven live name collisions, each a real defect the moment both halves are built

Standing means three things. Disposition means three. Officer is a unit commander and a political rank. Territory in code is what the ruling calls a Province. Mandate is both a directly-written stat and a derived aggregate of a field that does not exist. And five scale vocabularies are live, with only "Personal" appearing in all of them.

The scale row is not a naming cleanup: two different concepts — what size of thing an event is about, and what administrative tier owns it — have been forced into one field. Each of the five rosters was right about a different question. The consequence for sequencing is that the naming pass is a precondition of the person primitive, not a tidy-up after it.

F6

Three rival action economies, and the only live budget belongs to none of them

Faction scale carries a single weighted RNG draw (the one that runs), a typed card-hand-with-cooldowns economy, and a six-tier AI posture stack — none superseding another, none acknowledging the others. Settlement scale carries two, and both were already ruled; the rulings are unexecuted, so the fork keeps re-presenting itself to anyone reading the documents rather than the rulings.

Every proposed action economy wants a budget. One exists — AP = 2 + facility_tier. A repo-wide grep for its accessor returns no matches at all.

F7

The proposal corpus is roughly four ideas with ~150 instantiations

About 117 of 471 catalogued gaps are measurably duplicates in disguise. Twenty-one deeply-researched fiscal mechanics — iltizam, the Ferme Générale, John Law, the Salt Certificate — all want the same two things: a Wealth quantity that flows, and a durable claim tag. Both are single primitives named elsewhere in this list. Nine parallel bounded personal meters differ only in their trigger lists.

F8

The one working cross-scale crossing works because it needs no person

Eight scale handoffs are specified; one pair is production-reachable, through a dispatch dict whose two entries both point at it. Eight mandatory zoom-in triggers are specified; one is evaluable. It fires an emergency council whose two sides are derived from the same faction's own aggregates — one scoring round(L), the other round(7 − Sta). The single working personal↔strategic crossing in the game is a faction arguing with itself, and it works precisely because it needs nobody in the room.

F9

What is good, and must survive every proposal

The Key substrate — typed, append-only, with deferred-apply that preserves same-tick causal chaining. The resolution kernel, which closed its TN question by making non-conformance impossible rather than merely discouraged. ledger.py. derive_parties returning None on a derivation gap rather than fabricating an actor. The mass-battle engine. A cut list is only credible next to a defend list.

F10

The binding constraint is not cost. It is attribution.

Eight changes are individually cheap — call it 250 lines. Land them in one commit and you get eight simultaneous golden movements and no way to attribute any of them. A number without a control is not a measurement, so that is not a fast path; it is an unmeasurable one.

The real constraint on integration is ordering. Determinism-neutral changes first, state-writing changes in increasing order of blast radius, each with a stated control. That ordering separates the four proposals below more than their content does.

§6 · four rival proposalsEach attacked against NERS, scope gate first

NERS applies to systems that resolve by rolling. A ledger, a budget or a loader does not roll, and manufacturing a verdict for one is the exact error the methodology warns against — so where a proposal is out of scope, that is said plainly and no verdict is invented. Two attacks broke their own proposals rather than blessing them, and the repairs are the most useful output here.

Proposal 1

Close the Circuits

Add nothing. Cut nothing. Connect what exists, one variable at a time.

Eleven steps, ordered by blast radius, each a single-variable experiment with a stated control. Determinism-neutral changes first — the RNG substream and re-pointing the population guards at world.npcs rather than a counter they cannot see through. Then the writers, with the Muster 100× scaling fix deliberately landing after seasonal income so the correction is visible rather than fatal.

Its weakness is stated rather than hidden: it makes the existing game work. It does not make it good.

NERS · Passes

Nine of eleven steps touch no resolution — out of scope, no verdict offered. Two are in scope and pass; Wealth saturates at the registry ceiling, so income is negative feedback on the leader. Conditional on one addition: a grudge counter with no decay is an unbounded ramp that feeds the selection which generates more grudge. Decay is a hard precondition, not a nicety.

Proposal 2

Three Primitives

Person, ledger, budget — build them once, merge everything that duplicates them.

Six representations of a person collapse into one entity with a composable roles set. Six bespoke memory stores collapse into tags on a ledger widened from settlement-scoped to any-entity-scoped. Nine parallel personal meters distil to two — one public, one private. The weighted draw and the card-hand economy are both cut in favour of a generalised action budget.

NERS · Fails → repaired

A flat AP currency spanning both canonical engines breaks P-ii. One point returns a flat 0.10 in the Domain Action Resolver; an added die in the sigma engine returns ≈0.204σ at Pool 5 and ≈0.115σ at Pool 18 — about 8.1 versus 4.6 percentage points at the mode, and obstacle-dependent on top. The same AP is worth ~1.8× more on a small pool, so a player routes it wherever it pays. This is the flat-shift trap one level up. Repair: AP buys actions, never modifiers — which also takes the budget back out of the resolution arithmetic entirely.

Proposal 3

The Disposal

Delete, as-if-built, what would not survive contact with a player.

Six dead modules and the dead surfaces of two more; the six faction stubs that are why two of four factions are string-comparison-identical in play; a 74-rung ladder across twelve ladders distilled to one track plus a power-base type; the non-compiling Godot skeleton; and a canon table of four unique faction actions the code implements none of — which makes it retracted, not unimplemented.

NERS · out of scope, except one

Deleting inert modules changes no resolution path, so no verdict is offered. The exception: cutting the four upper Sanction tiers fails P-iv — it leaves parliament with exactly one severity setting. The repair is better than either option: keep the parameterisation, drop the tiers, and let the proposer buy a magnitude. A continuous dial where the design had five discrete rungs.

Proposal 4

The Season Is a Person's Season

Stop iterating factions. Iterate people. A faction acts because someone in it acts.

The most radical, and the one that answers the question every within-system analysis ends on. It forces the person primitive, gives parliament motions an author, gives battles a commander, makes six of seven Sufficient Scope conditions reachable, and turns Key.causes[] from a provenance chain into a biography — which is what the substrate was built for and has never once been used as.

NERS · Fails → repaired

A roster-aggregated pool is a monotone ramp to certainty, violating P-iii. μ grows linearly with roster size while σ grows as a square root, so z grows as √Pool with no ceiling: four people at score 3 reach 84% against Ob 2; twelve reach 99.5%. Nothing caps a roster. Repair: the acting person's own score is the pool; the roster sets how many actions, not how big each one is. A 1–7 faculty maps to pools of 8–14 — squarely inside the band the engine is calibrated for.

§6.5 · recommendationThe order the attacks produced

They are not mutually exclusive, and the NERS attacks shaped the sequence more than the content did.

  1. Run the disposal first. Close to free, and it removes the standing hazard of a future session building toward a ## Status: BUILT header with no code under it. Two holds: the Sanction item the attack converted from CUT to REFINE, and the fact that no verdict has been steelmanned — so run the deletions as rehearsals with an independent KEEP argument attached, not as a sweep.
  2. Then the circuits, in order, with the accord echo deferred behind its two rulings. Ten execution artifacts is ten more than the seven milestone junctures currently have — and under the repo's own standard, that, not this document, is what would move the milestone.
  3. Then the primitives, once the naming pass is done. A roles field cannot be typed while officer, Standing and Disposition each mean two or three things.
  4. The person's season whenever the schema exists — it is not sequenced behind the primitives. Its failure is repaired by any bounded action counter and it can define its own; what the two proposals share is the shape of that counter, which argues for building it once, not for ordering them.

The most valuable single item is not the one it first appears to be. The obvious candidate is the accord echo — a finished, tested, correct, Key-driven state-write loop that has never fired. The adversarial pass showed it is not cheap: it is dormant on two declarations, and the second, a faction→settlement targeting rule, is a ruling the code is deliberately refusing to guess. Worth doing, worth doing properly, not worth mistaking for a one-line write.

The best cheap item is giving InsurgencyRecord.L a writer. One accrual rule, in a pipeline that already forms insurgencies from real contiguity data and already checks every open record for promotion each season. It is the only cheap change here that adds an agent to the world — neglected ground starts producing rebellions that grow into factions that can invade the power whose neglect made them.

And the one that needs measuring rather than ruling. Parliament's permanent Mandate penalty is widely assumed to be the strategic layer's anti-runaway damper. It is an uncontrolled asymmetry whose sign depends on which branch fires: on one branch it docks the leader, on the other the weakest faction. That is a campaign-level balance claim with no control, and the balance oracle can settle it in about thirteen minutes. Run it before anyone rules on it, and before anyone quietly patches the comment.

§7 · the critiqueValoria judged against the precedent for its own systems

Not a feature list. For each system: what the genre treats as table stakes for a system of this kind, where Valoria sits relative to that floor — and, in two cases, where it is ahead of the precedent and does not know it.

BELOW THE FLOOR no surveyed title ships this system in this state AT THE FRONTIER where the genre is, on a problem the genre has not solved AHEAD, UNAWARE already built, not running

Faction strategybelow the floor

Table stakes: a faction has an interior that can disagree with itself. Victoria 3 has interest groups holding clout. CK3 has negotiable vassal contracts and crown-authority levels gating what the liege may even attempt. Old World emits goals from a ruler crossed with the influential families. Kremlin holds influence over politicians rather than owning them.

Valoria: one rng.random() draw per season, two of four factions with any branch at all, and personality implemented as a string comparison. No surveyed title models a faction as an undifferentiated scalar bundle, because a thing with no interior cannot have politics inside it — so most of the genre's faction-scale vocabulary is not merely unbuilt here, it is untypeable.

Parliamentbelow the floor

Table stakes: procedure is the game; the vote is the formality. Agenda control, speaking order, the chair's choice of which motion to put, the veto and its record, and who drafts the response.

Valoria has the arithmetic of a vote and none of the procedure — no chamber, no seats, no agenda, no subject, no cost, no scarcity. And it fails in the exact mirror of EU4: EU4's estates never fire; Valoria's motion fires unconditionally every season for free. Same defect — a firing rate decoupled from anything the player does — at zero and at one.

Settlement governanceahead, unaware

There are no table stakes, and that is the finding. Total War added, removed and re-added the governor role three times in twenty years. "There is no convergent answer — this is a real, unsettled design tension, not a solved problem you are behind on."

But one component is agreed, and Valoria already solved it. Dwarf Fortress's documented failure is that demotion leaves no residual — a comeback that resets to zero is a reset button. succeed_governor calls ledger_sweep, and durable tags survive the handover. That is exactly the residual DF lacks, built correctly, with zero callers. The critique is not the design: a finished-and-parked system is indistinguishable from an unbuilt one from the player's seat, and the genre has no example of one shipping that way.

Territory & conquestbelow the floor

Table stakes: conquest is a negotiation; a decree is a compliance roll. Venice's dedizione left subject cities their statutes and councils in exchange for loyalty — what you leave standing determines what governing costs for the rest of the game.

Valoria commits the survey's named "single most common error in governance games" — the decree that just works — one level up, at conquest. And Turmoil, IP, PI and Strain are set at world-gen and never written: four gauges painted on, one of which is the third clause of the sole victory condition, so the win condition is materially one clause shorter than it reads.

Peoplebelow the floor, inside-out

The quantitative version is that the population is zero. The structural version is sharper. Every surveyed personnel system is primarily a relationship ledger with a roster attached — JA2's pairwise matrix, CK's opinion web, RoTK's loyalty. The roster is the index; the relationships are the mechanism.

Valoria authored identity on 46/46, capability on 1/46 — and that one records its social value as the string "3–4" — and relationships on 0/46. It has the half that is content and none of the half that is mechanism, which is why the loader keeps reading as easy and keeps not being. There is nothing to load the content into.

Economybelow the floor

Four Wealth write sites in the whole engine, all costs, no income. No surveyed title has a resource with no source, so the survey supplies no failure mode — there is nothing to compare it to. And the 4/4 levy-versus-professional finding indicts Muster specifically: its own grounding is Wallenstein, a contractor paid regardless, which is the professional model. The levy half is absent and unnamed.

Mass battleengine aheadseam below

The engine expresses troop types, equipment, formations, per-cell morale, Lanchester signatures and encirclement. The seam above it builds both armies with identical shape, tier, position, facing, command, discipline and morale — only one integer differs. Every strategic battle is geometrically symmetric before it starts; no surveyed title does that.

terrain is a declared parameter that appears nowhere in the function body. derive_command exists, is clamped, and its flag defaults on — and the adapter never sets either attribute, so the campaign path falls back to a hardcoded 4.

Cross-scaleat the frontier

Nobody has solved this — no precedent makes a personal actor's contribution leverage-in-band from N=1 to N=1000+. So Valoria is at the frontier, not behind it. That is the uncomfortable part.

Valoria's own doctrine defines its differentiating clause against "Mount & Blade's faction politics vs character combat" as the paradigm of mechanically isolated layers. And Valoria currently sits where M&B sits: one reachable handoff, one evaluable trigger, a default-off bridge with no producer — and both leverage failure poles live in the same sixteen lines, one flat regardless of battle size, one decaying as 1/√N. Being unreachable is why nobody has had to decide; when a producer lands, those two become the semantics by default.

Valoria has the resolution chassis and has not authored the manoeuvre set — so it stands at the point before the documented failure, knowing what causes it. The pattern. Six systems sit below a floor the genre established decades ago — and in four of those six the missing piece is a caller rather than a mechanic. Two are at or past the frontier, and both are throttled by a seam below them.

Nothing here is a case that Valoria is over-ambitious. It is a case that the ambitious parts are built and the ordinary parts are not.

The four moves

U-1 · cost: DOC

The Disclosure Contract

Publish every input. Show a band, not a number. Never publish the trigger point. One rule, owned once, inherited by every surface that will ever show the player a reason.

Valoria has no GM — nobody narrates why a governor defected. The only direct evidence about that constraint is JA2, where the same game's social layer was loved and its tactical math resented, separated by nothing but whether the model was visible, and whose community fix exposed the models rather than changing them.

DisplacesThe unstated default — which in a no-GM engine means the player is told nothing. Free, and a precondition for every later import being felt rather than merely computed.

U-2 · cost: MOVES + one field

One accrual, one ledger, one budget

A place fills up; a ledger remembers; a budget spends. Every Tier-1 and Tier-2 import lands on one of the three — and the ledger alone receives six independent borrowings.

This is the integration master's Proposal 2 reached from precedent instead of from code, and it supplies the accrual primitive P2 never names — the one that would finally give facility_tier a writer.

DisplacesSix bespoke memory stores, three rival accrual clocks, and the weighted draw as top-level driver. The budget buys actions, never modifiers — otherwise one currency spanning two engines is worth ~1.8× more on a small pool.

U-3 · cost: RULING, then MOVES

The person as a relationship ledger with a roster attached

Invert how it has been built. Define the person object by its edges first — who owes whom, who was passed over by whom, who is whose client — because that is what every surveyed system actually is.

Unblocks five imports at once: Old World's emitted ambitions, Kremlin's mortality clock and custodian split, the three-band demoted state, and the idleness convergence.

Displacesif faction.name == 'Crown' as faction personality. Two preconditions, both non-negotiable: the naming pass, and the NPE's own RNG substream.

U-4 · cost: RULING — and a genuine fork

Couple the scales, or stop claiming the differentiator

Valoria's doctrine defines its differentiating clause against Mount & Blade's isolated layers. Part 7 finds Valoria sitting in the same place. There are two defensible options, and Valoria is taking a third that has no precedent behind it.

Either ship an explicit, imperfect crossing — every surveyed game does one or the other — or say the layers are adjacent rather than coupled, and revise the doctrine that claims otherwise.

DisplacesA flag whose off-state is indistinguishable from the seam not existing. ⚠ The guard belongs before the producer: a personal→unit effect must be a fraction of the unit's own size, never a flat amount.

methodHow this was built, and how it was checked

Producing and checking are different jobs, so different agents did them. Eleven parallel harvest lanes read the corpus and emitted 1,079 structured records; an adversarial gate attacked the harvest; three synthesis groups flattened and analysed; then a second independent critic — read-only, with no write tools, so its independence is structural rather than declared — attacked the finished comparison and proposals against the working tree. Corrections are edits to the document, not appendices to it.

Two instrument rules came out of it, and they generalise past this document:

i

Reachability here is not an import graph

Subsystem dependencies in this repository are declared in references/ and resolved by string at first call, so a module with zero textual importers can still be load-bearing — two are reached only through the composition registry, during save-restore. Any claim that something is dead has to be checked against module_contracts.yaml, not only by grep.

ii

Agreement is evidence only between independent sources

Prose descended from a common ancestor agrees with itself whatever the ancestor said, so counting the documents that carry a claim measures propagation rather than truth. A repeated claim gets resolved against the code and the register instead. That decided several verdicts here — among them which ledger tag families exist, and how a term-limited fiscal claim is modelled.

Claims that were attacked and held: the ledger's tag families, the budget's zero readers, the four Wealth cost sites, the four unwritten clocks, the emergency council's self-derived parties, and the monotone-ramp arithmetic — recomputed, including a check that dropping the continuity correction was right, since applying one would have strengthened the finding.

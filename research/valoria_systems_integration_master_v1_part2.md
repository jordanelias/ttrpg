### FLATTEN — "People"

`personnel-roster` and `npc-social` are flattened as one system, because the corpus's live vocabulary collision runs *between* them, not inside either. The split that matters: **npc-social owns who a person is** (convictions, disposition, relational edges, resonant style, memory) and is heavily authored and almost entirely unbuilt; **personnel-roster owns what a person holds** (rank, office, power base, climb) and is almost entirely *unauthored as canon* — it lives in research and proposals. A person object that satisfied one would not satisfy the other. Rows are marked `[id]` / `[office]` where the split is load-bearing.

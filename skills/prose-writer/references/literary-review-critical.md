# Literary Review — Critical Risks and Cross-Author Combinations

Audit-time reference. Per author: the deployment risks the critical tradition exposes, each with the check the audit must run. Then the cross-author combinations the tradition supports, those the synthesis invented, and those it warns against. Extractable techniques: `literary-review-technique.md`. Weights: `coherence-tiers.md`. Critic attributions here are unverified: an audit found roughly 20–40% of the original bibliography entries carried citation errors, and one citation (Menon on Tartt) was fabricated — verify before quoting a critic.

## Recruit the technique, never the tradition

This is the standing caution for every author (former anti-patterns #47 and #62). Each technique below was developed inside a specific position, politics or lineage. The synthesis takes the structural device and builds Valoria's own content; it never imports the source's cultural references, and it never claims the depth of a tradition the worldbuilding does not supply. The flattenings the synthesis accepts on these terms:

- **Tolkien** — Old English roots (Fimi: not politically neutral). Keep the principle (domain vocabulary, untranslated specificity) and substitute Valoria's own lexicon.
- **Lispector** — the *écriture féminine* frame (Cixous) and the class-and-gender content of G.H.'s encounter in the maid's room (Helena, Peixoto), and the postdictatorial politics of authorial intrusion (Avelar). Extract the veer as a sentence-level move, not a politics. Do not use Lispector for class-marked practitioner–servant encounters without that specificity being present.
- **Borges** — the Argentine-periphery position (Sarlo) and the Kabbalistic structure of hidden orders (Aizenberg, Alazraki). The Valoria practitioner-position (Warden, Restoration scholar, Heterodox academic) must be developed enough to be the periphery the move is made from; hidden orders carry only the depth the worldbuilding gives them. The colonial-archive caution (Márquez, below) applies to Borges too.
- **Ishiguro** — Walkowitz's post-national, world-literature frame.
- **Márquez** — the colonial-archive register (González Echevarría) and the foundational-romance lineage (Sommer). The prose cannot carry postcolonial weight.
- **Mistry** — the Parsi-Indian-Canadian standing (Bharucha), the Shahrazadic storytelling lineage (Malak), the politics of caste and capital (Erritouni). No Parsi liturgical detail, no Bombay-tenement vocabulary. Mistry's discipline (refuse to summarize, refuse to interpret) came from his position; the synthesis must enforce it structurally because it cannot inherit it positionally, and develop the Valoria practitioner-positions enough to supply the position.
- **Ocampo** — the Argentine-fantastic lineage (Negroni).
- **Tartt** — thin critical apparatus: the calibration anchors, not consolidated criticism, specify which Tartt is extracted.

## I. Tolkien

**Landscape-as-agent without the cosmology.** Flieger's Barfieldian reading licenses landscape-as-agent but roots it in a metaphysics of language Valoria need not share. Deploy the agency grammatically (mountains glow, valleys fall) without committing to Barfield's framework.

**Parataxis-as-default (V.1).** Turner's marked-vs-default distinction is fragile under sustained generation: LLM prose drifts toward paratactic chaining as baseline rhythm. Check parataxis frequency against marked-moment frequency.

## II. Lispector

**Coherence failure as illumination (III.3).** Cixous's reading licenses pre-rational access as a positive perceptual mode; coherence failure is *not* one unless TS is also present. Borrow Cixous's mechanism, refuse her elevation of it.

## III. Borges

**Thin contexts.** Deployed without a developed practitioner-position, Borges-mode is decoration rather than position.

**The Balderston requirement.** References must be plausible inside the Valoria world — actual or defensibly possible referents. Apocryphal references that are merely apocryphal are fantasy decoration. The defences are I.7 and a Codex apparatus rich enough to support the references; an unworked worldbuilding cannot support Borges-mode reliably.

**Compressed paradox vs too-clever construction (V.6).** Does the paradox serve the limitation it displays (mental limitation through formal consistency), or is it the writer's flourish?

## IV. Ishiguro

[ASSUMPTION: "quantity-violation" / "pragmatic-maxim violation" is a synthesis-internal framing of Walkowitz's covert-unreliability argument — verify before attributing the Gricean terms to her.]

**Restraint without unreliability (V.2).** Test: is the narrator reading something wrongly that the reader can detect? If yes, Ishiguro. If no, formal prose — possibly competent, not Ishiguro.

**Class-position of hedging.** Hedging was developed for narrators whose social position required composure. Check that the focalization licenses it; a Heterodox practitioner with no professional need for composure who hedges is probably producing an AI tic.

**Contradiction without sharpness (V.2).** The reader must have the contradicting evidence: stage it in the same passage as the misidentification, in plain prose, without commentary. Absent evidence turns the technique into plausible truth.

## V. Márquez

**Genre instead of technique.** "Magical realism" became a marketing category that flattened distinct practices. Draw the technique narrowly — matter-of-fact impossibility, hyperbolic specificity, time-fold — never the genre.

**Content impossibility vs rendering failure (II.3).** Both produce ordinary syntax for content not marked as wonder. Check focalization: from a chronicler-position the impossibility is content; from a low-coherence PC it is rendering. Conflating them produces world-surrealism.

**Deviation as such.** Short's framework tends to license deviation for its own sake. Does the deviation produce the dream-reality effect, or is it irregularity? Prose that feels casually wrong rather than purposefully deviating has not deployed the technique.

## VI. Mistry

**Tautology aestheticized (V.7).** Mistry's accretion plus Lispector's tautology at low coherence produces a meditative rhythm that reads as lyric. Add disruption — a dissociative gap, a perceived ontic alteration. Mistry never wrote under coherence pressure; this failure mode is specific to it.

**Restraint as coldness.** Mistry's anti-Romantic discipline preserves complexity; it does not deny emotion — disciplined warmth, not coldness. Check that restraint is preserving complexity rather than producing VI.2 flat-affect omniscience.

## VII. Ocampo (Silvina)

**Imported gender politics.** Klingenberg reads domestic-as-horror as female domestic constraint. Check: is domestic-as-horror being used in a scene that *would* carry that politics in its original setting? If yes, the politics comes along whether wanted or not.

**"Innocent" cruelty.** The synthesis uses Borges's framing (narrators who do not register); later criticism reads the not-registering as itself a politics. The cruelty in Valoria prose is whatever the focalization registers it as; the technique is the not-registering.

**Flat-affect aestheticized into irony.** Is the flatness producing the gap between content's magnitude and prose's flatness (Ocampo), or detachment that reads as the narrator's coolness (VI.2)? Structural absence, not narratorial coolness.

**Escalation.** Ocampo techniques produce quiet disorientation, not Enríquez-style explicit horror. Check for quietness, not escalation.

## VIII. Tartt

**Atmospheric excess under length (I.4).** Prose's critique of *The Goldfinch*: immersion loosens its discipline when sustained. The pull is strongest in long passages.

**Beauty-is-terror as self-licence.** The grotesque-elegant pairing can become the prose's licence for its own decoration. Check: is the pairing doing work for the focalization?

**Loaded object at low coherence (V.4).** The 5/4 boundary is the loaded-object ceiling; below it the object is heavy but unread.

**Overt vs covert unreliability.** Tartt's narrator declares unreliability and continues; Ishiguro's does not know. Do not claim Ishiguro technique for overt confession.

**Class-outsider focalization.** The aesthetic mode requires the focalizer's class-position to be established; otherwise the obsession reads as personal taste rather than class-marked perceptual access.

## IX. Beckett

**Pastiche minimalism.** Minimalism without precision is just short sentences. Each stripped construction must carry weight the fuller version would not.

**Negation-dialectic as default.** "I can't go on. I'll go on" repeated becomes a tic. A specific technique for specific moments.

**Conflation with Lispector dissolution.** Beckett's self grips; Lispector's yields. If the prose could be either, it has not committed to the Spirit axis.

## X. Lem

**Clinical register as costume.** The register must be motivated by the character's training or disposition.

**TS-gating failure.** Without TS-perceived content there is nothing for the apparatus to fail against; the technique becomes unmotivated precision.

## XI. McCarthy

**Violence as atmosphere.** McCarthy's violence is specific and clinical; used for mood rather than a depicted event, it becomes mood.

**Biblical parataxis as default (V.1).** McCarthy at base register is hypotactic; parataxis surfaces at peaks. If every combat scene runs paratactic, the marking is lost.

**Landscape overwrite.** In extended passages without action, geological landscape becomes atmospheric immersion without function.

## XII. Le Carré

**Institutional register as default.** Not every conversation is a debriefing; in personal or domestic scenes the register is wrong.

**Conflation with Ishiguro restraint (V.2 vs V.12).** Le Carré's speaker knows what they are not saying and withholds for positional advantage; Ishiguro's narrator does not know they are evading.

**Professional courtesy overdeployed.** Politeness-as-threat is devastating once per scene; in every NPC interaction it turns the institutional world into a spy novel.

## Cross-Author Combinations

The twelve authors do not share a critical tradition, so composed techniques can produce a hybrid frame none of their critics would recognize (Cixous's Lispector, Klingenberg's Ocampo and Walkowitz's Ishiguro deployed together). They can also produce effects no single tradition names — the Lem texture most of all.

### C.1 Combinations the critical tradition supports

- **Borges + Ocampo** — collaborators in the Argentine fantastic (the 1940 *Antología*). Borges precision with Ocampo flat-affect is faithful to the tradition's own practice.
- **Lispector + Ishiguro** — both unreliabilities run on the narrator-not-knowing axis: Lispector's narrator does not know they cannot represent; Ishiguro's does not know they are evading. Licenses commit → veer → recommit.
- **Tolkien + Mistry** — both build reality by accumulation rather than statement; both refuse summary and rely on the reader's synthesis. Tolkien holds the named landscape; Mistry the body and trade.
- **Márquez + Borges** — both inherit the archival chronicle register; they deploy together for codex and chronicle modes.
- **Lispector + Ocampo** — categorical failure (subject) and belonging-failure (subject in domestic space) on different axes; they compose because both refuse elevation.

### C.2 Combinations the synthesis invented — audit with extra care

No criticism validates these; the audit must do the work a tradition would otherwise do.

- **The Lem combination (Borges + Ocampo + Tolkien-closing).** Clinical incommensurability: precision applied to substrate, the description correct and complete and telling you nothing about what the configuration is. Also reachable through Lem directly (V.10).
- **Subject/object lamination at high coherence + high TS** (III.2). Subject-axis Tolkien/Mistry/Ishiguro at full strength; object-axis Lispector veer + Borges precision + Ocampo flat-affect.
- **Spirit-axis distinction at low coherence** (IV.1). Same tier, different Spirit, categorically different prose.
- **Within-observation gradient** (III.1; `techniques-skeleton.md` §11.5).

### C.3 Combinations the tradition warns against

The per-author risks above, plus two not stated there:
- **Mistry community-ensemble misidentified as scene-break (V.3).** The continuous-scene rotation is the technique; a scene break with a perspective change is a different, valid device.
- **Lispector veer aestheticized into mood.** The veer is structural enactment, not lyric atmosphere; the V.7 risk applies to the veer at low coherence as much as to Mistry tautology.

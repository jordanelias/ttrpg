# Literary Review — Technique Reference

Composition-time reference: per author, how the synthesis deploys each technique beyond its `techniques-skeleton.md` row, and how the author's contribution transforms across coherence tiers. Weights are owned by `coherence-tiers.md`; deployment risks by `literary-review-critical.md`.

<!-- author:tolkien -->
## I. Tolkien

The ground beneath the prose. Even at the lowest coherence Tolkien supplies fragments — names, landscape details, ground-checks — that mark the moments where rendering produces structured perception.

### Techniques

**Parataxis as marked, not default.** Hypotaxis is the default register; parataxis lifts the register at heroic peaks, ruined cities, deep-time framings, and must stay marked to work (V.1). Calibration anchors: *The Hobbit*'s opening sentence (default, one hypotactic cascade) and the Ithilien panorama (marked).

**Anglo-Saxon lexical rescue.** Rescued vocabulary is philological: word-roots imply word-relations, so it builds an implied language family the reader infers without being told. In Valoria: domain vocabulary stands untranslated when it is the most apt word, because translation flattens what the word carries. Test (I.1): does the word save syllables against its periphrasis? If yes, philological; if no, costume.

**Landscape as weight-bearer.** Landscape is agent, not setting — the bog that swallows, the forest that watches — and the agency is grammaticalized: mountains *glow*, valleys *come falling*, streams have *stony voices*. Tier 10–8's primary structuring device: physical world first, named specificity intensified, world as agent.

**Asterisk reality.** The Tolkien mode of I.6. The philologist's method: describe the surviving context with care and let the unrecovered thing take shape from it.

**Deep-time framing.** Chronology is generative, not backfill. Scenes sit inside longer chronologies; dates and ages are named; the chronicler-position is implicit even when the focalization is character-internal. The chronotope of Tier 10–8.

### Coherence-tier transformations

At Tier 10–8 Tolkien runs at full strength: structured world-presentation, named specificity holding, deep-time framing without strain.

At Tier 7–5 the rendering wobbles. Names arrive with a slight lag; deep-time references require effort. The dissonance is produced through Ishiguro and the temporal micro-slip, not through Tolkien; Tolkien continues the landscape work.

At Tier 4–3 names persist but chains break. Instead of "iron-coloured stones" the prose produces tautological ground-checking ("the eggs are eggs"). Asterisk reality becomes structurally necessary: the prose cannot name the central thing, so it builds the surrounding structure and leaves the centre unnamed.

At Coherence 2 and 1 Tolkien is reduced to fragments — a name, a landscape detail, an isolated ground-check — the rendering producing one more structured perception before failing again. The surviving fragments imply the structure they used to belong to.

## II. Lispector

The primary tool for categorical failure on the subject axis.

### Techniques

**The veer.** The sentence reaches for what it is trying to name; the name proves wrong; another is tried; the sentence ends somewhere other than where it was going. The accumulation of wrong names traces the shape of what cannot be named. Recruited for content that exceeds rendering — philosophical reaching beyond conceptual capacity — not as a politics of refusal.

**Ontological estrangement.** At Tier 10–8 it serves content-appropriate philosophical territory; from Tier 7–5 down it intensifies to serve the PC's rendering failure.

**This-instant-now, inverted.** Lispector's narrator reaches for radical now-ness at full capacity; the Coherence 1 PC has only the now because temporal sequence has failed. Same surface, inverted depth: Lispector's reaching becomes Valoria's failure of holding.

**Repetition as ontological intensity.** Tautological ground-checking at Tiers 4–3 and below: "the eggs were eggs" is the speaker producing the deepest available truth at that moment, not failing. This is the licensed exception to I.5, and the distinction is easy to lose — it holds only while each instance is a structurally placed point of strain (`techniques-skeleton.md` §11.5), never a run.

**Author-intrusion.** Available for Borges codex-mode and, at Coherence 2, where the rendering no longer holds the boundary between the prose's voice and the focalizer's. It must be a confession of representational limit, never a stylistic flourish.

**Pre-rational access.** At low coherence the meta-cognitive capacity is gone (II.5); what remains is access beneath the analytical.

### Coherence-tier transformations

At Tier 10–8 Lispector touches the edges: the veer surfaces only where content is genuinely philosophical or ontological — a substrate fragment, the codex's recursive entry. Content-appropriate, never rendering-failure (II.2).

At Tier 7–5 the veer becomes available at moments of micro-slippage; the narrator is functional but reaches occasionally for content the rendering cannot quite hold.

At Tier 4–3 Lispector is the dominant irreal author. The veer takes over the named-specificity work Tolkien used to do; tautologies appear; pre-rational access becomes the available mode of cognition; intensity-repetition operates as ground-checking.

At Coherence 2 authorial-voice intrusion becomes available. The tautologies are not failure but the tier's available truth.

At Coherence 1 instant-now is the dominant temporality, inverted as above. The veer is structurally necessary; predication of self breaks; the body's parts replace the agent.

## III. Borges

The primary tool for the intelligibility axis decomposing — the conditions of possibility becoming visible as the limitation that screened them fails.

### Techniques

**Fictive-as-real.** The Codex, chronicle entries and institutional histories use scholarly apparatus as their primary mode, and the apparatus cites *in-world* scholarship (I.7): the Codex cites its third revision, never the Piety Track. A doctrine attributed to "the third revision of the Codex" is plausible if the Codex has revisions. Worked example: calibration anchor V4.

**Erudite compression.** The footnote does the work the body of the sentence cannot; the reader is positioned as a competent inferrer. Without the compression discipline (I.1: the rare word saves syllables), Borges-mode is purple prose.

**Tolerance for the impossible inside scholarly register.** Feeds the Lem combination and the within-observation gradient's middle layer: the count is correct and tells you nothing.

**Compressed paradox.** Extended, a paradox becomes argument; compressed to clause length, it becomes structure. Use for codex-mode and moments of structural recursion in narrative. It must serve the content, not display cleverness (V.6).

**Library / labyrinth.** The chronotope of Borges-mode prose: place rendered as conceptual, the layout being the internal logic of the place made visible.

**Eternal return / time as labyrinth.** The Coherence 1 temporal structure when TS is present: Lispector supplies present-tense being; Borges supplies the recursive structure that being moves through. Unlike Lispector's, the structure stays Borges's even at low coherence.

**Heresiarchs and hidden orders.** Heterodox theology, suppressed doctrines, the recursive editorial revisions of the Codex. The hidden order is the position from which official doctrine is exposed, not exoticism.

### Coherence-tier transformations

At Tier 10–8 Borges is rare, available for structurally recursive content — codex entries, institutional histories — never for the PC's cognition failing (II.2).

At Tier 7–5 micro-slippage may include structural-recursive perception: a Codex entry that contains itself, an institutional history the practitioner cannot quite trace. Still content-appropriate.

At Tier 4–3 the intelligibility axis is fracturing. Apparatus-as-fiction becomes the prose's way of holding what the rendering cannot organize; the Lem combination becomes available.

At Coherence 2 Borges supplies the structural recursion the failing limitation produces: the prose loops, categories return, the apparatus keeps operating while its content is no longer organizable. Compressed paradox is the available form of statement.

At Coherence 1 recursion is constitutive: time-as-labyrinth replaces time-as-sequence; the library is the chronotope of perception; the apparatus is the prose's mode of articulating what it cannot say.

## IV. Ishiguro

The most stable weighting across tiers, because unreliability is inherent to rendering failure at every level: wherever the rendering fails, the narrator's reading of the failure is itself unreliable.

### Techniques

**Covert unreliability through quantity-violation.** The marker is excess, not contradiction: the prose is exorbitantly wordy precisely where reticence would be expected, and the reader catches what is held back from the volume of language deployed to hold it back. Licensed deviations: §0.2. Requires unreliability, not just length (V.2).

**Hedging as armour.** The narrator hedges precisely when they should not — at intimacy, grief, confession — so the hedging marks where the truth is. Recruit it for focalizations whose social position requires composure: a Solmund cleric at a humiliation, a Crown vassal being dismissed, a Restoration Chronicler losing an argument.

**Digression as revelation.** Licensed when the digression holds content the surface narration cannot bear; the formulaic return-phrase signals the digression is doing the work.

**Restrained reported speech.** "He said something to the effect of." Use where verbatim recall is implausible: memory, a chronicler's reconstruction, an interrogation transcript.

**Late-modernist covert unreliability.** The lineage is Marlow and Dowell, not theatrical unreliability: the narrator does not know, the prose does not know it is performing, the reader does the work.

### Coherence-tier transformations

At Tier 10–8: situational restraint. Any evasion is social, not ontological — a Solmund cleric hedging at a difficult sermon, a Restoration scholar evading a question.

At Tier 7–5 the temporal micro-slip arrives: Vael hears the door from the lane before there has been a door, identifies it as wind, walks on. The reader detects what Vael does not. Ishiguro becomes the primary tool for the dissonance, which is precisely the gap between what the narrator perceives and what is happening.

At Tier 4–3 repair sequences become visible; recommitments grow increasingly desperate; the narrator reads a case they cannot see is theirs.

At Coherence 2 repair sequences break: commit, veer (Lispector), recommit — the recommitment barely convincing even to the narrator. Covert unreliability is at the edge of becoming visible.

At Coherence 1 the narrator's reading of their own perception is the only narration available: they read what is happening as something familiar (the wind, the hour, fatigue), and the prose holds together only through the focalization's commitment to its own reading.

## V. Márquez

Carries the temporal axis — compression, time-folding, matter-of-fact impossibility. His weight plateaus once temporal structuring has failed, because the contribution is structural rather than scaling with intensity.

### Techniques

**Three temporal frames per sentence.** (Anchor and analysis: `calibration-infill.md`.) The fold serves chronicle scale — the narrator stands where all frames are available at once. Without the chronicler-position the fold is ornamental complexity.

**Ordinary syntax for extreme content.** Valoria's impossibilities are mechanical-ontological (substrate phenomena, threadwork events), not mythological-historical: the technique transfers, the tradition does not.

**Hyperbolic specificity.** Precision attached to impossible scope makes the impossibility documentable: magical realism documents, fantasy invents. The precision must be lexically concrete, never abstract.

**Time as spiral.** Narrative architecture: prophecy and fulfilment, recurring pattern, the founding event that determines everything after.

**Numerical anchors at deep time.** "For four years, eleven months, and two days." The number signals the chronicle-position and the reader trusts it because the narrator is a chronicler. Use for deep-time framing and institutional histories.

### Coherence-tier transformations

At Tier 10–8: narrative architecture (founding events, prophecy, deep time) and content-level impossibility — the impossible is part of the world, not the PC's rendering failing.

At Tier 7–5 time-folding may surface in micro-slippage: recall of an event that has not yet happened, precision attached to a memory the rendering cannot place. The chronicler-position is partly preserved.

At Tier 4–3 time no longer enforces exclusivity; two temporal states coexist; compression operates as a rendering effect. The impossible is rendered ordinarily not because the world is ordinary but because the rendering cannot produce wonder-markers.

At Coherence 2 the chronicler-position is gone. What remains is the syntactic discipline — impossibility in ordinary syntax — now serving the rendering's inability to mark the impossible.

At Coherence 1 Borges and Lispector take the temporal work; Márquez supplies the syntactic flatness holding them together. Numerical precision still surfaces — a count, a name, a date — as the last evidence of the chronicler's position.

## VI. Mistry

Declines steeply with coherence because accretion and embodied occupational specificity both need the rendering apparatus. What remains at low coherence is body-as-record reduced to fragments.

### Techniques

**Accretive particularity.** The accretion does the work; universalization, if it comes, is the prose's permission to stop. The explaining sentence is the failure mode (I.4). At low coherence the accretion continues but its function — portrait-through-particulars — breaks (test 24, V.7).

**Occupational language.** Recruit for any focalization with occupational standing: the Warden's training vocabulary, the Solmund cleric's liturgical lexicon, the cooper's tool-names. The vocabulary is the focalizer's own, never the designer's (I.7).

**Free-indirect editorialization.** Licensed only where the community's perspective has already been established by accreted particulars, so the reader knows whose word "sinister" is (VI.1 is the inverse failure).

**Community-as-ensemble.** Valoria settlements are the right scale: too small a focus loses the ensemble; too large breaks the continuous-scene constraint (V.3).

**Body as record.** Maren's calluses, the binder's curved spine, Karelin's weather-marked hands; at low coherence, fragments — the callus, the first joint, the thickness of the skin. The detail does the political work the prose does not state.

**Routine as structure.** Name the routine in detail; name the disruption without remarking on it. The routine is also the chronotope.

**Dignity through refusal to editorialize.** The positive form of VI.4: a load-bearing NPC's anchored dimension is established through accretion.

### Coherence-tier transformations

At Tier 10–8: full accretive particularity, occupational language, body-as-record, community-as-ensemble; the universal arrives after specifics, if at all.

At Tier 7–5 accretion continues with slight effort; occupational vocabulary is slightly less reliable; the ensemble rotation may be less smooth.

At Tier 4–3 the accretion happens; the portrait does not (test 24: the callus, the pockets, the bone folder). V.7 is the failure mode here.

At Coherence 2 body-as-record persists as fragments; community-as-ensemble is gone — the rendering cannot rotate through consciousnesses. Naming without commentary becomes the available way of holding presence.

At Coherence 1 single fragments survive — a hand, a callus, the smell of leather. No editorial step-back is possible because the rendering cannot produce one.

## VII. Ocampo (Silvina)

The primary tool for the ontological-belonging axis — the practitioner ceasing to fit in the human world.

### Techniques

**Flat-affect rendering.** Distinguish from Ishiguro: Stevens *evades* what he registers, which leaves signs (hedging); Ocampo's narrator does not register at all, which leaves none. Formal-measured prose is neither.

**Domestic interior as horror-site.** For the practitioner's belonging-failure in familiar settings — the parish house, the workshop, the household worked in for years. The space has not changed; the practitioner's relation to it has (II.3).

**Children doing terrible things, narrated placidly.** The cruelty is incidental to the surface concern; no editorializing; close on the wrong moment. It must emerge from a fully realized scene the prose appears not to be focused on — never cruelty for atmosphere.

**Refusal of explanation closing.** Closing discipline generally, and the closing register at low coherence (the pin, the cup, the eggs).

**Charged objects.** Anchored to a focalization whose relation to the object has shifted. Ocampo's uncanny loading is distinct from Tartt's aesthetic loading (`techniques-skeleton.md` §7).

**Servants seeing what employers cannot.** A focalization principle: lower social position grants perceptual access the higher lacks — a Restoration servant, a peasant herding sheep on the headland.

**Animals as witnesses.** The animal sees differently, not more. Use for environmental focalization at C0 scenes and to intensify a Tolkien landscape-as-agent reading — the wren on the cap-stone (test 37).

**Metamorphosis without explanation.** For C0 encounters and visibly shifted outward facing; explanation breaks it.

### Coherence-tier transformations

At Tier 10–8: content-level uncanny — a charged domestic object, an animal as witness, a familiar surface holding something unnamed. Never rendering failure.

At Tier 7–5 the focalizer's belonging in a familiar space wobbles: the kitchen feels off; the parish house's silence is too long. Domestic-as-horror is content-appropriate dissonance, not yet belonging-failure.

At Tier 4–3 belonging-failure becomes audible: the practitioner is present in scenes but does not fit, and the flat-affect mode starts applying to their own perception.

At Coherence 2 belonging-failure is structural; charged objects (the ledger, the binding, the chair) carry weight the practitioner cannot read; closing on the wrong moment dominates.

At Coherence 1 the practitioner is in the world the way an object is in a room. Metamorphosis-without-explanation applies to their outward materiality: the body is wrong, and the prose reports it flatly.

## VIII. Tartt

The most coherence-dependent author: the 5/4 boundary is the loaded-object ceiling (test 36). What survives low coherence is the atmospheric anchor and the punch sentence.

### Techniques

**Forward-foreshadowing dread.** For retrospective narrative. Pairs with Márquez's three-frame sentence: both establish a chronicler-position from which the future is settled. The announced disaster must be held across substantial narrative space; releasing it quickly collapses the structure.

**Loaded specific (aesthetic).** Each loaded detail is fully realized at the literal level; the loading comes through precision the prose never calls attention to. Coherence-gated: `techniques-skeleton.md` coherence-gated availability table, V.4.

**Atmospheric immersion.** Must do work — establish focalization, anchor temporal position, supply reality-effect detail — not merely produce tone. The details must be specific (Mistry-tested), never generic (I.4).

**Punch sentence.** Three to seven words beside thirty-word sentences; the asymmetry is the rhythm. It needs placement, not frequency (I.2; repeated in a passage, it is drumbeat).

**Decorous punctuation.** Justified when it improves impact, never as default style (§0.2).

**Grotesque-elegant juxtaposition.** For revelation, intensified atmosphere, charged objects given full weight. It must do work the prose cannot do otherwise, never license atmospheric excess.

**Aesthetic narrator / class outsider.** Richard in *The Secret History*, Theo in *The Goldfinch*: the narrator sees what their class-position makes salient. The inverse of Ocampo's servants — here the outsider observes the dominant class. Requires the focalizer's class-position to be established.

**Stream of memory with overt unreliability.** Tartt's narrators know and admit their unreliability; use for first-person retrospective where the focalization can declare its own position. Worked example: calibration anchor V2.

### Coherence-tier transformations

At Tier 10–8: full atmospheric immersion, loaded objects at full operation, punch sentences at distributed moments, grotesque-elegant juxtaposition for revelation.

At Tier 7–5 the loaded object requires more effort but still operates (C5 is its upper edge). The precision of atmospheric detail may slip slightly.

At Tier 4–3 the loaded object fails (V.4). Immersion may continue, but the precision that licensed it is degrading. Punch sentence and grotesque-elegant juxtaposition survive.

At Coherence 2 immersion is unsustainable — the rendering cannot hold long sensory passages. Punch sentences remain; the grotesque-elegant pairing operates at content level: the body's wrongness, the detail that surfaces flat.

At Coherence 1 only the punch sentence and closing-on-the-loaded-detail remain; the aesthetic narrator no longer focalizes.

## IX. Beckett

The will-continuation author. Primary quadrants: Q3, Q4 high Spirit.

### Techniques

**Permutational enumeration.** Advancing through a combinatorial space: "Left foot. Right foot. Left." is enumeration of discrete steps, sameness as structure and difference as position. *Watt*'s exhaustive permutations are the extreme case. Use where the low-coherence rendering cycles through possible predications of a recalibrating experience.

**Anaphoric litany with progressive variation.** "This body homeless. This mind ignoring. These emptied hands." Repetition is structural, not semantic; each instance adds (distinct from I.5). Use at high-Spirit moments at low coherence — the will naming what it still has.

**Dialectical succession.** "I can't go on. I'll go on." Contraries held in sequence, neither cancelling the other; the prose advances by contradiction rather than development. At low coherence high Spirit, the impossibility and the continuation coexist.

**Austere negation.** "All of old. Nothing else ever. Ever tried. Ever failed." Maximal weight at minimal length; every word displaced from its expected position does work. For C1–C2 high Spirit, where the frame does not generate extended structure.

**The will as grammatical subject.** The self contracts to one capacity — deciding — but the deciding is visible in the body, not declared. "She had decided" may appear once as anchor; thereafter the will is inferred from the behaviour. At high Spirit the act the self still performs closes the passage (exteriority, IV.1).

### Coherence-tier transformations

At Tier 10–8 near-absent: will-continuation is inaudible when rendering is intact. Available only at thematic extremity — resolve tested at the boundary of endurance.

At Tier 7–5 still near-absent; the frame has not shifted enough for the will to structure the prose.

At Tier 4–3 Beckett enters; the Spirit axis becomes audible. High-Spirit PCs begin to show dialectical succession — the rendering fails at something, the will reasserts, both coexist.

At Coherence 2 anaphoric litany and permutational enumeration enter: the will grips by naming what it still has, testing each predication.

At Coherence 1 the Beckett voice is fully realized at high Spirit: the self contracted to deciding, austere negation, dialectical succession. "She can't go on. She goes on."

## X. Lem

The clinical-substrate author. Primary quadrant: Q2.

### Techniques

<!-- concept:observing-around -->
**Observing around.** The principle is owned by SKILL.md. Lem's models: *Solaris*'s ocean described through what it produces (the mimoids, the symmetriads), what it does to the station's instruments, how it responds to stimuli — never what it is; *Fiasco*'s civilization through its artifacts, emissions and effects — never its intentions. Valoria applications: the practitioner's trained observation of substrate phenomena at high TS; the interior of a low-coherence high-Spirit character through exteriority; the threadcut being at C0 through its surface effects.

**The failed taxonomy.** Elaborate classification of phenomena that resist classification. For codex entries, scholarly NPC observations, institutional attempts to categorize thread phenomena — Hafenmark field reports with systematic notation, Church records, practitioner journals — all precise, internally consistent records of things that do not belong in the categories assigned.

**Defamiliarization through trained observation.** Where other authors defamiliarize through metaphor or distortion, Lem applies disciplined observation to objects that resist it. The observer's method is real; the object exceeds it. For PCs whose Hafenmark training or scholarly disposition gives them the habit.

### Coherence-tier transformations

Lem is gated entirely by TS: without substrate perception there is nothing for the apparatus to fail against. At TS 50+ his share is roughly constant across tiers, because the technique concerns the object's resistance to the apparatus, not the observer's coherence. A high- and a low-coherence observer at high TS both perceive the substrate; the low-coherence observer's integrating frame is itself recalibrating while they describe it.

## XI. McCarthy

Primary quadrant: Q1 (combat, violence, landscape extremity).

### Techniques

**Violence rendered without editorial.** The prose reports and stops; the reader registers the event through the prose's refusal to interpret it. For combat encounters where the physical world's indifference to human categories is the content.

**Landscape as geological agent.** Rendered through accumulated geological detail — mineral, formation, erosion — without personification. Not hostile or welcoming but indifferent, operating on a timescale that makes human presence trivial. Overlaps with Tolkien's landscape-as-agent but without its implied agency: McCarthy's landscapes do not watch or remember; they endure.

**Unpunctuated dialogue.** Speech on the same prose surface as action and landscape. Use sparingly, where the distinction between speech and action is the content (combat dialogue, terse exchange in extremity).

**Biblical parataxis at extremity.** Marked register at combat peaks and landscape extremity, never sustained base register.

### Coherence-tier transformations

Content-gated, not coherence-gated: combat, violence and harsh landscape can occur at any tier. At low coherence the world's indifference is amplified — the landscape endures regardless of the PC's recalibration — but McCarthy is not licensed for the PC's rendering: what the PC observes (McCarthy's landscape) is unchanged even as the frame fails. At TS 50+ he rises slightly at C2 and C1, where the substrate's physical manifestation suits his clinical precision.

## XII. Le Carré

Primary quadrant: Q1 (institutional-political, tradecraft, bureaucratic manoeuvring).

### Techniques

**Institutional indirection.** Power read from procedural language — euphemism, the passive voice, the qualified statement that withdraws more than it offers. Every conversation is a negotiation in which the stated content is secondary to the implied positioning. For faction politics, Church bureaucracy, Hafenmark intelligence operations.

**Controlled information release.** The reader learns at the pace the institution permits; each conversation unlocks one fact while locking three others. For faction-encounter pacing: the player learns about an institution at the rate its representatives choose to disclose.

**Professional courtesy as threat.** "Do sit down." "How kind of you to come." Professional politeness where informality would be natural signals that the interaction is institutional, not personal. For NPC dialogue in institutional settings — Church officials, faction leaders, Hafenmark operatives.

**The exhausted professional.** Competent, thorough, tired — and the tiredness is occupational, not dramatic. For characters who have served an institution long enough for its weight to be audible in their register: the sedimentation of procedural life, not burnout.

### Coherence-tier transformations

Content-gated, not coherence-gated. At low coherence the Church committee still operates through procedural language around the fragmenting PC. At TS 50+ he rises at C2 and C1: the PC perceives thread activity beneath the institutional surface that the institution either does not know about or chooses not to name, so the indirection becomes layered.

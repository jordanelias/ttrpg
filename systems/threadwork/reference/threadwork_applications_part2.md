## 6. `systems/factions` — politics, domain actions, and what threadwork buys a house

### 6.1 The principal cannot supervise the agent, and this is structural

A noble formed in a Church polity is, with high probability, maximally immunized: essentialist
formation forecloses the developmental preconditions of sensitivity, and nobility is the class most
heavily formed.

| fact | consequence |
|---|---|
| He cannot perform threadwork | He retains practitioners. Threadwork is a service bought, not a capability held |
| He cannot verify what was done | He is told the result and cannot connect the telling to anything. **He has no audit** |
| He cannot retain thread-level knowledge | Briefings go inert. He will re-commission the same intelligence repeatedly without knowing it |
| Acquiring sensitivity costs his formation | The confrontations that build reach erode the orientation his legitimacy rests on. **A real trade, not a flavour note** |

**The retained practitioner is an agent the principal structurally cannot supervise.** Not
"difficult to supervise" — the barrier is metaphysical rather than institutional, and publishing
every document would not close it. This is the sharpest principal–agent problem in the setting, and
no amount of money, loyalty or hostage-taking addresses the epistemic half of it.

### 6.2 The catalogue, by objective

**Succession and legitimacy**

| application | working | cost | detection | counter |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Remove a rival's antecedent marriage | past-pull, Relational | two steps | reach 50 reads the cut faces and dates it | the rival's diagnostician produces the reading in Parliament |
| Age a forged charter into a genuine one | manipulative, Object | one step + per season | reach 30 reads a held configuration on the parchment | any adept in the archive |
| Remove an heir's disqualifying act | past-pull, Personal | one step | reach 30 finds the absence; 50 dates it | orphaned configurations downstream are visible to **non-adepts** as anomalies |

**The structural problem with all three.** A past-pull alters factual history while every witness
retains the old one. The noble holds a title whose documentary basis is sound and whose living
memory contradicts it, **and the contradiction cannot be resolved by producing evidence, because
both sides' evidence is genuine.** Worse: orphans decay fast, so an office granted as payment for a
pulled act loses its cause and begins *failing to be noticed* — subordinates route around the holder
without deciding to. **Threadwork applied to legitimacy hollows out the appointments it was meant to
secure.** Verdict: high yield, catastrophic durability. Use for a claim that must survive one
season, not a dynasty.

**Parliament and faction actions**

| application | working | cost | notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bind a wavering member to your position | knot, Relational | two steps | He does not vote your way — he becomes partly renderable only through you. Others notice within a session, and it is exactly what an excommunication proceeding is built to name |
| Remove the grievance behind an opposing bloc | past-pull, Relational | two steps | The bloc dissolves. The grievance's *effects* remain as orphans and often re-form a bloc around nothing |
| Loosen a rival's doctrinal hold on his delegation | manipulative, Structural | two steps + per season | Their commitments go inert; they recite the position and cannot act on it |
| Sever a rival's alliance knot | destructive, Relational | two steps | Part of what holds the severed party in configuration goes with the knot. He is apperceived differently by different observers — which reads at court as instability, then as illness, then as something else |

**Diplomacy.** Treaties count toward territorial control, so a treaty is worth as much as a conquest
and costs less. **Diagnose before negotiating** — reach 50 reads whether the counterparty is knotted
to a third power, and it is the highest-value, lowest-cost application in the catalogue. **Loosen a
rival's treaty knot rather than severing it**: the binding weakens with no cut face to find, and the
rival's own adept reads it as natural decay of a poorly made knot.

**Economy and Accord.** **Weave the harvest, restorative** — a good year the configurations were
tending toward, arriving reliably. Free, undetectable below reach 30, raises Accord: the most
cost-effective political application in the set. The **manipulative** version buys a good year where
none was coming, and the grain and its sacking disagree in a way the population registers as
wrongness without naming it — Accord gain partly offset by unease.

⚠ **The blowback path worth building.** Doctrinal loosening drives piety down, and sustained piety
decay spawns an anti-Church emergent faction — extra-parliamentary, territorial, non-voting. **By the
prophylaxis's own logic, anti-Church formation is formation *outside* it, so such populations should
produce sensitives at a higher base rate than any established faction.** A house using doctrinal
loosening as an economic weapon is manufacturing a **thread-capable** insurgency that does not vote,
cannot be negotiated with parliamentarily, and can invade the parent faction whose neglect generated
it. This is the most underrated consequence in the catalogue and the most interesting unexplored one
in the setting.

**Church relations.** Any working that produces the confrontation signature in witnesses supplies the
Church with **true** testimony for a heresy proceeding — the testimony is accurate and the framing is
the Church's. The reverse holds: a rival's adept is a target for whom evidence need not be forged.
And a house allied to a threadcut being has a structural problem, because its uncanniness derives
from the disagreement between its features rather than from anything it does — **it cannot be made to
blend by behaving well.**

### 6.3 Three structural traps

**The record problem.** Political power in this polity rests on documents, precedent and lineage —
all retained history — and every manipulative working degrades some of it. **Threadwork is an
instrument that spends the substance of the authority it is used to acquire.** A house that uses it
heavily accumulates records that no longer compose and eventually cannot establish its own claims by
ordinary means, having spent them.

**The action-economy loop.** Sustained working raises provincial tension → incursions → Accord drops
→ mandatory military or governing response → slots consumed before anything else is scheduled.
**The instrument that wins the province spends the turns needed to hold it.**

**The retinue problem.** A retained adept accumulates displacement, and it propagates through every
knot they hold, in all three dimensions from onset at magnitudes individually below notice. Your
adept is knotted to your household. Two specific hazards: **heirs** are the most exposed and least
able to absorb it, and **a sensitive who develops in your household** is by the prophylaxis's own
logic someone whose formation has been compromised — which is what your legitimacy is built on.

### 6.4 Doctrine, and why threadwork does not win

1. **Accelerate, do not impose.** The heir who was going to inherit, arriving sooner. The alliance
   both sides were drifting toward, concluded this season. The harvest that was coming.
2. **Terrain over persons.** Fords, roads, breaks, boundaries — restorative, invisible below reach
   70, no propagation, no accumulation.
3. **Read freely, work rarely.**
4. **Reserve manipulative work for the irreversible** — the battle that decides the peninsula, not
   the vote that decides a committee.
5. **Rotate adepts.** A ten-year retainer is a liability with a household attached.

**And it does not win.** Sovereignty is the victory condition and there is no thread path to it.
Threadwork is tactical leverage toward the universal goal, exactly as a treaty network or a mass
seizure is — and it is an accelerant whose own strain generates the incursions that suppress the
Accord a house needs across every territory. **Very good at taking a territory; structurally hostile
to holding fifteen.**

## 7. `systems/settlements` and the economy — the twelve sectors

### 7.1 The viability test, and what it returns

Four questions, in order. **What fraction of the useful work is restorative?** Low → the sector runs
on the practitioner and cannot be staffed indefinitely. **Does it require *holding*?** A hold is a
debt with no principal repayment. **Who bears the co-movement externality?** Something always does;
the only question is who. **Does it require knotting?** Lattice density is the risk multiplier, not
working count.

| sector | restorative fraction | holding | externality lands on | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Healthcare** | high, in-window | rarely | patient history, family retention | **highest civilian value** |
| **Agriculture** | high | sometimes | the calendar, tenure | viable |
| **Transport & terrain** | high | no | land tenure | viable **under a sparsity cap** |
| **Materials & production** | moderate–high | often | provenance, market trust | viable **pre-assembly only** |
| **Civil defence** | diagnostic | no | — | **viable, no downside — build it first** |
| **Justice & forensics** | diagnostic | no | — | viable; transforms evidentiary law |
| **Military** | mixed | sometimes | the theatre, both armies | see Part 1 §4 |
| **Records & administration** | n/a | n/a | — | **inverted — the defending sector** |
| **Construction** | low | always | habitability itself | **fails** |
| **Water & climate** | very low | always | everything downstream | **fails** |
| **Education** | blocked | — | — | **fails except apprenticeship** |

### 7.2 Healthcare — the sector whose institutional shape is least like its counterpart

**Prognosis is the profession.** Whether a body is tending toward recovery stops being an estimate
and becomes the determination of cost, legality and outcome. So the order is built around
**diagnosis**, with intervention as a comparatively minor terminal step: its senior practitioners are
prognosticians and its junior ones operate.

| free, restorative | costly, manipulative |
|---|---|
| Closing wounds the body was going to close — the scar arrives at the age it would have reached | Suppressing a condition the body is not resolving: the patient stops taking the world's ordinary marks, and her physician can state the diagnosis, cannot connect it to the patient in front of him, and has stopped scheduling follow-ups without deciding to |
| Setting fractures already aligned (misaligned ones must be set by ordinary craft first — threadwork accelerates, it does not reposition) | Refusing a death: the community acquires a man it cannot keep in a room, whose daughter can describe his face and cannot picture it. **Nobody is deceived and knowing does not help** |
| Clearing infections already turning | Pulling traumatic memory "as therapy" — displacement, not treatment. The patient keeps a decaying behaviour whose cause is gone, plus a felt presence with complete certainty attached to nothing |
| **Accelerating convalescence — the largest population-level gain**, because it returns labour | |
| Obstetric cases tending toward survival — highest value by lives | |

**The window is the whole discipline.** Every free application has a period during which recovery is
the tendency and after which it is not, and the diagnostic corps exists to find the edges.

⚠ **The triage inversion, and it is the sector's most reliable source of grievance.** Threadwork
helps the people who were going to live. It cannot save the dying, because dying is what they are
tending toward and reversing it is manipulative. So the order allocates its free capacity precisely
to the patients with the best prognosis — **which looks like the opposite of triage** and is
defensible: it compresses recovery, returns labour, shortens infectious periods, and frees ordinary
nursing for what threadwork cannot touch. **The practitioners will be seen to walk past the dying to
attend the recovering, and the explanation is exactly the one that cannot be transmitted to the
families watching.**

**Epidemic response.** Accelerate recovery in every case already turning — an epidemiological
intervention in its own right, and it does not require touching the pathogen. Curing the cases not
turning is a Structural sustained hold and would burn an order in a season. **Reading which
households are tending toward outbreak costs nothing and is decisive.**

**And drift has no treatment arm.** An order that performs manipulative work cannot repair its own
people and must manage by exposure accounting, rotation and mandatory retirement.

### 7.3 Agriculture, terrain, materials

**Agriculture.** Free: bringing forward a harvest that was coming; closing damage in stored crop
already sound; accelerating soil recovery already underway; accelerating a blight's natural collapse;
herd recovery in animals tending to recover. Costly: **holding stored grain sound in a wet cellar** —
the sacking ages and the contents do not, handlers register the disagreement without naming it, and
the store becomes difficult to inventory, with counts differing by one or two in forty, consistently,
and repeated counts by the same person not converging.

**The real agricultural instrument is the tendency survey.** Knowing which fields were going to fail
is decisive on its own, before anyone touches a thread: it permits reallocation of labour, early
purchase, redistribution and controlled import. **Threadworking agriculture is famine forecasting
first and famine mitigation second** — the forecast is free and reliable, the mitigation expensive
and bounded.

⚠ **The calendar externality.** Restorative harvest work every season accumulates temporal accrual
across the same population, and the village stops being able to date its own seasons. Tithes,
tenancy, festivals, debt and service obligations are all scheduled against the year. **A province
with reliable harvests and an incoherent calendar has exchanged one insecurity for another, and the
second is harder to see.**

**Terrain — the cheapest large public good available.** Fords and river crossings (the land was going
to shallow there), tracks and droveways, drainage the land was already cutting, fire-breaks, passes
where the slope was tending to settle. All restorative, all free, all invisible below reach 70.

**The line it cannot cross: threadwork gives you crossings, not structures.** A ford is what the
river does; a bridge is a built thing. **Accelerate what use and weather would produce; never hold
what construction produced.**

⚠ **The tenure externality is systematic and must be budgeted.** A fire-break across four holdings
leaves four boundary records that conflict irreconcilably, each internally consistent, no two
agreeing on which family ceded what. Scale that across a road network and the society acquires a
permanent land-dispute layer as the standing cost of its infrastructure. **Budget the courts, not
just the roads.**

⚠ **And the sparsity rule, which is the one place a competent public-works programme reconstructs the
Calamity's mechanism exactly.** Knotting places to places to keep a network coherent produces a
generationally maintained lattice, and a configuration held only by a balance has nowhere to fall
back to when the balance goes. **Build the network. Do not knot the network.** Individual restorative
terrain workings are free and isolated: if one fails, one ford fails. A knotted network is a single
failure surface with a civilization standing on it.

**Materials — the pre-assembly rule.** Work the material before it becomes the thing; never the thing
after it is assembled. The ideal industries are processes the configurations are actively tending
through: **fermentation and brewing, cheese and curing, tanning, retting flax and hemp, seasoning
timber, ageing and tempering metal, weathering ore to fracture rock.** A threadworking economy's
comparative advantage is therefore in **fermented, cured, tanned, seasoned and aged goods** — a
distinctive export profile that leaves no residue, because nothing is being held. Ore does not tend
toward metal, cloth does not tend toward a garment, grain does not tend toward flour: smelting,
weaving and milling are manipulative at every step and belong to ordinary craft.

**Held goods are a maintenance liability, not an asset.** The per-season cost never stops. An economy
whose infrastructure depends on held configurations is spending a practitioner's career per held
item, permanently, and the cost is invisible in the first generation while the holds are new. Hence
an **assay regime**: guild marking, standing inspection, and an offence of unmarked holding. Two
market facts follow — threadworked goods are identifiable and unnerving without being defective,
which suppresses their price below their utility; and fraud is straightforward while detection is
cheap, which produces the stable adversarial equilibrium guilds are good at.

### 7.4 The three that fail, and why they are worth stating

**Construction.** A house held against its decay is sound, dry, uncrazed, with no insect track in the
beams — **and people will not go in.** Those who enter report the far wall reading further away than
it measures, a hallway that does not resolve into depth, objects holding their size wrongly when
approached. Nothing is misshapen. **Holding a standing structure preserves it perfectly and destroys
the thing it was for.** What remains: materials pre-assembly, ground that was going to settle
settling now, and reading which buildings are tending to fail.

**Water and climate.** Rivers do not tend to move, rainfall does not tend to change, coastlines do not
tend to relocate — all manipulative at Structural scale with downstream consequences no diagnosis can
bound. And it is the sector where the Einhir shape is easiest to reproduce innocently, a water
programme being by nature sustained, precise, fixed-site and generational. **Moving water is
manipulative. Finding it is free** — a tendency read locates where water is and where a well will
hold, at no cost, and that is the sector's entire viable contribution.

**Education.** Ordinary schooling is unaffected — arithmetic, letters, law, medicine-as-craft,
navigation all transmit normally. But thread-level knowledge cannot be taught: a non-sensitive can
recite and cannot act, and repeated reading makes it worse rather than better. **There is no academy,
no textbook, no curriculum, and publishing everything would not change it.** What transmits is the
form of attention, not the content it discloses — which makes apprenticeship the only mechanism that
works, and religious poetry the only thing that reaches everyone else.

⚠ **The safety trap, and it may be the most important structural finding in the sector set.**
Practitioner supply is set by how much genuine confrontation a society generates *and survives*.
**So a society that succeeds at civil defence reduces its own practitioner supply** — every cordon
that works, every threshold respected, every incursion contained before anyone is exposed removes the
developmental events that produce the next generation. Safety and capability are inversely coupled,
and policy cannot break the coupling, because induced confrontation runs at roughly half the rate and
costs the supervisor. **A threadworking society is always either dangerous and capable, or safe and
declining.** Its only stable answer is to keep a frontier — deliberately maintaining a dangerous place
in order to remain capable.

### 7.5 The institutions the model requires

| institution | function |
|---|---|
| **Tendency Survey** | Reads what configurations are tending toward, per district, per season. **The scarce asset**, and it determines whether everything else is free or ruinous |
| **Tension Register** | Territory-level tension against a tear threshold, on a survey cadence, with closure and evacuation triggers. **The only early warning that exists** for the event that ended the previous civilization |
| **Knot Registry and sparsity cap** | Every deliberate knot recorded, density capped per district, inter-district knotting prohibited |
| **Working-Site Model** | Manipulative work performed away from settlement, on rotation — keeps propagation out of households |
| **Assay and Marking** | Identifies held configurations in goods and legal instruments |
| **Attestation Cadence** | Many independent observers, frequently, re-sworn. The only check on an editable archive |
| **Apprenticeship Orders** | The only transmission that works. **Not schools** |
| **Exposure Rota** | Exposure accounting, mandatory retirement, no cure available |

**Note what is absent: no academy, no published corpus, no non-sensitive regulator, no ministry that
can be briefed.** Those are the four shapes a modern reader expects and the barrier forbids all four.

**And the regulator problem has no solution.** Only practitioners can audit practitioners; a
non-sensitive magistrate can be told what was done and cannot connect the telling to anything. The
profession is **structurally self-regulating** — which is the governance condition the Einhir were in.
Worse, even sensitives do not necessarily reconcile across differences of reach, so a society cannot
assume its practitioners can hold each other to account. **Oversight has a ceiling, and the ceiling is
structural.**

### 7.6 Three models a society can occupy

**The Einhir model** — refinement, density, lattice, generational accumulation at fixed sites.
Produced the highest civilizational achievement in the setting's history and ended it. Its residue is
the Locked Zones. **Check any programme against its three properties**: increasing precision and
density over generations at fixed sites; a lattice knotted through the built environment, social
organization and collective rendering; and a purpose of ever-greater refinement. The four rules are
the negations — **restorative bias, sparse lattice, density cap with mandatory fallow, no
generational accumulation.**

**The Southernmost model** — cultivated equanimity, high base-rate sensitivity from proximity and
culture, forensic rather than explanatory knowledge, transmission by apprenticeship. Survives.
Produces no comparable material output.

**The variance-reduction economy, which neither society occupies.** Threadwork confined almost
entirely to healthcare, terrain, the fermented and cured industries, and civil defence —
overwhelmingly restorative, with a large diagnostic corps and a small operative one. Ordinary output
ceiling; famine, plague and structural-failure rates far below ordinary. Thin lattice, standing
tension register, attestation cadence. It accepts a permanent land-dispute layer and a decohering
calendar as the price. **It would look unimpressive beside the Einhir and would still be there in
three hundred years** — and §7.4's trap is the one thing it cannot escape.

## 8. `engine/season` — the strategic layer

### 8.1 The loop, and where it already has a home

**Manipulative work raises substrate tension in the province → tension produces incursions →
incursions drop Accord → low Accord forces the local faction into mandatory response → mandatory
actions consume slots before anything else is scheduled.**

**A benign, competent, powerful practitioner destabilizes the region by existing**, and the
simulation makes that something the player watches happen on a map rather than something a document
asserts.

⚠ **The home for it is `systems/threadwork/sim/rendering.py`, and it is a wired stub.**
`apply_rs_strain` and `check_calamity_threshold` are declared and unimplemented, while the world
clocks they would feed — Turmoil, and Mending Stability at `systems/overview/sim/ms_track.py` — are
live and already ticking. **This is the single highest-value piece of unbuilt threadwork in the tree,
because it is the one place the personal scale and the strategic scale actually meet.**

**Two corrections to the shape the suite proposed.** Strain accrues from a **held** manipulative shape
rather than from workings as discrete events, which is what makes holding a debt rather than a
purchase. And a being past the band maintaining itself is the limiting case, not a special rule — its
contribution is the same term at its own scale, continuously.

**Mending Stability's existing settlement consequences survive**, but should be keyed to **direction**
rather than to verb: it is restorative work that raises Prosperity, whatever verb performed it, and
manipulative work that a settlement pays for. The ±1 per settlement stat per season cap stands.

### 8.2 Incursion response by mode, and Locked Zones

| mode | response | why |
|---|---|---|
| **ordinary incursion** | **cordon and wait** | No self-maintenance; it deteriorates within hours. Force is unnecessary and **the exposure is the hazard** |
| **the transient kind** | **record it** | An event, not an entity. **Nothing to fight and no stat block** — an evening that was organized, and then was not |
| **threadcut** | different institution entirely | See Part 1 §2.5. A past-pull against one produces a Gap |

**Locked Zones: cordon and survey; do not clear.** Nothing there is going anywhere — the process that
would repair ordinary damage is precisely the one precluded, so **a Locked Zone will still be one in
a thousand years**, not because the damage is severe but because time carries nothing there. Loosening
a margin to permit entry is manipulative, costs a hold, and the margin **re-tightens irregularly** —
and re-tightening on an occupied margin has produced the confrontation signature in every recorded
case. A standing cordon is cheap; a clearance programme spends a practitioner's career and produces
casualties in a category no medical order can treat. **A competent commander uses one as an anchored
flank.**

**And a Gap is not a Locked Zone.** A Gap is an absence — a standing breach, or the hole left where a
configuration was removed rather than changed. A large one will produce a Locked Zone around itself,
because configurations at the edge of an absence have nothing to tend toward across it, but a small
one need not lock anything.

## 9. Presentation, UI, and what the player is actually shown

### 9.1 One perception path, not two

**Do not implement truth and perception as separate paths.** One function per perceivable entity per
tick: an external term (presence in the scene, occlusion, lighting — zero if not there), an internal
term (priors primed by context, plus confrontation residue), noise, and a criterion. What crosses the
criterion is rendered; how far it crosses is its **force**.

Three states fall out with no additional code: external near zero with a high internal term is
hallucination; external high with the criterion raised is derealization — the thing is there, it is
drawn, and its force is minimal; external high with a normal criterion is ordinary sight.

**Force drives every presentation channel** — depth of field, specular response, audio wetness, haptic
amplitude, animation weight. ⚠ **Low force is not fog and not desaturation. It is correct rendering
with reduced consequence:** full colour, full detail, no weight.

### 9.2 Insight is a second variable and it never helps

Knowing changes the codex entry, the dialogue options and the quest log. It changes **nothing** in the
render. The entity keeps its shadow, keeps occluding geometry behind it, keeps its footstep audio, and
a melee swing still passes through it.

**No dispel verb. No see-through-the-illusion perk. No truesight item.** Player-facing: *"Confirmed
absent — third pass. It is still there."*

### 9.3 Drift is channel inconsistency, computed on the observer's side

Build a per-character consistency vector and **desynchronize channels** rather than degrading the
model — eye texture tier against skin shader, blink interval, saccade lead before a gaze turn,
dialogue response latency, shadow caster pose, foot IK plant. Weight the timing rows heavily: the
uncanny in the wild is temporal, not a still image.

**Avatar appearance is not authoritative from the subject's own client.** Each observer computes it
from their own perception state, and when the cost of apprehending someone exceeds an observer's
budget they avert their gaze, add avoidance to their pathing, and drop into a dialogue mode that
answers the *intent* rather than the utterance. In multiplayer this is exact: two players in the same
room genuinely see different avatars for a third, **and neither is a desync bug.**

⚠ **The hard art constraint, and it belongs in the style guide as a constraint rather than a note:
never degrade toward markers that read as disability, illness, or racial phenotype.** Inconsistency
between channels is simultaneously the ethical treatment and the supported mechanism.

**And the single-player UI shows no Coherence number, ever.** The player learns their drift the way
the fiction says they must — from other people.

### 9.4 Two histories that never reconcile

Two stores, and **no code path that merges them**: what the player character experienced, written at
play time and immutable; and the world's causal graph, which a past-pull edits. A pull deletes a node
and every descendant loses its parent and is flagged orphaned.

Orphans get a decay timer at a fraction of a grounded equivalent's persistence, plus a distinct
treatment — normal albedo, **no contact shadow, no dust settling**. An NPC saved by a working whose
cause was later pulled keeps living, keeps talking, and casts nothing.

The journal shows both columns side by side and **the game never resolves which is correct.** A
"reconcile timeline" button would break the mechanic it displays.

### 9.5 Felt presence: the entity with no mesh

A fully participating agent with a null renderer. It has a nav agent, occupies the audio occlusion
graph and muffles sounds behind it, is targeted by NPC gaze solvers, and has a collider that pushes
crowd flow. It has **no mesh, no shader, no silhouette, no particle**.

**Never reveal it. There is no reveal to withhold.**

### 9.6 The Leap made felt

In first person, self-rendering is the thing the engine normally hides best — the player's own body.
During the window: the viewmodel hands swap to their own thread topology, so the construction becomes
visible; input-to-camera latency rises, so the player feels their own agency as mediated; head-bob and
breathing sway detach from locomotion and run on a free clock. Damage taken in the window forces the
reconstitution on exit.

**On a failed reconstitution one channel stays desynchronized permanently**, applied to the player's
avatar *as other characters compute it*. The player never sees it directly. **They find out when a
shopkeeper stops meeting their eye.** ⚠ And it is a permanent set, not a cosmetic flag.

### 9.7 Four endgames, not four endings

Entered by continuing to play, gated by the reach the character had **at the moment of permanent
loss** — not before, and not any reach acquired after.

| reach at the crossing | mode | implementation |
|---|---|---|
| below Relational | **freefall** | Control authority degrades: camera yaw drifts toward the nearest configuration, movement input blends with environmental pull, and the dialogue system loses the words — options shorten, then empty. ⚠ **Not transcendence, and it must not play like it** |
| Relational | **persistence through others** | All verbs route through knots. The player can act only where a knotted NPC is present, and the ability list is generated from the surviving knot set. **A knotted NPC's death removes an ability, permanently** |
| Structural | **reconstitution** | A new verb set at institutional scale — operate on a guild, a road network, a doctrine. The character sheet swaps for a map layer. Strain accrues at low rate |
| Foundational | **resonant** | Full sandbox, and the passive strain term is generated by **existing** rather than acting. Standing still in a town long enough tears it. **The most capable state in the game is the one where the player's presence is the hazard** |

### 9.8 Safety requirements — requirements, not recommendations

**Photosensitivity.** Flicker as a *mechanic* is off the table as a shipped default: gate it behind
explicit opt-in, keep the default under the general and red-flash thresholds, and run automated
analysis before certification submission.

**Depersonalization.** Transient symptoms are near-universal and the disorder is not rare, so a design
whose explicit goal is inducing derealization-adjacent states will reach players with a history of it.
A settings group must independently disable the criterion manipulation, the observer-side avatar
computation, and the input-latency changes — **placed in the first-run flow, not three menus deep.**

**Dehumanization.** §9.3's constraint, restated because it is the one most likely to be lost in art
review.

## 10. What must never be built

- **A Coherence meter its owner can read** — the mechanic and its display contradict.
- **A cure, a therapy track, or a stabilizing item for drift.** Recovery is time in surroundings at
  equilibrium, accelerated by mending, and it is elastic only.
- **A mechanic that makes non-sensitives forget.** The barrier is **inertness, not amnesia**: they
  retain the propositions and cannot act on them.
- **A dispel verb, truesight, or any percept resolved by knowing better.**
- **Clean, undetectable memory erasure.** A pull displaces; the absence has an edge; the displaced
  configuration orphans and decays.
- **An alignment axis, a corruption track, or an arc from good to evil.** Drift departs from the
  register in which better and worse apply rather than failing within it. **No sin, no corruption, no
  fall, and nothing being punished** — grading it is a category error, not a kindness withheld.
- **Uncanniness from mere category ambiguity**, or drift rendered as deviance from a human norm.
- **A fourth operation minted to give the intelligibility dimension one of its own.** Workings are
  distinguished by their intended target configuration, never by which dimension they move.
- **A stat block for a transient-mode event** — there is nothing to kill.
- **Coherence applied to a threadcut being** — the layer it indexes is absent by structure, not
  degraded.
- **Freefall played as transcendence.**
- **Farming confrontation for reach at full rate.** Induced development runs at roughly half the rate
  of spontaneous and costs the supervisor.

## 11. What was carried across, and what was not

**Carried across in full:** the four adversarial and investigative domains and their worked examples
(Part 1 §2–§5); the political catalogue, its three structural traps and its doctrine (§6); the twelve
sectors, the institution list, the Einhir test and the three models (§7); the strategic loop (§8); the
videogame systems (§9); and the safety requirements (§9.8).

**Carried across with the pricing rebuilt:** every cost figure. The suite priced against a depleting
10→0 track that the rulings replaced, so absolute Coherence numbers do not transfer. Scale bands,
reach thresholds and detection tiers are unchanged.

**Dropped, and why:**

| dropped | reason |
|---|---|
| The aligned/opposed **binary** and the doctrine built on it | There are three directions, not two, and the third — destructive — is what the binary had no room for. "Aligned costs nothing" also understates it: restorative work moves the practitioner *toward* their own equilibrium |
| *"Threadwork is structurally better suited to war than to peace"* | It rested entirely on destruction being free. It is not. **The reverse holds:** the largest free surface is medicine, and a practitioner's career is sustained by the work that heals rather than eroded by the work that harms |
| **Loosening** as a fourth named operation | Its rationale was that the intelligibility dimension had no operation aimed at it. Workings are not distinguished by dimension — that is foregrounding, and it is the one thing the framework forbids outright |
| The **"primary aim"** column on the operation-signature table | Same reason. What each working moves in all three dimensions is kept; what it "aims at" is a target configuration, not a dimension |
| A separate **subject-side damage track** | Unnecessary: the target of a manipulative working bears Coherence cost on the same quantity, because everyone has one. This also dissolves the naming collision the suite flagged as its own blocker |
| A third practitioner axis built on **absorption** | The suite drafted it and rightly declined to ratify it on a disputed instrument. The slot is real and now has a better occupant: **resilience — how strongly configured a practitioner is.** Not size, not reach, not imbrication |
| **Word alienation as a model of the barrier** | Kept as texture; dropped as mechanism. A fluency-decay curve that greys out a dialogue option makes non-sensitives *forget*, and the barrier is inertness |
| The suite's **diagnosis-costs** branch, priced everywhere as a live alternative | Settled: reading is perception at reach, not contact. It is not an operation and it costs nothing |

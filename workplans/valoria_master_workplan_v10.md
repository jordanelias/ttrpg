## 0. What this is, and how far to trust it

Jordan asked (2026-10-24) for a test and evaluation of the engine, the season loop and the subsystems; then for a code-based report of it; then for a master workplan v10 built from PR #501 and the other recent proposals, covering every recommendation, gap and issue, and **every row the v9 work produced that is not yet fixed**. This directory is that.

**What v10 does that v9 could not.** v9 was written before three things happened: the remediation proposal (#493) found that the formation and instrument defects behind most refusals were unowned; the subsystem-completion proposals (#501) added 83 live items across ten subsystems; and the code report (`-01`) re-measured the failure classes at the current HEAD and found them all still present. v9's batches B-Q to B-Y and B-T then landed *without* the remediation's batches, so the order the remediation assumed no longer holds. v10 re-sequences all of it as one plan.

## 2. The state of play, in six lines

(Detail and method: `-01-code-report.md`. Figures are seeds 0-2, four seasons, HEAD `b7a1e1b`.)

1. The engine is deterministic and well tested (913 + 1,786 + 1,960 tests pass; 3 fail in this container: two shallow-clone, one 1-ULP combat pin).
2. 67% of resolved acts end in a refusal and no success; 16 of the 48 verbs attempted never once succeed; 8 are never attempted.
3. The largest single cause is formation outrunning feasibility: acts formed on the wrong kind of referent (a read that finds no referent of the kind the verb needs, 47.8% of refusal events: `exists:<Kind> = 0` and `work`'s `condition` on a non-Site) and admitted acts that change nothing (2,081 decisions, 1,240 of them migrations into a full rung).
4. The instruments undercount: 37% of admission refusals never enter `driver.resolved`.
5. Information does not circulate: half the persons know no one after four seasons at seed 0; ledgers fill to a 200-claim cap by season four; a person's own telling is rare (`tell` won 54 of 236 attempts) though `told_by` claims are 27% of those held after one season.
6. The deepest cross-person causal chain (14 steps at seed 0) runs through nine refusals and three successes; no ending is reachable; `stability` reads 0 everywhere; there is no text layer.

## 8. Definitions coined here (`CLAUDE.md` §4)

- **Question M1 to M21 and milestone M1 to M3**: two unrelated numberings. `M1`…`M21` in `-05` and its citations are Jordan-queue questions; a milestone is always written 'milestone M1' (`CLAUDE.md` §0.2).
- **Re-base**: re-deriving a position's sites and its base reading on the batch's base commit before building (rule V1).
- **Home**: the one file that holds a position's full spec; a plan row names it.
- **Carried entry**: a v9 position copied into `-04` in full because its v9 home is deleted on adoption.
- **R8**: a still-held item stays in `B10-Z` until ruled, then slots into the next unopened batch of its cluster, else runs as its own sub-batch (v9 `_part3` §B.0).
- **Admission decision**: one call of the fold's `_admits` (eligibility, `requires`, counterparty) for one act, in the fold or the contest branch of `resolve`.
- **Invisible refusal**: an admission refusal for an act that never enters `driver.resolved`, so `aperture` and `corpus_run.attribute` do not count it.
- **Sub-batch `b`** (B10-03b, B10-07b): a batch cut beside its numbered neighbour on the same spine, for one reason its card states (B10-03b: a declared mover that cannot ride B10-03's EQUAL card; B10-07b: the decision round's chooser items, run after B10-07).

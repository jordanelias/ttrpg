## 0. Status, licence and method

**The licence.** Jordan, 2026-10-30, on being shown that the content-architecture suite, the layers-v2 design and the parliament work are not in workplan v10: fold what can properly be folded, "and then plan how to resolve all open design items with permission to design", using the agonist-antagonist method for accuracy and logic. Relayed by the fold's orchestrator; no ledger row records it yet (§5 H-1).

**What it does and does not do.** It licenses DESIGNING answers: a design file under `proposals/` may set out options in full, price each in code and in play, and recommend one. It answers no question; a recommendation is not a ruling. It does not lower the escalation bar (`CLAUDE.md` §0, "`needs_jordan` IS NOT A PARKING SPACE"): every item runs the five steps first. An item whose answer would overwrite a confirmed default (ED-IN-0301), a (RULED) mark, a verbatim Jordan ruling or ratified canon is ESCALATE with its default in force unless step 1 or 2 shows its premise superseded or retired. It licenses no build: every lane in §2 exits on "a design file approved; nothing built", and a build is a position slotted by R8 (`-00` §8).

**Verdict words** (this file's; `-05` §1's words keep their meaning there):
- **ANSWERED / CLOSED** — steps 1 to 4 settle it; the successor, document or precedent is cited.
- **TAKEN** — step 5, one option clearly right for the architecture; `[ASSUMPTION; medium; Jordan to correct; revert: …]`. Never where a confirmed default, (RULED) mark, verbatim ruling or ratified canon would be overwritten.
- **DESIGN-FIRST** — cannot be answered or put to Jordan well until a design states the options at build grain; names its lane (§2), home, what must be designed, its falsifier, and its tiers. The default in force stays meanwhile.
- **ESCALATE** — survives all five: two defensible options lead to materially different games, or the answer overwrites ratified canon or a confirmed default. Given as a plain choice, with its default (§4). An item can be ESCALATE and have a design lane: the lane writes the brief, Jordan chooses.

**Method — one round per lane (`CLAUDE.md` §0, §10).**
1. **Agonist:** `valoria-author` writes the lane's design into the home §2 names and returns a receipt. It writes its head and first part BEFORE it finishes reading and appends the rest; when it cannot recall a citation, the claim without its line number beats the lost part. Its inputs are the V0 extract, made once and handed down, never re-read per producer.
2. **Antagonist:** read-only `valoria-critic` (`tools: Read, Grep, Glob`; it cannot write) gets the design and the extract and attacks four things: provenance (every `file:line` opened), the N-line (V1's form, §2), the falsifier (can it observe the failure it excludes, `CLAUDE.md` §0.1 pt 2) and layer placement (`layer-conformance` Lens A).
3. **Reconcile** in the orchestrator: each finding is applied or rejected in one line. No second critic, no audit of the fixes (`CLAUDE.md` §0, "REVIEW RUNS ONE ROUND, THEN STOPS").
4. **Stop condition:** the design exists, has had its one critic pass, and ends either on a recommendation inside a taken or confirmed default (the lane closes on approval) or on a plain choice for Jordan (§4). "More could be checked" is not a reason to go on; before any second wave of agents the orchestrator stops and reports.

**Tiers** (`CLAUDE.md` §10 owns the IDs). V0: `haiku` via `valoria-measure`, ONE batched call (V0 list below). Producers: `opus` where the output is a choice between competing considerations (CL-TROOP, CL-OPEN, CL-PERSON, CL-MGMT, CR-38-after-#527); `sonnet` where it is bounded (CL-ATTR's composition and brief, CL-SLICE). Critics: `opus` on every lane whose output reaches Jordan or gates a build (all but CL-SLICE, whose critic is `sonnet`). `fable` authors nothing (RULED); the orchestrator may price it as CL-PERSON's critic, the lane where the producer is likeliest wrong.

**Fan-out: seven producers, seven critics.** Eight lane blocks (§2), but CL-OPEN carries CL-EVENTS' widening and the CL-CHAR dossier option in one producer (one home, `02_P1_templates.md`, and one schema question: what a world builder may author).

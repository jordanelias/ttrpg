## 0. How to read

- **Order.** Batches run in the spine order of the head (§4). Inside a batch the order line is the orchestrator's merge of the order each member's home declares; **where a home states a different order, the home governs**, unless `-08` overrides it or the card names the edge that does; `proposals/2026-10-24-master-workplan-v10/2026-10-24-v10-09-build-00-sequence.md` §6 is the one list of such reorders.
- **Entry gate.** Every batch begins with the re-base step (rule V1): re-derive each site by symbol, re-take the batch's base reading on its base commit, compare with the figure the home cites.
- **Exit.** The instrument and reading that close the batch, and its hash expectation (rule P14). A batch's finished positions leave this file in one net-deleting commit and the run stops for a cleared window (P3).
- **Members.** One table per batch: the state index. Homes: `rem-0N-…` is `proposals/2026-10-21-v9-remediation/2026-10-21-v9-remediation-0N-<name>.md` (the home of REM items; 02 holds REM-01 to REM-32, 03 holds REM-40 to REM-52); `cp-<name>` is `proposals/2026-10-23-subsystem-completion/2026-10-23-subsystem-completion-NN-<name>.md`; a `:n-m` suffix is the line range of the item in its home as the extraction lane recorded it (line numbers drift: re-derive by symbol). The *hash* column reads E (equal / none declared), M (a declared mover), ? (read the home); *needs* lists first the positions that must land before it (the ids its gate names and the file edges of `proposals/2026-10-24-master-workplan-v10/2026-10-24-v10-09-build-00-sequence.md` §1.2), then the other ids its gate names (landed batches and positions, hole rows, questions); a token after 'not a dependency:' is named for the record and gates nothing. The order line is P13 serialisation and is not repeated in a cell, except where the earlier member is also a dependency or shares a file.
- **Held positions.** A held position whose batch closes moves to B10-Z's table as HELD and slots into the next unopened batch by R8 when it is released. The same holds for a DESIGN-FIRST position (CR-11 with CR-25, SC-02, IN-48) whose design is written but not yet approved when its batch closes: its row moves to B10-Z as HELD and slots by R8 once the design is approved. Approval of such a design is the adopting reviewer's, not a `-05` question. No row moves before its batch closes.
- **Verb-step pins.** Every step that adds or retypes a verb re-records, by symbol and never by line: the `len(VERB_TABLE)` asserts in `engine/season/tests/test_governance_build.py` and `test_season_shape.py`, and the always-refused and executed sets. The march-kind assertion in `test_build_realm_determinism.py` (`march.declared` or `march.refused` must reach seed 0's first season) is a presence check that must still hold, not a re-recordable pin.

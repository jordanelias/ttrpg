**What this part holds.** One batch of the SC lane, `B24p-sc-proceeding`: the social contest built as V2 states it. A proceeding is people in a room making and answering points about one matter, under a rite somebody arranged, until those who decide come down (V2 p1 §A). Everyone in the room is one record (`Present`) whose role is two independent cells, `side` and `decides`, so one person may be a party and decide (A-139 as corrected); each exchange is one speech that raises a point, answers the point on the table, presses the man who made it, concedes the issue or rests, and moves each listener's own track by one draw; the close is each adjudicator's own draw with his own track as its leverage, or under consent a party's own accept. The module reads cells and compares no type, move or person by name; it takes a typed input, returns a typed outcome, keeps no state and writes nothing; the host builds the input (type row × context × power), derives the card, grades the margins on the one ladder and performs the typed writes the disposal row names (Layer 1, `04` §A.2). Each of the six attributes acts as Jordan stated: R1 to R12 (R11, the dice a blend of Reason and Presence by content and venue; R12, the decider's judgement), A-136 to A-140, A-141 to A-143 (the division of labour on a speech) and A-144 to A-146 (the adjudicator's five; silence priced; a broken temper cannot hold its tongue).
